**ParaRNN: A Big Brain That Learns Together**

Imagine a huge school of fish swimming in the ocean. Each fish (a small neural network) follows simple rules—turn left if it sees another fish, swim straight if it’s alone—but every fish also talks to its neighbors about what it saw. When all the fish move at once, the whole school learns how to navigate currents faster than a single fish could.

ParaRNN does something similar with “nonlinear RNNs” (tiny brain‑like modules that remember past inputs). Instead of training one module after another—slow and serial—it trains many modules in parallel, sharing information through a fast communication network. The result is a massive, flexible model that learns complex patterns quickly, just as the fish school adapts to swirling waters together.
