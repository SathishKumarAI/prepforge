A **vector database** is the kitchen where all those recipes live. It stores every numbered recipe (embedding) in a tidy pantry and lets you quickly find the ones most similar to the one you’re cooking with right now.

So when an AI asks, “What’s close to this image?” it looks up the embedding for that image, then searches the vector database for other embeddings that sit nearest on the number‑map. The result is a list of items that share texture, color, or meaning—just as a chef would find dishes that taste alike.
