**Training the model is like that practice session**:

* **Data (sheet music)** – The model reads millions of text examples, just as you read many scores.  
* **Tokenizer (musical notes)** – It breaks words into smaller “notes” so it can handle any song.  
* **Model architecture (piano’s strings and keys)** – The layers are the piano’s parts that transform the input notes into a melody.  
* **Loss function (mistake checker)** – After each attempt, you compare your playing to the master’s; the difference tells how much you’re off.  
* **Optimizer (practice routine)** – It adjusts the model’s “musical memory” so future attempts sound closer to the master.
