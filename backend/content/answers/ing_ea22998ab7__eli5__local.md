**Attributability is like keeping a clear “who‑did‑what” trail for an AI’s decisions.**  
Imagine a kitchen where every recipe (the AI model) has a list of ingredients, the cook’s name, and the exact time it was prepared. If someone later finds a dish that caused an allergic reaction, they can trace back: which cook used which ingredient at what moment. In AI, *attributability* records the version of the algorithm, the data set it learned from, who trained or tuned it, and the conditions under which it made each decision. This trail lets regulators, users, and developers see why a result happened, verify that the system behaved as promised, and hold the right person accountable if something goes wrong. It’s the compliance primitive that turns opaque AI into a transparent, traceable tool.
