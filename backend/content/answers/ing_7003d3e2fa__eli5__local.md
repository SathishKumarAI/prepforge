**Chain‑of‑Thought (CoT)** is a way for an AI to solve a problem by “thinking aloud.”  
Imagine you’re cooking a recipe that has many steps: first you chop the onions, then sauté them, add tomatoes, simmer, and finally season. Each step builds on the previous one, and if you skip or mix up any part, the dish turns out wrong.

In CoT, the AI writes down each intermediate idea—like the chopping, sautéing, and seasoning steps—before giving the final answer. It checks its own work, can backtrack if a mistake appears, and ends with a clear conclusion.  
**Unavoidable term:** *Intermediate step* – a temporary result that helps reach the final answer.  

This “think‑aloud” process makes the AI’s reasoning transparent, easier to debug, and often more accurate than giving an instant, single‑shot reply.
