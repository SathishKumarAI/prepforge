**Just‑in‑Time Retrieval (JIT) and Context Engineering**

Imagine you’re cooking a recipe that calls for “a splash of the right sauce.” Instead of keeping every possible sauce on hand, you keep a pantry with many bottles but only pour in the one that best matches the dish at the exact moment you need it. In machine learning, JIT retrieval works like that: when a model (the chef) is asked a question, it *searches* a large knowledge base for the most relevant pieces of information and pulls them right then.

**Context engineering** is how we design those “bottles” so the model can find the correct one quickly. We format the retrieved data into a short, clear paragraph (the sauce), give it a title (“Sauce: Spicy Tomato”), and include any key tags (e.g., “vegan,” “low‑salt”). This tells the model exactly what type of information to use, just as a label on a bottle tells you its flavor. The result is faster, more accurate answers—just like adding the perfect sauce at the right time makes your dish shine.
