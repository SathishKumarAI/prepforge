**Ownership in machine‑learning projects is like being the gardener of a shared garden.**  
When you “own” a model, you’re responsible for every seed (data), pot (preprocessing code), and watering schedule (training routine). You plant it, tend to its growth, and when weeds (biases or errors) appear, you pull them out—no one else can just fix the problem without your permission. If the garden starts to overrun or a new plant needs more light, you decide how to rearrange things; you’re the decision‑maker.  

**Ownership means:**  
1. **Accountability** – you own successes and failures of the model.  
2. **Proactive care** – you continuously monitor performance, fix bugs, and improve data quality.  
3. **Clear communication** – you explain your choices to teammates so they understand why the garden looks the way it does.

Just as a gardener takes pride in every bloom, an ML owner ensures their model is healthy, fair, and useful for everyone who depends on it.
