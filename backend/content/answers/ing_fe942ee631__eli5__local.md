**Live video** is like a classroom that’s happening right now, not a recording you can pause later. Imagine a teacher standing at the front of a hall while students watch her on a big screen. The teacher speaks and moves in real time; everyone sees it instantly, with only a tiny delay (a few seconds). In tech terms, the video is captured by a camera, encoded into digital packets, sent over the internet, and streamed to viewers as those packets arrive. Viewers’ devices buffer just enough data to keep the picture smooth, but they can’t skip ahead because the source is still producing frames. That’s the core of live streaming—real‑time capture, instant transmission, and immediate playback for all participants.
