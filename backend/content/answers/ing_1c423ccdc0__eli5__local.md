**Self‑Correction & Backtracking – the “Tree of Thought”**

Imagine you’re hiking a forest trail that branches into many paths (the *tree*). You start at the root, choose one branch, and walk forward. If you hit a dead end or realize a better path exists, you backtrack to the previous junction and try another branch. Each time you turn around, you learn something new—perhaps a shortcut or a hidden sign—so your next choice is smarter.

In AI, *self‑correction* is that learning from mistakes: after a wrong answer, the system revises its reasoning. *Backtracking* is literally retracing steps to explore alternative solutions. Together, they let the model keep exploring like a curious hiker, refining its route until it finds the best destination—just as you’d eventually reach the trail’s goal with fewer missteps.
