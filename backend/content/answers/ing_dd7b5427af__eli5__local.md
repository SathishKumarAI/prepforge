Think of an AI as a very eager chef in a kitchen that only knows how to cook when you give it clear instructions.  
**Prompt engineering** is like writing the recipe card for that chef: you decide what ingredients (the prompt) to list, in which order, and with what details so the chef can whip up exactly what you want.

- **Prompt** – the text you hand to the AI; it’s the “recipe.”  
- **Prompt engineering** – tweaking that recipe—adding spices, clarifying steps, or removing ambiguities—to make sure the dish turns out right.  

Just as a seasoned cook knows that saying “a pinch of salt” can change flavor dramatically, small changes in wording (e.g., “friendly tone” vs. “formal tone”) steer the AI’s output. The goal is to craft prompts that consistently produce helpful, accurate answers—much like getting a perfect soup every time by mastering the recipe card.
