### Why the Gemini 3.5 “Transcribe Live” price is set this way

The core problem Gemini solves for developers is **real‑time speech‑to‑text** on a scale that matches the cost of the underlying compute and data pipelines.  
1. **Compute Cost** – Every 1 k tokens of audio (≈15 s) requires a forward pass through a multi‑modal transformer that runs on high‑performance GPUs or TPUs. The per‑token GPU‑hour cost is roughly \$0.00025, which translates to about \$0.025 for 100 tokens.  
2. **Data & Storage** – Audio streams are first encoded (≈1 kB/s) and stored temporarily before decoding; this adds a marginal fee (~\$0.001 per minute).  
3. **Model Licensing & Maintenance** – Gemini’s LLM weights, fine‑tuning scripts, and continuous monitoring contribute a fixed overhead that is amortized over the volume of tokens processed.

The API pricing reflects these components:  
- **$0.025 / 1 k tokens** for the neural inference cost.  
- **$0.001 / minute** for bandwidth/storage handling.  

These rates scale linearly, so a developer can predict costs directly from expected usage, which aligns with cloud‑billing best practices and keeps the model accessible to both small labs and large enterprises.

#### Non‑obvious insight
Because Gemini’s transformer shares weights between vision and language modalities, the same inference engine processes speech as if it were text. Thus the **token cost is identical** whether you feed a 100‑token prompt or 100 tokens of transcribed audio—this cross‑modal sharing eliminates a separate “audio” pricing tier that would otherwise be necessary.
