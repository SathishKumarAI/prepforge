Think of an AI agent as a **smart helper robot in your kitchen**.  
You give it a goal—say, “make me a cup of coffee”—and it figures out the steps: fetch the mug, boil water, pour, add sugar if you like, and finally hand it to you.  

Just like that helper, an AI agent receives **inputs** (the world around it), has a **goal or set of rules**, processes the information with its “brain” (algorithms), and then produces an **output**—an action or decision—to move toward that goal.

*Unavoidable terms:*  
- **Input:** Information from sensors or data sources.  
- **Output:** The agent’s action or recommendation.  

No math, just a clear picture: the AI agent is your intelligent assistant, always working to turn goals into real actions.
