**Output Parser – The “Translator” that turns a model’s raw guess into something useful**

Think of a machine‑learning model as a very smart chef who can cook almost any dish, but the recipe book it follows is written in a strange language—numbers and symbols. When the chef finishes cooking (the model makes its prediction), the result isn’t immediately understandable: it’s just a raw list of numbers.

An **output parser** is like a bilingual translator. It takes those raw numbers and converts them into plain, human‑friendly words or actions: “This picture shows a cat,” “The next word should be ‘run’,” or “Turn the light on.”  
It maps each number (or group of numbers) to a specific label, category, or instruction, so users can read and act on the model’s answer without deciphering math. The parser is essential whenever you want the model’s output to drive real‑world decisions.
