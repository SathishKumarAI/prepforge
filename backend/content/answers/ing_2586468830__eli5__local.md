Imagine you’re a chef who has to make a fancy dish but only has a few kitchen gadgets—like a blender, a whisk, and a sous‑vide machine. The **agent** is the chef; it’s the part that decides what to do next. The **tool use** step is when the chef grabs one of those gadgets to help cook.

The **core agent loop** works like this:

1. **Perceive** – The chef looks at the ingredients (gets data from the world).  
2. **Decide** – The chef thinks: “I need to chop, mix, or slow‑cook.”  
3. **Act with a tool** – The chef picks the blender and tells it what speed to run.  
4. **Observe result** – The blender turns the ingredients into puree; the chef checks it.  
5. **Update** – If the puree isn’t smooth enough, the chef changes the setting or uses another tool.

Then the loop repeats until the dish is ready.
