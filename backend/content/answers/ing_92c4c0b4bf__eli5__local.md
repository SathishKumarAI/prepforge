Think of a library that can answer questions.  
*The **number of requests** is like how many people walk into the library over a whole day. It tells you the total volume, but not when they arrive.  
***QPS (queries per second)** is the speed at which those people are asking for help right now—how many questions the librarian can answer each second.

So, while the number of requests counts all visitors, QPS measures the real‑time workload. A library with 10,000 visitors a day could have a peak QPS of 50 if everyone bursts in together, or just 5 if they spread out evenly. Both metrics matter: one shows overall demand, the other shows instantaneous capacity needed.
