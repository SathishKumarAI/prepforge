Imagine you’re ordering coffee at a busy café. The time between saying “I’d like a latte” and the barista handing you the cup is *latency*. In conversational AI that’s the delay between you speaking (or typing) and the system replying. Even a few hundred milliseconds can feel sluggish, like a lagging video game; longer waits break the flow, making the interaction feel mechanical or “offline.” For chatbots used in customer support, high latency can frustrate users, increase bounce‑rates, and hurt brand perception. For voice assistants that control smart homes, latency is critical—imagine saying “Turn on lights” and waiting a long time before they actually switch on. Reducing latency keeps conversations natural, responsive, and trustworthy.
