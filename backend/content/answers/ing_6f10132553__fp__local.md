**Why the pay gap exists (from first principles)**  
The *value* an engineer brings to a firm is essentially the expected *incremental revenue* they enable per dollar of investment. For AI roles this revenue stems from two sources:  
1. **Model deployment ROI** – a model that reduces churn by 2 % on a $200 M product line can generate $4 M in incremental profit.  
2. **Operational cost savings** – automating customer support with LLMs cuts labor costs by \$3 M annually.

AI engineers (product‑centric, responsible for end‑to‑end pipelines, inference optimization, and production‑grade reliability) directly unlock both revenue streams. ML engineers, by contrast, focus on algorithmic research and prototype experiments; their outputs often require further engineering to become profit‑generating. Thus the *marginal product* of an AI engineer is higher, justifying a premium.
