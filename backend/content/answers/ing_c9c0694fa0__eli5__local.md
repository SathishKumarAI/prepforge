Think of an AI like a very curious referee at a soccer game, but instead of watching balls it watches *ideas*.  
When you give the agent a problem (say “write a short story”), it first **judges** every possible way to answer—each draft is a player on the field. It scores them with a quick scorecard: how well does each draft use the requested words, follow the style, keep the plot tight?  
The highest‑scoring draft is chosen as the winner and sent out to you.  
So the agent’s “evals” are its own little judging rounds: it tries many options, grades them on simple rules, then picks the best one—just like a referee chooses the best play.
