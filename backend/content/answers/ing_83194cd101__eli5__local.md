WebRTC is like a magical walkie‑talk system that lets each phone or computer send audio, video, or data directly to another device over the internet.

WebRTC works in three simple steps: 1) **Signaling** – phones exchange “I want to talk” messages via any channel (email, chat, etc.). 2) **Negotiation** – they decide on the format and routes (like picking a language). 3) **Data transfer** – once agreed, audio/video data flows directly through secure, encrypted channels.
