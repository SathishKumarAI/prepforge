Think of a library that’s built to let anyone find, borrow, and share books instantly, no matter where the books are stored. That library is like a **data fabric** – an invisible network that stitches together all your data sources so they behave as one system.

A **data architecture** is the blueprint for that library. It decides:

* Where every book (data) lives—on shelves (databases), in boxes (cloud storage), or in lockers (edge devices).  
* How the books are labeled and catalogued, so you can locate them quickly.  
* Who may borrow a book and how they do it—through a simple app or a complex workflow.

Just as a library’s layout lets people find any title without digging through stacks, a data architecture lets software pull the right information from the right place at the right time. It’s the map that turns scattered data into a single, usable resource.
