Imagine your AI is like a chef who keeps tasting and adjusting a soup while it cooks.  
**1. Observe (Taste)** – After the AI makes predictions, collect results from real users or test data.  
**2. Measure (Score)** – Compare those results to what you expected; note errors or missed patterns.  
**3. Learn (Adjust)** – Feed that comparison back into the model: tweak weights, add new features, or retrain on fresh data.  
**4. Repeat (Serve again)** – Deploy the updated model and start the cycle over.  

In this loop, “feedback” is the user’s performance data, and “continuous improvement” means you never stop refining. Just as a chef keeps tasting to get the flavor right, an AI system must keep learning from its own outcomes to stay accurate and useful.
