Imagine a big office building where everyone can check in when they arrive and leave when they go. The *presence platform* is like the reception desk that keeps a live list of who’s inside. A *pub‑sub (publish–subscribe) server* works like an intercom system: people (apps) “subscribe” to specific rooms or topics, and whenever someone checks in or out, the intercom instantly announces it to all subscribers.

In this design, each user device publishes a “check‑in” message to the intercom. The intercom stores that status in a fast memory cache (so anyone can read who’s present right now) and then pushes updates to every subscribed client. If someone wants to know whether a particular colleague is online, they simply listen to the relevant channel—no need to ask the receptionist each time. This keeps presence information fresh, scalable, and real‑time for all users.
