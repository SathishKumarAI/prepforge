**Step 2: Create a high‑level design**

Imagine you’re building a new city. First, you sketch a map that shows where the main roads, parks, and neighborhoods will go—no details yet, just the big picture. In machine learning this “map” is called a *high‑level design*. It lists the major parts of your system (data source, preprocessing, model training, evaluation, deployment) and how they talk to each other, but it doesn’t dive into code or exact hardware.

*High‑level design* is a blueprint that lets you spot potential bottlenecks early. Think of it as laying out the city’s skeleton before digging foundations. Once you have this skeleton, you can fill in details (specific algorithms, libraries, servers) with confidence that everything fits together smoothly.
