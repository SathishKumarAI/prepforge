Think of your microservice architecture as a bustling city where each building (service) has its own lights, doors and windows. **Observability** is like installing smart street‑lights that not only show when the lights are on but also tell you how bright they’re glowing, whether a door is locked, or if a window is open. The pattern language gives you a map of these “smart lights” – what to monitor (metrics), how to see it (logs) and where to trace a problem (tracing).  
- *Metrics* are the city’s traffic counters: they count cars (requests), note speed (latency) and spot jams (errors).  
- *Logs* are the street‑lamps that record what happened at each corner.  
- *Tracing* is like following a single car from its start to its finish.  

By combining these three, you can “watch” the city’s health without peeking inside every building, quickly spotting and fixing problems before they become big traffic jams.
