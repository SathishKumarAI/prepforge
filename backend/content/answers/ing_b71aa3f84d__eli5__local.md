**Disaster recovery in the cloud is like having an emergency kit for your house.**  
Imagine you’ve built a beautiful home (your data and apps) and stored all its important items—photos, documents, receipts—in a safe place. One day, a fire or flood hits (a “disaster”). Disaster recovery plans are the steps you’d take to get those items back: you have copies in another house (another location), you know how to open the safe quickly, and you’ve practiced who calls the plumber and the electrician.  

In Google Cloud, *disaster recovery* means keeping backups of your data in different regions, automatically switching traffic to healthy servers if one goes down, and testing that everything still works after a simulated outage. The goal is to keep your services running with minimal downtime and data loss, just like a well‑prepared homeowner keeps life going during an emergency.
