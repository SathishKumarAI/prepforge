**DOE (Design of Experiments)**

Think of building a new recipe for an AI model like cooking a dish you’ve never tried before. You don’t want to randomly toss ingredients together; instead, you plan a series of systematic trials—changing one ingredient at a time, or combining them in controlled ways—to see how each tweak affects the taste. In science and engineering, this organized testing is called a **Design of Experiments (DOE)**. It’s a statistical method that tells you which variables (ingredients) to adjust, how many combinations to try, and how to interpret the results so you can reliably improve the AI “dish” without wasting time or resources.
