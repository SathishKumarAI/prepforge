Imagine you’re building a LEGO city, but instead of just snapping bricks together, you first read a detailed blueprint that shows every room’s purpose, how the plumbing runs, and where the power lines go. A **software white paper** is that blueprint for a complex system. It tells engineers *what* the system will do (the “why”), *how* it should be built (the “how”), and why those choices are best (the “proof”). In a System Design Academy, students learn to write these papers so they can explain their designs clearly to teammates, investors, or future developers—just like a city planner explains the layout before construction starts. This clarity prevents costly mistakes and keeps everyone on the same page.
