Think of a prompt as a recipe you give to a chef (the AI).  
If you only say “make dinner,” the chef might choose anything—pizza, soup, salad—because the instruction is vague.  
Prompt engineering is like writing a clear, detailed menu: list the main ingredient (“chicken”), the cooking method (“grilled”), and any extra flavors (“with lemon‑garlic butter”). The more precise your recipe, the closer the dish will match what you want.

Context is the kitchen’s background knowledge—ingredients already on hand, dietary restrictions, or a particular cuisine style.  
Giving the chef context (e.g., “use only locally sourced produce”) lets it adjust its cooking choices accordingly.  

So, by crafting specific prompts and feeding useful context, you guide the AI to deliver exactly the answer—or dish—you’re after.
