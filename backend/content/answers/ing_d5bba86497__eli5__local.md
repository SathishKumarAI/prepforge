**The Lineage: ReAct to Loop Engineering**

Think of an AI as a chef in a kitchen that must both *think* (plan) and *cook* (act).  
In **ReAct**, the chef writes down a recipe (a plan), then follows it step by step, checking the stove after each move. If something goes wrong—say the sauce boils over—the chef stops the current plan, re‑writes a new one, and starts again.  

**Loop Engineering** is like giving that chef a *smart cookbook* that learns from every cooking session. Each time a recipe fails or succeeds, the book records what happened, refines its suggestions, and updates the next recipe it offers. The lineage—the history of all plans and outcomes—remains visible so we can trace why the chef chose a particular step.

So, ReAct is “plan‑and‑act with instant feedback,” while Loop Engineering turns that feedback into an evolving, self‑improving cookbook that remembers every decision.
