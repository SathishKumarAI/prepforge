Imagine you’re preparing a huge pot of soup for a big family dinner. The *ingestion* stage is like collecting all the ingredients from different markets—fruits, vegetables, spices, and broth—into one pantry. In AI, “data ingestion” means pulling raw information (text, images, numbers) from many sources—databases, sensors, social media—into a central storage place.

Once in the pantry, you sort the items: keep only what’s fresh, discard spoiled or duplicate pieces, and label each jar clearly. That is *data engineering*: cleaning, transforming, and organizing the data so that when the AI “cooks” (learns) it has reliable, well‑structured ingredients to work with.
