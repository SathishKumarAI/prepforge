Imagine building a **castle** that keeps its treasure safe while still letting guests explore it.  
In machine learning, the “castle” is your model and data; the “treasure” is accurate predictions, and the “guests” are users who send queries.

1. **Strong walls (data encryption)** – Keep all training data locked in a vault so that only authorized keys can open it.  
2. **Watchtowers (access control)** – Allow only trusted people to add or change data; everyone else sees only what they’re allowed to.  
3. **Hidden tunnels (privacy‑preserving techniques)** – Use methods like differential privacy or federated learning so the castle’s secrets stay hidden even while learning from outside visitors.  
4. **Regular patrols (model monitoring)** – Continuously check for unusual activity that might hint at an attack, just as guards would spot intruders.
