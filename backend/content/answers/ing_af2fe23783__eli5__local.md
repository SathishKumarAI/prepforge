Think of **Docker** as a kitchen that can make any recipe in exactly the same way, no matter where you are.  
A *container* is like a sealed lunchbox: it holds all the ingredients (your code, libraries, settings) and the cooking instructions (the operating system bits). If you open that lunchbox on any computer, it will “cook” the same dish without missing an ingredient.  

A **Docker registry** is the pantry where these lunchboxes live. It stores many ready‑made lunchboxes (images) so you can fetch one quickly and share it with teammates or deploy it to a server.  
Just as you wouldn’t want to bring fresh ingredients every time, a registry lets you reuse the same container image repeatedly, ensuring consistency and speed across projects.
