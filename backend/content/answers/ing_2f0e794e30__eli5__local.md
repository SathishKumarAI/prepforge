Imagine an AI as a curious chef in a kitchen that never stops tasting and adjusting.  
The **Inner Agent Loop** is the chef’s daily routine:  

1. **Sense** – The chef looks at the pot (the environment) to see how thick the sauce has become.  
2. **Decide** – Based on that taste, she chooses what to do next: stir more, add salt, or let it simmer.  
3. **Act** – She turns the stove’s knob or pulls out a spoon.  
4. **Receive Feedback** – The sauce’s new thickness tells her whether the change worked.

This loop repeats thousands of times a second, just as the AI continually observes its surroundings, plans an action, executes it, and learns from the result. Like the chef fine‑tuning flavor, the agent refines its behavior to achieve goals, never stopping until the dish (or task) is perfect.
