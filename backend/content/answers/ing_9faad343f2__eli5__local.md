Think of a library that keeps two special shelves for books you’ll need again.

**Exact cache (exact shelf)**  
The exact shelf holds the exact copy of every book you borrowed. When you ask for a title, the library just hands back that same book without looking at its content or how it relates to other books. It’s fast but only works if you request the same book exactly as before.

**Semantic cache (meaning shelf)**  
The meaning shelf stores summaries and key ideas of many books instead of full copies. When you ask for information about a topic, the library can pull out those relevant snippets even if the exact book wasn’t stored—because it understands the meaning behind your request.

In AI, an exact cache saves whole data chunks; a semantic cache keeps distilled knowledge (like embeddings or summaries) that lets the system answer new but related questions without re‑fetching everything.
