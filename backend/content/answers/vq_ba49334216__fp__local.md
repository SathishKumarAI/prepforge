**Why the body needs a “thermal buffer”**

The human thermoregulatory system is essentially an energy‑balance optimization problem: minimize metabolic cost while keeping core temperature within a narrow physiological window (≈ 37 °C). To do this, the body modulates heat production (shivering, brown fat activation) and heat loss (vasodilation, sweating, evaporative cooling). The key variables are *ambient temperature* (Tₐ), *relative humidity*, *airflow*, and *clothing insulation* (I). The governing equation is

\[
Q_{\text{loss}} = \frac{(T_c - T_a)}{R_t},
\]

where \(T_c\) is core temp, \(R_t\) total thermal resistance. To keep \(Q_{\text{loss}}\) within a safe range, we adjust \(I\).
