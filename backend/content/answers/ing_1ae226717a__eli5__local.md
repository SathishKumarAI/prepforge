**Kubernetes (k8s) is like a smart traffic‑control system for software containers.**  
Imagine a busy city where each car is a tiny “container” that runs part of an app. The city’s traffic lights, roads and maps are the *control plane*—the brain that tells cars where to go, how fast to move, and when to stop or start. Kubernetes watches all the containers (cars) across many computers (roads), deciding which ones need more resources, moving them if a computer breaks down, and keeping everything running smoothly. It also lets developers say “I want three copies of this car” and automatically creates them, updates them, or removes them when no longer needed. In short, Kubernetes is the invisible traffic‑control center that keeps containerized apps reliable, scalable, and self‑healing.
