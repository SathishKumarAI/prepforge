When the teacher sorts the line from shortest to tallest, she has to decide where to place each “—”. Should they go at the very front (tallest) or back (shortest), or somewhere in between?

In Elasticsearch, **missing values** work exactly like those blank heights. A *sort* tells the system how to order documents by a field (e.g., price). If some documents don’t have that field, they’re “missing”. You can tell Elasticsearch where to put them—`_last` puts them at the end of the list, `_first` puts them at the beginning.
