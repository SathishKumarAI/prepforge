Imagine you’re hosting a party and need to bake cupcakes. Instead of buying an oven, you call a bakery that brings its own ovens whenever you order—no setup, no cleanup, just the cupcakes delivered.  
AWS Lambda is like that bakery for code: you write small “functions” (the cupcake recipe), upload them to Amazon, and tell Lambda when they should run (e.g., when someone clicks a button or uploads a photo). Lambda automatically spins up an environment, runs your function, then shuts it down instantly, so you pay only for the few milliseconds your code actually uses.  
No servers to manage, no worrying about capacity—just write, deploy, and let AWS handle the rest.
