Think of Amazon EMR as a giant kitchen that lets you cook huge batches of data instead of single recipes.  
In this kitchen, each “cook” (a virtual machine) is set up just right for the dish you want—like choosing a stove type or oven temperature.  
You bring in all your ingredients (raw data stored on S3), drop them into a big pot (an EMR cluster), and hand the recipe over to a chef framework such as Hadoop, Spark, or Hive.  
The chefs split the work among the cooks, stir, filter, and combine the results, then serve the finished dish back into your pantry.  
Just like a restaurant scales its staff when orders surge, EMR automatically adds or removes virtual machines so you pay only for the cooking time you need.
