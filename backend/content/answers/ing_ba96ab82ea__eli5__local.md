Think of your machine‑learning model as a chef who needs a kitchen to cook tasty dishes (predictions). **Vertical scaling** is like upgrading that single kitchen: you add a bigger stove, more burners, or a larger fridge so the chef can work faster and handle more orders at once.

* **Add CPU cores** – give the chef extra burners to sauté several ingredients simultaneously.  
* **Increase RAM** – provide a bigger pantry so the chef can keep all ingredients handy without fetching them repeatedly.  
* **Upgrade GPU** – install a high‑speed blender that mixes many parts of the recipe in parallel, speeding up deep‑learning training.  

Each upgrade makes the same kitchen stronger, but you’re still limited to one space; eventually you’ll need more kitchens (horizontal scaling). This approach is simple and quick, just like swapping out a small stove for a big one.
