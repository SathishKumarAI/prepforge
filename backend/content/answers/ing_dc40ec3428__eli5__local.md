**Step 4 – “Add Agent Skills” (How AI agents actually do their jobs)**  

Think of an AI agent as a smart robot chef in a busy kitchen.  
The *skills* are the recipes it knows: chopping, sautéing, baking, plating, and even tasting to adjust seasoning.  
When you give the chef a new dish order (a user request), the system first checks which recipe fits that order. It then pulls the right skill set—maybe “grill steak” plus “make béarnaise sauce”—and combines them in just‑in‑time. The chef doesn’t have to learn every cooking method from scratch; it reuses and stitches together existing recipes, adding tiny tweaks when needed.  

So, *adding agent skills* means building a library of reusable recipe modules (functions, models, APIs) that the AI can mix and match on demand, just like a seasoned chef pulls ingredients and techniques to create any dish on cue.
