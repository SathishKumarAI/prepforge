Think of AI like a super‑fast calculator that can also read and write.  
If only a few big companies own the fastest calculators, they can solve problems, make money, and influence governments far quicker than anyone else. That’s the “concentrate power” risk: the smartest tools are held by a handful of players, so decisions about who gets what data, how laws are made, or which jobs survive go through those hands.

- *AI* (Artificial Intelligence): software that learns from data and can perform tasks usually done by humans.  
- *Concentrate power*: when a small group controls most of the tools or resources, giving them outsized influence over society.
