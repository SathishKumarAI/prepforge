**MLOps Level 1: ML pipeline automation**

Think of building a machine‑learning model as cooking a complex recipe.  
*Data ingestion* is gathering the ingredients; *feature engineering* is chopping and seasoning; *model training* is the actual cooking; *evaluation* checks if it tastes right; *deployment* plates the dish for customers.

MLOps Level 1 turns this kitchen into an automated factory: every step runs automatically, whenever new data arrives or a model update is ready.  
- **Continuous delivery** means you can “push” a fresh version of your model to production with one click, just like ordering a new batch of soup from the kitchen.  
- **Automation pipelines** are the conveyor belts that move ingredients through each station without human hand‑touch—data pulls, preprocessing scripts, training jobs, and deployment tasks all fire in sequence.

By automating these steps you avoid manual errors, speed up releases, and keep your models reliably up to date—just as a well‑programmed kitchen keeps customers happy.
