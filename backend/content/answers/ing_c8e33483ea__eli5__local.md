An **AI SRE (Site Reliability Engineer)** is the person who makes sure this “smart” system runs smoothly, just like a reliable heart for the house’s technology.

**Key parts of an AI SRE’s job:**

1. **Monitoring** – Watching the AI models and data pipelines 24/7 to spot glitches before they affect users.  
2. **Automation** – Writing scripts that automatically restart a stuck model or refresh training data, so humans don’t have to intervene constantly.  
3. **Scaling** – Adjusting computing resources (like adding more CPUs) when traffic spikes, ensuring the AI keeps answering questions fast.  
4. **Security & Compliance** – Protecting sensitive data and making sure the system follows privacy laws.
