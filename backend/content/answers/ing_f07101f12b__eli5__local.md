**Training – how ChatGPT learns**

Think of ChatGPT as a student who reads millions of books and then practices writing sentences. During *training*, the model is given a huge pile of text (the “books”). It looks at each word, tries to guess the next one, and then checks if it was right. If it’s wrong, it nudges its internal settings—tiny numbers that decide how much weight each clue gets—to make future guesses better. Repeating this many times, the student gradually learns patterns: grammar, facts, humor, and even subtle context.

**Key terms**

- *Model*: the collection of those tiny numbers (weights) that change during training.  
- *Training data*: the text the model reads to learn from.  

After training, when you ask a question, ChatGPT uses what it learned to generate an answer that fits the patterns it memorized—just like a well‑trained student who can write a paragraph on almost any topic.
