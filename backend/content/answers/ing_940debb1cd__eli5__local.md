**The Decision Framework – Tools vs. Subagents**

Think of an AI like a chef in a busy kitchen.  
*Tools* are the knives, pots, and spices that help the chef work quickly; they’re ready‑made, easy to use, and you can swap them out whenever you want. *Subagents* are like sous‑chefs who specialize in chopping, sautéing, or plating—each has its own skill set and follows a small plan.

When building an AI, you decide whether a task needs a simple tool (e.g., a pre‑trained language model that can answer questions) or a subagent (a separate module that learns to optimize routes).  
Tools keep the system lightweight and fast. Subagents give deeper control but add complexity. By choosing wisely—using tools for routine jobs and subagents only when extra intelligence is needed—you avoid over‑engineering while still getting smart, flexible AI agents.
