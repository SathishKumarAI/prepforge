Imagine you’re teaching a child how to recognize shapes. You show pictures of circles and squares, say “This is a circle,” “That’s a square.” After seeing many examples, the child can guess new shapes without being told again. That learning process is like **machine learning**.

In week 1 we learn the basics:  
- **Data** are the pictures you give the child.  
- A **model** is the child’s internal rulebook that maps pictures to labels.  
- The goal is for the model to *generalize*—to predict correctly on new, unseen data.  

We’ll also touch on two main families:  
1. **Supervised learning** (the child gets labeled examples).  
2. **Unsupervised learning** (the child finds patterns without labels).
