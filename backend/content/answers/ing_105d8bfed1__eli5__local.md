Think of an AI as a very clever but hungry chef in a kitchen that can cook any dish you ask for, but only if the recipe is clear enough to guide its hands.

**Prompt engineering** is like writing that recipe: you choose the ingredients (words), the cooking method (structure), and the seasoning (tone). A well‑crafted prompt tells the AI exactly what flavor you want—whether a formal report or a friendly story—and prevents it from getting lost in too many possible dishes.

**Context engineering** is how you set up the kitchen before the chef starts. You give it a pantry of background facts, a table of constraints, and maybe a few example plates. By feeding relevant context, you make sure the AI remembers important details (like brand names or industry rules) while it’s cooking, so its final dish stays on target.

Together they’re like giving a chef a clear recipe *and* a well‑organized kitchen—both are essential for a tasty outcome.
