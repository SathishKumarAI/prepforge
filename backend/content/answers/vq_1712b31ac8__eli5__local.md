Imagine you’re at a grocery store looking for a fruit.  
A **list box** is like a long shelf that shows every single fruit in the aisle—apples, bananas, oranges, grapes—so you can see all options at once and pick the one you want by clicking on it.  

A **combo box** is like a small card with a drop‑down menu: it only shows one fruit name (e.g., “Apple”) until you click the arrow, then a list of all fruits drops down so you can choose another.
