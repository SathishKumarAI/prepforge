**Prompt caching** is like having a favorite recipe ready to use again and again.

Imagine you’re a chef who often cooks the same dish—say tomato soup. Instead of writing the recipe from scratch each time, you keep a printed copy in your kitchen drawer. Whenever a customer orders it, you just pull out that copy, follow the steps, and serve the soup quickly. You save time because you don’t have to think about or rewrite the recipe every single time.

In AI, a *prompt* is the instruction you give to a model (the chef’s recipe). *Prompt caching* stores that instruction so the next time the same prompt is needed, the system can retrieve it instantly instead of re‑generating it. This speeds up responses and reduces computational effort, just like the chef’s saved recipe lets them serve customers faster.
