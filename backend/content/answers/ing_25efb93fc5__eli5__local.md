A *disaster recovery plan* is like having a duplicate cookbook hidden in a safe place—ready to be copied or swapped in if the original goes missing. In IT, it’s a written strategy that tells you exactly how to restore data, applications, and services after an unexpected event (like hardware failure, cyber‑attack, or natural disaster).
