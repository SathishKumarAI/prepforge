A “back‑of‑the‑envelope calculation” in machine learning is a quick, rough estimate you do by hand—like scribbling on a napkin—to get an idea of how big or slow something might be before you dive into code.  

**Analogy:** Imagine you’re planning a road trip. Before buying a car, you jot down the distance, average speed, and fuel cost to see if it fits your budget. You don’t calculate every mile precisely; you just need a ballpark figure to decide whether the trip is feasible. In ML, you might estimate how many training examples are needed for a model or how long training will take on a particular GPU. The goal is not perfection but to catch obvious problems early and set realistic expectations.
