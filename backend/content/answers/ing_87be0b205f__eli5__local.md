Imagine a group of chefs in separate kitchens all cooking the same dish at once.  
Each chef follows a shared recipe (the AI model) and, as soon as one adds an ingredient, every other kitchen instantly sees that change on its screen—no waiting for a delivery truck or a written note. That instant, continuous sharing is *realtime sync*.  

In collaborative AI, many users or devices are working together on the same data set or model. Realtime sync means their updates (new examples, tweaks to parameters, new labels) appear instantly across all participants’ systems. It keeps everyone on the same page, so the AI learns and improves together without lag or conflict.  

**Unavoidable terms explained:**  
- *Model*: a computer program that learns patterns from data.  
- *Data set*: a collection of examples used for training.  
- *Parameter*: a hidden number inside the model that gets adjusted during learning.  

Without realtime sync, collaborators would be like chefs cooking in isolation—slow, duplicated effort, and inconsistent results. With it, they create a single, ever‑improving masterpiece together.
