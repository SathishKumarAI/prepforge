**Prompt caching – a smart shortcut for “agentic” learning**

Imagine you’re teaching a child to bake cookies. Each time you ask the child to write the recipe, they have to remember every detail: flour, sugar, eggs, baking time. If you could give them a written copy of the recipe once and let them keep it on the kitchen counter, they would spend less energy recalling words and more energy actually mixing and tasting.

In machine‑learning “agentic engineering,” we do something similar with prompts (the instructions we give to an AI).  
*Prompt caching* means saving a ready‑made prompt somewhere safe so that every time the AI needs it, it can pull it from memory instantly instead of re‑generating it from scratch. This cuts processing time and keeps the AI’s responses consistent.

**Key terms**

- **Prompt**: The question or instruction you give to an AI model.  
- **Cache** (verb): To store something for quick future use.  

By caching prompts, agents run faster, use fewer resources, and stay more reliable—just like a child who has the recipe written down can focus on baking rather than remembering every word.
