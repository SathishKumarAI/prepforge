Think of a huge library (the big language model) that has read millions of books and knows how to write sentences in many styles, but it doesn’t yet know your favorite recipe or the exact way you like your coffee ordered. Fine‑tuning is like hiring a librarian who takes this vast knowledge base and trains it on just the few recipes you love and the specific phrasing you use. You give the model a small set of examples—your recipes, your coffee orders—and let it practice until it starts answering exactly as you’d expect. The core library stays intact; only its “tuned knobs” shift to fit your niche needs. That’s fine‑tuning: adapting a general AI to become expert in one particular domain.
