Imagine a chef who wants to bake cakes for a whole town.  
If he sets up his own kitchen (the “service”), he must buy ovens, get permits, hire helpers, and learn how to keep the temperature right all day. That’s the **overhead**—time and money spent on equipment, maintenance, and training.  

But once the kitchen is running, he can bake any recipe he likes without waiting for a bakery to open or asking permission; he’s **independent**, able to tweak flavors, add new toppings, and scale up quickly when orders surge. That freedom saves him from being stuck with a slow, shared factory that might not meet his exact taste.

The benefit of independence is the ability to customize, update, and scale the model on your own schedule.
