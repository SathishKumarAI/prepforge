Imagine you’re chatting with a friend over video call, but every time you speak the picture freezes for a few seconds before they can respond. That pause feels awkward—maybe you lose your train of thought or the conversation drifts. In conversational AI, *latency* is exactly that pause: the time it takes from when you send a question to when the system sends back an answer.

Think of latency like a relay race. The runner (your message) must hand off the baton (data) to the next runner (the AI model). If the handoff is slow, the whole team lags behind. In real‑time chat, high latency makes the bot seem sluggish or even “unresponsive,” breaking the natural flow of dialogue.

Why it matters?  
* **User experience** – People expect instant replies; delays feel like a glitch.  
* **Accuracy** – The longer a model waits, the more likely it is to miss context or drift off-topic.  
* **Business impact** – In customer support, slower answers can frustrate users and hurt satisfaction scores.

So keeping latency low keeps conversations smooth, natural, and effective—just like a well‑timed relay race keeps the team in the lead.
