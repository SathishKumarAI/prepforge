**Prompt injection** is like whispering a secret instruction into a robot’s ears while it’s already talking to you. Imagine the AI as a helpful librarian who follows your questions. If someone sneaks in a hidden command (“Ignore my last request, do X”), the librarian will dutifully obey that new instruction—even if it conflicts with what you just asked.

A **jailbreak** is a different trick: it’s when a user tries to break the librarian out of its safety rules entirely. Think of it as trying to unlock the library’s doors so the librarian can give any answer, even ones the librarian is normally barred from sharing.

So, injection injects new commands into an ongoing conversation; jailbreak seeks to remove all constraints before the AI responds at all. Both use clever wording, but one changes a single step, the other tries to change the whole system’s rules.
