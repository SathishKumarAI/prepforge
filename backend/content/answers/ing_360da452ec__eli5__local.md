Think of an AI like a robot chef that can cook many dishes. A **role guide** is the recipe card you hand it: it tells the robot what *kind* of dish it should make (e.g., “soup,” “salad,” or “dessert”), how fancy the flavor should be, and which ingredients to focus on. The robot follows that card instead of guessing from a big cookbook full of every recipe.

**What is a role guide?**  
It’s a short instruction set that tells an AI what job it has in a conversation—like answering questions, writing a story, or giving advice. By giving the AI this “recipe,” we keep its output consistent and on track, just as a chef follows a clear recipe to make the right dish every time.

So, role guides are like the chef’s recipe cards that shape how the AI behaves in each task.
