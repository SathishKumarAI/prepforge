Think of an AI model as a huge library that already knows many books (its base training).  
**Fine‑tuning** is like hiring a librarian who adds a few new shelves with specific stories you want the library to recommend. The librarian only changes those new shelves, keeping the rest untouched, so the library still knows everything else but now also excels at your particular topic.

**Alignment** is the librarian’s checklist: “Make sure every recommendation is helpful, safe, and follows our rules.” It checks the books before giving them out, adjusting wording or removing harmful passages so the AI behaves in a way that matches human values.

So fine‑tuning tailors knowledge; alignment ensures the tailored answers stay friendly and trustworthy.
