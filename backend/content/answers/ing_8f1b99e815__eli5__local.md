**Pitfall 11: Monologuing Without Interaction**

Imagine a brilliant chef who spends all day preparing a feast but never invites anyone to taste it. The dishes may be delicious, yet the guests stay silent, and the chef can’t learn whether they enjoyed the flavors or what improvements are needed. In AI, this is “monologuing without interaction”: the system keeps producing answers while no one checks if they’re helpful, accurate, or relevant.

A good analogy is a storyteller who never asks the audience if they understand the plot. If the story is confusing, the storyteller remains unaware and continues on the same path. To avoid this pitfall, designers must build feedback loops—ask users for clarification, let them correct mistakes, and use those corrections to fine‑tune the model. Interaction turns a one‑way monologue into a dynamic conversation that grows smarter over time.
