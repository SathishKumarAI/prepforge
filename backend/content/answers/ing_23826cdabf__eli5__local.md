Think of building an app like baking a cake that many people will eat every day.

**SDLC (Software Development Life Cycle)** is the whole recipe:  
1. **Plan** – decide what flavor you want and write the ingredients list.  
2. **Design** – sketch how the batter will look in the oven.  
3. **Develop** – actually mix the batter (write code).  
4. **Test** – taste a slice to make sure it’s not too salty or burnt.  
5. **Deploy** – put the cake on the table for everyone to enjoy.  
6. **Maintain** – fix cracks or add frosting when needed.

**CI/CD (Continuous Integration / Continuous Delivery)** is the kitchen automation that keeps the cake fresh:

- **Continuous Integration (CI)**: Every time a chef adds an ingredient, the oven automatically checks the batter (runs tests) so you catch mistakes right away.  
- **Continuous Delivery/Deployment (CD)**: Once the batter passes all checks, it’s poured into a new batch and slid onto the counter for customers without manual hand‑off.

The **CI/CD pipeline** is this automatic line: code → test → build → deploy → monitor. It ensures every change is instantly verified and safely released, just like a self‑baking cake that never goes stale.
