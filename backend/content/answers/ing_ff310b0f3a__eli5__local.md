**Context engineering** is like setting up the whole room before you give a speech. You decide who’s there, what furniture looks like, and how bright the lights are so that everyone hears and understands your message. In AI terms, it means arranging all the background information—previous conversations, user goals, or relevant facts—that the model can see before it answers.

**Prompt engineering** is more like choosing the exact opening line of your speech. It’s a short instruction or question you give to the model to steer its answer right away.

So, context engineering builds the *environment* in which the AI works; prompt engineering writes the *specific cue* that tells the AI what to do next. Both shape output, but one prepares the setting and the other gives the immediate direction.
