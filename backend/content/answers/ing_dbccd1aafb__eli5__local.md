The **DNS** (Domain Name System) is like the phone book that tells people the restaurant’s address.  
When many customers call at once, the phone line can get clogged, causing delays or dropped calls.

**Traffic management** is the manager who routes each caller to an available waiter or a backup kitchen so no single person gets overwhelmed.
