Think of an AI agent as a digital “smart assistant” that keeps a notebook in its head. **Memory governance** is the set of rules that decide what gets written, how it’s protected, and when it can be erased—just like you’d manage your own personal diary.

Best practices are simple habits:

1. **Label every note** (metadata) so you know who wrote it, why, and how long it must stay.  
2. **Store notes in a locked drawer** (encrypted storage) that only the agent and authorized people can open.  
3. **Set an expiration date** for each entry; after that time, the drawer automatically deletes it.  
4. **Audit the drawer regularly**, checking who opened which note and why.  
5. **Use a “least‑privilege” key**—the assistant only sees the parts of its notebook needed for the task.

By treating the agent’s memory like a secure, well‑organized diary, you keep data safe, private, and compliant with regulations.
