**AI UX Researcher – A Quick Primer**

The *Agentic‑AI* framework is a new way of looking at how artificial intelligence can act autonomously (“agentic”) while still being guided by human goals. In this context, an **AI UX researcher** is the bridge between users and the AI system: they study how people interact with AI agents, identify friction points, and design experiences that feel natural, trustworthy, and effective.

Key responsibilities:

1. **User‑centered data collection** – Conduct interviews, diary studies, or A/B tests to capture how humans use and perceive AI tools.
2. **Interaction analysis** – Map conversational flows, error patterns, and emotional responses to AI behavior.
3. **Design recommendations** – Translate findings into guidelines for UI/UX designers and AI developers (e.g., tone of voice, feedback loops).
4. **Ethics & trust audit** – Evaluate how the agent’s decisions align with user expectations and societal norms.

### Why It Matters

As AI agents become more autonomous—handling tasks from scheduling to creative content generation—their success hinges on seamless human‑machine collaboration. UX researchers ensure that this collaboration feels intuitive, reduces cognitive load, and builds trust.
