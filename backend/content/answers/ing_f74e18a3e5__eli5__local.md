**Case Study 07 – A “Text‑to‑SQL” Agent for a Data Warehouse**

Imagine you’re in a huge library that holds every book your company has ever written, but the books are all stored inside a giant filing cabinet (the *data warehouse*). You want to know how many customers bought product X last month. Instead of digging through shelves yourself, you ask a friendly librarian who can read your question in plain English and instantly fetch the answer for you.

That librarian is the *Text‑to‑SQL agent*. It listens to natural language questions, translates them into SQL (the special language used to pull data from databases), runs that query against the warehouse, and returns the result—all automatically. The agent learns which tables hold customer info, what columns mean “purchase date” or “product ID,” and how to join different parts of the database together.

So, just as a human librarian turns your request into a precise search in the library, the Text‑to‑SQL agent turns your question into a computer query that retrieves exactly the data you need from a complex data warehouse.
