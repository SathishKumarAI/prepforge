**What is UPI – the Unified Payments Interface?**  
Think of UPI as a *digital phone network for money*. Just like your mobile lets you call anyone with just a number, UPI lets you send or receive cash to any bank account using a single “phone number” called an **UPI ID** (for example, alice@bank). You don’t need the recipient’s bank details; you only need their UPI ID.  

When you tap “Send” on your phone, UPI instantly routes the request through the banks’ servers, checks that the sender has enough balance, and moves the money in a few seconds—just like a call connects almost instantly. The system is *unified* because all major Indian banks plug into the same network, so you can transfer funds across banks without switching apps.  

So, UPI is simply an open, real‑time payment channel that turns your phone into a universal money‑transfer hub.
