Think of a huge library that only has one door. If you want to find a book, you have to walk through every shelf until you spot it—slow and tiring.  
An **index** is like the library’s catalog card system: each card tells you exactly which floor, aisle, and shelf a book lives on. When you look up the title in the catalog, you jump straight to that location instead of wandering aimlessly.
