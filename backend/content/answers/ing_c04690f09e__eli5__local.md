Imagine your data as a garden of flowers. **PII (Personally Identifiable Information)** is the secret label on each flower—name, address, or phone number—that tells exactly who it belongs to.  

**Consent** is like asking the gardener’s permission before watering or pruning that particular flower; you can only touch it if the owner says “yes.”  

**Governance** is the garden’s rulebook: a map showing where each flower must be kept, who may walk on which paths, and how long the soil stays fertile. It sets standards for safety, privacy, and fairness so every gardener (data engineer) follows the same guidelines.

In AI projects, engineers first identify PII, obtain consent before using it, then apply governance rules to keep data secure, ethical, and compliant—just as a well‑managed garden stays healthy and beautiful.
