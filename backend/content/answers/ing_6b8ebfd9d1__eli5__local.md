Imagine you’re a chef in a busy kitchen.  
**Logging** is like leaving sticky notes on each station that say “Made 3 spicy tacos.” It records what happened, when, and where—so later you can see the exact recipe steps.  

**Tracing** is the chef’s walk‑through of a dish: she follows the taco from dough to plate, noting every hand‑off. In computing, tracing follows one request through all its services, showing how it moved and where delays popped up.  

**Metrics** are the kitchen’s scorecard—average prep time, number of tacos per hour, error rate. They give quick numbers that let you spot trends without reading every note.
