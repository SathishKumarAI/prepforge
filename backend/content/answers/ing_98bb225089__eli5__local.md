Think of a machine‑learning model as a **recipe book** that needs to learn how to make the right dish from examples. The `.fit()` method is the cooking lesson where you feed the recipe all the sample ingredients and their correct labels (the “training data”). 

During this lesson, the model looks at each example, tries to guess the label, sees how far off it was, and adjusts its internal knobs so that future guesses are closer. It repeats this over many passes until the adjustments stop changing much—just like a chef refining a recipe until the taste is consistently good.

So, `.fit()` is simply “train” in plain English: give the model data, let it learn from mistakes, and it will become better at predicting new, unseen examples.
