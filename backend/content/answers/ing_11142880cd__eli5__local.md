Imagine a giant library where every book is a sentence, and the shelves are arranged so that each word knows its neighbors. A **Transformer** is like a super‑fast librarian who can look at any part of the shelf at once, deciding how important each word is to the current story. It does this by giving each word a **weight**—a number that tells how much attention it should get—and then mixing them together to understand context.
