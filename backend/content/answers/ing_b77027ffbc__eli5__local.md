Think of a kitchen. A **workflow** is the recipe you follow—step by step: chop onions, sauté, add sauce, simmer, plate. It tells *what* must happen and in what order, but it doesn’t do anything itself.

An **agent** is like an automated cooking robot that follows that recipe. Once you give it the recipe (the workflow), the agent watches the kitchen, picks up the knife when needed, turns on the stove at the right time, and adjusts heat if something gets too hot. It can also ask for help if a step fails.

So, a workflow is the plan; an agent is the smart worker that executes it, adapts, and keeps the process running smoothly.
