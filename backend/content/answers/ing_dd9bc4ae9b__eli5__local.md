Imagine your large language model (LLM) is like a busy train station.  
The **metrics** are the signals that tell you how well the trains (the AI responses) run.

* **Latency** – How long it takes for a ticket‑machine to give an answer, measured in milliseconds.  
* **Throughput** – How many passengers (requests) can be served per second, like trains passing through at once.  
* **Error rate** – The fraction of tickets that are wrong or blank, expressed as a percentage.  
* **Accuracy / Precision** – How often the train’s destination matches what people asked for; think of it as the station’s signage being correct.  
* **Resource usage** – CPU, GPU, and memory consumption, like how much power the station’s lights and signals need.  
* **Model drift** – Changes in answer quality over time, akin to a station’s maps becoming outdated.

By watching these “train station” metrics you can spot delays, overcrowding, or faulty routes and keep the AI system running smoothly.
