**Framing the problem – the “AI‑native interview”**

Think of a job interview as a treasure hunt.  
The interviewer’s first task is to decide **what treasure they’re looking for**: is it a clever coder, a creative designer, or someone who can build smart systems? In an AI‑native interview, that decision is made *inside the computer*.

Instead of asking people to write code on paper, the system gives them a short problem (the “clue”) and lets the AI generate solutions. The AI’s job is to **interpret the clue correctly**—understand what skills are needed—and then craft an answer that shows it can solve real‑world problems.  

So, framing the problem means telling the AI exactly *what* you want it to do (e.g., build a recommendation engine) and giving it enough context so its “treasure” fits your needs. This keeps the interview fair and focused on genuine talent.
