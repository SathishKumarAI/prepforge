Think of an object store like a giant, super‑organized attic that keeps every photo, video, or document you ever saved.  
**1. Long‑term backup** – It’s the safest place to stash copies of files so they survive hard drives failing.  
**2. Disaster recovery** – If your office floods or a server crashes, you can pull the exact same data from the attic in minutes.  
**3. Data retention for compliance** – Regulations often require keeping records for years; the object store lets you lock them in place without extra effort.  
**4. Nearline storage** – “Nearline” means files are stored cheaply but still accessible when needed, like a book on a lower shelf that you can pull out quickly.  
**5. Data lifecycle management** – The attic automatically moves older items to cheaper layers (cold storage) as they’re rarely opened.  
**6. Content distribution** – Media companies upload videos once and the object store serves them worldwide with minimal delay, just like an online library.  

In short, an object store is a scalable, cost‑effective attic that keeps data safe, compliant, and ready to be retrieved whenever you need it.
