Think of an AI like a chef in a bustling kitchen.  
**Prompt engineering** is the recipe you hand the chef—what ingredients, how much, and what dish you want. The clearer your instructions (the better the prompt), the more likely the chef will cook exactly what you imagined.  

**Context engineering** is the pantry setup: all the spices, tools, and previous meals that influence flavor. By carefully arranging these “context” items—adding background facts, examples, or constraints—you give the AI a richer backdrop to work from, so its cooking (output) stays on track even when the recipe changes.

Together, prompt engineering gives the chef the dish, while context engineering supplies the kitchen’s atmosphere, making sure every bite tastes just right.
