Imagine you’re a chef asked to make a new dish.  
**Zero‑shot chain‑of‑thought (CoT)** is like the chef hearing the recipe only once, then thinking out loud as they cook: “I’ll chop onions… add garlic… simmer…” The chef has no prior notes; each step follows directly from what’s needed next.  

**Programmatic CoT** is like having a detailed kitchen notebook that lists every possible ingredient and technique. Before cooking, the chef consults this notebook to decide the best sequence: “If I want a smoky flavor, add paprika first; if I need sweetness, toss in caramelized onions.” The notebook guides the reasoning, but the chef still explains each step aloud.

Both methods let the AI (or chef) explain its reasoning, but zero‑shot does it spontaneously from scratch, while programmatic CoT follows a pre‑written plan.
