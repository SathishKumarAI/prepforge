Imagine you’re at a library looking for every book that mentions “climate change.”  
**Recall** is how many of those relevant books you actually find out of all the climate‑change books that exist in the library.

In other words, recall = (number of relevant items you retrieved) ÷ (total number of relevant items that should have been retrieved).  
If you pick up 80 out of 100 climate‑change books, your recall is 80 %. It measures how good you are at catching everything that matters, not just the ones you’re sure about. Think of it as a “catch all” score: higher recall means fewer important items slip through the cracks.
