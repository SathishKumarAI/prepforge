**Reference – the “road map” of a machine‑learning model**

Think of building a recipe from scratch. A *reference* is like a trusted cookbook that shows you how similar dishes were made before. In ML, a reference can be:

1. **Data reference** – a set of labeled examples (images, texts) that the model uses to learn patterns, just as a cook would use a list of ingredients.
2. **Model reference** – a pre‑trained neural network or algorithm that already knows how to solve a related problem; you “copy” its architecture and fine‑tune it for your own task.

When you cite a reference, you’re saying, *“I’m borrowing this proven method or data so I don’t have to reinvent everything.”* It saves time, guarantees quality, and lets others see exactly what was used—just like citing a recipe lets another chef replicate the dish.
