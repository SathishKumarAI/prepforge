Imagine a smart apprentice who learns to play chess not by memorizing rules, but by watching thousands of games and figuring out patterns on his own. That apprentice is like **machine learning (ML)**: a computer program that looks at lots of data, finds hidden relationships, and then makes predictions or decisions without being explicitly told every step.

**Artificial intelligence (AI)** is the broader idea of building machines that can act intelligently—like our apprentice, but also including robots that walk, speak, or solve puzzles. ML is one tool AI uses to become smart.

**Data science** is the craft of collecting, cleaning, and exploring data so the apprentice has good material to learn from. It’s about preparing the “books” (data) and interpreting the results, while ML focuses on the learning process itself.
