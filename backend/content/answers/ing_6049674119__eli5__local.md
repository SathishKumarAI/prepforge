**Agentic Retrieval‑Augmented Generation (RAG)** is like a smart student who writes an essay but keeps a notebook handy to look up facts whenever needed.

*Retrieval* = the student pulls specific information from their notes or a library while writing.  
*Generation* = the student composes new sentences, weaving those facts into a coherent story.  

“Agentic” means the system decides **when** and **what** to pull: it actively chooses the most relevant snippets rather than just using a fixed set of data. So instead of reading a whole textbook blindly, the agent asks, “What fact will best support this point?” and fetches only that piece before continuing.

In short, an agentic RAG model writes fresh content while dynamically searching its knowledge base to keep facts accurate and up‑to‑date—just like a thoughtful writer using their notebook on the fly.
