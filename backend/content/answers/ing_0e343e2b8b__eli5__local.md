Imagine you’re baking a cake and your first recipe (the **prompt**) tells the oven what temperature, how long, and which ingredients to use. You follow it once, get a cake, taste it, and maybe tweak the instructions a bit—add more sugar, lower the heat—and bake again.

**Prompting** is that single instruction set you give an AI: “Translate this sentence into French.”  
**Loop engineering** takes the recipe further: you keep feeding the AI new prompts, learn from each output, refine the questions, and sometimes let the model generate intermediate steps (like a list of ingredients) before producing the final answer.  

So, prompting is one‑off baking; loop engineering is an iterative cooking process where each batch informs the next, gradually improving taste—here, accuracy or relevance. The goal is a reliable, self‑correcting AI “chef” that can handle complex tasks by continuously learning from its own outputs.
