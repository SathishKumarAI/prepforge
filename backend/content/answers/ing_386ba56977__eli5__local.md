**Tier 1 – Short‑term “scratch paper.”**  
When the chef (the AI) is cooking, she scribbles quick notes on a notepad: what ingredients are being mixed now and which steps she’s following. This layer lasts only until the dish is finished; if you close the notebook it’s gone.

**Tier 2 – Medium‑term “recipe book.”**  
After a meal, the chef writes down how that recipe turned out—flavors, timing, tweaks. She can later open the book to remember what worked best for similar dishes. It stays for days or weeks but can still be rewritten if a new version is tried.

**Tier 3 – Long‑term “culinary archive.”**  
Finally, the chef stores her most successful recipes in an online database that survives years and many cooks. She can query it to find patterns across cuisines, learn from past failures, and plan future menus. This layer updates slowly but never forgets what it once stored.
