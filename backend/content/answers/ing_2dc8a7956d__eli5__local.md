**Natural Language Processing (Pre‑Training) – a recipe for learning to talk**

Imagine you’re teaching a child how to speak before they ever practice speaking with people. First, you give them a huge cookbook filled with every possible sentence—questions, commands, stories, jokes—from all kinds of books and websites. The child reads this “cookbook” over and over, noticing patterns: after *“I love”* comes often *“you”*, or that *“because”* is usually followed by an explanation. This first phase is **pre‑training**.

- **Natural Language Processing (NLP)** = making computers understand human language.
- **Pre‑training** = the broad, unsupervised reading stage where the model learns general patterns without being told what to do yet.

Later, when you want the child to write an essay or answer a quiz, you give them specific tasks—this is *fine‑tuning*. Pre‑training gives the model a rich vocabulary and grammar sense, so it can adapt quickly.
