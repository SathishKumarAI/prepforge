First, we pick a menu of “test recipes” – questions or prompts that cover many cooking styles (facts, stories, math). The LLM cooks each dish (produces an answer). Then we have a panel of judges (human reviewers or automated tools) taste the dishes and score them on flavor, texture, and presentation. We also check whether the chef used the right ingredients (accuracy), followed safety rules (no harmful content), and served the dish in a clear, friendly way.
