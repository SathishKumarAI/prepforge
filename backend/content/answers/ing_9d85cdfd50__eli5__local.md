Think of an AI model as a chef’s new recipe.  
**Regression testing** is like tasting the dish every time you tweak it—if something that used to taste good suddenly tastes off, you know a change broke something.  

**Continuous Integration (CI)** is the kitchen’s “always‑on” oven: whenever anyone adds an ingredient or a new cooking step, the oven automatically runs all the tests so no one ever forgets to check the flavor again.

**Evaluation** is the menu review—running metrics (accuracy, speed) on fresh data to see how well the dish performs.  
**Observability** is the kitchen’s smart sensors: logs of temperature, timing, and ingredient usage that let you trace why a batch failed or succeeded, so you can fix it quickly.

Together, they keep the AI “recipe” reliable, fast, and transparent—just like a busy restaurant that never lets a dish slip.
