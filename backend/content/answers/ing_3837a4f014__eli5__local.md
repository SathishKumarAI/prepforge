Imagine you’re a chef who needs a fancy oven to bake the perfect cake.  
**Self‑hosting** is like buying your own oven: you pay an upfront price, keep it in your kitchen, and can use it whenever you want. The cost comes from the purchase price plus electricity, maintenance, and maybe a small repair fund.

**GPU cloud arbitrage** is like renting ovens from a big hotel that has many chefs. You check the hotel’s rates each day—sometimes they’re cheap because the hotel isn’t full, other times they’re high when everyone wants to bake. By “arbitrating” (choosing the cheapest time or place), you can get the same baking power for less money, but you still pay a rental fee and must trust the hotel’s schedule.

So self‑hosting gives you ownership with steady, predictable costs; cloud arbitrage lets you save by hunting lower rates, though it requires flexibility and ongoing payments.
