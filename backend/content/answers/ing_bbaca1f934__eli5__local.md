Imagine your AI is a living book that never stops learning.  
Just like a book has chapters, sections, and footnotes, an AI system has *modules* (data input, processing, output) that fit together in a clear “table of contents.”  
When you read a new chapter, the book automatically updates its index so future readers find it instantly—that’s what **continuous training** does: the model learns from fresh data and rewrites itself without losing old knowledge.  
A good living book keeps an *editing log* that records every change; in AI we keep a “model registry” to track versions, performance, and safety checks.
