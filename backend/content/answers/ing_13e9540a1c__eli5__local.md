Think of an AI gateway as a smart traffic cop for data and model requests.  
When your phone asks the cloud to translate a sentence, the request first hits the gateway. The gateway reads the question, looks at its “route map,” and sends it straight to the right translation model—maybe one that’s fast but less accurate, or another that’s slow but perfect.
