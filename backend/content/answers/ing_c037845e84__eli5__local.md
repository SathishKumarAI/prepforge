**Step 6 – “Orchestrate with Agent SOPs”**

Think of each AI agent as a kitchen chef that can do one special task: chopping, baking, or plating.  
The *Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)* is the recipe book that tells every chef exactly how to perform its job—when to start, what tools to use, and which other chefs it needs to talk to.

When you “orchestrate” with SOPs, you’re setting up a well‑timed dinner service. The kitchen manager (the orchestrator) reads the recipes and sends signals: “Chef Chop, please cut onions now; Chef Bake, start the dough; after baking, hand over the dish to Chef Plate.” Each chef follows its SOP without needing to ask for directions every time.

This coordination lets many agents work together smoothly, just like a team of chefs producing a full meal from separate ingredients.
