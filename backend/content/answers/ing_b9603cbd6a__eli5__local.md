**TTS Time‑to‑First Audio (TTFA)** is the moment a spoken answer starts after a user asks a question. Imagine you’re at a coffee shop and you shout, “I need a latte!” The barista’s first pour of espresso marks the *first audio*—that instant when you hear something back. TTFA is that instant in voice assistants.

Why it matters? If the first sip (or sound) comes almost immediately, you feel the assistant is attentive and natural. A long wait feels like a dead‑end; you start to doubt whether your request was heard or if the system is broken. In practice, keeping TTFA low keeps conversations flowing, reduces user frustration, and makes AI seem more human‑like.
