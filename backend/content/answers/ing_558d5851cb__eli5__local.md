**Performance Gains (Cumulative) – Contextual Retrieval**

Imagine a librarian who not only knows every book’s title but also remembers the exact page you looked at last time and the questions you asked. When you return, the librarian instantly shows you the next relevant section without rereading everything.  

In AI, *contextual retrieval* works like that librarian: each query builds on what came before, so the system narrows its search to a smaller, more precise set of information. Because it never starts from scratch, every extra piece of context “cumulatively” improves speed and accuracy. The result is fewer mistakes, faster answers, and a smoother conversation—just as the librarian saves you time by using your own reading history.
