**Context Engineering in machine learning**  
Imagine you’re a chef preparing a dish for a guest who loves spicy food but can’t handle too much heat. The *context* is the guest’s taste preference, and *engineering the context* means you adjust the recipe—add just enough chili, maybe a splash of lime—to match that preference before cooking. In machine learning, “context engineering” is the same idea: we tweak the data or the way it’s fed into a model so that the AI understands the situation better. For example, if a language model must answer questions about medical guidelines, we first give it a short summary of those guidelines (the context). That brief, relevant information helps the model produce accurate, useful answers instead of guessing wildly. By carefully crafting this “background” before the main task, we guide the AI to behave exactly how we want.
