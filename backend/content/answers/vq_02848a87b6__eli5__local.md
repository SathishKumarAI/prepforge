**Precognition prompting** is a way of asking an AI to *think ahead* before giving an answer—just like telling a student to “first imagine the whole picture, then point out the details.”  
You give the model a short question (e.g., “What’s the best next step for a new product launch?”) and add a cue such as “First, list all the possible outcomes in order of likelihood.” The AI then creates a quick mental roadmap of what could happen, ranking each possibility. After that, it returns to the original question with a more informed answer.

Use this when you want deeper insight, risk assessment, or a strategic overview—especially for complex decisions where knowing the range of future possibilities makes your final choice stronger.
