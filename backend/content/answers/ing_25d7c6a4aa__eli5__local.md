To keep the kitchen running smoothly, you need a way to see what’s happening inside: which ingredients are being used, how long each step takes, and whether something goes wrong. That’s where **AI observability** comes in.

Pydantic Logfire acts like a high‑tech recipe book that records every action in real time. Pydantic validates the “ingredients” (the data the model receives), ensuring they’re the right type—just as a recipe checks you have flour, sugar, etc. Logfire then logs each step, timestamps it, and notes any errors or delays.
