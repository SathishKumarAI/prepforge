Imagine you’re hosting a dinner party with guests that can talk to each other automatically—this is your conversational agent. **Security requirements** are the rules and tools you set up so everyone stays safe and respectful.

1. **Authentication (Who’s talking?)** – Like checking IDs at the door, the system verifies who the user really is before letting them chat.
2. **Authorization (What can they do?)** – Once inside, guests have different “access passes”: some may only ask questions, others can change settings—this keeps sensitive data out of the wrong hands.
3. **Confidentiality (Keep secrets)**** – Just as you’d use a private table for personal conversations, encryption hides messages from eavesdroppers.
4. **Integrity (No tampering)** – Like having a lock on your notes so nobody writes false answers, checksums or digital signatures ensure the agent’s responses aren’t altered in transit.
5. **Auditability (Record keeping)** – Think of a guest book that logs who said what and when, enabling you to review if something goes wrong.

By treating every interaction as a guarded conversation, the agent can talk freely while protecting users, data, and its own trustworthiness.
