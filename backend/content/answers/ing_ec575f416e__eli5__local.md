**Review queue management in content moderation is like a busy post‑office sorting center for messages that need extra checking before they’re delivered to the public.**

When someone posts something—an article, photo, or comment—the system first tags it with a “needs review” flag. Those flagged items pile up in a *review queue*, just as mail sits on a conveyor belt waiting for a clerk to look at it. Moderators (or automated tools) pull items from the front of the queue one by one. They decide whether the content is safe, violates rules, or can stay as is.

*Queue* means a line that follows first‑in, first‑out order. *Moderation* is the process of examining and deciding on each item. By keeping the queue organized—sorting by urgency, topic, or user reputation—the system ensures that potentially harmful content is caught quickly while normal posts flow smoothly to readers.
