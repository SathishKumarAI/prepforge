Think of your phone’s voice assistant as a tiny chef who can cook a quick snack right in the kitchen instead of sending the recipe to a distant restaurant.  
A **small, on‑device AI model** is that chef: it lives inside your device (the “kitchen”) and uses only a few gigabytes of memory—just enough to recognize simple patterns or answer basic questions. Because everything stays local, the assistant can react instantly, never waiting for data to travel over the internet, and it keeps your private conversations hidden from outside servers.

These models are built by **compressing** larger “master chefs” (big AI systems) into leaner versions that still know enough to do useful tasks. They trade a bit of accuracy for speed, privacy, and low battery use—exactly what you need when you’re on the go or in areas with weak connectivity.
