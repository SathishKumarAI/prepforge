Imagine making a sandwich.  
With a **cascaded pipeline** you take separate steps: first you get the bread, then add lettuce, then turkey, and finally a dressing. Each step is done by a different “chef” that’s really good at its job. If one part goes wrong, you can swap just that chef without rebuilding the whole sandwich.

With **speech‑to‑speech** it’s like having a single master chef who prepares the entire sandwich in one go—flourishes, timing, and flavor all come from one recipe. It can be faster and more natural if the chef is skilled, but if something goes wrong you have to retrain the whole recipe.

So choose a cascaded pipeline when you want modular control and easy debugging; choose end‑to‑end speech‑to‑speech when speed and seamless flow matter most and you’re comfortable training one big model.
