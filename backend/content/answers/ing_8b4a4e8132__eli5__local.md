**Hidden Cloud Costs – a “Water Leak” in Your House**

Think of the cloud like a big house that you rent. You pay for the rooms (storage, servers) and the water bill (data transfer). But sometimes there’s a hidden leak: tiny pipes under the floor that drip unnoticed until the meter spikes.

In the cloud, those leaks are extra charges that pop up when you do things you didn’t plan for—like moving data between regions, keeping old snapshots, or having servers idle but still billed. They’re called *hidden costs* because they’re not in the basic price tag; they appear only after you start using more features.

**Key terms**

- **Instance** – a virtual computer that runs your code.
- **Storage tier** – levels of data storage with different speeds and prices (hot, cool, archive).
