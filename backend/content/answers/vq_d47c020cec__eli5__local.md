Think of a dataframe as a spreadsheet that lives in your computer’s memory.  
Just like you can sort, filter, and add columns to a sheet, you do the same with a dataframe, but faster because it’s built for code.

**Sort** – Re‑order rows so the most important ones come first (like arranging books by title).  
**Filter** – Keep only rows that meet a rule (pick out all students who scored over 90).  
**Select columns** – Pick just the pieces you need, like choosing which columns to print on a receipt.  
**Add/modify columns** – Compute new data from existing ones, as if adding a “Grade + Bonus” column.  
**Join** – Combine two tables by matching keys, similar to merging two spreadsheets on a common ID.

These are the core “operations”: rearrange, narrow down, pick, create, and merge—everything that lets you shape raw data into insights.
