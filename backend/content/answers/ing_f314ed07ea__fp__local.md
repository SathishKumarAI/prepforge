Mathematically we want  

\[
\max_{x \in \mathcal{X}} \; U(x)\quad\text{s.t.}\quad C(x)=0,
\]

where \(U\) rewards surprise and relevance, while \(C\) encodes feasibility (grammar, physics, brand guidelines).  
Traditional tools let humans evaluate \(U\) by intuition; AI systems now *sample* \(\mathcal{X}\) efficiently using generative models, gradient‑based search, or evolutionary strategies.

3. **Iterative refinement** – Human creators iterate; reinforcement learning lets AI “play” the same role, proposing variations, receiving feedback, and converging faster.
