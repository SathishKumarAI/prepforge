Imagine building a car (software) and then making a self‑driving version of that car (machine learning model).  
**DevOps** is the crew that builds, tests, and ships every new car model quickly—think assembly lines, quality checks, and instant delivery to customers.  

**MLOps** adds another layer: the car now has sensors, a GPS, and a brain that learns from traffic data. The crew must not only deliver cars but also keep the learning system updated, validate its predictions, retrain it with new data, and deploy those updates safely—all while monitoring for safety glitches.  

So DevOps = continuous delivery of code; MLOps = continuous delivery *plus* automated training, validation, and safe deployment pipelines for ML models in a cloud‑based environment.
