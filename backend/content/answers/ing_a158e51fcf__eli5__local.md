Think of a machine‑learning interview like asking a chef how they cook a new dish.  
**Interviewers want you to show:**

1. **Problem understanding** – Can you explain the recipe (the data problem) and why it matters?  
2. **Tool selection** – Do you know which ingredients (algorithms) fit best, and can you justify picking them over others?  
3. **Implementation sense** – Are you comfortable turning the recipe into code, handling spills (missing values), seasoning properly (feature scaling), and tasting for balance (validation)?  
4. **Evaluation & improvement** – Can you judge the finished dish with a taste test (metrics like accuracy or loss) and suggest tweaks?  

Your mental model: *data is raw ingredients; preprocessing cleans and prepares them; models are cooking techniques; training is seasoning; evaluation is the tasting; deployment is serving the meal.*  
If you can walk through this flow clearly, interviewers will see that you’re not just following recipes—you understand why each step matters.
