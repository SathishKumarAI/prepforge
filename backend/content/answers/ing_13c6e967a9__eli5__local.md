**Agentic** is the fancy word for “acting on your own behalf.”  
Think of a robot that’s just a tool—like a calculator or a vacuum cleaner. It does what you tell it to do and stops when you stop giving commands. An *agentic* AI, by contrast, is more like a helpful co‑worker who can decide how best to finish the task before you even ask for it. It sets its own short‑term goals (e.g., “I’ll draft this email first”) and then carries them out, while still staying aligned with your overall direction (“Make sure the tone is friendly”). In 2026 this means new jobs where people hire AI to be proactive assistants: project planners, creative brainstormers, or customer‑service moderators that initiate solutions rather than just reply to inputs.
