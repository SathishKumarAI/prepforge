**Non‑functional requirements (NFRs) are the “how well” rules that an enterprise AI system must follow, not the “what it does.”**  
Think of a high‑speed train: the *routes* (features) tell you where it can go, but NFRs are like the safety and comfort standards—track gauge, maximum speed, braking distance, noise limits, and emergency stops. For AI in business, they cover things such as **performance** (how fast the model answers), **scalability** (handling more users without slowing down), **security** (protecting data from breaches), **privacy** (respecting user consent), **reliability** (working 24/7 with minimal downtime), and **usability** (easy for staff to interpret results). These rules must be met before the AI can safely run in a real company, just as the train cannot operate until all safety checks pass.
