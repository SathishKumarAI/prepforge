- **Producer**: You, the person writing and preparing the postcards.
- **API (Application Programming Interface)**: A set of instructions that tells your computer how to talk to the mailing system—how to write a postcard, where to put it in the mail queue, and which city’s post office should receive it.

1. What message (postcard) you want to send.
