**The two phases of inference in an AI model are like a detective and a judge.**

First, the **detector (extraction)** looks at all the clues—words, images, sounds—and turns them into numbers that the computer can understand. Think of it as translating a mystery story into a list of facts.

Second, the **judge (prediction)** takes those facts and decides what they mean. It weighs the evidence to give an answer: “This picture is a cat” or “She’s probably going to win.”  

So inference = *detect clues → translate them → judge the outcome*. This two‑step process lets AI read, understand, and respond to new data it hasn’t seen before.
