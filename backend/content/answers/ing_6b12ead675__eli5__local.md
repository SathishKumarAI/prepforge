Imagine you’re trying to judge how good a new recipe is by letting only three friends taste it. If they all love it, you might think the dish is perfect, but the real test would be a large crowd with varied tastes. In AI, “small test set” means evaluating a model on just a handful of examples. A tiny set can make a model look great because it memorizes those few cases, not because it truly understands the task. Like your friends’ limited palate, the results don’t reveal how well the AI will perform in the real world with many different inputs.

**Key terms**
- **Test set**: The group of examples used to check if the model works after training.
- **Capability assessment**: Measuring what the AI can actually do, not just whether it got a few cases right.
