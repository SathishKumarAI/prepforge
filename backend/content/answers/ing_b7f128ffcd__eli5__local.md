**Key Takeaway – What Are “Agentic Systems”?**  
Think of an agentic system like a smart robot in a kitchen that can decide what to cook, how much seasoning to add, and when to start the timer—all without you telling it each step. It has **agency**, meaning it can set its own goals (e.g., make a healthy meal), choose actions (pick ingredients, stir), and learn from the results (if the dish was too salty, next time adjust). In AI, an agentic system is software that watches data, thinks about what to do next, acts on the world, and updates its plan based on feedback. The “agent” part comes from *agency* (self‑directed decision making), while *system* reminds us it’s a whole set of connected components—sensors, processors, and effectors—working together. This lets AI move beyond simple instructions to truly autonomous problem solving.
