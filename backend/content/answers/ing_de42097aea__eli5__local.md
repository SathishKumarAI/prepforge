Think of an AI model as a student taking a final exam, and **target metrics** are the scores you set for each section before the test starts. They tell the instructor (the developer) exactly how well the student must do on *accuracy*, *speed*, *fairness* or any other skill.

Imagine a cooking contest: the judge says the dish must score at least 8/10 in taste, no more than 5 minutes to prepare, and use no more than two ingredients. Those numbers are the target metrics. If the chef meets all of them, the dish wins; if even one falls short, it’s disqualified.

In AI, you pick a metric—say “accuracy must be ≥ 92%”—and the system is built and tuned until that number is reliably reached on fresh data. The targets keep everyone focused on what matters most and let you measure progress objectively, just like grading rubrics in school.
