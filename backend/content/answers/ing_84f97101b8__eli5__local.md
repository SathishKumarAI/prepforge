**MCP – Model Context Protocol**

Think of a conversation between you and a helpful robot that can answer questions, write stories, or solve puzzles. The *Model Context Protocol* (MCP) is the set‑of rules that tells the robot how to keep track of everything that has happened so far in the chat—what you asked, what it answered, any follow‑up hints, and even the tone you’re using.

Imagine a recipe book where each page lists ingredients (your messages), cooking steps (the model’s replies), and notes on flavor tweaks (contextual cues). MCP is like the book’s index: it ensures that when the robot goes back to cook, it remembers the exact order of ingredients and steps, so the dish stays consistent.
