**Observability for LLM Apps – a “Health‑Check Dashboard” for your AI**

Think of an LLM (large language model) as a busy kitchen in a restaurant. *Observability* is the set of tools that lets you see how the kitchen runs without opening every door: you can watch the stove temperature, hear the oven’s hum, and read the recipe logs. In software terms, observability means collecting three kinds of data:

1. **Metrics** – quick numbers (e.g., request latency, token usage) that tell you if things are fast or slow.  
2. **Logs** – detailed text records of every step (e.g., prompts received, errors thrown).  
3. **Traces** – a timeline that shows how one request moves through the system (from input to output).

By feeding these signals into a dashboard, you can spot bottlenecks, debug failures, and prove compliance—just as a chef uses a thermometer, a stopwatch, and a recipe book to keep the kitchen humming.
