Think of an online game where players press a button and a new level should load almost instantly.  
**Latency** is the time between that button press and the level appearing on screen – the “wait” you feel.  

A **latency budget** is like setting a rule: *“All levels must finish loading within 200 ms for 95 % of the players.”* The number 200 ms is the budget, and **p95** (the 95th percentile) means that only 5 % of loads can exceed it; the rest must stay below.  

Interviewers want to see your table because it shows how you split that 200 ms among different parts of the system: database query, image processing, network hop, etc., and whether each part keeps its share under the p95 limit. It’s a practical way to prove you can keep users from waiting too long while still doing all the work needed.
