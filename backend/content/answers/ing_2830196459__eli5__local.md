Think of training a machine‑learning model like teaching a child to recognize different kinds of fruit.

**The “lot” is the collection of examples (data) you give the child.**  
You show many apples, oranges, bananas, and occasionally some mixed‑fruit bowls. Each example has two parts:

The child (the algorithm) watches these pairs over and over.
