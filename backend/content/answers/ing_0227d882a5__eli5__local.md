**RAGAS** (short for *Retrieval-Augmented Generation Assessment System*) is a way of checking how good an AI that writes text really is when it uses extra information from the web or a database.

Think of the AI as a student writing an essay.  
* **Retrieval** = the student looks up facts in a library (the AI pulls data from its knowledge base).  
* **Generation** = the student composes sentences with those facts.  

RAGAS measures two things:  
1. **Accuracy** – Did the student use the right books? (Did the AI fetch correct info?)  
2. **Coherence** – Does the essay read smoothly? (Is the generated text clear and relevant?)

So RAGAS is like a teacher grading both how well a student cites sources and how well they write, giving us a single score that tells us if the AI’s “essay” is trustworthy and useful.
