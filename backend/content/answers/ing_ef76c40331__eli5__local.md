Imagine you’re a detective watching a crime scene that changes every minute—like the temperature in your kitchen or the number of people walking past a window. **Time‑series analysis** is the detective’s notebook: it records each moment (the data points) and looks for patterns, trends, or “clues” that repeat over time.

In practice, you line up all the observations by their timestamps—just like arranging photographs in chronological order. Then you examine how the values move: do they slowly rise (a trend), swing regularly (seasonality), or jump randomly? By spotting these behaviors, you can predict what will happen next—say, estimating tomorrow’s temperature or forecasting sales for the next quarter.
