Imagine a chef who can whip up any dish from scratch—this is a generative AI model that “creates” text, images, or music.  
**Evaluating** is like tasting the food: we give the chef a set of test recipes (known inputs) and check if the dishes match what we expect. We look for flavor balance (accuracy), missing ingredients (errors), and consistency across many plates.  

**Debugging** is the chef’s recipe notebook. If a dish tastes off, we trace back to which ingredient or cooking step caused it—maybe the oven temperature was wrong or a spice was omitted. For AI, this means inspecting the model’s layers, data labels, or training parameters until the output improves.

Key terms:  
- **Generative AI** – a system that produces new content from patterns it learned.  
- **Model** – the internal set of rules (weights) the chef follows.  
- **Training data** – the cookbook the model learned from.  

By tasting and refining, we keep the AI’s creations delicious and reliable.
