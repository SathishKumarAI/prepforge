**Dimension 3: “Reasoning Reliability – Capability Assessment”**

Think of an AI like a smart robot chef. *Reliability* is the confidence you have that the chef will actually finish the recipe correctly, not just say it can. *Capability assessment* is how we test that confidence: we give the chef a set of dishes (questions, tasks) and watch whether it follows the right steps, checks for mistakes, and finishes on time.

If the robot can cook many dishes well but sometimes leaves a sauce too salty, its reliability is low even though its skill level is high. A truly reliable AI consistently produces correct answers across varied problems, just like a chef who always tastes before serving. This dimension measures that consistency, not just raw ability.
