You look for people who share key traits—same last name, same birthday month, or the same favorite hobby. Those who match these common features belong to the group; everyone else is considered an outlier because they don’t fit the family pattern.

In data science, an **inlier** is a data point that fits well with the rest of the dataset according to a chosen rule or model. Think of it as a family member who shares the defining characteristics of the group. An **outlier**, by contrast, deviates noticeably from those shared traits.
