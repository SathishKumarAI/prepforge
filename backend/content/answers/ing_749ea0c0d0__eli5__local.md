**What is Docker? (for image‑based apps)**  

Imagine you want to bake a cake that everyone can eat the same way, no matter what kitchen they’re in. Instead of giving each person a recipe and all the ingredients, you put the batter, the oven settings, and the instructions together in a single “cake kit.” Whenever someone opens the kit, they get a perfectly baked cake without needing to know how baking works.

Docker does that for software. It takes an application (like a web site or database), bundles it with all its libraries, settings, and even a mini‑operating system, into one portable package called an **image**. Anyone can run that image on any computer that has Docker installed, and the app will work exactly the same way—just like opening your cake kit and getting a ready‑to‑eat dessert.
