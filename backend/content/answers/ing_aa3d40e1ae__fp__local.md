The core of the problem that drives AI adoption is *efficiency*: turning unstructured data into actionable knowledge at scale. In tech, the infrastructure (cloud, GPUs, open‑source libraries) already exists, so the cost of prototyping a model drops from months to weeks. This rapid feedback loop creates an early‑adopter cycle—every successful demo spurs another, and media coverage follows.

The *optimization* problem shifts from “build faster” to “adapt the model to domain constraints” (regulation, explainability, bias).
