**Snapshots in HDFS – a “Time‑Travel” photo album**

Imagine you’re building a large family photo book on a cloud shelf (HDFS). Every day you add, edit, or delete pictures. Sometimes you want to remember exactly how the book looked at a certain date—maybe for a school project or because someone asked for the old version.

A **snapshot** is HDFS’s way of taking that “photo.” When you create one, the system records *only the differences* (new or changed files) from that moment onward. It keeps the original files intact while letting you view or copy them as they were when the snapshot was taken. If later you delete a picture, the snapshot still holds the old version, just like a photo in your album.

So, think of snapshots as instant, lightweight “time‑stamps” that let you step back into any past state of your data without re‑uploading everything.
