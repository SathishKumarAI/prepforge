Think of data like water flowing through a river.  
A **real‑time dashboard** is like a small stream gauge that shows the current level every second—useful when you need to act fast, such as monitoring traffic on a website or detecting fraud.  
A **batch report** is like measuring the average depth of the whole river each morning after it has run its course—it gives a complete picture but only once per day, good for budgeting or trend analysis.

If your job needs instant reactions (e.g., alerting a user when their account balance drops), go real‑time.  
If you just want to review what happened over the past week or month, batch reports are enough and simpler to build.

*Batch*: collecting data in large groups over time before processing; *real‑time*: handling each piece of data as it arrives.
