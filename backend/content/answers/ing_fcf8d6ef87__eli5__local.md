Think of a pre‑trained model like a seasoned chef who has already mastered the art of making soup from scratch.  
The chef (the model) knows how to chop vegetables, sauté aromatics, and simmer stock for hours. When you hand them a new recipe—say a spicy Thai soup—they don’t start from zero; they just tweak seasoning, add fresh herbs, or change the cooking time. That’s **transfer learning**: taking knowledge learned on one task (making general soup) and applying it to a new but related task (making Thai soup).  

A *deep learning model* is a stack of “layers” that learn patterns from data, just as the chef learns flavors from experience.  
By reusing the pre‑trained layers, you save time and need fewer examples—like using a well‑seasoned stock instead of making every ingredient from scratch. The new recipe only needs a few adjustments, and the dish turns out quickly and reliably.
