Imagine a kitchen where you can order a dish by telling the chef exactly what you want—“make me spaghetti with tomato sauce and garlic.” The chef (the AI) knows all the ingredients and cooking steps but doesn’t decide how to cook it alone; it asks the stove, oven, and blender for help. This is **Function/Tool Calling**: the AI writes a recipe (a function call), then hands each step off to the right appliance (a tool). The chef keeps track of what’s done and what still needs doing, so you get a finished meal without the chef having to handle every tiny detail. In short, the AI delegates specific tasks to specialized tools while orchestrating the whole process.
