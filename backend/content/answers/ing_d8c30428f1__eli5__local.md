**Compaction in Context Engineering**

Imagine you’re sending a postcard to a friend who can only read a short message—say, 200 characters. You have a long story about your day, but the postcard will cut it down automatically if it’s too big. *Compaction* is that automatic trimming: the AI takes all the information you give (the “context”) and squeezes it into the size the model can handle without losing its meaning.

Think of the context as a packed suitcase. You want to fit clothes, shoes, toiletries, but only have a small bag. Compaction selects what’s essential—like folding shirts tightly or compressing socks—to keep everything inside while still being usable. The AI does this by summarizing, removing redundancies, and rearranging data so the model can “read” it all in one go. This keeps the conversation coherent and prevents the model from ignoring important details because the context was too large.
