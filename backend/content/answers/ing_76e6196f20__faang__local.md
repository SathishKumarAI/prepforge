**3️⃣ Depth (core answer)**  
Fake familiarity is when a candidate *recalls* terminology or high‑level ideas but lacks the underlying intuition, implementation details, or edge‑case handling. In ML this shows as:  
- **Overreliance on “model‑agnostic” phrases** (“just tune hyper‑parameters”) without knowing why overfitting occurs or how to diagnose it.  
- **Misuse of jargon** (e.g., calling a decision tree “a neural network”).  
- **Skipping algorithmic complexity** (claiming KNN is O(1) because predictions are instant).
