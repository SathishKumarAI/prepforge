Imagine building a city. A **consolidated tool** is like a single, huge skyscraper that houses many offices—everything runs together in one place. It’s quick to set up and easy for people to use because all the functions are bundled, but if you need a tiny office on a specific floor, you can’t change the layout without affecting the whole building.

A **granular tool** is like a neighborhood of independent houses. Each house (or “module”) does one thing—perhaps just a kitchen or a bedroom. You can pick and choose exactly which houses to bring into your city, combine them in any order, and upgrade one without touching the others.

Decide based on your needs:
