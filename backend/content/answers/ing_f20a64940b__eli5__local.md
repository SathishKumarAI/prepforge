Imagine you’re a chef trying to make the perfect soup. To know if it’s good, you taste it, check its color, smell it, see how thick it is, watch how it bubbles, and ask friends for their opinions. The **six AI evals** work just like those five checks—each one tests a different part of an AI “recipe” to make sure it behaves right.

1. **Accuracy** – Like tasting the soup; does the AI answer correctly?  
2. **Robustness** – Like stirring slowly; can it handle noisy, unexpected inputs?  
3. **Fairness** – Like using equal ingredients; does it treat all groups equally?  
4. **Explainability** – Like a recipe card; can we see why the AI made its choice?  
5. **Efficiency** – Like cooking speed; how fast and resource‑light is the model?  
6. **Safety** – Like checking for choking hazards; will it avoid harmful or biased outputs?

By running all six, developers get a full “taste test” of an AI’s performance before serving it to users.
