**TL;DR – What is AI?**

Imagine a recipe book that learns from every meal you cook. Every time you make something, the book notes which ingredients worked, how long it took, and how much people liked it. After enough meals, the book can suggest new dishes, tweak recipes to suit your taste, or even start cooking on its own.

That’s AI in a nutshell: a computer system that “learns” from data (like the recipe book learns from meals) and then uses those lessons to make predictions, solve problems, or create something new—without being told every single step. The learning part is called *machine learning*, and when it can perform tasks humans normally do, we call it *artificial intelligence*.
