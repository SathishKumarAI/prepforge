Imagine your data is like a long, winding river that feeds a big farm.  
**Lineage** is the map that shows every bend, tributary and source of the water—where it starts, which ponds or streams it passes through, and how each drop has changed (filtered, cleaned, merged). Knowing this lets you trace any problem back to its root, just as a farmer can see if a crop issue came from irrigation or fertilizer.  

**Observability** is the set of gauges and cameras that constantly monitor the river’s flow, temperature, and quality. If something goes wrong—say a sudden drop in water level—you instantly know where it happened and why.  

A **data architecture** for machine learning is the whole farm system: the river (raw data), its map (lineage) and its monitoring equipment (observability). Together they let you build reliable ML models, track their performance, and quickly fix any drift or errors in the underlying data.
