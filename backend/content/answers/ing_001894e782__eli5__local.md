First, you show how you *plant* data: explain what data you collect and why it matters (the “seed” of your model). Next, you demonstrate how you *tend* the plant: describe preprocessing steps, feature engineering, and any cleaning—this is like pruning leaves so the plant grows healthy.

Finally, you explain *harvesting* the plant’s benefits: how the model’s predictions help real users, what metrics matter, and how you’ll monitor performance over time.  

Key terms:  
- **Model** – a mathematical recipe that turns input data into predictions.  
- **Training** – teaching the model by showing it many examples.  
- **Validation/Testing** – checking how well the model works on new, unseen data.  
- **Over‑fitting** – when a model learns the training data too perfectly and fails on fresh data.
