**Ball‑Mark Estimation on Social Networks**

Imagine a huge jar full of colored marbles that you can’t see inside. Each marble is a person who uses Facebook or Instagram. To guess how many marbles are in the jar, you pull out a handful and look at their colors. If most of the marbles you grabbed were blue, it’s likely that blue dominates the whole jar. In data terms, you “sample” a small group (the handful) to estimate the total user base (“ball‑mark estimation”).  

*Key terms:*  
- **Sample**: The handful of marbles you actually look at.  
- **Population**: All marbles in the jar – all users of the platform.  
- **Estimate**: A guess about how many marbles (users) there are, based on the sample.

Just as the color mix in your handful hints at the overall jar’s composition, a few random user accounts can give you a good idea of the entire network’s size. This simple sampling trick lets analysts keep track of millions of users without counting each one individually.
