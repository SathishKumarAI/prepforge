NeMo Guardrails is the “seatbelt and safety system” for this robot. It’s an open‑source toolkit—free to use and modify—that lets developers put in rules (the guardrails) that the LLM must follow before it speaks. For example, a rule might say “don’t mention politics” or “only answer if you’re sure.” The Guardrails code checks every response, blocks or rewrites anything that breaks a rule, and logs what happened.
