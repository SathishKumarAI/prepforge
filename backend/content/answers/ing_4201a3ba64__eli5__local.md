**Breaking into system design – the “recipe‑book” approach**

Think of building a big app like cooking a gourmet meal for a crowd. First, you ask: *What dish are we making?* That’s the problem statement (e.g., an online store). Next, list the ingredients: users, products, payments, inventory. Then decide on the kitchen layout—your high‑level architecture (frontend servers, database cluster, cache, CDN). After that, choose cooking techniques: which recipes (algorithms) will handle search or recommendation? Finally, you set up a schedule and cleanup plan to keep the kitchen running smoothly (monitoring, scaling, backups).

In system design interviews, “break into” means taking this recipe‑book step by step: define the problem, identify components, sketch high‑level structure, drill down on key parts, then consider trade‑offs. It keeps the conversation organized and shows you can plan a complex system like a chef plans a feast.
