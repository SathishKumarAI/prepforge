Imagine building a LEGO castle. Each block is a data point, and the castle’s design is your model. A **workflow** is like the instruction booklet that tells you which blocks to use first, how to stack them, and when to add windows or towers. In machine learning, workflows do the same: they line up steps—collecting data, cleaning it, training a model, testing it, and finally deploying it—so everything happens in the right order and without forgetting anything.

A **model** is just a set of mathematical rules that learns patterns from your data. A **pipeline** (the workflow) ensures those rules are trained on clean data, evaluated properly, and then pushed to production reliably. Without such a roadmap, you risk mixing up steps, losing data, or deploying an untested model—just like trying to build the castle without the guide. The workflow keeps the process organized, repeatable, and error‑free.
