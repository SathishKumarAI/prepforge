Imagine an AI model as a chef in a busy kitchen.  
When the kitchen is open (inference), we want to know how fast and well the chef can serve dishes, not how they’re trained.  

**Latency** = time from placing an order to getting the dish—how quickly one request finishes.  
**Throughput** = number of orders served per minute—overall speed when many customers arrive at once.  
**Batch size** = how many orders the chef handles together before cooking; larger batches can be faster overall but may increase latency for each dish.  

These metrics tell us whether the chef is ready to keep the line moving smoothly, just like they tell us if an AI model will perform well in real‑time applications.
