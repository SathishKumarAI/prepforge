Imagine you’re training a chef (the AI) to make a perfect soup. First, the chef tries a recipe and produces a batch (the model’s output). Then a food critic tastes it and writes notes on what’s wrong—too salty, not enough spice, or missing texture (these are the *label errors*). The chef learns from those notes, adjusts the ingredients, and cooks again. This back‑and‑forth cycle is the interview loop: each round “interviews” the AI with real feedback, shrinks its mistakes, and makes it better.
