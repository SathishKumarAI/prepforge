**Distributed logging** is like having a team of friends each keeping a notebook while you all work on a big project in different rooms. Every time someone writes something important—an error, a status update, or a question—they jot it down in their own notebook and send it to a central “master” notebook. The master then stitches the notes together so you can read the whole story later, even though no single friend had all the information at once.

**Distributed tracing** is similar, but instead of just writing notes, each friend also draws a little map showing *how* they reached that point—what doors they passed through, how long it took, and which other friends they talked to along the way. When you look at the master map, you see a complete picture of every step your request (or task) took across all rooms, making it easy to spot where delays or errors happened.

*Unavoidable terms:*
- **Log** – a record of events.
- **Trace** – a sequence that shows the path an action takes through multiple parts.
