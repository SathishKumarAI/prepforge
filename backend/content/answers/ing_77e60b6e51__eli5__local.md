Think of an AI model as a chef in a busy kitchen.

**Inference (Recipe Checking)** – First the chef looks at the recipe card (the input data). He checks each ingredient’s quantity and quality, deciding how much to use and when to add it. In AI, this is the *inference* phase: the model reads the input, runs its internal calculations, and figures out the best output for that single request.

**Production (Cooking the Dish)** – Once the plan is set, the chef starts cooking—mixing, heating, seasoning—until the dish is ready. That’s the *production* phase in AI: the model takes the inference results and turns them into a final answer or action that the user receives.

So, inference is planning; production is execution—just like a chef plans before he cooks.
