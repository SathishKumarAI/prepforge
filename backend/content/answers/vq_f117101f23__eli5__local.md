Think of the “four SPs” as the main ways you can get a computer‑based service without building it yourself—just like choosing different kinds of food delivery.  

1. **IaaS (Infrastructure as a Service)** – You rent raw computing muscle: virtual machines, storage and networking. It’s like renting a fully furnished apartment where you still decide what furniture to bring in.  
2. **PaaS (Platform as a Service)** – The provider hands you a ready‑made kitchen with appliances and utensils; you only bring the ingredients (your code). You can build, test and run apps without managing servers.  
3. **SaaS (Software as a Service)** – A finished meal delivered to your table. The software runs in the cloud and you just log in—no installation or maintenance on your end.  
4. **FaaS (Function as a Service)** – Tiny, single‑purpose snacks that appear only when you order them. You write small functions; the platform triggers them on demand and bills you for exactly what’s used.

Each SP lets you focus more on your data problems rather than the underlying hardware or operating system.
