**What to ask interviewers when you’re interviewing for an AI role (Amazon style)**  

> *Leadership Principles:* **Customer Obsession**, **Ownership**, **Dive Deep**  
> *Goal:* Show that you understand the problem space, own the solution path, and can quantify impact.

| # | Question | Why It Matters | Sample Insight |
|---|----------|----------------|----------------|
| 1 | “What is the most critical customer pain point this AI feature solves?” | Validates **Customer Obsession** and focus on real outcomes. | If they say *“reducing churn by 12%”*, you can tie your solution to that metric. |
| 2 | “Can you walk me through the data pipeline currently used for model training?” | Demonstrates **Dive Deep** into existing infra; shows ownership of end‑to‑end flow. | You’ll learn about S3 buckets, Glue jobs, SageMaker endpoints, etc., and can suggest cost or latency improvements. |
| 3 | “What are the key success metrics you track post‑deployment?” | Connects to **Deliver Results**—you need to know what matters after launch. | If they mention *latency < 200 ms* and *accuracy > 95%*, you can propose specific monitoring dashboards. |
| 4 | “How do you handle model drift or data shift in production?” | Tests your ability to own the model lifecycle and build robust systems. | Answers may reveal use of CloudWatch alarms, retraining triggers, or feature store updates. |
| 5 | “What’s a recent failure (model or infrastructure) and what did we learn from it?” | Shows **Ownership** + learning culture; looks for honest reflection. | If they talk about an over‑fitted model that caused revenue loss, you can discuss regularization techniques you’d apply next time. |

- *Quantified Impact:* Can they tie questions to measurable outcomes (e.g., cost savings, latency reduction)?  
- *Learning from Failure:* Do they show a pattern of turning mistakes into process improvements?

Use these questions to steer the interview toward concrete metrics and actionable insights—exactly what Amazon expects.
