Imagine training a new chef by letting them taste thousands of dishes before they ever cook for anyone. The *pretraining objective* is the recipe that tells the chef what to learn from those tastings. In AI, this means showing the model huge amounts of text and asking it to guess missing words or predict the next word in a sentence. This pre‑learning phase is like a culinary school: the chef gains general skills that can later be fine‑tuned for specific tasks, such as answering questions or writing stories.
