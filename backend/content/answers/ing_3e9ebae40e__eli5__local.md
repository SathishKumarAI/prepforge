When you ask it to “cook” (answer) something, it pulls together ingredients—words and facts—from those books. Sometimes it mixes up recipes or adds a spice that isn’t in the original dish. That’s a *hallucination*: the AI makes up details or misstates facts because it’s not checking against real‑world taste (data). 

This is a safety problem because, like a chef who serves food with wrong ingredients, an AI that hallucinate can give misleading advice, cause harm, or spread false information.
