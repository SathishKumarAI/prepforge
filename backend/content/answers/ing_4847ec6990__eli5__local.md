2. **The judges**  
   - Human reviewers read the answers and rate them on a few simple points: clarity, tone, relevance, and style.

3. **Scores and feedback**  
   - The ratings are averaged for each bot, giving a clear ranking—just like the final score in a competition.

### Strengths
- **Human intuition:** Captures nuance that algorithms miss.  
- **Transparency:** You can see exactly why a bot got a higher or lower score.  
- **Flexibility:** Works for many styles—friendly, formal, humorous, etc.

### Limits
- **Subjectivity:** Different reviewers may have slightly different tastes.
