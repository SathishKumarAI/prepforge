Think of an AI system as a delivery service that promises to get your package to the right address on time.  
**SLA (Service‑Level Agreement) metrics are the “promises” written in numbers**:  

| Metric | What it means for the AI |
|--------|--------------------------|
| **Accuracy** | How often the model’s predictions match the true answer (like a delivery that arrives at the correct address). |
| **Latency** | The time from asking a question to getting an answer (the speed of the courier). |
| **Availability** | The percentage of time the AI is up and running, ready to serve (the service center’s open hours). |
| **Throughput** | How many requests the system can handle per second (how many packages it can deliver in a day). |

Just as a courier company would agree to deliver 99 % of parcels on time and within two days, an AI provider agrees to meet these numbers. If the promise isn’t kept, penalties or credits apply—just like a refund if your package is late. This keeps users confident that the AI will perform reliably, just as you trust a delivery service with your important items.
