Imagine you’re packing a suitcase for a trip. You want to fit as many clothes as possible without overloading it, and you also want the items to stay in good shape during travel. Video encoding is that smart packing trick for digital movies.

**Encoding** means converting raw video (all its pixels and sounds) into a compressed format—like folding shirts neatly so they use less space. The **codec** (compressor‑decompressor) decides how tightly to pack: it removes redundancies, keeps the important details, and labels what’s left for later reconstruction.

When you play the video, the codec “unpacks” it back into a viewable stream, just as you’d unpack your suitcase. This lets huge movies fit on streaming servers or mobile devices while keeping quality close to the original.
