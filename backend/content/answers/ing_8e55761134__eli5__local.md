*Episodic memory* is like a photo album—each picture remembers a specific dish it cooked before, with details such as ingredients and time.  
*Procedural memory* is the recipe book that tells the chef how to stir, season, or fold batter without looking at each step again.  

When the agent chats, it pulls from its “recipe book” (procedural memory) to generate sentences: a grammar rule is a cooking technique, while word choice is seasoning.
