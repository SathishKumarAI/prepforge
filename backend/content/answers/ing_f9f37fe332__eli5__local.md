Imagine you’re texting a friend in another country. Every word your friend writes has to travel across the internet, be understood by their phone, and then they reply back. **Conversational AI latency** is how long that round‑trip takes for an artificial “friend” (a language model) to read your message, think of a response, and send it back.

Why does it matter? If the delay is short—like a quick ping of a few milliseconds—the chat feels natural; you can keep talking without noticing pauses. If the delay stretches to seconds or more, the conversation feels broken, like waiting for someone who’s far away or slow on their phone. In real‑time applications (voice assistants, customer support bots, gaming), low latency keeps interactions smooth and keeps users engaged. Thus, reducing latency is essential for making AI feel human‑like and responsive.
