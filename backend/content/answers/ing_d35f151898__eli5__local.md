**Confidence‑Based Escalation in a Chatbot**

Think of the chatbot as a helpful librarian who first tries to answer your question from its own shelf of books (its knowledge base). While it’s answering, it keeps an eye on how sure it feels—like a quick “I’m pretty confident” or “Hmm, not so sure.”  
If the confidence score is high, the librarian gives you the answer straight away. If the score drops below a set threshold, the librarian says, “Let me double‑check this for you,” and passes the request to a human expert or a more powerful system that can dig deeper.

- **Confidence score**: A numeric estimate (0–1) of how likely the chatbot’s answer is correct.  
- **Threshold**: The cutoff below which the bot decides it needs help.  

This way, users get fast answers most of the time, but still receive accurate help when the bot isn’t certain—just like a librarian who knows when to consult a specialist.
