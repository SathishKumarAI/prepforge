- **System (recipe book)** tells the sous‑chef what style to use—formal or friendly, how much detail, whether it can browse the internet, etc. It sets the overall tone and limits.
- **User (chef)** gives the ingredients: a question, a request, or a goal. The chef decides *what* they want cooked.
- **Assistant (sous‑chef)** follows the recipe book’s rules while using the chef’s ingredients to prepare the dish—generating answers, suggestions, or explanations.
