Imagine training a language model as teaching a child to read. **Data curriculum** is like the teacher’s reading list: first give simple picture books (easy, clear examples), then gradually move to chapter novels (more complex sentences). The model learns foundational patterns before tackling harder material.

**Quality** is how trustworthy those books are. A good book has correct spelling, clear grammar, and no hidden typos; a bad one might have mistakes or misleading content that confuses the child. In pretraining, we pick many high‑quality texts (books, articles) and arrange them in an order that starts simple and grows harder. This way the model builds strong language skills step by step, just like a well‑guided reader.
