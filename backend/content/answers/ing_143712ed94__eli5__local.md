**Problem Statement – Whiteboard Exercises (AI)**  

Imagine you’re a chef who must create a new recipe before any cooking starts. The *problem statement* is the menu card that tells everyone what dish to make: the ingredients, the flavor profile, and how many people it should serve. In AI whiteboard exercises, we write this “menu” on paper (or a digital board) so that the whole team—engineers, designers, data scientists—understands exactly what the model must do before we start building or training.

Key parts of the menu:  
- **Goal**: What should the AI answer or predict?  
- **Inputs**: Which data points will it see?  
- **Outputs**: In what form does the answer appear?  
- **Constraints**: Time limits, privacy rules, or accuracy thresholds.  

Just as a chef shares the recipe with the kitchen crew to avoid surprises, the problem statement gives every AI developer clear direction. It prevents wasted effort on the wrong model and keeps everyone aligned from the first line of code.
