Think of a library that needs to answer questions about its books.

* **SQL** is like a librarian who has an index card system. If you ask “How many mystery novels were published after 2010?” the librarian flips straight to the right section, counts, and gives you the number in seconds. It’s fast when the question fits the cards—simple filters and totals.

* **AI Agents** are like a smart assistant who learns the library layout and can answer almost any question—“Which mystery authors have the most recent books?” or “Predict the next bestseller.” They’re slower to set up because they need training data, but once trained they can tackle complex patterns that neither SQL nor Pandas handles well.
