### Why the Five Differentiators Matter

When an interviewer asks *“What makes you stand out?”* they are probing **information asymmetry**: how you convert scarce data (your résumé) into a high‑utility signal for them.  
From an optimization standpoint, your goal is to maximize expected interview score \(E[S]\) under the constraint that the interview panel can only observe a limited set of features.
