Think of an agent as a student taking a test.  
**Trajectory‑based evaluation** watches every move the student makes while answering: each question answered, time spent on each step, and any detours or mistakes along the way. It tells you *how* the student solved problems—whether they followed good strategies or got stuck early.  

**Outcome‑based evaluation** only looks at the final grade: how many questions were correct, how high the score is. It ignores the process and just rewards success.  

In practice, trajectory methods are like a coach reviewing video footage to spot bad habits; outcome methods are like giving a certificate for passing. Both matter: trajectory helps improve skills, outcome shows overall performance. The best assessment blends both—watch the journey while also checking the destination.
