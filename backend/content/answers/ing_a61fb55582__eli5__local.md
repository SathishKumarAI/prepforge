1. **Data Source (the fridge)** – stores raw ingredients (datasets).  
2. **Feature Store (the prep counter)** – pre‑cooks and labels ingredients so every recipe can use them quickly.  
3. **Training Engine (the stove)** – runs the cooking process, turning ingredients into dishes (trained models).  
4. **Experiment Tracker (the recipe book)** – records each cook’s steps, temperatures, and results so you can compare versions.  
5. **Model Registry (the pantry shelf)** – keeps a tidy inventory of finished dishes that are ready to serve.  
6. **Deployment Pipeline (the serving line)** – moves the best dish from the kitchen to diners (production).
