Imagine building a smart robot that learns to walk.  
**Evaluation** is like the coach who watches the robot step by step, noting when it stumbles or lands on the right spot. The coach gives scores and tells the robot where to improve—this is your *evaluation* phase: you test its performance, measure accuracy, and compare against a standard.

**Observability** is the set of cameras and microphones glued all over the robot’s body. They record not just the final step but every muscle twitch, sensor reading, and internal calculation. If something goes wrong later, you can replay the footage to see exactly what happened inside—this is *observability*.

Together, evaluation tells you “how well” the robot does its job; observability lets you understand “why” it behaves that way. As your AI system matures, you add more sensors (logs) and refine your tests, just as a coach would give more detailed feedback to help the robot walk faster and safer.
