**Context length** is like the size of a notebook you give an AI writer.  
The notebook holds all the sentences it can look at while composing its next line. If the notebook is tiny, the writer only remembers the last few pages and may repeat ideas or miss earlier plot points. A larger notebook lets the writer see more of the story, keep track of characters, and make better choices.

In a business AI, a short context length means it can remember only recent customer messages or data snippets, so its answers feel shallow or inconsistent. Extending the context lets it pull in past sales notes, policy documents, or earlier chat turns, giving richer, more accurate insights.

**Unavoidable terms**  
- *LLM (Large Language Model)*: a computer program that learns language patterns from huge text collections and can generate new sentences.  
- *Context*: the set of words or tokens the model reads at once to decide what comes next.

By choosing an appropriate notebook size—enough to cover all relevant business information without overloading—the AI stays sharp, consistent, and truly helpful.
