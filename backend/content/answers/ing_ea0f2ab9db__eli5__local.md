**RAG = “Retrieve‑and‑Generate.”**  
Imagine you’re writing a story but can’t remember all the facts. Instead of trying to recall everything, you first ask a quick Google search for the exact information you need (that’s the *retrieve* part). Once you have those snippets—like a paragraph about the Eiffel Tower—you read them and then weave them into your own narrative using your own voice and style (that’s the *generate* part).  

So RAG is a two‑step process:  
1. **Retrieve** – pull relevant text from a big database or the internet.  
2. **Generate** – let an AI model rewrite that text in a fresh, context‑aware way.  

This lets the AI stay up‑to‑date and accurate while still sounding natural and original.
