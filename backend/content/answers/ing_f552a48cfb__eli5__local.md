**Building an AI agent is like training a smart robot helper in your kitchen.**  
First, you give it a clear goal—“make breakfast” (the *objective*). Next, you set up a *environment*: the stove, fridge, and ingredients that the robot can see and touch. The robot starts by making random moves: grabbing a pan, flipping a pancake. After each move, it receives a tiny reward or penalty: “Nice flip!” (+1) or “Burnt the bread” (–1). This is *reinforcement learning*: the agent learns which actions lead to higher rewards. Over time it builds a policy—a recipe of actions that reliably gets the best breakfast. In short, you give an objective, let the robot explore, reward good behavior, and it gradually learns how to act smartly.
