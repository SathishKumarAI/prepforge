Imagine you’re baking a huge batch of cookies and want every friend in the world to taste them at the same speed. If every friend had to travel all the way back to your kitchen, some would wait hours while others get their bite almost instantly. A Content Delivery Network (CDN) solves this by creating little “cookie‑shops” (edge servers) scattered across the globe. Each shop stores copies of the cookies (web pages, videos, images). When a friend asks for a cookie, they go to the nearest shop instead of your kitchen. The CDN keeps the shops synchronized with fresh batches from the main kitchen, so everyone always gets the latest version quickly and reliably.
