Imagine a smart kitchen robot that can cook on its own but has a chef watching the screen. **Interrupts** are like the chef shouting “Pause! Check the sauce!” whenever something looks off—an unexpected spice level or a burnt smell. The robot stops, asks for guidance, and resumes once the chef says it’s okay.

A **breakpoint** is more deliberate: before starting a new recipe, the chef sets a checkpoint—“Let’s review the ingredients first.” The robot halts at that exact point, lets the chef confirm everything, then moves on. In AI, these patterns let humans step in when models face uncertainty or potential errors, ensuring safety and quality while still benefiting from automation.
