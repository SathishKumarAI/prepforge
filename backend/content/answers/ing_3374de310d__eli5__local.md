When you want a new dish, you simply open a box, follow its steps, and it cooks itself in a tiny kitchen (a *container*) inside your main kitchen—no extra mess or special tools are required. The **Docker daemon** is the invisible chef who watches over all those boxes: it keeps the recipe library up to date, starts or stops the little kitchens on demand, and ensures each dish runs exactly as written without interfering with others.
