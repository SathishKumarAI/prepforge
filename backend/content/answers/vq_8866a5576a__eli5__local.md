In data engineering, a **variable** is like a labeled container that holds a piece of information you’ll use later—just as a shoebox keeps your socks or keys. When you create a variable, you give it a name (the label) and put a value inside the box: a number, a string of text, a date, or even a whole dataset. Later steps in your pipeline can read from or write to that container without having to carry the actual data around. Think of it as a “scratch pad” for intermediate results: you jot something down (store it), work on it, then hand it off to the next step. This keeps the flow organized and lets each part of the process focus on its task.
