Imagine you’re chatting with a friend over video chat. If they pause for a moment before replying, the conversation feels slow—like your phone is lagging. That pause is **conversational AI latency**: the time it takes from when you speak to when the AI “answers.” It matters because every extra second can make users feel frustrated or lose interest, especially in quick‑response apps like customer support or virtual assistants.

Think of the AI as a chef who must read your order (your words), cook a dish (process language and generate a reply), and serve it back. If the kitchen is cramped or the stove is slow, the dish takes longer to reach you. Similarly, if the model runs on distant servers, data hops over networks, or the hardware is sluggish, latency rises. Keeping that “chef” swift—by using faster processors, smarter algorithms, and nearby servers—keeps conversations smooth and natural.
