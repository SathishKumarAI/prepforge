Imagine your agent system as a busy kitchen.  
**Observability** is the set of tools that let you see what’s happening inside without opening every cupboard: a camera in each station, a thermometer for heat, and a voice‑activated assistant that reports back.

* **Logs** are like sticky notes on the fridge – they record every step (e.g., “agent decided to fetch data from X”).  
* **Metrics** are the kitchen’s thermostat and counter weights – quick numbers such as “time spent in decision phase” or “memory used.”  
* **Tracing** is a breadcrumb trail that follows a single dish from start to finish, showing which ovens and pans were used.

Good observability means you can ask any of these questions: *What did the agent do? How long did it take? Why did it fail?* and get clear answers instantly. It’s like having a live, real‑time view of every plate being prepared, so you can spot delays or mistakes before they spoil the meal.
