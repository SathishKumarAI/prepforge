**Solution Highlights – Whiteboard Exercises**

Think of a whiteboard exercise like planning a road trip with a map that everyone can see and edit. In AI, the “map” is your problem (e.g., building a chatbot), and the “road trip” is figuring out how to get there step by step.

1. **Sketch the Route** – Draw the big picture: what you want the AI to do and why it matters.
2. **Lay Out Landmarks** – Break the journey into milestones: data collection, model choice, training, testing, deployment.
3. **Draw Detours** – Spot potential roadblocks (bias, overfitting, privacy) and plan how to avoid or fix them.
4. **Share & Refine** – Let teammates add notes, question assumptions, and tweak the path together.

By writing everything out on a whiteboard, you see the whole journey at once, spot gaps early, and keep everyone aligned—just like a clear map keeps travelers on track.
