Imagine training a recipe app that learns how to make soup. Every time it cooks, it asks a friend—“Did this soup taste good?” The friend’s answer is the **evaluation**: a quick check of whether the dish meets certain standards (taste, texture, appearance). In AI, evaluation patterns are like those friendly checks but for machine learning models.

When the model “cooks” (makes predictions), it sends its output to an evaluator that compares it with the real answer. The evaluator counts how many times the model got it right and how often it made mistakes. This score tells us whether the recipe is improving or needs more seasoning.
