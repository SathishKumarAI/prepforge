Imagine you’re looking for a recipe in a huge cookbook, but the book is written in many different languages and styles.  
**Clarifying questions** are like asking the cook what kind of dish you want—vegetarian? spicy? quick to make? Each answer narrows the search so the right pages pop up.  
**Assumptions** are the chef’s guesses about your taste based on past orders: maybe they think “you liked pasta last time, so this is a good fit.”  

In semantic search, the system does the same. It asks clarifying questions (e.g., “Do you mean *bank* as in financial institution or riverbank?”) and makes educated assumptions from context (previous queries, user profile). This lets it retrieve documents that truly match what you’re after, not just those containing the exact words you typed.
