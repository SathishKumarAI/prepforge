Imagine you’re buying a new kitchen gadget—say, an espresso machine—and you want to know if it’s worth the money. **Quality metrics** in financial analysis are the checklist that tells you how good the gadget is and whether it will pay for itself over time.

1. **Return on Investment (ROI)** – Think of it as the number of cups of coffee you’ll get back compared to what you paid. A higher ROI means more “cups” per dollar.
2. **Net Present Value (NPV)** – Picture each future cup’s value shrinking a bit because you’d rather have that money today. NPV adds up all those discounted cups; if it’s positive, the machine is a good buy.
3. **Pay‑back Period** – Count how many months of extra coffee sales are needed to recover your purchase price.

In AI projects, these metrics replace “cups” with revenue or cost savings generated by the model. They help investors decide whether the AI investment will deliver real, measurable financial value.
