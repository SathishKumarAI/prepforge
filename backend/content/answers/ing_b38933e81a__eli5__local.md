Think of a library that’s built to answer any question you ask.  
A **data architecture** is the blueprint for that library: it tells where books (data) are stored, how they’re labeled, who can borrow them, and how the shelves connect so a librarian can find a book quickly.

* **Metadata** = the labels on each book’s spine—title, author, genre, date.  
* **Governance** = the library rules: only certain people may check out rare volumes, every new book must be catalogued before it arrives, and copies are tracked so nothing gets lost.

Together they keep the library organized, trustworthy, and easy to navigate. In a company, this “library” is the data system that lets analysts and AI models find the right information fast while staying secure and compliant.
