You first decide the **route** (the high‑level architecture), then choose the **stops** (components such as databases, caches, load balancers) and figure out how people move between them (data flow). To practice:

1. **Study famous routes** – read articles on popular systems (e.g., Twitter’s timeline or an e‑commerce checkout).  
2. **Draw your own map** – sketch a simple design from scratch for a given problem, labeling each stop and the traffic that passes through.  
3. **Ask for feedback** – show your map to friends or mentors and discuss trade‑offs (cost vs. speed, consistency vs. availability).  
4. **Simulate the trip** – run through the interview question aloud, explaining every stop as if you’re giving directions.
