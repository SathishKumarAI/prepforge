Imagine a chef who remembers every ingredient in the kitchen and knows how people taste each dish.  
**Context engineering** is like telling that chef exactly which ingredients (words, sentences, images) are in the current recipe so the AI can choose the right flavors for the moment.  
**Memory engineering** is the chef’s pantry— a special storage that keeps favorite recipes, past conversations, and learned patterns so the AI can pull them back when needed.  

Together they let an AI “cook” responses: context gives it the immediate kitchen layout; memory supplies long‑term knowledge from previous meals. By designing both parts carefully, we give the AI consistent, relevant answers without forgetting what matters.
