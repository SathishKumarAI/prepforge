**Chain‑of‑Thought (CoT) in prompt engineering** is like giving a recipe book to a cook who needs to finish a dish step by step.  
When you ask an AI a hard question, it can skip straight to the answer—like a chef shouting “Serve!” before tasting. With CoT, you first tell the AI to *write out* each reasoning step (“first I consider X, then Y”) before giving the final result. This mirrors how a human solves problems: they think aloud, check each part, and only after confirming everything do they deliver the answer.

**Key terms:**  
- **Prompt engineering** – crafting the words you give to an AI to guide its reply.  
- **AI model** – a computer program trained on huge text that predicts what comes next in a sentence.

By forcing the AI to “think aloud,” CoT improves accuracy, especially for math or logic tasks, because each step can be inspected and corrected before the final answer is produced.
