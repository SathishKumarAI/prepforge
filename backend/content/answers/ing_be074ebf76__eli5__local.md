**Hybrid search is like a two‑step treasure hunt in a library that mixes books and a map.**  
First, you quickly scan the library’s catalog (the “vector space”) to find all books that *might* mention your keyword – this gives you a broad list of potential matches. Then, you look at each book’s actual pages (the “exact search”) to see if the keyword really appears in the right context.  

Use hybrid search when you need both speed and precision:  
- **Speed** comes from the vector scan that narrows the field quickly.  
- **Precision** comes from checking the exact text, so you avoid false hits.  

It’s ideal for large document collections or databases where a quick idea of relevance is useful but you still want to confirm details before acting.
