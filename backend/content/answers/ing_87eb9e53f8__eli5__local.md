**Think of a machine‑learning model like a radio that learns to play the right song from a huge playlist.**

* **Forces (Data)** – These are the notes you feed into the radio: every image, sentence or click is a note. The more diverse and plentiful the notes, the richer the music can become.

* **Pattern (Learning)** – The radio’s tuner looks for repeated chords in those notes. It adjusts its internal settings so that when it hears a particular chord progression again, it automatically plays the same tune. This tuner is the algorithm, and its adjustments are called *weights*.  

* **Messaging (Prediction)** – Once tuned, you can press a button with any new chord (a new data point). The radio uses the learned pattern to decide which song to play next—this is the model’s prediction or “message” back to you.
