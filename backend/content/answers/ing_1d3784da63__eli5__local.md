Imagine you’re at a cooking contest where each judge tastes a different dish made by one of several chefs.  
Each chef (a “model”) uses its own recipe and may get some dishes right or wrong.  
If the judges simply pick the best single dish, they risk favoring a chef who happened to hit the mark that day but isn’t reliable overall.  

Instead, the contest organizers ask every judge to vote on which dish is tastiest. The final winner is chosen by majority vote—this is an **ensemble method** in AI.  
It combines many simple models (the chefs) so that their mistakes tend to cancel out, giving a more accurate and robust decision than any single model alone.  

Key terms:  
- **Model** – a single algorithm that makes predictions.  
- **Ensemble** – a group of models whose outputs are combined, usually by voting or averaging.  
By pooling diverse opinions, ensembles achieve higher performance with less risk of one bad guess dominating the result.
