Think of logging into a big hotel that lets you use one key for every room.  
**Token** is the key card you receive when you first check in – it’s a short‑lived code that says “you’re allowed to enter.”  
The **cookie** is like a tiny note stuck on your door: it lives in your browser and remembers that you already have a key card, so you don’t need to ask for one again each time you walk into a new room.  
A **session** is the actual stay in the hotel: while the key card (token) is valid, the hotel keeps track of where you are, what services you’re using, and when your stay ends. Once the session expires or you leave, the note (cookie) can be cleared, and a new token will be needed for the next visit.
