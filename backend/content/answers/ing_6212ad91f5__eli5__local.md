Imagine a hospital’s electronic records as a giant library where every patient’s story is stored in its own book. **EHR Integration** means letting different computers talk to that library so they can read, add or update the books without fighting each other.  

**FHIR (Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources)** is like a universal library card: it tells any computer exactly how to ask for a specific page (e.g., “give me Jane’s blood‑pressure chart”) and how to write new information back in the same language everyone understands.

When you add **Voice AI**, it’s as if a friendly librarian sits beside you, listening to your spoken requests (“Show me John’s last visit notes”), translating them into FHIR commands, pulling the right pages from the library, and even speaking the answers back.
