Think of machine learning as teaching a robot chef how to cook your favorite dish. The chef starts with no recipe, just a bunch of ingredients (data). You show it many samples: pictures of the dish, taste notes, and what you liked about each version. Each time the chef tries a new batch, you tell them “good” or “not good.” Over time, the chef learns which ingredient amounts and cooking steps make the dish just right for you—this is the model learning from data.

**Key terms**  
- **Data**: The examples (pictures, notes) we give.  
- **Model**: The robot’s recipe that keeps adjusting itself.  
- **Training**: Repeatedly showing the chef and correcting it.
