**Latency** is the time it takes for a machine‑learning model to finish one request and give an answer.  
Think of it like ordering coffee at a café: you tell the barista what you want (the input), they brew it, then hand you the cup (the output). The latency is how long you sit waiting from placing your order until you receive the coffee.  

In ML, when you feed data into a model—say, asking a voice assistant to recognize speech—the system must load the model, run calculations on the input, and produce the result. Each of those steps adds tiny delays; together they form the latency. Lower latency means faster responses, just like a quick barista makes for a smoother customer experience.
