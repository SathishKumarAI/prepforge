Think of an AI model as a new recipe you’re trying out.  
**Evaluation** is like tasting the dish: it asks, “Did it taste good?” and measures things such as accuracy (how many times the answer was right), speed, or how much memory it uses.  
**Observability** is like looking at the kitchen while the chef cooks: it watches which ingredients are being mixed, how long each step takes, and whether any alarms pop up (like a model crashing).  

Together they form a *taxonomy*—a map that classifies every test you run into two groups: “What does the final result look like?” (evaluation) and “How did the system behave while producing it?” (observability). This way, developers can quickly spot whether a model is simply wrong or if something deeper in its process is broken.
