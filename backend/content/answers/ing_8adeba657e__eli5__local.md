**Pitfall 12: Not Leading with Structure**

Imagine you’re baking a cake but skip the recipe first and just start mixing flour, sugar, eggs, and butter all at once. You’ll end up with a sticky mess that may or may not rise. In AI training, “structure” is like that recipe—it’s a clear plan of how data will be organized, what features to use, and which model architecture fits the problem.

If you jump straight into feeding raw, unstructured information (text, images, numbers) into a neural network without deciding on input shapes, preprocessing steps, or output formats, the learning algorithm gets confused. It might learn noise instead of useful patterns, wasting time and resources.

**So: always start by mapping out your data pipeline—cleaning, feature selection, model choice—before you let the AI “cook.”** This keeps training focused, efficient, and more likely to produce a tasty result.
