**ChatGPT Prompt Engineering (DeepLearning.AI)** is like giving a chef a recipe but also telling them exactly how to taste each ingredient before cooking.  

*Prompt engineering* means writing the question or instruction you give ChatGPT so it understands what you want and gives the best answer. Think of the prompt as a “menu card.” If the card says, “Make me a soup with tomatoes,” the chef (ChatGPT) can improvise wildly—maybe adding onions or garlic. But if the card says, “Make a tomato soup: use fresh ripe tomatoes, no onions, and serve hot in a bowl,” the result is predictable.

Key terms:
- **Prompt** – the text you type into ChatGPT.
- **Model** – the underlying AI (like GPT‑4) that processes your prompt.
- **Fine‑tuning** – training the model on more specific data so it follows certain style or rules better.

By experimenting with wording, adding context, and setting clear constraints, developers can “tune” ChatGPT’s output—just as a chef refines a dish by tweaking ingredients and instructions.
