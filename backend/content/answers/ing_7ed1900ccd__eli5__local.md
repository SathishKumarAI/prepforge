**“Contains‑Keywords” in LLM evaluation**

Imagine you’re judging a recipe contest. The judge’s rule is simple: the dish must include certain secret ingredients (like basil, garlic, or lemon). If those key flavors are present, the dish passes; if any are missing, it fails. In AI, “contains‑keywords” works the same way for language models (LLMs).  

When we evaluate an LLM’s answer, we give it a list of *required keywords*—specific words or phrases that must appear in its response (e.g., “machine learning,” “neural network”).
