Imagine a busy kitchen where chefs are your AI helpers.  
**Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR)** is like a sous‑chef who listens to you speak and immediately writes down the recipe in plain text—turning spoken words into written ones.  
**Text‑to‑Speech (TTS)** flips that process: it takes a written recipe and, using a vocal synthesizer, speaks it back out loud so you can hear it again.  
A **voice agent** is the head chef who uses both tools to chat with you—understanding your questions via ASR, answering in natural voice through TTS, and even following up on visual cues (e.g., looking at a screen).  
When the system also sees images or touches a screen while talking, it becomes **multimodal**: it integrates sight and sound like a chef who watches ingredients while cooking.
