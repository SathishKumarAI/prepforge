Think of your resume as a **shopping list** that tells a recruiter exactly what you’re buying—your skills, experience, and the “why” you fit the job.  
Your job description is the **menu** for that store: it lists the items (tasks, tools, goals) the employer wants.  

1. **Match ingredients** – Highlight the same words the menu uses (e.g., “Python”, “data analysis”) so your list looks familiar.  
2. **Show value** – Just as a shopper might note the price and quality of each item, add numbers to show results: “boosted sales by 20%.”  
3. **Keep it tidy** – Use bullet points like separate boxes on a list; keep sentences short so they’re easy to scan.  

During your interview, treat the questions like a conversation about that menu.  
When asked, “What’s your biggest achievement?” reply with a specific item from your list—describe the problem, what you did (the recipe), and the outcome (the delicious dish). This clear, concrete approach keeps your story focused and memorable.
