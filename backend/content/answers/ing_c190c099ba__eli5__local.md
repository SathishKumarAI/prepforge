Imagine a chef who wants to make the perfect soup. She tastes it, writes down what she likes and dislikes, then tweaks the recipe—adding more salt or simmering longer—and tastes again. That cycle of “taste‑comment‑adjust‑taste” is like **the interview loop** in AI training.

In this loop an artificial intelligence (AI) model first generates a response to a question (the chef’s soup). A human evaluator then reviews the answer, scoring it and sometimes providing feedback or corrections (the chef’s tasting notes). The model learns from that feedback, adjusting its internal parameters—just as the chef would change ingredient amounts. This process repeats many times, gradually improving the AI’s performance.

Key terms:  
- **Model** – the AI program that produces answers.  
- **Evaluator** – a human who judges the quality of those answers.  
- **Iteration** – one cycle of generate‑evaluate‑learn.  

The loop keeps tightening until the model’s responses consistently meet the desired standard, much like a chef perfecting a signature dish through repeated tasting and refinement.
