Task Success Rate (TSR) is like the score a student gets on a homework set. Imagine you give an AI robot a list of chores—“open the door,” “pick up the ball,” “turn off the light.” For each chore, the robot either does it right or not at all; there’s no partial credit. TSR counts how many chores were finished correctly out of the total asked and divides by that number. The result is a percentage that tells you how often the AI actually succeeds on its tasks, just as a student’s grade shows how well they completed their homework. It’s a simple, clear metric for judging an agent’s reliability.
