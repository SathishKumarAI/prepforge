Imagine you’re building a smart garden sprinkler that learns when plants need water.  
**Prototype** is the first model you train in a big lab kitchen—think of it as a fancy, powerful computer that can test many recipes quickly. Once the recipe looks good, you want to put it on a small, everyday device (the sprinkler itself) so it can decide on its own whether to water.  

**On‑device deployment** means installing the finished model inside that sprinkler’s tiny computer, just like fitting a new firmware onto your phone.  
**Edge deployment** is when many sprinklers share their learning over a local network—each one runs the same model but can also send small updates back to a central hub without going out to the internet.

So you move from a powerful lab prototype to a lightweight, self‑sufficient device that works locally, often with occasional help from nearby “edge” servers.
