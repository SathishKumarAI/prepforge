Imagine an AI moderator as a diligent school librarian who must decide which books are allowed on the shelf and which must be taken down. **Accuracy** means the librarian gets it right most of the time—only the truly inappropriate books (the *false positives*) get removed, and the harmless ones (the *false negatives*) stay where they belong.

In AI terms, “accuracy requirements” set how many mistakes are acceptable before the system is considered unreliable. If an AI flags too many innocent posts as harmful, users feel censored; if it lets too many dangerous posts slip through, safety is compromised. The goal is a balance: high precision (few false positives) and high recall (few false negatives).
