If you say **“start at bead 0 and keep going to the end,”** you’re telling Python to give you every bead from the very beginning onward. In code, that’s written as **`0:`** inside brackets: `my_list[0:]`.  

*Unavoidable terms:*  
- **List** – an ordered collection of items.  
- **Index** – a number that points to a specific position in the list (starting at 0).  
- **Slice** – a way to grab a continuous chunk of a list by specifying start and end indices.
