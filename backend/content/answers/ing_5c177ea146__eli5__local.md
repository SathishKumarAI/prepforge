**Step 9: Monitoring and Delivery Confirmation**

Think of a machine‑learning model as a smart delivery robot that learns the best route to bring packages (predictions) from the warehouse (data source) to customers (users).  
After the robot is deployed, we still need to keep an eye on it. **Monitoring** means watching its “speed” and “accuracy” in real time—checking how many deliveries arrive on time and how often they’re correct. If a road closes or traffic changes, the robot’s performance drops; monitoring spots that drop.

**Delivery confirmation** is the final check: we confirm each package actually reached its destination. In ML terms, this means verifying that predictions have been used (e.g., a recommendation was clicked) and that the system logs show successful completion. If something fails, alerts are sent so developers can retrain or tweak the model.

Together, monitoring keeps the robot running smoothly, while delivery confirmation guarantees the work it does is actually delivered to users.
