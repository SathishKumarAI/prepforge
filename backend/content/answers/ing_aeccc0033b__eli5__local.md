Imagine a recipe that calls for several different spices—cumin, coriander, and pepper—to give a dish its full flavor. Each spice alone tastes good, but together they create something richer and more balanced. In machine learning, an **ensemble method** is the same idea: we train several **agents** (small models) that each try to solve a problem in their own way. The **Mixture of Agents (MoA)** is one such ensemble technique where the agents’ predictions are combined—often by averaging or voting—to produce a final answer. Think of it as a panel of chefs: if one misjudges the seasoning, another can correct it, and the group’s consensus yields a more reliable result than any single chef alone.
