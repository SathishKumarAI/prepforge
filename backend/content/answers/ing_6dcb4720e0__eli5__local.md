**Step 2 – How a Password Manager Stores and Protects Your Secrets**

Imagine your password manager as a *high‑security safe* that lives on your computer or phone.  
You put all your real passwords into the safe (they’re the “items” you save).  
But you don’t want anyone else to open it, so you lock it with one key: your **master password**.

When you open the safe, the manager uses a special recipe called *encryption*—a math trick that turns every stored password into gibberish. Only when you type the master password does the safe reverse the trick and reveal the real passwords again.  

So, Step 2 is all about **encrypting** your data so it stays unreadable on disk, and then **decrypting** it only when you prove you’re the rightful owner with the master password. This keeps your secrets safe even if someone steals the file or device.
