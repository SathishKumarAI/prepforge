Think of an AI engineer’s interview as a cooking class where the teacher asks you to prove you can follow a recipe, tweak it, and explain why each step matters. The “role guide” is like the menu: it tells you what dishes (skills) are expected—data cleaning, model training, deployment, ethics—and in which order they’re served.

During the interview, you’ll be asked questions that mirror these courses:

* **Prep course** – “How do you clean and balance a dataset?”  
* **Cooking course** – “Walk me through building a neural net for image classification.”  
* **Plating course** – “What steps would you take to deploy this model in production?”  
* **Taste test** – “How do you handle bias or explainability?”

Each question checks that you can not only execute the recipe but also justify your choices, just as a chef explains why they use a certain spice. The role guide ensures both sides see what’s on the menu and evaluate each dish fairly.
