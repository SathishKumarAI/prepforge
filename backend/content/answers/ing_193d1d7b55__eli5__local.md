Imagine you’re a chef who makes a signature soup. **MLOps Level 0** is like cooking that soup by hand: every ingredient (data), each stir (model training), and final taste test (evaluation) are done manually, one batch at a time. There’s no kitchen robot to add the right amount of seasoning automatically or to serve the soup instantly whenever someone orders it.

In **continuous delivery** we’d set up an automated “soup‑making machine.” Once you drop in fresh ingredients, the machine mixes, cooks, and tests the soup, then pushes a new batch to customers without human intervention. The **automation pipeline** is that machine’s recipe: data ingestion → preprocessing → training → validation → deployment, all chained together so one change triggers the whole flow.

So Level 0 is manual, slow, and error‑prone; continuous delivery + automation pipelines make it fast, repeatable, and reliable—just like a smart kitchen robot.
