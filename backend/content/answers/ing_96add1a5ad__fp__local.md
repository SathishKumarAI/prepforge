### Path 2 – *AI Product Specialist (Business Track)*

At its core this role solves the **“how do we turn an algorithm into revenue?”** problem.

This mirrors portfolio optimization: each feature is an asset whose expected return must be weighed against risk (model drift, regulatory constraints).

2. **Lifecycle orchestration** – From data ingestion to deployment, they design feedback loops that treat the AI as a *closed‑loop control system*. Continuous monitoring of loss functions and drift metrics guarantees that the product remains optimal under changing market conditions.

3. **Stakeholder alignment** – By quantifying trade‑offs (latency vs accuracy) in business terms, they become the bridge between engineers and executives, ensuring resource allocation follows Pareto efficiency rather than technical curiosity.

4. **Regulatory & ethical scaffolding** – They embed fairness constraints directly into the objective function, turning compliance from a legal requirement into an optimization variable that can be visualized as a *constraint surface* on the performance landscape.

A high‑accuracy model that requires constant human oversight may underperform a slightly less accurate but “easy to maintain” solution—an optimization principle rarely highlighted in traditional product discussions.

In sum, an AI Product Specialist turns predictive models into *business‑driven, continuously optimized assets*, ensuring every byte of data translates into measurable profit.
