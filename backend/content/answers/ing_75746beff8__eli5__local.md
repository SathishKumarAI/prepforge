**What’s an API Gateway?**

Think of a bustling train station where many trains (services) arrive and depart. The *API Gateway* is the station master: it receives all passengers’ tickets (requests), checks what destination they want, and hands them to the correct train. It also ensures safety—only legitimate tickets are accepted—and can give extra help, like translating languages or keeping a log of who boarded.

**Key points**

- **Gateway:** The single entry point for all external calls into your system.
- **Routing:** Directs each request to the right backend service (like the correct train).
- **Security & control:** Handles authentication, rate‑limiting, and logging so you don’t have to add these checks everywhere.

So, an API Gateway is the friendly traffic cop that keeps your services organized, secure, and easy for clients to use.
