**From first principles**

A system’s *availability* is a function of two variables: the probability that its components are healthy (P healthy) and the probability that failures are detected and corrected fast enough to keep traffic flowing (T response). In conventional IT, operations teams focus on monitoring and patching; in AI, the “components” are data pipelines, model serving endpoints, and training workloads—each with non‑stationary, stochastic behavior. An **AI SRE** is an engineer who formalizes *P healthy* and *T response* for these components using probabilistic models that capture drift, concept shift, and resource contention.

Thus, reliability engineering must be coupled with continual validation—embedding a lightweight validation loop into every inference request—to guarantee that *service quality* remains bounded across evolving distributions.
