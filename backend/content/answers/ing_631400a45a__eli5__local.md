Imagine reading a mystery novel where the detective has to guess what’s happening before all clues are revealed.  
BERT (Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers) is like that detective for computers. Developed by Google in 2018, it learned to read sentences **from both sides**—left and right—so it knows context better than earlier models that only looked forward or backward.

Key features:

* **Bidirectional training** – looks at the whole sentence at once.
* **Transformer architecture** – uses self‑attention to weigh every word’s importance.
* **Pre‑training then fine‑tuning** – first learns general language patterns, then is adapted to specific tasks (sentiment, question answering, etc.).

Because it can “understand” words in context, BERT set a new bar for natural‑language tasks and inspired many successors.
