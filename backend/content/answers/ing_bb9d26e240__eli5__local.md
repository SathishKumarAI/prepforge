**Supervised learning is like training a smart child to sort fruit.**  
You show the child many apples and oranges (the *training data*). For each picture you say, “This is an apple” or “This is an orange.” The child’s brain starts noticing patterns: color, shape, size. When you later give it a new, unlabeled fruit, it guesses based on what it learned—just like the child says “apple” if the fruit looks round and red.  
*Training data* = examples with labels; *model* = the child’s memory of those patterns; *prediction* = the child’s guess on unseen fruit. The more varied examples you give, the better the model becomes at spotting subtle differences.
