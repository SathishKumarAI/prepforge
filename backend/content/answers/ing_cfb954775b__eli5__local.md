Think of a **data architecture** like the plumbing system in a huge office building.  
The pipes (the “connectors”) move water (your data) from one room to another, while the valves and fixtures (the APIs) decide how much water flows where and let you control it on demand.

In a company’s digital world, the **data architecture** is the map that shows where every piece of information lives—databases, cloud storage, spreadsheets—and how those pieces talk to each other.  
APIs are the “faucets” that let applications pull or push data without opening up the whole system, while connectors are the “pipes” that carry raw data from one place to another.

So, just as a well‑designed plumbing network keeps water flowing smoothly and safely, a good data architecture keeps information moving efficiently, securely, and reliably across all parts of an organization.
