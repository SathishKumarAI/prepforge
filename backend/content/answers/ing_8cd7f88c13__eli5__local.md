Think of an LLM that learns to talk like a child who watches and listens to countless conversations. **Speech and audio models** are the “listening ears” that turn sounds into text, just as a kid turns spoken words into ideas.

1. **Feature extraction** – Imagine squeezing each sound wave through a filter that highlights its rhythm and pitch, turning it into a simple list of numbers (like taking a photo’s pixels).  
2. **Tokenization** – Those numbers are grouped into “tokens” (tiny units of meaning) so the model can handle them like words.  
3. **Sequence modeling** – The LLM then predicts what comes next in the token stream, just as a child guesses the next word while listening.
