Think of an AI app like a team of chefs in a busy kitchen. Each chef is an **AI agent**—a small program that follows one simple recipe: “If I see this ingredient, add it here.” The *best way to build any app* is to give each chef exactly what they need (data and instructions) so they can work side‑by‑side without stepping on each other’s plates.

1. **Define the goal** – decide what dish you want: a recipe for answering questions, translating language, or recognizing pictures.  
2. **Collect ingredients** – gather data that shows how the dish should taste (images, text, numbers).  
3. **Train each chef** – let them practice on many examples until they learn the right moves.  
4. **Add a manager bot** – this “partner” coordinates the chefs, decides who works on which part of a request, and stitches their outputs into one final dish.
