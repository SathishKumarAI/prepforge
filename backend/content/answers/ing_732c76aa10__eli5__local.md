Think of an AI agent as a chef in a bustling kitchen. **Context engineering** is like giving the chef a clear recipe card that lists all the ingredients, their quantities, and the cooking steps before the meal starts. If the chef only sees a vague “make something tasty” note, they might pick the wrong spices or overcook the dish. With a detailed context card, the chef knows exactly what to do—no guessing, no wasted time.

In AI terms, context engineering supplies the model with precise background information and rules so it can answer questions accurately, follow instructions correctly, and stay on topic. Without this “recipe,” even a powerful model can wander into irrelevant or incorrect territory. It’s the difference between a chef who improvises wildly and one who produces a perfectly seasoned dish every time.
