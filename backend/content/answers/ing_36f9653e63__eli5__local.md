Think of a behavioral interview like cooking a meal for guests you don’t yet know.  
**Behavioral questions** are the recipe cards—each one asks you to describe how you handled a situation in the past (e.g., “Tell me about a time you solved a conflict”).  

1. **Gather ingredients (STAR method)** – Structure your answer with *Situation*, *Task*, *Action*, and *Result*.  
2. **Prep ahead** – Write down 3–4 real stories that show teamwork, leadership, problem‑solving, or resilience.  
3. **Taste test (practice)** – Run through each story aloud, timing yourself to keep it concise (about 1–2 minutes).  
4. **Season to taste (adaptability)** – Read the interviewer’s cues and tweak your emphasis: if they ask about teamwork, highlight collaboration; if about deadlines, stress time‑management.  
5. **Clean up** – Ask a follow‑up question or share what you learned, showing curiosity and growth.
