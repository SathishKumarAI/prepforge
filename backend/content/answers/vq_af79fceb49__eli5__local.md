Imagine you’re packing a suitcase for a trip.

A **list** is like a flexible backpack. You can add, remove, or change items at any time—just open the zipper and shuffle things around. Because it’s so changeable, your computer treats it as “mutable.”  
A **tuple** is like a sealed luggage tag. Once you put the contents in, you can’t touch them without opening the lock and re‑sealing it. That makes tuples “immutable,” meaning their items stay fixed after creation.

So: lists are mutable (changeable) containers that can grow or shrink; tuples are immutable (fixed) containers. Use a list when you expect to modify data; use a tuple for constant, reliable values—just like choosing the right packing gear for your travel plans.
