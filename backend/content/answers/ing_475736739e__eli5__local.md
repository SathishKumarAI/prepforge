**What’s “Disaster Recovery” on Google Cloud?**  
Think of your data and apps as a big, valuable house. If a fire (or a hacker attack) destroys the original home, you want a spare house ready to move into right away so life can keep going. In cloud terms, disaster recovery is that spare house: a backup copy of all your files, databases, and services stored in another location.  

When something bad happens—say a server crash or a cyber‑attack—the system automatically switches traffic to the backup “house.” Google Cloud keeps these copies synchronized so they’re up‑to‑date. If the original fails, you can spin up new virtual machines from the backup instantly, minimizing downtime and keeping your customers happy. In short, disaster recovery is the safety net that lets you bounce back quickly after a big problem.
