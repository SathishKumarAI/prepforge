**System Design 101 for Machine Learning**

Think of building a machine‑learning system like planning a road trip with a GPS.  
You start by choosing the *destination* (the problem you want to solve). Next, you pick the *route* (your algorithm and model type) and decide how many stops you’ll make along the way—these are the data‑collection points that feed your training set. The *vehicles* are the computing resources: CPUs, GPUs, or cloud servers that actually run the math. You also need a *navigation system* to keep everything on track: monitoring tools that report accuracy, latency, and cost, so you can tweak the route if it’s too slow or expensive. Finally, just as a driver needs a reliable map, your ML system relies on a solid architecture—data pipelines, storage, APIs, and deployment scripts—to deliver predictions consistently to users.
