**Step 3 – “Create Cluster”**

Think of a *cluster* as a small town where each house (a **container**) is managed by a friendly mayor (**Kubernetes**). The mayor keeps track of who lives where, makes sure everyone has enough food (resources), and moves families if the weather changes. In DevOps‑SRE terms, we use Kubernetes to automatically set up this town on cloud servers so developers can drop in their apps without worrying about the underlying machines.

*Unavoidable terms explained:*  
- **DevOps**: The practice of blending development and operations teams to ship software faster.  
- **Kubernetes**: An open‑source system that automates deploying, scaling, and managing containers across many computers.  
- **Cluster**: A group of servers (machines) that Kubernetes treats as one big computer for running containers.
