**Step 9 – “Improve State Management” (AI Agents)**  

Think of an AI agent as a chef in a bustling kitchen. The *state* is the current inventory: ingredients, recipes on hand, and what dishes are already being prepared. Good state management means the chef keeps an up‑to‑date pantry list so they never run out or waste food.
