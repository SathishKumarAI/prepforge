Think of an AI model as a recipe that turns raw ingredients (data) into a dish (prediction).  
A **model parameter** is like the spice amount you add to that recipe—salt, pepper, or sugar. Each parameter tells the model exactly how much influence a particular piece of input should have when it makes its decision.

Just as a chef tweaks the spice level to get the perfect flavor, an AI learns by adjusting many such “spice” knobs (parameters) during training. The more parameters you have, the richer and more flexible your recipe can become, but also the harder it is to tune them all right. In short, a model parameter is a single adjustable setting that shapes how the AI interprets its inputs.
