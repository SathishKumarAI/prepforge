**Spaced Repetition with Paper Flash Cards**

Imagine a stack of index cards, each holding a single fact or question. When you first study a card, you’ll likely remember it for only a short time—maybe the next few minutes. Spaced repetition is like a smart reminder system that tells you when to look at each card again, just before your memory starts to fade.

You spend less time re‑learning the same facts and more time building new knowledge, all while keeping the material fresh in your mind.
