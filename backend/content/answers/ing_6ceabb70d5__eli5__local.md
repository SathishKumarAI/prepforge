**The Hybrid Approach – a “Smart‑Robot Sidekick”**

Think of an AI agent as a robot helper that can both *plan* and *act*.  
In the **hybrid approach**, two parts work together like a human’s brain:  

1. **Planner (the “brain’s map”)** – a rule‑based system that looks ahead, says “If I want to book a flight, first find a cheap ticket, then confirm payment.” It’s slow but careful, using stored knowledge and logic.  
2. **Performer (the “hand’s muscle”)** – a neural network that reacts instantly, reading real‑time data (e.g., current weather or traffic) and adjusting the plan on the fly.

The robot alternates: the planner lays out a safe route; the performer checks sensors and nudges the plan if something changes.  
This blend gives agents the best of both worlds—structured safety from rules and flexible adaptation from learning—so they can handle tasks that are too complex for one method alone.
