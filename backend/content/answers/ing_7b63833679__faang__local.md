**Depth**  
Procedural memory is a *procedural* store of motor and cognitive routines acquired through repetition. Unlike declarative memory, which encodes explicit facts (“Paris is the capital of France”), procedural memory encodes *how‑to* knowledge (e.g., “when I see a red traffic light, I stop”). Neurologically it relies on basal ganglia circuits for habit formation and cerebellar networks for fine motor timing. In AI, this maps to policy learning in reinforcement learning (RL) or sequence modeling with RNNs/Transformers that capture implicit procedural patterns from data without explicit supervision.
