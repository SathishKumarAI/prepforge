Think of a smart recipe book that learns how to cook dishes just by looking at past meals.

**Supervised learning** is like giving the book a set of finished dishes (the “training data”) and telling it exactly what each dish is called or how much spice it needs.  
- **Regression** is when the book must predict a *continuous* value, such as the exact amount of salt to add. It learns a smooth curve that best fits all past examples so future meals get just the right taste.  
- **Classification** is when the book decides which *category* a dish belongs to—“salad,” “soup,” or “dessert.” It draws boundaries in its mental space, so when it sees new ingredients it can shout the correct label.

In both cases the book is guided (“supervised”) by known answers and then uses that guidance to guess correctly on new, unseen recipes.
