**Context Engineering in machine learning**

Imagine you’re a detective who has a pile of clues (data). To solve the mystery, you first decide which clues matter and how to arrange them on a board so the picture becomes clear. **Context engineering** is that process: it’s the art of selecting, formatting, and arranging data so the model can “see” the right patterns.

*Unavoidable terms:*  
- **Feature** – an individual piece of information (e.g., age, color).  
- **Model** – a program that learns from features to make predictions.  

You might group words into topics, normalize numbers, or remove irrelevant noise—just like placing evidence in the right spots on your board. The better you engineer context, the easier it is for the model to spot the truth and give accurate answers.
