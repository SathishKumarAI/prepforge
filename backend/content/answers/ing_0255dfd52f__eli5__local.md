Imagine you and a teammate are solving a crossword together on a big whiteboard that everyone can see.  
You write down clues and possible answers in your own corner of the board (your “scratchpad”).  
Your teammate does the same, but both of you can also read each other’s notes, suggest edits, or add new ideas right there on the shared board.  

In AI this is called **Co‑Reasoning**: a human and an artificial system work side by side, each keeping its own “scratchpad” of thoughts (the chain of reasoning). The key twist is that they share a common space—like the whiteboard—so both can see, modify, or build on the other’s ideas.  

The **Human‑in‑the‑Loop** pattern simply means the human stays actively involved: they watch what the AI writes, correct mistakes, and steer the conversation until the goal is reached. This shared, visible workspace lets complex problems be solved collaboratively, just like two people solving a crossword together.
