When AI companies interview candidates, they often ask questions that test how well the candidate can predict the next word or sentence—essentially measuring *perplexity* of their thinking.

A common analogy: think of a crossword puzzle where you’re given only the last three letters of each answer. The harder the puzzle, the more uncertainty (higher perplexity) you have about the full words. Interviewers ask candidates to solve similar puzzles—like predicting missing code or completing a sentence—to see how efficiently they reduce uncertainty.
