Imagine a kitchen where you can either cook everything yourself or hire a sous‑chef (the agent) to help.  
Use an AI agent when the task is **repeated, rule‑based, and requires gathering information**—like checking weather updates for a travel plan or ordering groceries each week. The agent can keep learning from your feedback and handle small decisions on its own, freeing you to focus on higher‑level choices.  

Don’t rely on an agent when the job needs **deep creativity, complex judgment, or emotional nuance**, such as writing a heartfelt letter or designing a novel artwork. In those cases, the human is still the best cook; the agent can offer suggestions, but you should steer the final outcome.
