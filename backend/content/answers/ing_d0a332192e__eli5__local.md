**Disaster recovery** on Google Cloud is like having a backup power generator for your house.  
When something bad happens—say a flood knocks out the main power (a server crash or data loss)—you need to get everything running again quickly and cheaply. Google Cloud’s disaster‑recovery tools let you copy (“replicate”) your important files, databases, and applications to another safe spot in the cloud. Because all the data is already stored elsewhere, you don’t have to rebuild from scratch or pay for expensive on‑site repairs.
