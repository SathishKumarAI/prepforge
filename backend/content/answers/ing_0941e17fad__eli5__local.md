**Retrieval‑Augmented Generation (RAG)** is like having a trusty librarian who can fetch the exact page from any book when you ask for a fact. The AI writes the answer, but it pulls in fresh snippets on the spot, so it stays current and can cite sources.

**Long‑context models** are like a giant notebook that remembers everything you’ve written so far. They keep all earlier sentences in mind, so they can weave a coherent narrative across many pages without needing new research each time.

**Fine‑tuning** is akin to training a ghostwriter on a specific author’s style. You give the model a bunch of their books; it learns patterns and voice, then writes new content that feels like it came from that author.
