**Why a “Python Full Course for Beginners” is structured the way it is**

At its core, learning to program is an *optimization problem*: we want to convert human intent into executable steps with minimal cognitive friction. Python’s syntax was deliberately chosen to reduce that friction—whitespace‑based blocks eliminate braces, and dynamic typing lets novices experiment without boilerplate.
