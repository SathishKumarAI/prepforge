**DevSecOps** is a way of building software that mixes three ideas: *Development* (making new code), *Security* (protecting it from attacks), and *Operations* (running it in the real world). Think of it like baking a cake for a big party.

- **Developers** are the bakers who mix flour, sugar, eggs, and flavorings.  
- **Security** is the oven’s temperature control and the safety check that makes sure no fire starts or toxic ingredients slip in.  
- **Operations** are the people who deliver the cake to guests, keep it fresh, and replace crumbs.

In DevSecOps, everyone shares a single recipe book (the code repository) and works together from day one: developers write code, security experts review it automatically while it’s being written, and operations teams deploy and monitor it right away. The goal is to catch problems early, so the final “cake” (your software) arrives safe, reliable, and ready for users.
