**Pitfall 5: “Cost Blindness – Capability Assessment”**

Imagine you’re buying a new kitchen gadget. You stare at the shiny price tag but ignore how much energy it uses or how many times you’ll have to replace its parts. In AI, people often look only at how powerful a model looks—its accuracy or speed—and forget the hidden costs: the electricity needed to train it, the servers that must run it, and the data that must be collected. This “cost blindness” means we may choose a huge, expensive model when a smaller one would serve just as well for our purpose.
