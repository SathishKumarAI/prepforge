**Agentic Retrieval‑Augmented Generation (RAG)** is like a curious student who first looks up facts in a big library, then writes an essay from those notes.  
The “agent” (the AI) decides which books to open and how much of each page to copy—so it can weave together fresh, detailed answers that feel very human.  

Because the student chooses different passages each time, even with the same question the final essay can change: a small shift in what’s read changes wording or emphasis.  
Thus **quality** (richness, nuance) goes up, but **reliability**—the guarantee of getting exactly the same answer every time—goes down.  

In short, letting the AI pick its own sources makes it more creative but less deterministic.
