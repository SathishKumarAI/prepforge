Imagine your favorite video‑chat app as a huge library that must always be open for visitors.  
**Reliability** is the promise that the library will stay open whenever someone wants to enter—no sudden closures, no broken doors. In Facebook or Instagram this means servers that keep your feed and messages running even if one part of the system fails.  

**Redundancy** is having backup copies of every book in multiple rooms. If a fire takes down one room, another copy is ready to serve visitors right away. For social networks it’s duplicate data centers, mirrored databases, and spare servers that automatically take over when a component crashes.
