Imagine a machine‑learning model as a recipe that you want to share with friends who live in different kitchens.  
**Build** is like writing the recipe and gathering all the ingredients (data, code, libraries) into one tidy cookbook.  
**Release** is putting that cookbook on a shared shelf so anyone can pick it up; it’s just the finished recipe—no cooking yet.  
**Run** is when each friend opens the cookbook in their own kitchen and follows the steps to make the dish (the model actually predicts).  

In 12‑Factor terms, “build” creates a *slug* (a portable bundle), “release” stores it as an immutable artifact, and “run” spins up a container that reads only its configuration from the environment. The result is a reproducible, versioned model that can be deployed anywhere without surprises.
