Imagine you’re at a giant library that has millions of books, but the librarian (your computer) can’t read every page before answering a question.  
**Query understanding** is like the librarian first asking you to describe what you want—“Do you need a recipe, a history lesson, or a math trick?” The librarian turns your words into a clear “search request.”  

**Retrieval** is then the librarian quickly pulling out only the books that match that description from the shelves.  
**RAG (Retrieve‑Augment‑Generate)** takes those selected pages and lets the computer read them on the spot, weaving their content into a fresh answer just for you.  

So RAG is the librarian who first figures out your need, grabs the right books, reads the relevant parts, and writes a personalized reply—all in real time.
