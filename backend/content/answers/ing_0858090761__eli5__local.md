Imagine a huge library where each book is a possible sentence your model could produce.  
**Sampling (inference)** is like picking a page at random from that library, but with a twist: you’re more likely to choose pages that fit the story so far. The model looks at the words it has already written and assigns a *probability* to every next word—think of these as “likelihood scores.” It then flips a weighted coin for each candidate word, picks one, adds it to the sentence, and repeats until the story ends or you stop.

In nanoGPT, this process is called inference. Sampling is just a fancy way of saying “generate new text by drawing from those probability weights.” No equations needed—just a smart random choice guided by past context.
