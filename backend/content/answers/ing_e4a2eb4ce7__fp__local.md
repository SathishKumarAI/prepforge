**Why a behavioral interview matters before the code‑challenge**

At its core a hiring panel is solving an *optimization* problem: choose the candidate who will deliver the most value under uncertainty.  
Behavioral questions expose the *latent variables* (communication, resilience, learning speed) that are hard to quantify but strongly correlate with future performance. By asking candidates to narrate past experiences you transform qualitative traits into observable patterns—an implicit Bayesian inference of their future contributions.
