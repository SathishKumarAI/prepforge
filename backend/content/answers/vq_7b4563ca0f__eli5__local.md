**Accuracy** in AI is like checking how often a guessing game’s answers match the real ones.  
Imagine you’re flipping a coin and your computer has to say “heads” or “tails.” After many flips, you look at all its guesses: every time it was right counts as a correct guess; wrong guesses don’t count. Accuracy is simply the ratio of correct guesses to total guesses—so if out of 100 flips it got 85 right, its accuracy is 85 %.  
In machine‑learning terms, “accuracy” is just this percentage of predictions that exactly match the true labels. It’s a quick, intuitive way to see how often the model gets things right, but it doesn’t tell you about mistakes that look similar or about rare events—just a straight count of correct versus all attempts.
