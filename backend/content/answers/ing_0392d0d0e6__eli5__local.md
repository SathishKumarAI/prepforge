Imagine you’re ordering food at a restaurant.  
With **REST**, you walk up to the counter, ask for a “burger,” and the waiter brings you exactly that plate—no extras, no missing bits. Each dish (API endpoint) is fixed: /burgers, /salads, /drinks. If you want more than one item, you go back to the counter again.

With **GraphQL**, think of a smart kitchen where you can write a single note saying “give me the burger with lettuce and no onions, plus the fries and a drink.” The kitchen (server) reads that request and gives you only what you asked for—no extra data, no repeated trips. You control exactly which parts of each dish appear.

So REST is like ordering one preset menu item at a time; GraphQL lets you pull together precisely the pieces you need in one request.
