Imagine you’re renting a library that grows as your books (data) do.  
**Serverless** is like a pay‑as‑you‑borrow model: you only pay for the exact space and search power you use at any moment—great when traffic spikes or drops, but you don’t control where the servers sit.  
**Hosted** is like renting a whole shelf in a fixed location: you pay a steady monthly fee that covers a set amount of storage and compute, plus you can choose which data center (region) hosts your library. This gives predictable costs and consistent performance, but you must plan for capacity ahead of time.  

Elastic’s official Cloud pricing shows these two options side by side so you can pick the one that fits how much data you’ll hold and how often people will search it.
