**Functional requirements** are the “what” part of a machine‑learning project – they spell out exactly what the system must do once it’s finished.  
Think of building a smart vending machine that only dispenses drinks when it recognizes you. The functional requirements would list things like: *“The machine must identify a user’s face, match it to a database, and then allow access to the chosen drink.”* Each requirement is a clear instruction for the software, just as a recipe tells you which ingredients to add and when.

In this analogy, the vending machine is the **model**, the user’s face is the input data, and the database of faces is the training set. By writing down each required action (identify, match, dispense), we give developers a concrete checklist that keeps the project focused and measurable—no fancy equations needed, just precise, testable steps.
