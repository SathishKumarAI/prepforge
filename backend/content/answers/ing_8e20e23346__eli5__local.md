Imagine a busy newsroom where reporters write stories and editors review them before printing. In “human‑in‑the‑loop” AI, the computer is the reporter—it drafts content or flags problems—but a human still checks it to keep quality high.  

**Human‑in‑the‑loop validation** means that after the AI makes a suggestion, a person reviews it and gives a thumbs‑up or edits it. The goal is **reviewer throughput**: how many pieces an editor can check in a day without feeling rushed.  
Designers make this fast by giving reviewers clear prompts, highlighting only the parts that need attention, and letting them approve or tweak quickly—just like an editor who sees a highlighted sentence and can click “accept” or “change” in seconds. The result is a smoother workflow where AI handles routine work and humans focus on the nuanced decisions.
