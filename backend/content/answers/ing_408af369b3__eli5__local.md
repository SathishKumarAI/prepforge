**Step‑by‑Step Prompt Optimization**

Imagine you’re giving a recipe to a cook who speaks your language but doesn’t know the dish yet. If you hand them the whole cookbook at once, they’ll be overwhelmed and might miss key steps. Instead, you give one clear instruction, wait for them to finish, then add the next detail. Each tiny instruction builds on what just happened, so the cook ends up with a perfect meal.
