Imagine your brain is a huge library, but sometimes the book you want is buried deep inside. **Contextual Retrieval** is like having a smart librarian who first looks at what you’ve already read (the “context”) and then pulls out only the most relevant sections from the entire collection to answer your question.

In AI terms, the model remembers earlier parts of the conversation or text, searches its internal knowledge base for related pieces, and stitches them together.  
Use it when a task needs deep, accurate facts—like troubleshooting a complex problem or writing a detailed report. Skip it if you’re just asking a quick yes/no question or want fast, lightweight responses; then the extra search can slow things down without much benefit.
