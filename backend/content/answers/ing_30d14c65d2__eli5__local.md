**Latency in machine learning is how long it takes from asking a model to give an answer until the answer arrives.**  
Think of a busy restaurant kitchen: you place your order (the input), the chef prepares the dish (the model processes data), and the waiter brings it back to your table (the output). Latency is the time between when you say “I’d like that salad” and when the waiter hands it to you.  

In practical terms, lower latency means a smoother experience—your app feels instant, while higher latency can make users wait too long. It’s measured in milliseconds or seconds, just like how we talk about traffic speed (how fast something travels). In machine learning, reducing latency often involves faster hardware, smarter code, or lighter models so the “kitchen” can cook quicker and serve you promptly.
