1. **Ingredients (Data)** – You must pick clean, relevant samples and explain why some are better than others.

3. **Cooking method (Training & tuning)** – Show how you’ll train the model, avoid over‑fitting (a cake that tastes great only in the kitchen), and tune hyper‑parameters like oven temperature.  
4. **Serving (Deployment)** – Explain how the system will run in production—scaling, monitoring, and updating without breaking service.  
5. **Quality check (Evaluation & validation)** – Use metrics such as accuracy or F1-score to prove the cake tastes good on fresh data.
