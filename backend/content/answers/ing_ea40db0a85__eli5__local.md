Imagine your machine‑learning model is a chef who can serve dishes at three different types of restaurants.

**Agent Engine (the food truck)**  
It’s lightweight and runs right on the customer’s own kitchen appliances (like a laptop or edge device). You bring the code, it “docks” to any place you need—no big restaurant setup required. Great for quick tests or low‑traffic services.

**Cloud Run (the pop‑up kiosk)**  
Here the chef stays in a shared building that automatically opens and closes with demand. You upload your recipe; the system runs it in containers that scale up when orders spike and shut down when they’re quiet, all managed by the cloud provider. No server maintenance on your side.

**GKE (the full‑service restaurant)**  
This is a big kitchen cluster where you can keep many chefs (models) working together, sharing resources, and handling complex workflows. You control everything—networking, storage, security—but you also manage the servers that run them.

So, choose the food truck for speed, the kiosk for effortless scaling, or the restaurant for full power and flexibility.
