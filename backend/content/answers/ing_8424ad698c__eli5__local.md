**Model Cascading (Efficiency Tiers) – a simple playbook**

Think of a big library with many shelves. The *most expensive* books are rare first‑edition volumes (the high‑cost, high‑accuracy AI models). If you can answer most questions from the *cheaper* paperback section (low‑cost, fast models), you’ll save money and time. In model cascading, a quick, inexpensive model first scans a request. If it’s confident enough, it gives the final answer—no need to borrow the pricey volume. Only when the quick model says “I’m not sure” does the system pull in the more expensive model for a deeper dive. By layering models from cheap to costly and only escalating when needed, you keep costs low while still getting high‑quality answers when they truly matter.
