Imagine a library where every book is a piece of personal data. **Data sovereignty** means that only the owner of each book decides who can read it, how it’s stored, and whether it can be copied to another shelf. In AI, this translates into laws and technical safeguards that let people keep their most sensitive information—like medical records or financial secrets—inside a trusted “shelf” that they control.

**Control** is the set of tools (encryption, access logs, audit trails) that let the owner see who’s looking at the books and for what purpose. By keeping the data in a secure, legally protected environment, AI systems can learn from it without exposing or misusing the information.
