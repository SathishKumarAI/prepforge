**Contextual Retrieval in AI Systems**

Imagine a library where every book is tagged with tiny sticky notes that describe its content—“history,” “science,” “recipe.” When you ask the librarian for a story about dinosaurs, she doesn’t just pull the first dinosaur book; she looks at all the sticky notes, reads their brief summaries, and then shows you the one that best fits your question.  

In AI, *contextual retrieval* works the same way. The system scans many pieces of information (the “books”), checks a quick summary or label for each (“sticky note”), and picks the most relevant piece to answer your query. This lets the AI give accurate answers without having to read every single text in detail, just like the librarian uses sticky notes instead of reading every book aloud.
