Think of building a LEGO castle that you and your friends keep adding new rooms to every day.  
**Continuous Integration (CI)** is the rule that says: *whenever someone adds a room, it must be glued on right away and tested immediately.* You drop the new piece into the castle, run a quick “does everything still fit?” check, and if something falls apart you fix it before anyone else moves on.  

**Continuous Delivery (CD)** is the next step: once the castle passes all checks, it’s automatically ready to show to guests—no manual hand‑off needed. If you want even faster delivery, **Continuous Deployment** drops the updated castle straight onto the exhibition floor as soon as tests pass.

So, CI = “add and test instantly”; CD = “test then ship automatically.” It keeps your project stable while still letting you add new features every day.
