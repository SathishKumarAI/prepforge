Imagine you’re at a birthday party where everyone is wearing matching outfits. Suddenly someone shows up in a bright orange suit— that odd outfit is the *anomaly*. In machine learning, anomaly detection is like a vigilant party host who spots that mismatched guest quickly and calmly.

The algorithms are simple “rule‑checkers” or “crowd‑behaviour monitors.”  
* **Statistical methods** set a boundary around the normal outfits (e.g., 95 % of guests wear blue). Anything outside—our orange suit—gets flagged.  
* **Distance‑based techniques** measure how far each guest is from the nearest group; outliers are those standing alone.  
* **Density‑based models** look at crowdedness: a solo person in an empty corner is suspicious.
