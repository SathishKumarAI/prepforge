Think of a **tuple** as a *fixed photo album* that you can open and see all the pictures inside, but you cannot add or delete any picture once it’s taken. In Python, a tuple is a collection of values written in parentheses, like `(1, “apple”, 3.14)`. Because it’s immutable (unchangeable), you can rely on its contents staying exactly the same, which makes it safe to use as keys in dictionaries or to share data that should never be altered.
