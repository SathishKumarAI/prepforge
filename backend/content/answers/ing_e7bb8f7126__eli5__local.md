**Collect – gathering the ingredients for a recipe**

Think of building a machine‑learning model like baking a cake.  
The *collect* part is where you go to the kitchen and pick out all the raw items: flour, eggs, sugar, milk, and spices. In data terms, this means finding the facts you’ll later train on—customer clicks, sensor readings, photos, or text snippets.

- **Data source** = the place that holds your ingredients (a database, a web API, a file).  
- **ETL** (Extract‑Transform‑Load) is the kitchen counter: you pull (“extract”) the items out of their storage, clean and shape them into a consistent format (“transform”), then put them in one common pantry (“load”).

Just as a good cake starts with clean, well‑measured ingredients, a good ML model needs data that’s reliably collected from all relevant sources.
