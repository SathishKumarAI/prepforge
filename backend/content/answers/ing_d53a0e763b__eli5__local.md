**Verification and Grading in Loop Engineering**

Think of a robot that learns to walk by trying many steps, like a child learning to hop on one foot. **Verification** is the teacher’s check: after each hop, the teacher looks—“Did you keep your balance? Did you land safely?” If the answer is yes, the hop passes; if not, it fails. The robot keeps a log of all its hops and the teacher’s verdicts.

**Grading** is like giving a scorecard for each hop: “Good (3 points) – you balanced well,” or “Needs improvement (1 point).” Over time the robot sees which types of hops earn high grades, learns to repeat those patterns, and improves its walking style. In AI, loops run many times, each iteration is verified against rules and graded so the system knows what works best—just as a child refines their hop after every teacher’s check.
