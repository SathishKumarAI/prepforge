**Think of your AI like a smart door‑bell that decides whether someone is “important” (yes) or not (no).**  
You can set the bell’s sensitivity: make it ring for every visitor (high recall) or only for the most likely VIPs (high precision).  

- **Precision** = when the bell rings, how often a real VIP actually shows up.  
- **Recall** = of all the real VIPs that came, how many did the bell catch.

In business you choose the setting that balances two costs:  
1) Ringing too much (low precision) wastes staff time and annoys customers.  
2) Missing too many (low recall) means you lose sales or security breaches.  

So threshold tuning is like adjusting a dial on the door‑bell to match what your company cares about most—more accuracy in catching the right people, or fewer missed opportunities—while keeping costs acceptable.
