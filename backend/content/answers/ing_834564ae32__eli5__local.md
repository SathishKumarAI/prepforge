Think of a classroom where most students follow the same routine: they sit at their desks, raise hands, and answer questions. The teacher’s job is to spot any student who behaves oddly—maybe someone sitting on the floor, whispering to themselves, or ignoring class altogether. In machine learning, **anomaly detection** does exactly that for data: it learns what “normal” looks like and flags anything that deviates.

A simple analogy is a security guard watching a crowd at an airport. The guard knows how most people walk—straight, with a slight shoulder tilt—and quickly notices someone staggering or moving backwards.
