Imagine your brain as a giant library.  
**Embeddings** are like the library’s catalog cards: each card turns a book’s whole content into a short, unique number string that tells you what the book is about without opening it. In AI, embeddings turn text (or images) into these “number cards” so computers can compare ideas quickly.

**RAG (Retrieval‑Augmented Generation)** is the librarian who, when you ask a question, looks up the most relevant cards in the catalog, pulls out the actual books, reads them, and then writes a fresh answer that blends your question with the new information.
