Think of a machine‑learning project as building a house that will later host your AI “home.”  
A **data architecture** is the blueprint that tells you where each room (dataset) goes, how the plumbing (data pipelines) connects them, and what kind of furniture (storage format) fits best. It decides whether data lives in a quick‑access pantry (in‑memory cache), a long‑term attic (data lake), or a secure vault (database).  
Just as a good blueprint prevents leaks, misplacement, and costly rework, a well‑designed data architecture ensures the model can reach clean, consistent information fast, keeps privacy rules intact, and lets you add new rooms without tearing down the whole house. In short, it’s the plan that turns raw data into reliable “rooms” for your ML algorithms to learn from.
