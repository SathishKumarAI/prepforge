**LLM‑as‑a‑Judge** is a way of using a big AI model (an “LLM” – *Large Language Model*) to act like a courtroom judge. Imagine a robot that reads every argument, sees the evidence, and then gives a verdict in plain language—just as a human judge does.  

The model receives the case text, legal rules, and any relevant facts, processes them all at once, and outputs an opinion: “This claim is likely true” or “No evidence supports it.” It doesn’t replace lawyers; it simply gives a consistent, quick, unbiased first pass that humans can review.

Key terms  
- **LLM (Large Language Model)** – a computer program trained on huge amounts of text to understand and generate language.  
- **Judge** – the person in court who decides whether evidence meets the law.

So LLM‑as‑a‑Judge is like having an ever‑available, impartial referee that helps speed up legal decisions while still letting humans keep the final say.
