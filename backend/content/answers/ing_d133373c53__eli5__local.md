Think of an **AI agent** as a smart helper in a kitchen. It watches what’s happening—like seeing you reach for a cookie—and can decide to grab it, stir the batter, or even suggest a new recipe. It acts based on its goals (keep you happy, cook dinner) and can use many tools: sensors, a stove, a pantry, or the internet.

A **chatbot** is just the kitchen’s talking window. When you ask it for directions to the cookie jar, it replies with words (“Go straight, then turn left”). It talks but doesn’t do anything itself; it relies on other parts of the system (the agent) to actually fetch the cookie or stir the pot.

So, an agent is the full kitchen crew that can perceive, decide, and act. A chatbot is only the friendly voice you talk to, asking questions and receiving answers.
