Think of a recipe book that tells you how many people the dish can feed and how long it takes to cook. In data engineering, *measures* are those “how‑much” facts we pull from tables—just like calories or cooking time. There are three common kinds:

1. **Raw measures** – the straight numbers already stored (e.g., sales amount, page views).  
2. **Derived measures** – values you compute on the fly (average price, growth rate).  
3. **Aggregated measures** – summaries that combine many rows into one figure (total revenue, total clicks).
