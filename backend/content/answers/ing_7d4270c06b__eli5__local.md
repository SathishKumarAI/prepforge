Imagine a chef who already knows how to cook thousands of dishes (the pre‑trained model). You want that chef to master a new recipe—say, a spicy Thai curry—but you don’t need to teach every cooking skill from scratch. Each time they cook, they tweak their seasoning until the taste matches the book.

This lets us quickly create specialized AI without starting from zero.
