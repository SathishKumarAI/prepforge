Imagine you’re texting a friend who lives in the next town over. If they reply almost instantly, the conversation feels natural; if there’s a long pause, you start to feel out of sync and the flow breaks. In the world of AI chatbots, **latency** is that pause – the time it takes for the system to receive your message, think about an answer, and send it back.

Latency matters because even a one‑second delay can make a chatbot feel sluggish or unhelpful. Short latency keeps users engaged, mimics human timing, and lets businesses offer real‑time support. Long latency feels like waiting at a slow elevator: the experience stalls, frustration builds, and people may abandon the conversation altogether. Keeping latency low is key to a smooth, trustworthy AI chat.
