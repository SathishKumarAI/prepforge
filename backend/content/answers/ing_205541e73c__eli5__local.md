Think of building a smart robot that can learn from its environment.  
An **AI engineer** is like the architect who plans the whole house: they decide what rooms (tasks) the robot needs—seeing, speaking, moving—and design how those rooms will talk to each other. They build the overall system and make sure all parts fit together.

A **ML engineer** is more like a builder who actually constructs one of those rooms—the learning room. They take data, choose a training method, run experiments, and fine‑tune the “brain” so it can recognize patterns or make predictions.

So, the AI engineer maps out the big picture, while the ML engineer fills in the details that let the robot learn and act intelligently.
