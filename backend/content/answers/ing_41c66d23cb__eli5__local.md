Imagine a robot librarian that can help you find books.  
A **stateless** librarian looks at your request, pulls the right book from the shelf, and then forgets everything about that interaction. If you ask again later, it treats you as a brand‑new visitor and starts over.  

A **stateful** librarian remembers who you are, what you’ve read before, and even what you said last week. When you come back, it can suggest new titles based on your past choices or continue a conversation without repeating the same questions.

**Key difference:**  
- *Stateless AI* treats each input as an isolated event; it has no memory of previous inputs.  
- *Stateful AI* keeps a record (a “state”) of earlier interactions, allowing it to personalize responses and maintain context over time.  

Both work well, but the stateful version feels more like talking to a human who remembers you.
