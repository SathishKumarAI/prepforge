Think of a system‑design interview like building a tiny town on a sandbox. You’re given a prompt (e.g., “create a ride‑sharing app”), and you must decide how the streets, houses, power grid, and police station connect so everyone can get where they need to go quickly and safely.

**Where to learn this craft**

1. **Books** – *Designing Data-Intensive Applications* gives you the big picture of how data moves through a town (databases, caches, queues).  
2. **Online courses** – Coursera or Udemy’s “System Design” tracks walk through common towns: social networks, search engines, and messaging systems, showing layout choices and trade‑offs.  
3. **Practice sites** – LeetCode, InterviewBit, and Grokking the System Design Interview provide prompt‑by‑prompt town‑building scenarios with model answers to study.  
4. **Community discussions** – Reddit’s r/cscareerquestions or Discord channels let you ask real‑world design questions and see how others sketch their maps.
