**Context Engineering in AI – Think of it as setting up a stage for a play**

Imagine you’re directing a drama. Before the actors speak, you decide where they stand, what props they hold, and what lighting shines on them. That “stage setup” is the *context*—the background information that shapes how the story unfolds.

In AI, context engineering does exactly that: it supplies the model with carefully chosen facts, rules, or examples so the answer comes out just right. For instance, if you want a recipe in French, you give the model a short note saying “respond in French” and maybe show one example sentence. The model then uses those clues to generate a response that fits the requested language.

**Key terms**

- **Context** – Background info fed into the AI before it writes its answer.  
- **Prompt** – The question or instruction you give the AI, often combined with context.  

By crafting good context—like setting up a clear stage—you guide the AI to produce useful, relevant results every time.
