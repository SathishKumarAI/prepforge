Think of a learning robot that asks you, “Did I get the right answer?” After it makes a guess (the *receiver*), you tell it whether the guess was correct or not—this is the *acknowledgment*. In machine‑learning training, this feedback is called the **loss**: a number that tells the model how far off its prediction was.
