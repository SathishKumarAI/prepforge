Imagine you’re trying to understand how a smart home assistant works.  
**White‑box testing** is like opening the box and looking inside: you see the wiring, the tiny circuits, and you can trace exactly which sensor triggers which action. In AI terms, white‑box tests look at the model’s internal parameters, loss functions, and gradients to verify that each part behaves as expected.  

**Black‑box testing** is like giving the assistant a list of commands (“play music,” “turn on lights”) without peeking inside. You only care about the answers it gives—does it play the right song or turn the light on? AI black‑box tests evaluate performance based solely on inputs and outputs, ignoring how the model reaches its conclusions.

Both are useful: white‑box checks that the internals are correct, while black‑box confirms that the overall behavior meets user needs.
