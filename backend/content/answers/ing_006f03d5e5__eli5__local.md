Imagine you’re cooking a new recipe that mixes two styles—say, Italian and Japanese. The *hybrid strategy* in machine learning does the same: it blends a rule‑based system (the “chef’s manual”) with an AI model (the “smart sous‑chef”).  

**Context engineering** is like preparing the right mise‑en‑place before you start cooking. It means deciding what background information the AI should see—what ingredients, how they’re measured, and which kitchen rules matter. For example, if your AI is predicting customer orders, context engineering would give it the day of the week, weather forecast, or local events so it knows whether people crave pizza or sushi that day.
