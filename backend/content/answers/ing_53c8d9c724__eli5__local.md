Imagine you’re writing a story while talking to a friend who’s listening on the phone. As soon as you finish one sentence, your friend can hear it and react before you even think about the next part. **Streaming generation** is like that: the AI sends each word or chunk of text out immediately, instead of waiting until the whole answer is finished.

Now picture that while you’re speaking, your friend’s phone is also pulling in relevant news headlines from a database to keep the conversation up‑to‑date. That “pulling in” is **early retrieval**: the AI fetches useful information (facts, recent data) *before* it writes the answer, so what follows feels fresh and accurate.

So, streaming generation + early retrieval = an AI that talks back word by word while already gathering the best facts to include right away—just like chatting with a well‑prepared friend who never keeps you waiting.
