Imagine you’re building a LEGO set that’s also a recipe book. In **Spec‑Driven Development (SDD)**, the “spec” is like the instruction sheet: it lists every piece (feature) and tells exactly where it should fit. The developers build each part only after reading its spot in the spec, just as a chef follows a recipe before cooking.

Because the spec is written first, it becomes **documentation** automatically—no extra manual writing needed. When you later look back at the finished model, you can trace every piece back to its line in the spec, so the whole structure is transparent and easy to explain. Thus, SDD turns requirements into a living document that both guides coding and records what was built, satisfying documentation needs without extra effort.
