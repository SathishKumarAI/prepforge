**Why AI helps a Meta‑style coding interview**

The interview is an *optimization* over two variables: the candidate’s **knowledge** \(K\) and the **problem difficulty** \(D\). The interviewer seeks a sweet spot where  
\( \text{Success}(K,D)=1 \) with minimal time. An AI that can generate, evaluate, and adapt problems turns this into an *online learning* loop: observe the candidate’s responses, update a belief over \(K\), and select the next prompt that maximizes expected information gain about \(K\).

**Non‑obvious insight**

The *information‑theoretic* value of a question is not just its difficulty but how **orthogonal** it is to prior answers. An AI can compute the mutual information between candidate responses and latent skill dimensions, ensuring each subsequent prompt narrows uncertainty most efficiently—something human interviewers rarely formalize. This turns the coding test into a data‑driven Bayesian experiment rather than a static script.
