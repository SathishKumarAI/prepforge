At a data‑analytics startup, our product team needed to expose “session duration” for every user in the BI dashboard. The raw logs had two timestamp columns: `start_ts` and `end_ts`. **Action**

2. **Design & AWS Services** – created a materialized view in Redshift:

   ```sql
   CREATE MATERIALIZED VIEW session_durations AS
   SELECT user_id,
          DATE_PART('epoch', end_ts) - DATE_PART('epoch', start_ts) AS duration_seconds
   FROM logs
   WHERE end_ts > start_ts;
   ```
