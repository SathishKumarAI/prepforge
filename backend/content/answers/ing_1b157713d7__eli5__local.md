**Planning and Decomposition in AI**

Think of an AI like a robot chef who must cook a full meal from scratch. **Planning** is the recipe: the AI decides what dishes to make, when to start each one, and which ingredients are needed, all while keeping track of time and resources. **Decomposition** breaks that big cooking task into smaller steps—mixing batter, chopping veggies, baking bread—so the robot can focus on one simple action at a time.

- *Planning*: Choosing a sequence of actions to reach a goal (e.g., “serve dinner by 7 pm”).  
- *Decomposition*: Splitting a complex action into manageable sub‑actions (e.g., “make sauce” → “heat pan,” “add tomatoes,” “stir”).

Together, they let the AI handle big problems by first drawing an overall map and then tackling each piece one by one. This is how many real‑world AIs—like autonomous cars or game characters—decide what to do next.
