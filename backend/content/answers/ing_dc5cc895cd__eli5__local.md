**“Don’t ship vibes” means don’t release an AI system just because it feels right or looks good on paper—ship only after you’ve proven it works reliably in the real world.**

Think of building a self‑driving car. Before letting it hit the road, you test it on a closed track, run thousands of simulated drives, and monitor every sensor for errors. You don’t just hand it to drivers because it *seems* safe; you ship it only when data shows it behaves predictably under all conditions.

In AI terms, “vibes” are intuition or visual dashboards that suggest success. But without rigorous evaluation (Evals) and continuous monitoring (Observability), those vibes can hide bugs or biases. So, before releasing an agent, first prove with concrete metrics—then ship.
