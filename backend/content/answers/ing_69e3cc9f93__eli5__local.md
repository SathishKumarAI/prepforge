Imagine you’re planning a road trip. A **chain** is like following one single map: you start at home, drive to the first town, then straight on to the next, and so on until you reach your destination. Each step depends only on the previous one; if you miss a turn, the whole route can break.

A **tree** is like having a branching set of maps. From each town you consider several possible detours—maybe a scenic drive, a shortcut, or a rest stop—and from those new routes you again branch out. You’re exploring many paths at once, keeping track of all the choices and their outcomes.
