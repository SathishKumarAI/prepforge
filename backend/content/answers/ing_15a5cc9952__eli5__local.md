Imagine building a giant LEGO city that must adapt to new rules every day. A **software architect** is the master builder who plans where each block goes so the whole city stays solid, can grow, and still works when new blocks arrive.

Key skills for this role in machine‑learning projects are:

1. **System design** – sketching big‑picture diagrams that show how data moves from sensors to models to dashboards.

3. **Scalability thinking** – planning for millions of users or petabytes of data without breaking.
4. **Security & privacy** – keeping sensitive data safe while still usable for training.
