Imagine you’re learning a new language by reading a huge library of books *before* you ever try to write or answer questions about them. BERT is like that library‑reader: it first “pre‑trains” on all the text it can find, learning how words usually appear next to each other and what hidden meanings they might carry.

BERT uses a special kind of neural network called a **transformer** that reads sentences from both sides at once—*bidirectional*. This is like looking at a picture with your eyes closed and then opening them; you get context from the left *and* the right, so you can guess what a missing word should be. After this big practice run, BERT can quickly understand new sentences, answer questions, or translate, because it already knows how language usually works.

So think of BERT as a super‑readable student who first devours all books, then uses that knowledge to solve any language puzzle you throw at it.
