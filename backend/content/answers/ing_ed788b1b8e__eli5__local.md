**Implementation Requirements – Safety and Governance**

Think of an AI system as a smart robot gardener that can plant, water, and prune plants on its own. *Safety* is the set of rules that make sure it never harms the garden—like not over‑watering (avoiding overheating) or cutting the wrong branch (preventing harmful outputs). *Governance* is the team of gardeners who design those rules, monitor the robot’s work, and update the instructions when new plants arrive.

**Key parts:**

- **Clear objectives** – tell the gardener exactly what “healthy garden” means.  
- **Checks and balances** – put sensors that flag dangerous actions before they happen.  
- **Human oversight** – have gardeners review the robot’s decisions regularly, especially in complex situations.  
- **Transparent logs** – keep a notebook of every action so you can see why something was done.

By combining these safety nets with strong governance, the gardener AI stays helpful and trustworthy, just like a well‑managed garden.
