**1️⃣ Clarify the goal & scope**  
   - Ask whether the focus is on *software* AI engineers (model training, pipelines) or *systems* AI engineers (infrastructure, deployment).  
   - Define the level (junior vs senior) and any domain‑specific requirements (NLP, CV, RL).  

**2️⃣ Adopt a layered evaluation framework**  
   - **Technical core:** algorithms, math, programming.  
   - **Applied skillset:** data handling, model lifecycle, experimentation.  
   - **System & ops knowledge:** CI/CD, cloud services, observability.  
   - **Soft skills & culture fit:** communication, ownership, ethics awareness.

**3️⃣ Step‑by‑step reasoning**  

1. *Design a structured interview* (coding + case study).  
2. *Prepare concrete prompts*: e.g., “Explain how you’d deploy a model with low latency” or “Walk through a recent A/B test you ran.”  
3. *Use a rubric* that rates each dimension on a 5‑point scale, ensuring consistent scoring across interviewers.  
4. *Include a practical exercise*: a small notebook task (data cleaning + metric calculation) and an architecture diagram for a production pipeline.  
5. *Collect behavioral evidence*: past projects, contributions to open source or research papers, and references.

**4️⃣ Avoid common traps**  

- Over‑emphasizing “machine learning buzzwords” instead of depth.  
- Ignoring the candidate’s ability to explain trade‑offs (accuracy vs latency).  
- Neglecting to test real‑world constraints like data privacy or regulatory compliance.  
- Relying solely on whiteboard coding—many great engineers excel in notebooks or versioned repos.

**5️⃣ Sanity‑check & communicate**  

- Run a pilot interview with a senior engineer to validate the rubric.  
- Summarize each candidate’s strengths/weaknesses in a one‑page “candidate profile” for hiring managers.  
- Encourage interviewers to verbalize their reasoning (“Why did you rate them 4 on scalability?”) to surface implicit biases.  

This systematic, multi‑dimensional approach ensures you capture both the *technical acumen* and the *operational mindset* essential for AI engineering roles.
