**Why NVIDIA’s PM interview is a “design‑by‑learning” exercise**

At its core, the product‑management role is an *optimization problem*: allocate limited engineering, budget and time resources so that user value (and revenue) is maximized while staying within technical feasibility. NVIDIA treats each interview as a miniature design loop:

2. **Solution ideation & trade‑offs** – The next step is to propose multiple solutions, rank them by cost/benefit, and discuss “why this works.” Here the deeper principle is *Pareto efficiency*: you show that your chosen approach yields the best marginal improvement for the least incremental cost, a hallmark of data‑driven product decisions.

3. **Execution & metrics** – Finally candidates must outline a roadmap (MVP → scaling) and define key performance indicators. NVIDIA evaluates whether the candidate can turn theoretical trade‑offs into measurable outcomes—mirroring how they monitor GPU adoption rates in production workloads.
