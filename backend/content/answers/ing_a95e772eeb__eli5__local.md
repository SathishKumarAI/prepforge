Imagine a security guard (the rule engine) standing at the front door of a bank. The guard knows the exact list of “bad actors” – people who have tried to break in before, or who are on watch lists. If someone matches those red flags, the guard immediately locks the door and calls the police.

Now picture an invisible detective (machine‑learning model) who watches every visitor’s subtle behaviors: how quickly they move, the way they look at their phone, the pattern of their voice. The detective can spot new tricks that the guard’s checklist doesn’t cover.

In fraud detection we use both:  
*Rules* give us hard, clear conditions (e.g., “any transfer over $10 000 to a new account is blocked”).
