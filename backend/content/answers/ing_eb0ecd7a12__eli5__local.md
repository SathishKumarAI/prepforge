**LLM security** means keeping a large language model (like ChatGPT) safe from misuse, errors, and attacks while still letting it help people.

Think of the model as a *smart recipe book*. The book is full of great recipes (answers), but if someone can change the ingredients list or add hidden steps, they could make harmful dishes. Security checks are like a kitchen inspector who verifies that every ingredient stays in place, no secret spices sneak in, and only trusted chefs can flip the pages.

Key terms:  
- **Model** – the collection of learned patterns that generate text.  
- **Adversarial attack** – an attempt to trick the model into giving bad or dangerous output.  

By guarding the “ingredients” (data, code) and monitoring how the book is used, we keep the recipe safe for everyone.
