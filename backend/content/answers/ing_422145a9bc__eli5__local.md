In an AI evaluation, a **trace** is like the paper trail left by a detective after solving a mystery. Imagine you’re watching a robot answer questions. Every time it thinks—choosing a word, looking up information, or deciding how to respond—you write down that step on a notepad: “selected word ‘apple’, consulted knowledge base, generated sentence.” The trace is the full list of those tiny notes, recorded in order.

A trace lets you see exactly *how* the AI reached its final answer, not just what it produced. It shows the internal decision points and any data it fetched, so developers can spot mistakes, improve reasoning, or debug unexpected behavior. Think of it as a detailed logbook that turns the AI’s black‑box thinking into something transparent and reviewable.
