**Layer 6 – Presentation (in the OSI model)**  
Think of a **translator at an international conference**. The presenters speak their own language (the raw data from the application layer). The translator’s job is to make that speech understandable to everyone in the room, no matter what language they use.

In the same way, the Presentation Layer takes the “speech” of the Application Layer and converts it into a common form for the network. It handles:

* **Data formatting** – changing dates, numbers or text so every device can read them.  
* **Encryption/decryption** – protecting information like a secret code that only the intended listener can decode.  
* **Compression/expansion** – shrinking messages to fit better on the wire and expanding them back at the other end.

So, just as the translator lets people from different nations communicate smoothly, the Presentation Layer lets devices of different types share data reliably and securely.
