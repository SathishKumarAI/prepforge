1. **Data size vs. book length** – Enterprise data are often huge but noisy; the model may get overwhelmed or miss the most useful pages.  
2. **Privacy safety net** – You must keep sensitive details hidden, like covering private notes in a shared book, which can limit how much the model learns.  
3. **Balancing act** – Too much tweaking and the model forgets its general knowledge (over‑fitting); too little and it still reads the wrong books.
