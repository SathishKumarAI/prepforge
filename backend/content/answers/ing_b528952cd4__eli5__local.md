Imagine you’re at a long conference call and want a copy of the minutes, but not just a list of what was said— you also want to know who spoke when, so you can follow up with the right person.  
**ASR (Automatic Speech Recognition)** is like a super‑fast typist: it listens to everyone’s voices and writes down exactly what they say in text.  
**Diarization** is the part that tells the typist “that sentence was spoken by Alice, not Bob.” It separates overlapping conversations into distinct speaker tracks, almost as if each person had their own invisible microphone.  

When combined, a **meeting‑assistant system** records the call, transcribes it, and tags every line with its speaker. The result is an instant, searchable transcript that lets you jump straight to “Alice’s point about the budget” or “Bob’s question about deadlines,” saving time and keeping everyone accountable.
