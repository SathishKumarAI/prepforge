The core of everyday stress is an *information bottleneck*: my brain must decide which stimuli are worth allocating limited attentional and physiological resources to. If I treat every demand as urgent, the system saturates and performance drops. 1. **Quantify stakes** – I estimate each task’s payoff (deadline, impact) and effort.  
2. **Set thresholds** – If payoff / effort < a personal baseline, I defer or delegate.  
3. **Chunk and schedule** – By batching similar tasks, I reduce context‑switch overhead, effectively lowering the “cost” part of the ratio.
