Imagine a sales support team as a busy call center that answers customer questions about products.  
**TPS (Transactions Per Second)** is how many individual calls or chats the system can handle each second – it’s a measure of raw speed, like how fast a waiter can serve plates in a restaurant.  

**QPS (Queries Per Second)** counts how many distinct “questions” the system receives every second, regardless of how long each takes to answer – think of it as counting how many customers arrive at the front desk, not how many dishes they order.

The difference: TPS tells you the overall throughput of interactions, while QPS focuses on the number of unique inquiries. A high TPS with low QPS means the system is busy handling a few long conversations; high QPS with lower TPS indicates many quick questions being answered swiftly.
