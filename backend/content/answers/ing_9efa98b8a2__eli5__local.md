Think of an AI system as a kitchen in a busy restaurant. The interviewers are the head chef watching how you set up the kitchen before service starts.

**Red flags** are like clues that the kitchen won’t run smoothly:

- **Too many cooks, no clear recipe** – If the design has a lot of moving parts but no single owner for each part, it’s hard to keep track and fix problems.  
- **Missing ingredients list** – Skipping data‑quality checks or privacy safeguards means the food (the model) can be off‑spec or unsafe.  
- **No backup stove** – Relying on one algorithm or dataset with no fallback makes the system fragile if something fails.  
- **Ignoring customer reviews** – Not planning for monitoring and updates after launch leaves bugs unchecked.

A good design keeps a clear “recipe book,” assigns responsibility, includes safety checks, and plans for future tweaks—just like a kitchen that can keep serving great meals under pressure.
