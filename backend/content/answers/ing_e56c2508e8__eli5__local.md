Think of a machine‑learning system as a recipe book that turns raw ingredients (data) into tasty dishes (predictions). The cheat sheet is the kitchen checklist you use before cooking:

1. **What’s the dish?** – Define the problem and the desired output (classification, regression, etc.).  
2. **Where do the ingredients come from?** – Identify data sources, volume, freshness, and privacy rules.  
3. **How to prep the ingredients?** – Plan preprocessing steps: cleaning, feature engineering, scaling.  
4. **Which chef will cook it?** – Choose algorithms or model families (tree, neural net) and justify the choice.  
5. **What kitchen tools are needed?** – List required compute resources, storage, and frameworks.  
6. **How to serve the dish?** – Design APIs or batch jobs for inference, monitor latency and accuracy.  
7. **Quality control?** – Set up logging, metrics (accuracy, drift), and retraining triggers.
