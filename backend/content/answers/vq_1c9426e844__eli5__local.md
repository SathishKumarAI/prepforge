Imagine you’re in a kitchen and have a basket of apples.

* **COUNT()** is like counting how many apples are in the basket. It tells you “there are 10 apples,” but nothing about their weight or size.

* **SUM()** is like weighing all the apples together. It gives you the total weight: “the apples weigh 4 kilograms.” It adds up every individual apple’s weight, just as SUM() adds up numbers in a column.

* **AVG()** (average) is what you get if you divide that total weight by the number of apples. It says “each apple weighs about 0.4 kilogram.” AVG() first sums everything and then divides by the count to find the typical value.

So, COUNT() counts items, SUM() adds their values, and AVG() finds the average value per item.
