Imagine training a transformer as teaching a choir how to sing in harmony. First, you give the choir a song (the data) and ask each singer (each token in the sequence) to listen to everyone else’s voice at once—this is *self‑attention*. Every singer decides how much weight to give each other voice; some voices are louder, others softer. The choir practices many times (training epochs), each time adjusting their volume so that the final chorus sounds perfect (the model learns useful patterns). Once practiced, the choir can sing any new song by following those same listening rules—just like a trained transformer can generate or understand text without extra instruction. In short: training is repeated rehearsal of “listen‑and‑adjust” until harmony emerges.
