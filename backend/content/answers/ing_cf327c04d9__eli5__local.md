**Context relevance** is like a librarian who knows exactly which book a reader needs.  
Imagine you ask the librarian for “tips on baking bread.” A good librarian first checks what kind of bread you’re after—sourdough, rye, or quick‑loaf—and then pulls out only those recipes that match your request and your skill level.  

In AI, *context relevance* works the same way: the system looks at everything it already knows (the conversation history, user profile, past questions) and uses that “background” to decide which information is most useful right now.  
The **Rag Evaluation Patterns** are the rules the librarian follows—like “if you asked about sourdough, ignore rye recipes”—to keep answers tight, accurate, and directly tied to what you’re actually looking for.
