You show many photos, saying “this one is a cat” or “this one is a dog.” The child looks at each picture, notes patterns—long ears, whiskers, pointy noses—and gradually learns what makes a cat different from a dog. After seeing enough examples, the child can guess correctly even for new pictures.

In machine learning, the computer plays that child.  
- **Data** are the pictures (or any input).  
- **Features** are the patterns it extracts (like ear shape or fur texture).  
- **Model** is the child’s memory of how features map to labels (cat vs dog).  
- **Training** is the repeated showing and correcting, so the model adjusts its internal “rules” until it can predict new data accurately.
