Think of a library that wants to keep every book in order so anyone can find the right one instantly.  
In machine‑learning projects, *data architecture* is that library’s blueprint: it decides where books (datasets) live, how they’re labeled, and which shelves (storage systems) they go on.

**Key parts**

- **Data sources** – the original “books” (sensor logs, customer records, images).  
- **Ingestion pipelines** – librarians who bring new books into the library.  
- **Storage layers** – a mix of fast‑access shelves (databases) and archival rooms (data lakes).  
- **Metadata catalog** – a master index that tells you what each book contains and where it sits.  
- **Governance rules** – policies that ensure only authorized people can borrow or edit books.

By building this organized system, a data science team can quickly pull the right information, train models faster, and keep everything secure—just as a well‑run library lets readers find exactly what they need without confusion.
