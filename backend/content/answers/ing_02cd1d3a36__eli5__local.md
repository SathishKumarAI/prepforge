Imagine you’re baking a giant, fancy cake (your LLM app) and you want to keep adding new flavors or decorations without ruining the whole thing. **CI** (“Continuous Integration”) is like testing every new ingredient as soon as it arrives: you mix a little bit into the batter, taste it, and if it’s good you keep it; if not you throw it out right away. This keeps the cake from turning sour.

**CD** (“Continuous Deployment” or “Delivery”) is what happens after that tasting phase. Once the new flavor passes the test, you bake a fresh slice and put it on the finished cake for everyone to try—automatically and instantly, without waiting for a big ceremony.

So CI/CD for LLM apps means every time you tweak the model, code, or data, the system automatically tests it (CI) and, if it passes, rolls the new version out to users right away (CD). No manual “big bake‑off” needed; the cake stays tasty and fresh.
