Think of building a house.  
With plain **Spring** you get the blueprint (walls, roof, plumbing) but you have to decide on every detail yourself: choose a furnace, install wiring, write the paperwork for permits, and set up each room’s lighting. It gives you full control but demands lots of extra work.

**Spring Boot** is like a pre‑furnished modular home kit. The basic structure (walls, roof) is still there, but most of the heavy lifting—electricity, heating, plumbing, and even legal permits—is already wired in for you. You just pick the modules you need (database, web, security), plug them in, and the house is ready to live in.

So Spring Boot speeds development by bundling sensible defaults and simplifying configuration, while still letting you customize if you want. It’s the “just‑install‑and‑go” version of Spring that most beginners find friendly.
