Think of a machine‑learning pipeline like a relay race: each team member (data collection, preprocessing, training, deployment) passes a baton. If you only check the final runner’s speed at the finish line, you’ll miss mistakes that could have been caught earlier—like a wrong handoff or a misaligned shoe. By validating each stage as it goes on, we catch errors early, save time and resources, and make sure the whole system works together smoothly.
