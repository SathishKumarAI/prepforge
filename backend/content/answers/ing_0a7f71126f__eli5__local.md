**Semantic Routing – a “traffic‑control” system for data**

Imagine a city where every street has a sign that tells cars not only *where* to go but also *what kind of car* should use it (bike, bus, truck). Semantic routing works the same way inside an AI model: each piece of information (a word or image patch) is tagged with its “meaning” (its semantic label), and the model routes that data through the parts of the network best suited for that meaning.

**Model taxonomy**

Think of different AI models as neighborhoods in this city.  
- **Linear models** are like a straight avenue: data flows straight through one lane.  
- **Tree‑based models** resemble a branching street grid where each intersection splits traffic by category.  
- **Deep neural networks** are multi‑layered highways, with each layer acting as a new set of intersections that refine the route based on deeper meaning.

By letting the model “read” these semantic signs, it can send each piece of data to the right neighborhood (model component) for the most accurate processing. This makes AI faster and smarter, just like a well‑organized city keeps traffic moving smoothly.
