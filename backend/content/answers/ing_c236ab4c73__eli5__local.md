Think of an AI system as a smart robot that can open doors in a big building.  
An **allowlist** is like a “VIP guest list” for the front door: only people whose names are on the list may enter, and everyone else is automatically turned away. This keeps the building safe because you know exactly who’s allowed.  

A **denylist** (or blacklist) works opposite: it starts by letting everyone in, but then a guard checks each person against a list of “bad actors” and blocks only those names. If someone slips through that list, they can still get in—so the system is less secure unless the denylist is very complete.

In AI safety, allowlists give tighter control over what data or actions are permitted, while denylists rely on catching every dangerous case later. Using an allowlist is like locking the door until you’re ready; a denylist is like hoping no one slips through before you notice.
