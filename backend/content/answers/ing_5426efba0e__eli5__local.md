Imagine an AI as a curious robot in a huge library.  
**Agent fundamentals** are just the rules that let it read, decide, and act.

1. **Perception** – The robot’s “eyes” (sensors or data input) scan books (information).  
2. **Goal** – It has a mission: find the most relevant book for a reader’s question.  
3. **Policy** – A simple recipe tells it which page to turn next based on what it sees.  
4. **Action** – It flips pages, highlights text, or writes an answer.  
5. **Feedback** – If the reader likes the answer, the robot learns; if not, it tweaks its recipe.
