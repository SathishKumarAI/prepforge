1. **Privacy by Design**  
   Data never leaves the device; only model parameters (often compressed) travel over the network. This aligns with *information‑theoretic* privacy: entropy of raw data is preserved, and differential‑privacy guarantees can be enforced locally.

3. **Model‑as‑Service (MaaS) Economics**  
   By serving only *weights* rather than full training pipelines, bandwidth costs drop from gigabytes to megabytes—an economic equivalent of *model distillation*. The browser acts as a lightweight inference node in a distributed system.
