Think of a chef’s kitchen. **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** is the whole kitchen—everything that can help a person cook, from chopping knives to ovens and recipe books. It’s the big idea: systems that act smartly.

**Machine Learning (ML)** is like a sous‑chef who learns from watching you cook. By tasting many dishes, it figures out which ingredients work best for each recipe. ML gives AI the ability to improve by studying data instead of being told every step explicitly.

**Deep Learning (DL)** is the sous‑chef’s secret spice grinder that can turn raw spices into a perfect blend automatically. It uses layered “neural” networks—tiny decision makers stacked together—to discover patterns in huge amounts of data, like images or speech, without you hand‑coding each rule.

So: AI = kitchen; ML = learning chef; DL = the auto‑spice grinder that learns from vast spice jars.
