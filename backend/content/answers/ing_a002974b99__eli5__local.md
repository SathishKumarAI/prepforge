Imagine you’re baking a cake and want to make sure it turns out just right. You have three simple checkpoints: **Red (bad), Amber (needs attention), Green (good)**. The *RAG Triad* is the same idea, but for evaluating how well an AI system works.

- **Red** means something’s wrong—like a missing ingredient that ruins the whole cake.  
- **Amber** signals a warning—maybe the batter’s too thick; it’ll need tweaking before you bake.  
- **Green** shows everything is on track and the AI is performing as expected, like a perfectly risen loaf.

These colors help teams quickly see where an AI needs fixing, where it’s okay, or where it’s already great, just as a chef uses them to keep the kitchen running smoothly.
