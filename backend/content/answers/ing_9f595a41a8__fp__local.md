2. **Information bottleneck shapes curriculum.**  
   - The scientist’s information channel is *data → inference*, so courses emphasize probability, causal reasoning, and exploratory analytics.

   - The manager’s channel is *business outcome → AI strategy*, focusing on case studies, ethics, and stakeholder communication.
