Think of an LLM (large language model) as a giant library that can answer any question you throw at it.  
**Inference** is the moment you ask a question and the library pulls out the right books to give you an answer – it’s just one “reading” session.  
**Serving** is like opening the library’s front desk: a computer (the server) keeps the shelves ready, receives many questions from people at once, and hands back answers quickly, so everyone gets help in real time.  
**Production LLM systems** are the whole operating room that runs the library 24/7. They monitor traffic, update the books, keep backups, and make sure no one gets stuck waiting or loses data. Together, inference is the single reading, serving is the front‑desk workflow, and production is the full, reliable library system that keeps everything running smoothly.
