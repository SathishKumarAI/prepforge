Imagine you’re baking a cake (the AI model) and you want to make sure it turns out exactly right. The *human review* is like the baker’s taste test: after each batch, a person samples the cake and says whether it meets the recipe’s standards—this is your **ground truth**.  

In Pydantic Logfire, that “taste test” becomes a systematic check of what the large language model (LLM) or Retrieval‑Augmented Generation (RAG) app outputs. The system records every step—inputs, intermediate reasoning, final answer—and then lets a human flag anything off‑track. Those flagged examples are fed back into the training loop so the AI learns to “taste” better next time.  

So, just as a baker uses taste tests to perfect a recipe, Logfire uses human reviews to make LLMs more reliable and transparent.
