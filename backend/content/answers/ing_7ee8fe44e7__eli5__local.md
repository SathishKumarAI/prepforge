**Step 5 – Scaling a Website for Millions of Users**

Imagine your website is a coffee shop that suddenly has a million customers lining up every day. If you keep the same single barista (one server), everyone will wait forever. The fix is to hire more baristas, set up multiple coffee machines, and let them work side‑by‑side. In computing terms:

* **Load balancer** = traffic cop that distributes visitors to many servers so no one gets stuck.
* **Multiple servers** = the extra baristas; each can serve a chunk of users at once.
* **Database replication** = several copies of your menu (data) in different locations, so customers never have to wait for the original copy.
