Imagine you’re trying to solve a giant crossword puzzle where every word is hidden inside another word—like nested Russian dolls. **Latent reasoning** is the hidden logic that tells you how one clue (a piece of text) relates to the next, even if it’s not written out. A **looped language model** is like a smart friend who keeps checking the puzzle over and over: each pass uses what they learned in the previous pass to guess better words.

To *scale* this, you let many friends work together on different sections of the puzzle simultaneously, then share their best guesses back to everyone. Each round (or loop) refines the hidden connections until all the clues fit perfectly. In short, scaling latent reasoning with looped language models means repeatedly and collaboratively tightening the unseen logic that stitches text together, just as many people polishing a complex crossword until every hidden word shines.
