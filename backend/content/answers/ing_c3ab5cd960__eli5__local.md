**Disaster recovery for machine learning** is like having a backup plan for a recipe that’s critical to your kitchen.

Imagine you’ve spent weeks perfecting a special cake (your trained model). One day, the oven breaks or the ingredients spoil (a server crash, data loss, or cyber‑attack). Disaster recovery means you have a fresh batch of ingredients and a spare oven in another room—ready to bake the same cake again. In practice, this involves regularly saving copies of your data, training code, and model weights on separate servers or cloud storage, plus automated scripts that can reload them if something goes wrong.

So, disaster recovery is simply **a set of procedures and backups that let you rebuild or restore a machine‑learning system quickly after an unexpected failure**.
