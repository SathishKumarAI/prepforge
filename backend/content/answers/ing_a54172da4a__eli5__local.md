**Per‑frame prompt** is like giving a short, specific instruction to a photographer for each single picture they take while shooting a video.

*Imagine you’re filming a dance routine.*  
Before the camera starts, you tell the photographer: “For frame 1, capture the dancer’s left foot in mid‑air; for frame 2, focus on the right hand holding the ribbon.” Each instruction is a *prompt*, and because it changes from one picture (frame) to the next, it’s called **per‑frame**.
