Instead of guessing, you keep a small notebook beside your desk that you can quickly flip to find those facts. Retrieval‑Augmented Generation (RAG) works the same way for an AI: it “writes” answers but also *retrieves* relevant information from a trusted library before finalizing its response.

- **Retrieval** – the AI searches a database of documents or texts for snippets that match the question.  
- **Generation** – it then writes a coherent answer, weaving in those retrieved facts.
