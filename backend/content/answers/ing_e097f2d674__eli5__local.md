Think of machine learning as training a chef to make a special dish.  
During **training**, the chef (the model) practices many times, tasting each batch and tweaking spices until the flavor is just right—this is like feeding data and adjusting weights.  

When the chef finally leaves the kitchen and starts serving customers, that’s **deployment**. The restaurant opens its doors, and every time a customer orders the dish, the chef follows the recipe instantly, without looking back at the cookbook. In ML terms, the trained model sits on a server or in an app; whenever new data (a user request) arrives, it runs through the model’s fixed “recipe” to produce a prediction or recommendation right away.

Key terms:  
- **Model** – the learned recipe that turns inputs into outputs.  
- **Deployment** – putting that recipe into a live environment so users can get instant results.  

So deployment is simply making your trained chef available to all customers, letting them enjoy the dish on demand.
