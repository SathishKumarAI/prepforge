## Why a *Research Radar* even exists

In AI research you’re constantly juggling three competing forces:

1. **Exploration** – trying new ideas before they are mainstream.  
2. **Exploitation** – building on proven methods to deliver impact quickly.  
3. **Visibility** – knowing what the community is actually doing so you don’t duplicate effort.

A Research Radar turns these abstract trade‑offs into a concrete map: each point on the diagram is an *active research area* that balances novelty against maturity. By visualising the space, the radar forces you to ask: *Is this idea too early to invest in? Is it already saturated?*

## How to read and use the page

4. **Identify gaps** – areas that are high in novelty but low in performance indicate fertile ground for breakthrough research; those high in performance but low in novelty may be ready for productisation.
