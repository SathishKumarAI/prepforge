Imagine you’re a chef who wants to learn how to make the perfect soup from scratch, but you don’t have a recipe book. Instead, you collect thousands of pictures of soups and read every description that comes with them—how long it simmered, what spices were added, how salty it tasted. A machine‑learning model is like a curious apprentice who watches all those pictures and notes the patterns: “soup with red peppers tastes spicy,” “if you stir for 10 minutes, it thickens.” After seeing enough examples, the apprentice can predict how to cook any new soup just from its ingredients.

**Unavoidable terms**

* **Model** – the apprentice’s set of learned rules.  
* **Training data** – all the soup pictures and descriptions the apprentice studied.  
* **Prediction** – the apprentice’s guess about a new soup’s taste or texture.
