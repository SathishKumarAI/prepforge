**Reliability in machine‑learning systems** is the promise that your model will keep working correctly over time, even when things change.  
Think of it like a well‑trained delivery robot: it must find its way to every house (accuracy), run safely on different sidewalks (robustness), and keep operating after weather or battery changes (fault tolerance).  

*System design* is the blueprint that tells you where to put sensors, how to update software, and what backups to use so the robot never stops.  
If a component fails—say the camera gets dirty—you need a quick fix (re‑training) and a backup plan (another sensor or a human check).  

In short, reliability means building in checks, balances, and redundancy so your ML model keeps delivering trustworthy results no matter what hiccups arise.
