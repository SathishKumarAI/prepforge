2. **Workers as mail carriers** – Separate workers pull letters from the mailbox one by one and deliver them to the right house. Adding more carriers keeps up with higher traffic.

4. **Health checks and monitoring** – Keep an eye on how many letters are waiting and how long they’re stuck. If the queue grows too large, add more carriers or increase mailbox size.
