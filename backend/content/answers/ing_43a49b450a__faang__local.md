**Depth**  
- A **SequenceNumber** is an opaque, monotonically increasing string generated by Kinesis when a record is added to a shard.  
- It uniquely identifies the record’s position *within that shard*; higher numbers mean later records.  
- Consumers request a **ShardIterator** starting at `TRIM_HORIZON`, `LATEST`, or a specific `SequenceNumber`.  
- When calling `GetRecords`, Kinesis returns all records with sequence numbers greater than the iterator value, preserving order.  
- The consumer persists the *last* sequence number read (checkpoint). On restart it resumes from that point, ensuring **exact‑once** semantics and no duplication or gaps—critical for downstream analytics.

**Edge Cases**  
- If a shard splits/merges, the old sequence numbers become invalid; consumers must use the new shard’s iterator.  
- Sequence numbers are *per shard*, so ordering across shards is not guaranteed.  
- High write rates can cause `ProvisionedThroughputExceededException`; checkpointing too slowly may lead to replay loops.
