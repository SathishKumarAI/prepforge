Imagine a giant, digital library where every book is a *property listing*.  
The **data model** is the library’s catalog: it tells you where each book lives, who wrote it, what genre it belongs to, and whether it’s currently on loan.

In Airbnb’s case:

* **Property (Listing)** – the “book”; contains address, photos, price, and a unique ID.  
* **User** – the “librarian” who can write or borrow books; users have profiles, reviews, and payment info.  
* **Reservation** – a loan record that links a user to a property for specific dates, plus its status (pending, confirmed, cancelled).  
* **Review** – a note left on a book after it’s read, attached to both the reviewer and the listing.

The model keeps all these pieces connected so the system can quickly find available listings, show past stays, or suggest similar homes. It’s like having a well‑organized shelf system that lets you locate any book in seconds without mixing up authors or genres.
