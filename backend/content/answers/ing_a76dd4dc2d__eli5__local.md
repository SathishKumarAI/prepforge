Think of searching as finding a book in a huge library that’s split into two parts.  
One part is the **full‑text index** – like a librarian who knows every word on every page and can quickly point you to any chapter where a keyword appears.  
The other part is the **vector index** – like a smart “mind map” that stores the meaning of each passage in a compressed shape, so it can instantly tell you which passages feel most similar to what you’re thinking about.

A hybrid search first asks the librarian for all places that contain your exact words (fast, precise). Then it lets the mind‑map rank those places by how close their ideas are to yours. The result is a real‑time answer that’s both accurate and semantically rich—exactly what we need when users want quick, relevant results in today’s AI‑powered systems.
