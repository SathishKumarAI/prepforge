Imagine you’re preparing a huge recipe book for a cooking show that will learn to make new dishes every day.  
**Storage types** are the different places where you keep all those recipes and ingredients.

1. **Local storage (on‑device)** – like a small kitchen counter in your house. It’s fast, but only holds what fits on the counter.  
2. **Cloud storage (remote servers)** – think of a giant pantry that anyone can access from anywhere. It holds far more recipes, but you need to travel (send data over the internet) to fetch or add them.  
3. **Hybrid storage** – a combination: keep the most‑used recipes on your counter for instant cooking, while the rest stay in the pantry for backup and sharing.

In machine learning, these are where training data, model weights, and results live—each choice balances speed, capacity, and cost.
