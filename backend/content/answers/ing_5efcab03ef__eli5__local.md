**Step 5 – “Count and Prioritize” (AI Eval’s Core)**  
Think of your evaluation like a grocery list for a big family dinner. First you *count* every item on the menu—how many recipes need ingredients? That tells you how much food you’ll actually cook. Then you *prioritize*: which dishes are most important to taste, which can be skipped if time is tight, and which need extra seasoning.

In AI evals, “count” means tallying all the prompts, model responses, or test cases you’ve run—how many data points did you generate? “Prioritize” then asks: Which results matter most for your goal (e.g., safety, fairness, speed)? You rank them so you focus on the highest‑impact ones first.
