Think of a chat‑bot like a live radio DJ who listens to your request, writes a quick reply, and then speaks it back. **Latency** is the time between when you speak and when the bot’s voice comes out—like the pause before the DJ starts talking after you’ve asked a question.

In speech synthesis (the part that turns written text into spoken words) this pause can be caused by three things:  
1. Your words reaching the server,  
2. The computer converting text to sound, and  
3. The audio being streamed back to your device.  

If the delay is long, the conversation feels robotic or “out of sync,” just as a DJ who lags behind the music makes the show feel sluggish. Short latency keeps the dialogue natural, making the AI seem more present and responsive—exactly what users expect from a real‑time chat.
