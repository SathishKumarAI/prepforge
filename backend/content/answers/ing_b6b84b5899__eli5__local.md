Think of an AI system as a smart robot chef that’s learning to cook in a busy kitchen.  
**Memory** is the recipe notebook it carries—notes on ingredients, cooking times, and how customers liked each dish. It can look back at any past meal to decide what to do next.  
**State** is the current moment of the kitchen: the stove’s heat, the fridge’s temperature, and the chef’s own energy level. The state changes every time a new order comes in or an ingredient runs out.

In production AI, “agent memory” stores all past interactions (like the notebook), while “state” tracks everything happening right now (the kitchen snapshot). Together they let the agent make informed, real‑time decisions—just as a seasoned chef blends experience with current conditions to serve the perfect meal.
