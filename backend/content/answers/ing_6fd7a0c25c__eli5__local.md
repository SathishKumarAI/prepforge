**Indirect Prompt Injection – When AI Remembers Too Much**

Imagine a kitchen where every recipe you ever cook is written on sticky notes that stick to the fridge forever. You’re asked to make a new dish, but the fridge still shows all those old recipes. The chef (the AI) keeps pulling from them, even though they’re not relevant now. That’s *indirect prompt injection*: the AI’s memory of past instructions or data quietly nudges its current answers.

*Memory* is the AI’s internal “fridge.” *Prompt injection* means someone slips a hidden instruction into that fridge, so the AI follows it later without noticing. Because the fridge never clears old notes, those hidden prompts keep influencing every dish (response) you ask for—creating persistent, unwanted behaviors in the agent.
