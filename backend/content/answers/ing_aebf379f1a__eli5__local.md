Imagine you’re at a crowded party where everyone is talking in their usual rhythm. Most guests mingle in small circles, but one person keeps wandering off into a quiet corner, standing out from the rest. An **Isolation Forest** is like a team of detectives who walk around the room with very simple tools: they randomly pick a wall and decide whether to cut a path that isolates a guest. If a guest can be separated quickly—by just a few cuts—it means they’re in an unusual spot, much like that lone person in the corner. The more cuts needed, the more “normal” the guest is, because they blend into many groups. By counting how fast each guest gets isolated, the forest flags those few who truly stand out as **outliers**—the guests who don’t fit the usual party pattern.
