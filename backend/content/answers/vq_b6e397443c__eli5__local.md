In data engineering, a **clause** is like a rule written into a recipe that tells the computer exactly how to handle data. Imagine you’re baking cookies: one clause says “use only flour from the pantry,” another says “fold in chocolate chips after the sugar.” In SQL (the language most data engineers use), clauses are those little instructions—`SELECT`, `FROM`, `WHERE`, `GROUP BY`, etc.—that together form a full recipe for pulling, filtering, or organizing information. Each clause has a single clear purpose:  
- **SELECT** tells which ingredients (columns) to keep;  
- **FROM** says where the dough (table) comes from;  
- **WHERE** applies a filter like “only sweet cookies.”  

Just as each step in a cookie recipe is essential, every clause must be correct for the final data output to come out right.
