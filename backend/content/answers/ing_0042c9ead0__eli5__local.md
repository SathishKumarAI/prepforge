Think of an AI as a very fast kitchen robot.  
When it *executes*, it follows a recipe exactly: chop onions, stir sauce, set timer—doing the steps one after another. That’s like the “execution” part: the machine applies instructions it has been given.

When it *reasoning* is needed, the robot must decide what to do next without an explicit recipe. Maybe the onions are burning, or the sauce needs more salt. It looks at the situation, compares possibilities, and chooses a new action—much like a cook improvising on the fly.
