**Why salaries diverge across sectors**

The core problem is *value creation*: an AI engineer’s output must translate into measurable business gain (revenue lift, cost cut, risk reduction).  
Different industries monetize that value differently:

| Industry | Monetization levers | Demand‑price elasticity |
|----------|---------------------|------------------------|
| Finance | Low‑latency trading, fraud detection | High – a $1M error = $10M loss |
| Healthcare | Diagnostic accuracy, drug discovery | Medium – regulatory safety adds cost |
| Retail | Personalisation, inventory optimisation | Low–Medium – margins thin but volume high |

The *price* an employer can pay is the product of **(1) scarcity of talent in that niche** and **(2) the marginal benefit per engineer**.  
When a skill (e.g., LLM fine‑tuning) is rare yet unlocks a $5 M revenue jump, firms are willing to offer premium packages.
