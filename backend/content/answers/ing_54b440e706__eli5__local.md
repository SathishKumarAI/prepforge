Imagine a chef who has tasted thousands of recipes and can predict what flavor a new dish will taste like. **Perplexity** is that chef’s “confidence score” – the lower the number, the better the chef can guess the next ingredient or word in a sentence. In AI, we give a language model a long paragraph and ask it to predict each next word. If the model is very good, its guesses are almost always right, so perplexity drops. Interviewers love this metric because it tells them how sharp a company’s models really are—like asking a chef how many times they get a dish exactly right. A low perplexity means the AI “understands” language well; a high one means it’s still guessing wildly.
