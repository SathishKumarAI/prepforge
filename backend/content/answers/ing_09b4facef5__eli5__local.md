If you just bolt it together without a safety switch or clear rules, it could wander into dangerous places or do things people didn’t intend. **Safety** is that built‑in emergency stop: it keeps the robot from harming itself or others when something goes wrong. **Governance** is like the instruction manual and legal guidelines that tell the robot who can control it, what tasks are allowed, and how to report problems.
