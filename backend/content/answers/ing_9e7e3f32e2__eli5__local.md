Imagine you’re baking cookies at home versus in a local bakery.

**On‑device** is like making the cookies yourself in your kitchen. All the ingredients, mixing, and baking happen right where you are, so you control every step and don’t need to send anything outside. In AI terms, the model lives inside your phone or laptop; it processes data locally without reaching out to a distant server.

**Edge deployment** is like using a nearby bakery that has a small kitchen. You bring raw dough (your data) to the shop, they finish baking and give you the finished cookies, then send them back. The model sits on a local server or network device close to your user—fast, but still separate from the end device.

Both keep most computation near you for speed and privacy; the choice depends on how much power, memory, or connectivity you can afford.
