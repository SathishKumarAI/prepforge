Think of a conversation with a friendly barista who remembers your favorite coffee order.  
**Stateful protocol** is like that barista: each time you talk, the server keeps track of what happened before—your login status, items in a shopping cart, or the steps you’ve taken in a multi‑step form. The next request can rely on that stored “state” to make sense of it.

A **stateless protocol** is like walking into a new coffee shop where no one knows who you are. Every time you ask for something, you must give all the details again—your name, order, payment method. The server never remembers past interactions; each request is independent.

So:  
- *Stateful*: remembers previous exchanges (like the barista).  
- *Stateless*: treats every exchange as a fresh start (like the new shop).
