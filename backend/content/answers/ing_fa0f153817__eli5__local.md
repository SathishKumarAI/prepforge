Imagine your AI project is a garden and every engineer is a gardener who must show how well their plants are growing.  
**Set clear “growth goals” (evaluation metrics) that matter for the whole garden**—like plant height, fruit size, or soil health—so everyone knows what truly counts.  
**Make the evaluation process a shared walk through the garden**, not a secret report. Ask each gardener to explain why a plant looks good and how they got there; this turns data into discussion, not a game of tricks.  
If a metric feels too easy to cheat (e.g., “number of lines of code”), replace it with something harder to fake, such as the impact on user experience or energy usage.  

Finally, let gardeners review each other’s plots together and vote on which plants deserve extra water. When metrics are transparent, relevant, and tied to real outcomes, engineers can’t game them; they’ll focus on growing better AI instead of just boosting numbers.
