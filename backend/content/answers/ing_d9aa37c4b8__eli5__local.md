**Batch Processing – the “pot‑and‑pan” way of paying for AI**

Think of a recipe that takes a big pot: you pour in all the ingredients at once, let it simmer, and then scoop out what you need later. *Batch processing* works the same with AI: instead of asking the computer to answer each question one by one (which would be like cooking each meal separately), you bundle many requests together into one “batch.”  

Because the computer does all those calculations at once, it can use its resources more efficiently—just as a single pot uses less stove time than many small pots. That efficiency usually means **lower cost per request** and **faster overall turnaround**, but you do have to wait until the whole batch finishes before getting any results.

So, if you’re planning a big project or need many predictions at once, think of AI batch processing as your kitchen’s “batch‑cook” mode: one pot, more savings, and a little extra waiting time.
