Imagine you’re in a busy café that serves coffee (the data).  
*Latency* is how long it takes from the moment you place an order until you actually get your cup—think of the wait time for one customer. *Throughput* is how many cups the barista can finish per minute, like the total number of orders handled in a given period. *Bandwidth* is the size of the coffee pot: the maximum amount of coffee that can be poured at once.

In system design you want low latency so each request gets answered quickly, high throughput to keep many users happy, and enough bandwidth so the “pot” isn’t too small for large requests. Balancing these three is like training a barista to serve fast, many orders, and big cups without spilling or running out of coffee.
