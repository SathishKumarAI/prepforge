Imagine an agentic system as a smart robot chef that can pick up ingredients, decide what to cook, and adjust seasoning on its own. To see if the robot is doing its job well, we test it like we would any kitchen gadget: give it a recipe (a goal), let it choose its steps (actions), watch how it reacts when something goes wrong (feedback), and check if the final dish meets taste expectations (outcome).

- **Goal** – what we want the agent to achieve, like “bake a cake.”  
- **Action** – each step the agent takes, such as “mix flour and sugar.”  
- **Feedback** – information the agent receives about its progress, like temperature readings.
