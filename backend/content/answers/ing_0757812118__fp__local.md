### One non‑obvious insight

**Auto‑configuration is a form of *implicit contract* between the framework and your code.**  
When you annotate a bean with `@SpringBootApplication`, the framework scans for other components, injects them, and configures default beans (e.g., an in‑memory H2 database). This means that **your only responsibility is to declare “what” exists; how it’s wired is handled automatically.** The repository demonstrates this by letting you focus on the *Todo* domain while Spring Boot silently wires JPA, MVC, and testing support.

In short, the repo is a micro‑manifestation of Spring Boot’s promise: **write once, run anywhere, and let the framework do the heavy lifting so you can concentrate on business logic.**
