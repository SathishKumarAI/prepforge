Imagine your data is a garden that needs watering.  
**Serverless** is like a smart sprinkler system: you only pay for the exact minutes the sprinklers run, so if the garden stays dry most of the day you spend almost nothing. The cloud takes care of turning it on and off automatically, but you can’t easily keep a set schedule or control how much water each plant gets.

**Hosted (self‑managed)** is like owning a traditional hose: you pay for the whole house every month regardless of whether you’re using it, but you can decide when to turn it on, how fast, and which plants receive more water. You also own the hose, so you know exactly where it goes.

In Elastic Cloud terms, serverless gives you elastic pricing tied to actual usage (good for unpredictable workloads), while hosted offers a fixed monthly rate with full control over resources (ideal when you need predictable capacity).
