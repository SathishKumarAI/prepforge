### Why you need a receipt  
Reimbursement is essentially a *claim* against your employer’s budget. A claim must be **verifiable**: the amount, date, and purpose of the expense must be traceable. The receipt is that proof—your employer’s audit trail and a safeguard against fraud.

### One non‑obvious insight  
Many employees assume that *any* receipt will do, but most expense systems now use OCR and machine learning to parse receipts. If the vendor’s logo is upside down or the text is blurry, the system may flag it for manual review—adding days to your reimbursement. Therefore, **quality matters**: ensure clear, legible scans; if you’re unsure, ask a colleague to double‑check before submission.

By treating each step as a data validation point, you guarantee that your claim will pass through the employer’s automated checks without friction.
