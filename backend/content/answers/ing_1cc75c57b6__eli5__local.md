Imagine you’re at a crowded party, and someone whispers a secret to you. If you keep talking to many people at once, the whisper gets lost in all the noise—no one hears it clearly. In AI, that “whisper” is the useful information you want the model to remember while answering a question. The model has a limited “attention span” (the context window). When the conversation or text pile grows, the earlier parts start getting pushed out or blended with later words. That’s **context dilution**: the important details get smeared and the model can’t retrieve them accurately. Think of it like trying to read a long book while someone keeps flipping pages—you’ll keep missing the crucial plot points. The trick is keeping the “party” small enough that every whisper stays sharp and reachable.
