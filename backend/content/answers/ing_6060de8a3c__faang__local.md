**Behavioral Questions Framework (STAR‑L)**  
*A concise model for answering behavioral interviews in a way that scores high on the SIGNAL rubric.*

| **Component** | **What it is** | **Why it matters** |
|---------------|----------------|--------------------|
| **S – Situation** | Set context: *“In my last role at X, we faced Y.”* | Shows you can frame problems. |
| **T – Task** | Clarify your responsibility: *“I was tasked with Z.”* | Demonstrates ownership and focus. |
| **A – Action** | Detail the concrete steps you took: *“I did A, B, C.”* | Highlights skills, decision‑making, and initiative. |
| **R – Result** | Quantify impact: *“We achieved a 30% increase in X.”* | Provides evidence of success; metrics are gold. |
| **L – Learn/Reflect** | Share insights or what you’d do differently: *“I learned that…”* | Shows growth mindset and self‑awareness. |

### How to use it

1. **Prepare a portfolio**  
   • Keep 3–5 stories ready covering leadership, conflict, failure, and innovation.  
   • For each, jot down S/T/A/R/L bullets.

2. **Answer concisely**  
   • Aim for ~30‑45 s per story; keep the flow natural but focused.

3. **Tie back to the role**  
   • End with “This experience taught me X, which will help me in this position because…”.

### Why it scores high

- **Structure (Signal: Clarity)** – Interviewers can instantly grasp your narrative.  
- **Depth (Signal: Technical Detail)** – Action and Result sections show concrete skills.  
- **Growth mindset (Signal: Adaptability)** – The Learn component signals continuous improvement.

**Tip:** Practice with a friend or mirror, recording to ensure you stay within time limits and hit each bullet.
