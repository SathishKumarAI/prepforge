Imagine a city map that learns to give the best route every time you ask it.  
**Machine learning** is like teaching that map: instead of hard‑coding every road, we let it look at many past trips—start point, end point, traffic, weather—and notice patterns.  

*Data* are those past trip records.  
*A model* is the “brain” that turns a new start‑and‑end pair into a predicted fastest route.  
We *train* the model by feeding it lots of examples and letting it adjust its internal numbers until its predictions match real trips as closely as possible.
