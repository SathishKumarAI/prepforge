Imagine you’re at a theme park. A **chatbot** is like a friendly ticket‑seller who knows the park’s layout and can answer questions about rides, but it only talks to you; it doesn’t move around or make decisions on its own. An **AI agent**, on the other hand, is more like an autonomous tour guide that not only answers your questions but also walks through the park, chooses which attractions to recommend based on what you’ve liked before, and even adjusts its route if a ride closes.  

**Chatbot**: software that exchanges text (or voice) with users using pre‑written or learned responses.  
**AI agent**: a program that perceives its environment, decides what actions to take, performs those actions, and learns from the outcomes—all while interacting with humans. The key difference is that agents act in the world; chatbots simply converse.
