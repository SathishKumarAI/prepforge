Imagine a courtroom where several seasoned judges, each trained in different legal schools, review the same case. The **Panel of LLM Judges (PoLL)** works like that: a group of large language models (the “judges”) read the same input and give their own answers. Because each model has its own quirks—some are great with facts, others excel at nuance—they often disagree. PoLL resolves this by letting every judge cast a vote, then taking the majority opinion or averaging their scores. This ensemble method smooths out individual biases and errors, just as a panel’s consensus is usually more reliable than any single judge’s verdict. The result: stronger, more trustworthy answers from AI.
