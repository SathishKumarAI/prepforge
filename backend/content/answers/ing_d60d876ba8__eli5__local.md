Think of building a smart robot as learning to play a new sport with a coach who keeps asking, “How did you do that?” The **interview loop** is the robot’s practice cycle:  

1. **Ask** – The robot poses a question (e.g., “What should I do next?”).  
2. **Act** – It tries an answer, like taking a shot in basketball.  
3. **Check** – A judge (the environment or a human) evaluates the move and gives feedback (“Good pass!” or “Missed the target”).  
4. **Learn** – The robot updates its internal playbook based on that feedback.  

The loop repeats many times, just as a player practices until the coach’s critique becomes a natural instinct. In AI, this continuous cycle of asking, acting, judging, and learning lets the system improve without explicit programming for every situation.
