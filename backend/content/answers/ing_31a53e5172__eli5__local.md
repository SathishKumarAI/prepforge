**Context as “instant seasoning”** – when the chef is asked for a recipe, they grab fresh herbs from the counter and sprinkle them on the dish right away. The flavor comes from what’s in front of them at that moment; it’s quick and changes with each order.

**Memory engineering as “long‑term spice cabinet”** – instead of reaching out every time, the chef keeps a well‑organized pantry of spices, sauces, and notes about past meals.
