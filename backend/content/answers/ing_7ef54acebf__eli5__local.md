Imagine you’re ordering pizza at a restaurant.

**REST (Representational State Transfer)** is like calling the kitchen and saying, “Give me a pepperoni pizza.” The kitchen has many separate menus—one for pizzas, one for drinks, one for desserts. If you want a drink too, you have to place another call. You’ll get exactly what you asked for, but if the chef’s recipe changes or you need a custom combo, you’re stuck with whole new orders.

**GraphQL** is like walking into a kitchen that lets you build your own pizza right there: “I want a pepperoni base, extra cheese, no onions, and a side of garlic bread.” You describe the shape of the dish in one request, and the kitchen sends back exactly those parts—no more, no less. If later you decide to add olives, you just tweak that single description.

So, REST is a fixed menu of endpoints; GraphQL is a flexible query language that lets you ask for precisely what you need in one go.
