Imagine building a smart robot that learns to play chess by watching grandmasters. **The Agent Development Life Cycle** is the recipe you follow, step by step, to turn raw data into that clever robot.

1. **Define the goal** – Decide the game rules and what “winning” means.  
2. **Collect experience** – Record many chess games (the agent’s training data).  
3. **Create a model** – Design a neural network that can evaluate board positions.  
4. **Train** – Show the model thousands of moves, letting it adjust its internal weights so it predicts good plays.  
5. **Test** – Let the robot play against other programs to see how well it learned.  
6. **Deploy** – Release the robot for real users or tournaments.  
7. **Monitor & update** – Watch its performance; if it starts losing, gather new games and retrain.

Just as a chef follows a recipe—gather ingredients, mix, cook, taste, refine—you guide an AI from idea to useful agent, always looping back to improve.
