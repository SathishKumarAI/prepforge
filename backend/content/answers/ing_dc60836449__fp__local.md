**Why MLOps matters**  
At its core, an AI system is a *pipeline* that turns raw data into actionable predictions and then feeds those predictions back to users or other systems. Just as a chemical plant needs precise control of temperature, pressure, and timing to produce consistent products, an ML pipeline must guarantee reproducibility, scalability, and safety. MLOps formalises this by treating model training, evaluation, deployment, monitoring, and governance as first‑class *software engineering* stages that can be automated, versioned, and audited.

**Model Deployment as a geometric optimisation problem**  
Deploying a model is essentially finding the optimal mapping from *state space* (data distribution + runtime constraints) to *action space* (predictive inference). The deployment architecture must minimise latency, maximise throughput, and satisfy resource budgets—an NP‑hard multi‑objective trade‑off. Techniques such as edge‑to‑cloud hybrid serving, model distillation, and dynamic batching emerge from this optimisation view.
