**Behavioral questions** are like interviewers asking you to “show me how you handled a real situation.”  
A handy recipe for answering them is **STAR‑L**:

- **S – Situation** (the background story)  
- **T – Task** (what you were asked to do)  
- **A – Action** (exact steps you took)  
- **R – Result** (what happened because of your actions)  
- **L – Learning** (what you learned or how you’ll use this skill next time)

*Analogy:* Think of a recipe for baking a cake. The **Situation** is the kitchen set‑up, the **Task** is “bake a chocolate cake,” the **Action** is mixing ingredients and setting the oven, the **Result** is a delicious cake that everyone likes, and the **Learning** is remembering to preheat next time.

Using STAR‑L keeps your answer focused, shows evidence of skill, and ends with growth—exactly what interviewers want.
