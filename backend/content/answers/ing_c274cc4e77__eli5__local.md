Instead, you split the cake into several smaller cakes and let them all bake at once in separate ovens. Once they’re done, you stack the layers together to get the final masterpiece faster.

The agent’s job can be broken into many small tasks—parsing language, looking up facts, planning actions, checking safety rules.
