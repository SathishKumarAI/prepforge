**Brooklin is a software tool that helps you move big streams of data from one place to another almost instantly, like a high‑speed courier for information.**

Imagine your company has several warehouses (databases, log files, cloud services) and you need to keep every new package (data record) in a central hub (a data lake or analytics system) as soon as it arrives. Brooklin acts as the courier network: it watches each warehouse for fresh packages, picks them up, and delivers them to the hub without waiting for scheduled batches. It can handle millions of packages per second across many warehouses—hence “near real‑time at scale.” The name comes from “Brook,” a stream, because it streams data smoothly, and “lin” from “line,” indicating its line‑by‑line processing.

* **Data streaming** – continuous flow of information.  
* **Near real‑time** – delay is only milliseconds or seconds, not hours.
