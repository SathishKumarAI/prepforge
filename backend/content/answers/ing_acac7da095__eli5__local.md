Think of a machine‑learning system as a bakery that wants to make the best cupcakes ever.  
**Ingest** is the moment you bring in all the ingredients—flour, sugar, eggs, and secret spices—from different suppliers (your data sources). A **data pipeline** is like a well‑planned kitchen workflow:  

1. **Collect** – gather raw ingredients from each supplier’s storage.  
2. **Clean** – trim away spoiled bits and standardize the units (e.g., convert all weights to grams).  
3. **Transform** – mix, fold, or bake to create consistent batter.  
4. **Store** – put the batter into a fridge so it’s ready when the baking day arrives.

In machine learning, each step turns raw data into a neat, usable form that the model can “taste” and learn from. The pipeline keeps everything organized, repeatable, and error‑free—just like a kitchen recipe keeps your cupcakes consistent every time.
