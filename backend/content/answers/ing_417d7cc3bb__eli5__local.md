Think of machine learning as a waiter in a busy restaurant. The “latency” is the time it takes from when you place an order (send a data point) to when you receive your dish (the model’s prediction). In ML, low latency means the system can give answers almost instantly—critical for real‑time applications like self‑driving cars, fraud detection, or voice assistants.
