Imagine you’re planning a road trip with friends.  
**Propose:** First, each friend suggests a possible route—maybe through the mountains or along the coast.  
**Evaluate:** You all quickly weigh the pros and cons: travel time, scenery, fuel costs, and how much fun each path might be.  
**Search:** The group then looks up real‑time traffic, weather, and gas prices for the top choices, refining the plan until you pick the best route.

That’s the **Tree of Thought (ToT) Loop** in AI: the model first “proposes” several candidate ideas or steps, then “evaluates” them against a goal or constraints, and finally “searches” deeper—looking up facts, recomputing, or exploring variations—to converge on the best solution. Each cycle builds a branching tree of thoughts, just like your friends’ route options, until the AI settles on the most promising answer.
