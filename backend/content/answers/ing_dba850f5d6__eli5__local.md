Imagine an advanced robot that can learn from any internet page, but you don’t want it to accidentally spread harmful rumors or break privacy rules. **Sandboxing** is like putting that robot in a special “learning room” with walls made of glass and a one‑way mirror.

Inside the room, the robot can read books (data), practice writing sentences (model updates), and test new ideas. The glass walls let you watch its work but stop it from touching anything outside—no leaking secrets or copying copyrighted text. A “mirror” inside the room shows how each new idea would look if it were shared with the world, so you can catch problems before they escape.

This strategy keeps the robot’s powerful learning safe (safety) and makes sure its actions stay within legal and ethical boundaries (governance). By confining its reach, we let AI grow while protecting people and society.
