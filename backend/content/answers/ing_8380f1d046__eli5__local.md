**Context engineering** is like preparing a recipe before you start cooking.  
Imagine you want the kitchen’s oven (the AI model) to bake the perfect cake (give useful answers). If you just drop all ingredients into the bowl without any guidance, the result will be messy. Instead, you first mix the right flour, sugar, eggs and spices in the correct proportions—this is *context engineering*.  

In machine learning, the “ingredients” are pieces of information you feed to a model: text prompts, images, data snippets. By carefully selecting, arranging, and sometimes re‑phrasing these inputs, you set the stage for the AI to understand what you want and how to respond. The better the context you engineer, the more accurate and relevant the model’s output will be—just as a well‑prepared batter yields a delicious cake.
