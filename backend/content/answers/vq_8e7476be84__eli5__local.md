**End users in data engineering are the people or systems that finally use the cleaned, organized data for their own tasks.**

Think of a library: the *data engineers* sort and shelve books (cleaning, cataloguing, storing). The *end users* are the readers who pick up those books to learn, research, or write stories. In the tech world they can be business analysts creating reports, data scientists training models, marketing teams running campaigns, or even other software applications that need reliable data.

So, while engineers build and maintain the “bookshelves,” end users are the ones who actually read the information and make decisions or create new products from it.
