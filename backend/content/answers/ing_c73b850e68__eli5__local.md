Think of a modern data architecture like the plumbing in a smart house.  
The **data architecture** is the blueprint that tells every pipe (or data flow) where to go so water (information) reaches each room (application) just when it’s needed.

In this “smart house” there are three main patterns:

1. **Data Lake (the big storage tank)** – a single, inexpensive container where all raw water arrives, no matter its source or quality.  
2. **Data Warehouse (the filtered faucet)** – a clean, organized tap that pulls only the well‑filtered water for the kitchen and office, ready to use immediately.  
3. **Streaming Pipelines (the instant faucet)** – tiny pipes that deliver fresh water in real time to devices like smart thermostats or security cameras.

Together they let the house run efficiently: raw water is collected, processed, and delivered instantly where it’s needed, all while keeping costs low and flexibility high.
