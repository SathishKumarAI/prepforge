**Centralized logging** is like a school’s cafeteria where every student brings their lunch bag, and the teacher keeps a single notebook of what everyone ate. All food items (log messages) from different classrooms (servers or apps) are sent to one place so the teacher can see everything at once, spot problems quickly, and make sure no one forgets anything.

**Distributed tracing** is like following a paper trail in a big office building. Each person writes a note on their envelope about where they went next—“to HR,” “to Accounting.” By collecting all these notes, you can reconstruct the exact path an envelope took through the whole building, even though it passed through many rooms (micro‑services). This lets you see delays or errors that happen along the way.
