Think of Elastic Cloud like a coffee shop that can change its size depending on how many customers you bring in.  

**Elastic pricing** means the price “stretches” with your usage: if you only need a small cup (few searches or logs), you pay little; if you suddenly order a large pot for an event, you’re billed more. The shop automatically scales up or down without you having to move tables.

*Serverless* is like ordering coffee on demand—no need to keep a café open all the time. You only pay when someone actually drinks it, and the system hands out the right amount of beans instantly.  

*Hosted* (or “self‑managed”) is like owning your own café: you rent space, buy equipment, and pay for the whole shop whether or not anyone visits. You control everything, but you also bear all costs.

Elastic pricing sits between them: it’s a hosted service that automatically scales resources as needed, so you only pay for what you actually use, just like a coffee shop that expands its seating when more customers arrive.
