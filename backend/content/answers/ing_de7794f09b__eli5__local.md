**Automatic Registration by Orchestrators**  
Think of a smart kitchen where every appliance (oven, fridge, blender) is a *service* that can be used in recipes (workflows). The **orchestrator** is like the kitchen manager who keeps track of what appliances are available and where they’re located. When you add a new appliance—say, a sous‑chef robot—it automatically announces itself to the manager. The manager writes down its name, location, and what it can do (its “API”). From then on, anyone cooking a recipe can ask the manager for that robot without having to look up its details themselves.  

*Unavoidable term:* **Service** – an independent program or device that offers specific functions over a network.  
This automatic registration lets new services join the kitchen instantly, making it easier for chefs (developers) to build complex recipes (applications) without manual setup.
