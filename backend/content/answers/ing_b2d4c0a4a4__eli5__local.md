**Step 2: The Technical Phone Screen – what it feels like and why it matters**

Think of the interview as a “virtual coffee chat” with a future teammate, but with a twist: instead of talking about your hobbies, you’ll discuss how you’d solve real data problems. You’ll be given a short problem or a piece of code to analyze on a shared screen (or just describe what you’d do). The interviewer watches how you break the problem into smaller parts—like chopping a big pizza into slices before baking it. They want to see your logical flow, not just correct answers: can you explain why you choose one algorithm over another? Can you spot bugs or suggest improvements?

During this call, the interviewer may ask about:

- **Basic concepts** (e.g., what is supervised learning?)  
- **Practical coding** (write a quick function to clean data)  
- **Problem‑solving** (how would you handle missing values in a huge dataset?)

It’s not a final test; it’s a conversation that shows whether your thinking style and technical skills fit the team. A clear, calm explanation—just like describing how you’d slice pizza for everyone—will impress them most.
