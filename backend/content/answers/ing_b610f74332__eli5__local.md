**Consistency** is like making sure every frame of the trailer follows the same style and tone as the original film—no sudden changes that would confuse the viewer.  
**Relevance** is choosing only the scenes that actually tell the story’s main point, dropping background fluff that doesn’t help the audience understand what happens.
