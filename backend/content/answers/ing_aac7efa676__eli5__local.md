Imagine your AI assistant as a tidy kitchen where recipes (the instructions it follows) are stored on a whiteboard. **Context pollution** is like letting random crumbs, broken utensils, and half‑finished dishes pile up around the board. Each new crumb adds noise that can confuse which recipe to follow.

When an agent receives a long conversation or many prompts at once, every extra piece of text becomes another crumb in its “kitchen.” The agent’s internal memory (its short‑term context) gets cluttered, so it may misinterpret what the user actually wants—like reaching for a salt shaker when you asked for sugar.

To keep the kitchen clean, give the agent only the most recent, relevant snippets of dialogue. Think of sending a fresh whiteboard each time: this keeps its focus sharp and prevents those pesky crumbs from derailing the task.
