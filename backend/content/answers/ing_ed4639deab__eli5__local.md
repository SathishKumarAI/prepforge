**Multi‑Stage Extraction Pattern – Structured Generation**

Think of writing a recipe book.  
First you **pick the ingredients** (the data). You sift through a pantry and pull out all the items that fit the dish—flour, sugar, eggs. That’s the *extraction* step: gather the raw facts.

Next, you **organize them** into sections: “Dry Ingredients,” “Wet Ingredients.” You’re arranging the same stuff in a tidy order so readers can find what they need quickly. That’s the *structuring* step.

Finally, you **write the instructions** that use those organized ingredients—step‑by‑step directions that turn the list into a finished dish. That’s the *generation* step: produce readable text from the structured data.

In AI, the Multi‑Stage Extraction Pattern does exactly this:  
1. **Extract** relevant facts from raw input.  
2. **Structure** those facts into a clear format (tables, hierarchies).  
3. **Generate** coherent output that uses the organized information.  

Like a recipe book, the pattern turns scattered items into a useful, easy‑to‑read guide.
