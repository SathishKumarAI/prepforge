Imagine you’re a detective who needs to solve many mysteries (answer questions) but the clues are spread across thousands of books in a giant library.  
**Retrieval‑augmented generation (RAG)** is like giving the detective a smart assistant that first scans the library and pulls out only the most relevant pages, then writes a clear explanation using those pages as its source.

When the knowledge base grows—think moving from a single shelf to an entire city of books—the assistant must be smarter about finding clues quickly. It uses “indexing” (like a detailed map) so it can jump straight to the right aisle, and “filtering” (like only looking at books on certain subjects).
