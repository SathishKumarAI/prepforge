Imagine you’re building a giant LEGO city.  
The **data architecture** is the blueprint that tells where each piece goes: which blocks form roads (databases), which ones hold traffic lights (security layers), and how all the neighborhoods connect (integration).  

First, **discover** means scanning every LEGO box to see what pieces you have—finding the right colors and shapes.  
Then **clean** is like sorting out broken or missing bricks so the city runs smoothly.  
Finally, **secure** is putting a protective cover over the whole set so that only you can rearrange it and no one else can tamper with your masterpiece.

In machine‑learning projects, this blueprint keeps raw data organized, reliable, and safe before AI turns it into insights.
