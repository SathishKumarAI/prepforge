**Semantic caching** is like keeping a “smart” photo album for your brain.  
When you ask a question, the system first looks at its cache—an index of past answers that already match what you need. But it doesn’t just copy‑paste; it checks the *meaning* of the new request against those stored snippets. If the meanings line up, it reuses the old answer, saving time.  
If the meaning is slightly different (say you’re asking about “dogs” but the cache has a picture of a “cat”), it only pulls the parts that match (“four‑legged animal”) and then fills in the new bits from scratch.
