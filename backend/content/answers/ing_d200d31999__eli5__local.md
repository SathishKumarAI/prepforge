Think of a recipe book that needs to be sent out to everyone in your town. **Batch processing** is like printing the whole cookbook at once and mailing it to every house. All customers receive the same copy, and you only see the results after the entire batch has shipped.

**Stream processing** is like delivering each page as soon as it’s finished, one by one, to people who request it right away. Readers can start reading the first page immediately, and new pages arrive continuously without waiting for the whole book.

In machine learning, *batch* means you train or predict on a fixed set of data all at once—useful when you have enough time and resources to process everything together. *Stream* means you handle each piece of data as it arrives—ideal for real‑time applications like fraud detection or live recommendation systems where speed matters more than waiting for a full batch.
