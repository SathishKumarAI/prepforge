Finetuning is like teaching a recipe book how to cook exactly the dish you love. You give it your favorite recipe (the data) and let it practice until it can make that dish every time. OpenAI charges for this training in two parts:

1. **Training cost** – Think of it as paying the chef’s hourly rate while they learn your recipe. The price depends on how many “batches” of ingredients (data chunks) the model sees and how long it keeps practicing.

2. **Inference cost** – After the chef is ready, every time you ask for a new dish (an API call), you pay a small fee for the chef’s effort to cook it.

So you pay once for the chef’s learning phase and again each time you order a meal. The exact numbers depend on how big your recipe book is and how often you use the trained model.
