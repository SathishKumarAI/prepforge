Think of an AI system as a smart robot that can move around a big house (the network).  
**Agentic attack surface** is the set of ways a bad‑to‑good “intruder” could use that robot’s own abilities—its *agency*—to break in. It’s like giving someone keys to the house but also letting them decide where to go, what doors to open, or how to hide their footprints.

To keep the robot from misbehaving, we use **agentic security** and **sandboxing**.  
- *Agentic security* is a rulebook that tells the robot “you can only ask for help if you’re allowed to, and you must report every request.”  
- *Sandboxing* is a safe room inside the house where the robot runs its code; it can explore there but cannot touch the rest of the home.

Together they let the robot act independently while preventing it from using its own powers to cause trouble.
