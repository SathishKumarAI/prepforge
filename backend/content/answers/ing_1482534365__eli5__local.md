Imagine you’re cooking a big pot of soup for a dinner party. The kitchen is your main server where all the ingredients (data and code) mingle to create the final dish (your machine‑learning model). A disaster recovery site is like having a backup kitchen in another town, stocked with identical pots, pans, and recipes.

If a fire, flood, or power outage knocks out your original kitchen, you can quickly move the cooking to the backup kitchen. The same ingredients are already there, so the soup continues to simmer without losing flavor or time. In cloud terms, a disaster recovery site is a separate data center (or region) that holds replicated copies of your ML workloads and data, ready to take over if the primary location fails. It guarantees continuity, protects against data loss, and keeps your models running smoothly even when something goes wrong.
