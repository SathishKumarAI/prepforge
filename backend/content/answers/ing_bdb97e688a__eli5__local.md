Instead of buying everything separately, she keeps a small pantry (the **retrieval** part) where she stores key spices and herbs that can be quickly grabbed when needed. While cooking, she also has a recipe book (the **generation** part) that tells her how to mix those ingredients into a delicious dish.

The retrieval component looks up relevant documents or facts from a large database—just like pulling spices from the pantry.
