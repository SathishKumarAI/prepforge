**What is SSO?**  
Imagine you have a key that opens every door in your apartment building—your front gate, the laundry room, the gym, and even the office of the building manager. You don’t need to carry separate keys for each place; one key gives you access everywhere.  

In the digital world, *Single Sign‑On* (SSO) is that key. Instead of logging in separately to dozens of websites or apps—each with its own username and password—you enter your credentials once on a trusted “identity provider” (like Google or Microsoft). That provider then gives each service a short‑lived ticket that says, “This person is already verified.” The service trusts the ticket, lets you in, and you’re done.  

So SSO means **one set of login details granting access to many services**—just like one key opens every door.
