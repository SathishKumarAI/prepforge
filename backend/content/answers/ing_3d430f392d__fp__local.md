**Why Microsoft’s ML interview is a *model‑training* exercise**

At its core, hiring at Microsoft is a supervised learning problem: the company has labeled data (past hires) and wants to predict future success on the job. The interview pipeline is designed as a *feature extractor* that reveals traits most predictive of performance—technical depth, problem‑solving speed, and cultural fit.

1. **Phone / Technical Screening**  
   *Feature*: Code correctness & efficiency.  
   *Why*: In production ML pipelines, bugs propagate quickly; this stage checks basic coding hygiene and algorithmic intuition—our first “loss function”.

2. **On‑site (or virtual) Interview**  
   *Features*:

   The interviewers essentially compute a weighted sum of these features to approximate the candidate’s expected contribution.
