**Why “AI ethics” is now a core engineering skill**

The fundamental problem any AI system solves is *decision‑making under uncertainty*.  
Those decisions are made by algorithms that learn from data; the data are a proxy for reality and the learning objective is an optimization of some surrogate loss. In practice, two forces dominate:

1. **Statistical bias** – the training distribution rarely matches the deployment environment.  
2. **Human values** – society imposes constraints (fairness, privacy, safety) that cannot be captured by a single loss term.

To guarantee that an optimized model respects human values, engineers must *formalize* those values into mathematical constraints or regularizers. This is the essence of responsible AI: turning abstract ethics into concrete algorithmic guarantees.
