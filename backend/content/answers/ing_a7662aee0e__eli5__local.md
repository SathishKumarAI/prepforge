**What is “Time to First Token”?**

Imagine you’re at a coffee shop that makes your latte as soon as you order it. The *time to first token* is the moment the barista hands you the first sip of espresso—your first tangible sign that the drink is brewing. In AI, when a model receives a prompt, “first token” means the very first word or piece of text it outputs. *Time to First Token* measures how long the system takes from receiving the prompt until that first output appears.

Think of it like a relay race: the prompt is the baton handed to the runner (the AI). The faster the runner reaches the finish line, the quicker you see any result. A short time means the model can start responding almost instantly; a longer time means there’s a noticeable lag before anything shows up. This metric matters for real‑time applications—chatbots, voice assistants, or live gaming—where waiting even a few hundred milliseconds feels like a delay.
