Think of an AI system as a kitchen that must always be ready to serve food, even if something goes wrong. **Redundancy** is like having two identical ovens and two separate power supplies. If one oven burns out or the main electricity line trips, the other oven can keep cooking while technicians fix the first one. In AI terms, we duplicate key components—servers, storage, network paths, and even software modules—so that if one fails, another takes over instantly. This “backup” setup keeps the service running (high **availability**) without interruption. Just as a restaurant with two ovens never runs out of pizza, an AI system built on redundancy stays online and reliable, no matter what hiccup occurs.
