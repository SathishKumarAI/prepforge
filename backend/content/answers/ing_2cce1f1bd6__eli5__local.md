Instead of giving the assistant one big instruction, DSPy lets you build it from small “ingredients” (code blocks) that each do a simple job—like “clean up spelling,” “find key facts,” or “rewrite in plain language.” You then mix these ingredients in the right order, just as a cook follows a recipe. The result is a custom program that tells the language model exactly how to behave for each task.

- **Language Model (LM)** – an AI trained on huge text that can generate or understand words.  
- **Prompt** – the question or instruction you give the LM.  
- **DSPy** – a Python library that lets you chain together small prompt‑crafting functions into a full “recipe” for the LM, making it easier to build reliable, reusable AI tools.
