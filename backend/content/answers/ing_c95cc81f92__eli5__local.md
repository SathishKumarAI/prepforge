Imagine a smart kitchen robot that can cook, clean, and order groceries—an “agent” that acts on your behalf.  
**Audit logging** is like the robot’s diary: every command it receives, every action it takes, and the time of each event are written down in an unchangeable notebook. If something goes wrong—say the robot spills sauce—you can read the diary to see exactly who told it what and when.

**Agentic security** means treating that robot as a mini‑person: we give it its own identity (a username) and set strict limits on what it may do, just like a child in a playground.  

**Sandboxing** is the kitchen’s safe zone: the robot can only use utensils and ingredients inside a designated area, so if it misbehaves it can’t damage the whole house.

Together, the diary (audit log), the identity (agentic security), and the safe zone (sandbox) give us confidence that the robot acts responsibly, and we can always check its record to hold it accountable.
