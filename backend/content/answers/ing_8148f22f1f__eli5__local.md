Think of an **AI Site Reliability Engineer (SRE)** as the “caretaker” for a busy kitchen that prepares dishes (the AI models) every minute.  
The caretaker’s job is twofold:  

1. **Remediation** – When a stove goes hot or a spice jar runs out, the SRE fixes it right away so the chefs can keep cooking without interruption. In AI terms, that means spotting and repairing bugs in model code, retraining when data drifts, and patching security holes before they cause downtime.  

2. **Documentation** – The caretaker writes clear recipes and logs: what ingredients were used, how the stove was set, and why a particular dish tasted different last week. For AI, this is keeping detailed records of model versions, training data, hyper‑parameters, and performance metrics so anyone can reproduce results or audit decisions later.

So an AI SRE keeps the system running smoothly (remediation) while ensuring every change is traceable and understandable (documentation).
