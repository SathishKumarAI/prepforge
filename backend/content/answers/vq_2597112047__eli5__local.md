Think of data as ingredients in a soup.  
You want the taste (your result) to be reliable, not just a random mix that could change if you add more water or salt. The “enough” rule is like cooking: start with a small pot, taste it, and keep adding until the flavor no longer shifts noticeably.  

In data engineering, this means collecting enough samples so that when you run your analysis again on another batch, the answer stays roughly the same. If you’re measuring average sales per day, for example, 30–60 days often give a stable estimate; if the numbers keep swinging wildly, you need more days or more customers in your sample.
