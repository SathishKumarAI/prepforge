**RAG Taxonomy: The “Library‑and‑Librarian” Model**

Think of an AI as a smart librarian who writes answers (generation) but can also pull up the exact book it needs (retrieval).  
The RAG taxonomy sorts how these two parts work together:

| Part | What it does | Analogy |
|------|--------------|---------|
| **Retriever** | Scans a big database and hands the best‑matching snippets to the writer. | A librarian skimming shelves for relevant titles. |
| **Generator** | Takes those snippets, blends them with its own knowledge, and writes a polished reply. | The librarian writing a summary from the books it found. |
| **Feedback Loop** | After seeing the answer, the system checks if the chosen snippets were useful and refines future searches. | A librarian asking readers if the recommended book helped, then updating her list. |
