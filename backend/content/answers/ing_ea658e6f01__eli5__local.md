**Commitment‑Based Pricing for AI is like renting a bike with a deposit.**  
You want the bike (the AI service), but you also want control over how much it costs. So, you agree to pay a *commitment fee*—a small upfront amount that locks in a lower hourly rate for the whole month or year. If you use more than you expected, you still get the cheaper rate; if you use less, you’re not penalized, just keep the deposit (or get it back).  

In practice, an AI platform charges a *subscription* or *commitment* fee that guarantees a discounted per‑use price for each inference or training job. You pay the commitment up front, then enjoy lower costs as your workload grows, while the provider knows you’ll stay and can plan resources accordingly. This balances predictability for both sides—just like the bike rental keeps your cost stable no matter how many rides you take.
