Imagine you’re building a giant library of stories (the **Large Language Model**, or **LLM**) that can read any text and predict what comes next.  
The **Transformer** is the librarian’s special notebook. Instead of reading line by line, it looks at all the words in a sentence at once, noting how each word relates to every other—like a group chat where everyone shares their thoughts instantly.  

Key ideas:  
- **Tokens** are simply pieces of text (words or parts of words).  
- **Attention** lets the model weigh which tokens matter most for predicting the next one.  
- The model learns by adjusting tiny numbers called **weights**, so that over time it guesses more accurately, just like a student improves after many practice quizzes.  

So, an LLM is a huge, well‑trained “story predictor,” and the Transformer is the clever system that lets it consider all context at once, making predictions fast and surprisingly human‑like.
