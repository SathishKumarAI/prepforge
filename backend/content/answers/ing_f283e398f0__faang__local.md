• The evidence base includes randomized trials, quasi‑experiments, and observational studies.

**Depth**  
- **Randomized controlled trials (RCTs)**: Show modest gains—employment ↑ 5–10 % and wages ↑ $1–2k annually—but often short follow‑up (<12 mo).  
- **Quasi‑experiments (difference‑in‑differences, regression discontinuity)**: Larger effects in high‑skill fields; effect sizes drop when controlling for self‑selection.  
- **Observational studies**: Broadly positive trends but suffer from omitted‑variable bias; some find negligible wage growth after 2 yrs.  
- **Program heterogeneity**: Intensive, industry‑aligned training (e.g., coding bootcamps) yields higher employment than generic soft‑skill courses.  
- **Moderator analysis**: Older workers and those with lower baseline education benefit less unless programs include job placement support.

**Edge cases**  
• Attrition bias in RCTs (participants dropping out).  
• Spillover effects where employers adjust hiring practices post‑intervention.  
• Measurement error in self‑reported skill gains.  

**Optimize & communicate**
