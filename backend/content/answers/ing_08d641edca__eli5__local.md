Imagine the chefs stand at a long conveyor belt that moves dishes forward. Instead of pulling each plate out for tasting, they use a quick “tasting station” placed midway: a small sampler takes a bite, sends the flavor score back instantly, and the dish keeps moving. The robots keep cooking at full speed; only a tiny part of the flow is paused for human review.
