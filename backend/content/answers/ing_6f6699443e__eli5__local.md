**Context engineering** is the art of telling an AI exactly what “scene” it should be looking at before it tries to answer a question or write something. Imagine you’re giving a recipe to someone who only knows how to bake bread, but you want them to make a chocolate cake instead. You would first explain the whole kitchen: the oven’s temperature, the ingredients on hand, and the type of cake you want. That “kitchen set‑up” is like context engineering for AI—providing all the relevant background so it can give the right answer.

In practice, you supply the model a short paragraph or list of facts (the *context*) that frames its task: who you are, what you’re asking about, and any special rules. The model then uses that frame to generate a response that fits your specific situation.
