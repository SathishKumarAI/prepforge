Think of an **embedding** as a secret recipe that turns a word (or image, or sound) into a list of numbers—just like turning a picture of a cat into a string of coordinates on a giant map. Those lists live in a **vector space**, which you can imagine as a vast room where every point is a possible recipe.

The **dot product** is the room’s “friendliness meter.” Take two recipes (two points) and slide one along the other, adding up their matched numbers. If they’re pointing in roughly the same direction—meaning the words share meaning—the meter lights up high; if they’re opposite or unrelated, it stays low. So the dot product tells us how similar or “aligned” two embeddings are, just by multiplying corresponding coordinates and summing them up.
