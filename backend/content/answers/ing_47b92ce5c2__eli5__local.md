**Loop engineering in AI** is a way of building systems that keep learning and improving by themselves, just like a child practicing a skill over and over.

Imagine a robot learning to throw a ball.  
1. **Action** – it throws the ball (the *output*).  
2. **Feedback** – sensors measure where the ball lands (the *input*).  
3. **Adjustment** – the system updates its throwing strategy based on that feedback, then tries again.

That cycle—action → feedback → adjustment—is a **feedback loop**. In AI, we design many such loops: a model predicts something, receives real‑world data telling it how right or wrong it was, and uses that information to tweak its internal parameters. By repeatedly looping this process, the AI “learns” from experience without human intervention. The key idea is continuous self‑improvement through repeated observation and correction.
