Imagine a giant library that receives books from every corner of the world—some in neat folders, others as loose pages, some in languages you don’t speak yet. A data engineer is like the librarian who makes sure each book ends up on the right shelf so anyone can find it quickly.

**Common challenges**

1. **Inconsistent format** – Like books written in different styles or missing covers, data often comes in many shapes (CSV, JSON, images).  
2. **Missing or wrong values** – Some pages are torn out or printed incorrectly; these gaps break analysis.  
3. **Huge volume** – The library keeps growing faster than the shelves can hold—handling millions of records without slowing down is tough.  
4. **Speed vs accuracy** – You want to check every book (slow but reliable) or skim quickly (fast but risky). Balancing both is hard.  
5. **Security and privacy** – Some books contain sensitive information; you must keep them safe while still allowing legitimate readers.
