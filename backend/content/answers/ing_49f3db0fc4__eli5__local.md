**Handshake – Think of a phone call**

When you want to talk with a friend, you usually dial their number, wait for them to answer, then chat back and forth. That initial dialing is like the *handshake* in WebSockets.

A **WebSocket** is a special two‑way “phone line” that stays open between your web browser (the caller) and a server (your friend). Once the handshake succeeds—just as you get a voice on the line—you can send messages any time, without having to keep dialing or opening new windows. This is useful when a site needs instant updates: a news ticker, a multiplayer game, or a live chat. Traditional web requests open a new “call” for each message and then hang up; WebSockets let you keep the conversation going smoothly, just like staying on the line while you talk.
