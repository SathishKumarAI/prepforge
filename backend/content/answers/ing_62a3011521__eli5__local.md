Think of HTTPS as a secret envelope for your online letters.  
When you visit a website, your browser (the sender) writes a message (“I want to see this page”) and puts it in an envelope (HTTP). Without protection, anyone on the street could peek inside or tamper with the letter.  

HTTPS adds two safeguards:  

1. **Encryption** – It’s like writing the letter in invisible ink that only the intended receiver can read.  
2. **Authentication** – The website signs the envelope with a digital “stamp” (a certificate) so you know it really came from that site and not an impostor.

So HTTPS keeps your data private, prevents eavesdropping, and verifies the website’s identity before any information travels across the internet.
