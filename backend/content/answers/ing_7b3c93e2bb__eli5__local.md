Imagine you’re preparing a recipe for a big pot of stew.  
*Feature engineering* is like creating new ingredients—mixing carrots, onions, and spices into a flavorful puree that didn’t exist before. You’re inventing fresh “features” (data pieces) that might help the model taste better.  

*Feature selection* is then picking which existing ingredients actually make the stew delicious. From all the vegetables on your shelf you choose only those that add flavor, discarding ones that are bland or redundant.  

So, feature engineering builds new data components; feature selection chooses the most useful of whatever you have (engineered or original). Both steps refine what the machine learning model receives, but one creates, the other prunes.
