Think of building a smart city that runs on data, like a giant brain.  
The **tech stack** is the set of tools you pick: the roads (network), the power plants (servers), the traffic lights (databases), and the sensors (APIs).  

A **high‑level design (HLD)** shows how those parts fit together without getting lost in wiring details—just like a city map that marks where each district is, how buses travel between them, and where water pipelines run. It tells you:

* **What components** exist (frontend, backend, database, cache).  
* **How they talk** (REST, gRPC, message queues).  
* **Where data lives** (SQL for records, NoSQL for logs).  
* **Scaling rules** (more servers when traffic rises).
