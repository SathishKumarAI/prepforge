**Dual‑Representation Strategy – Multimodal Retrieval‑Augmented Generation (RAG)**  

Imagine a student writing an essay with two notebooks: one holds only text notes, the other holds pictures and diagrams. When the student needs to explain a concept, she flips to both notebooks—reading the words and looking at the images—to pull out the best explanation for her audience.  
In AI, *retrieval‑augmented generation* works like that: the system first searches a large database (the “notebook”) for relevant documents or media. Then it feeds those retrieved snippets into a language model that writes the final answer. The *dual‑representation* part means the search can use both text and other modalities—images, audio, or video—to find the most useful material.
