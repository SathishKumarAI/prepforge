**Cost metrics** are the gauges on the control panel—fuel usage, maintenance costs, and how many miles it can travel before needing a refill.  
**Observability** is the camera feed inside the tunnel: it shows you exactly where the train is, what parts of the track are worn, and whether any brakes are slipping.
