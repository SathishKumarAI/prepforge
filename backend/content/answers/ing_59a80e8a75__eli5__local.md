Imagine a library that not only keeps books but also remembers why each book was recommended to you before. When someone asks the librarian for a suggestion, she can pull up her notes instantly instead of re‑thinking every reason from scratch.

In an AI recommendation engine, “explanation caching” works exactly like those notes. Each time the system recommends something (a movie, a product, etc.), it also writes down a short explanation—why that item fits your taste, what past choices influenced it, and which patterns in the data were used. Later, when you want to know why the recommendation was made again, the engine fetches the stored explanation instead of recomputing everything.
