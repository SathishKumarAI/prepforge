Imagine you’re at a big community bulletin board in a town square. Anyone can **post** a note (publish) on the board, and anyone who wants to see that kind of news can **subscribe** to receive copies of those notes whenever they appear.

In computing, *Pub/Sub messaging* works the same way. A sender (the publisher) places a message onto a virtual “board” called a topic. Listeners (subscribers) sign up for that topic and automatically get every new message that’s posted, without the publisher needing to know who is listening or how many are there. The board handles delivery so each subscriber gets its own copy, keeping publishers and subscribers independent and scalable—just like a town bulletin board lets people share news freely while anyone interested can read it whenever it posts.
