Imagine you’re building a LEGO set that everyone can use, but each person might bring their own colors and pieces. **JSON Schema** is like the instruction booklet for that set: it tells every builder exactly which bricks (data fields) are required, what shapes they must be (types), and how big or small they should fit (length limits). If someone tries to add a brick in the wrong place or with the wrong shape, the booklet catches it before the whole structure falls apart.

*JSON* is just a plain‑text way of writing data—think of it as the bricks themselves. *Schema* is the set of rules that says, “This spot must have a number between 0 and 100; this field can’t be empty.” By checking data against its schema first, you avoid bugs, keep everything consistent, and make sure everyone’s LEGO creations look exactly how they’re supposed to.
