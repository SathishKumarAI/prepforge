Imagine your brain as a recipe book that’s trying to finish a sentence it started writing. **Speculative decoding** is the way an AI “predicts” what comes next by guessing several possible words at once, then quickly checking which guess makes the most sense with everything it has already written.

Think of a puzzle where you can see only a few pieces ahead. You lay down a handful of potential pieces (the guesses), look at how they fit together, and pick the one that best completes the picture so far. The AI does this every step: it speculates on several next words, evaluates them quickly, and keeps the most promising chain. This speeds up writing sentences while still staying coherent, just like a skilled puzzle‑solver who looks ahead to avoid dead ends.
