**Why behavioral interviews matter at xAI**

2. **First‑round panel (30 min)** – 3–4 interviewers ask “STAR” questions (Situation, Task, Action, Result). They probe how you handled ambiguity, interdisciplinary communication, and failure—key for an AI team that constantly re‑defines problem spaces.  
3. **Deep‑dive technical–behavioral (45 min)** – You solve a real‑world case with a mentor while narrating your decision process. The interviewer listens for *principled trade‑offs* rather than just the final answer, mirroring how xAI balances accuracy, interpretability, and scalability.
