**Week 3 – “Data Definition & Baseline” (Coursera)**  
Imagine you’re a chef who wants to bake the perfect cake in a busy kitchen. First, you need to know exactly what ingredients are available and how they’re measured—this is **data definition**. You write down that flour is in grams, sugar in cups, and eggs by count so everyone uses the same units. Next, you make a simple “test” recipe: mix the basics just enough to see if the batter rises at all—that’s your **baseline model**. It doesn’t try to be fancy; it only tells you whether the system works before you add spices or adjust temperatures. In machine learning, we do the same: define every data field clearly and run a quick, simple algorithm that shows how well the current setup predicts outcomes. If the baseline fails, we know something’s wrong with our ingredients (data) or process. This step keeps the production line running smoothly and reliably.
