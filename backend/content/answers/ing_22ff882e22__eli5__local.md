**Multi‑Hop Reasoning Loops – “Agentic Retrieval‑Augmented Generation” (RAG)**

Think of a detective who needs to solve a mystery but can’t remember everything off the top of her head. She first asks a quick question (“Who was at the party?”) and pulls out a list of names from a database (retrieval). Then she follows each name, asking more questions (“What did that person do?”), pulling up new clues each time. Each answer helps shape the next question, and the detective keeps looping until the whole story makes sense.

In AI, *multi‑hop reasoning loops* let a language model ask itself a series of connected questions, fetch relevant text for each step, and update its answer after every loop. “Agentic” means the system behaves like an agent that decides what to ask next, rather than just parroting one answer. The result is deeper, more accurate explanations that feel like a smart conversation with evidence at every turn.
