Imagine you’re teaching a child to recognize animals by showing them many pictures and telling the name each time.  
**Artificial Intelligence (AI)** is like giving that child a whole toolbox of learning tricks—so it can not only point out animals but also answer questions, play games, or drive a car.  
**Machine Learning (ML)** is one of those tricks: you give the computer lots of data (e.g., photos of cats and dogs) and let it find patterns on its own, so later it can guess whether a new picture shows a cat or a dog without being told explicitly.

- *Data* are the examples you feed in.  
- A *model* is the set of rules the computer builds from that data.  
- *Training* is the process of feeding data and adjusting the model until it gets good at the task.

So AI is the big idea of smart machines, and ML is a practical way to build them by learning from experience.
