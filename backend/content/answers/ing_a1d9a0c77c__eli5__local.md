Think of GraphQL as a smart waiter in a restaurant that lets you pick exactly what you want from the menu, no more, no less.

* **Schema** – The menu itself: it lists every dish (type) and how they’re made (fields).  
* **Queries** – Your order: you ask for specific dishes and only the parts of each dish you need.  
* **Mutations** – Changing a dish: adding an ingredient or changing the price—operations that modify data.  
* **Resolvers** – The kitchen staff: they know how to actually fetch or change the ingredients (data) when you place an order.  
* **Subscriptions** – Live updates: like having a waiter hand you a new dessert whenever it’s ready, so you’re always in sync.

Just as the waiter gives you exactly what you asked for without extra courses, GraphQL delivers precisely the data you request, making APIs leaner and faster.
