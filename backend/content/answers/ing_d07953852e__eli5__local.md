Imagine you’re in a neighborhood where everyone has a small garden. Each gardener learns which plants grow best on their own soil, but nobody wants to share the exact recipe for fertilizer because it’s personal. A *federated learning* protocol is like a community potluck: each gardener trains a plant‑care model on their own garden, then only sends the *improvements* (tiny tweaks) back to a central kitchen. The kitchen aggregates all these tweaks, updates a common recipe, and sends the new recipe back out. No one ever sees anyone else’s private soil or exact measurements—only the collective wisdom is shared.
