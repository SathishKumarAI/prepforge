Imagine your smart watch as a diary that writes down the amount of sleep you get every night, one entry per day. **Time series analysis** is like reading that diary and looking for patterns: maybe you sleep more on weekends or your nights become shorter when you travel. It treats each nightly measurement as a point in time, so it cares about *when* the data was recorded, not just what the numbers are. By sliding a small “window” over the diary—looking at the last few days, weeks, or months—it can spot trends (overall increase or decrease), cycles (weekly rhythms), and sudden changes (a night of poor sleep). In short, it turns your daily sleep log into a story that shows how your rest evolves over time.
