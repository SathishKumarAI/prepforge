**RAG (Retrieval‑Augmented Generation) is like a smart student who never forgets what they’ve read.**  
When the model gets a question, it first *retrieves* relevant passages from a big library (the retrieval step). Think of this as flipping to a bookmarked page in a textbook before answering. Then, instead of just pulling out a single fact, the model *generates* a full answer that weaves together those retrieved snippets with its own language skills.

- **Retrieval**: Looking up exact pieces of text from an external database or document set.  
- **Generation**: Creating new sentences based on what it knows and what it just read.

In interviews, expect questions like “How does RAG improve accuracy?” or “What are the trade‑offs between retrieving too much or too little information?” They’re probing whether you understand that retrieval gives factual grounding while generation keeps responses natural.
