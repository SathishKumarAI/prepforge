Think of an AI agent as a **smart robot assistant** that lives inside your computer or phone. Like a real‑world helper, it has three simple parts:

1. **Sensors (perception)** – It looks at the world through data: text you type, images you upload, or sensor readings from a device.
2. **Brain (policy)** – A tiny program decides what to do next based on that input and its goals—e.g., “answer the question” or “send an email.”
3. **Actuators (action)** – It produces an output: text, a button press, or a command sent back out.

Just as you might ask your personal assistant to draft a note, an AI agent receives instructions, processes them with its internal model, and returns the result. The key idea is that the agent *acts* on behalf of you, not just passively displays information.
