**Step 4 – “Scale the Design”**  
Think of your system like a city plan. In the first steps you pick where the roads go (the basic architecture). Step 4 asks: *What if the city suddenly doubles in population?* You must add more streets, traffic lights, and parking so cars don’t jam.

In machine‑learning terms, “scaling” means adding capacity—more servers, faster storage, or distributed processing—so that the model can handle larger data volumes and higher request rates without slowing down. It also involves balancing load (dividing work evenly) and ensuring reliability (backup systems if one part fails).  

So, just as a city expands its roads and traffic system when more people move in, you expand your ML infrastructure so it keeps running smoothly even under heavy use.
