Think of a whiteboard exercise like a cooking class where the instructor first asks you, “What dish do we want to make?” and then writes down every ingredient and step before anyone starts chopping. In AI projects, the same idea helps teams avoid costly mistakes.

**Why ask clarifying questions?**  
When people start building a model, they often assume the problem is clear. By asking simple, focused questions—“Who will use this tool?”, “What data do we actually have?”, “How will success look?”—you uncover hidden assumptions and missing pieces. It’s like checking the recipe before you cook: if you discover you’re short on salt or the oven temperature is wrong, you can fix it early.

**Whiteboard exercise steps**

1. **Write the problem statement** in plain words.  
2. **Ask one question at a time**, jotting answers beside each.  
3. **Spot gaps**—missing data, unclear goals, ethical concerns—and add them to the board.  
4. **Re‑define the goal** with all new information.

By turning questions into visible notes, everyone sees exactly what’s known and what still needs answering, just as a cook sees every ingredient before mixing. This keeps the AI project on track and prevents “surprises” when the model finally runs.
