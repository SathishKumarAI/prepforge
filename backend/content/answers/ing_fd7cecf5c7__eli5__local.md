Think of Azure Marketplace as a grocery store where you can buy fresh produce (Elastic Cloud) in two ways: **hosted** and **serverless**.

*Hosted* is like buying a ready‑made meal that comes with a set portion size, cooking instructions, and a price per kilogram. You decide how many meals to buy, pay for the exact amount of compute you need, and Elastic runs them on dedicated virtual machines in Azure. You control the “kitchen” – scaling up or down by adding more servers.

*Serverless* is like ordering a meal that’s cooked just for you when you place an order. You don’t think about portions; you only pay for the exact time your data is processed (seconds of CPU). Elastic runs it on shared infrastructure, automatically starting and stopping resources as traffic comes in, so you never pay for idle kitchen space.

In short: hosted gives you predictable, dedicated servers with a fixed cost per unit, while serverless charges you only for actual usage, scaling instantly without your intervention.
