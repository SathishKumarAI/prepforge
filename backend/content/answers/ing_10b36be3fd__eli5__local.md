Think of an AI system as a chef in a busy kitchen.  
**Inference** is the moment the chef looks at a plate (the input data) and decides what dish it is, what flavors are present, and how to serve it. The chef uses all the recipes they know (the model’s parameters) to make that judgment quickly.  

**Production** is when the chef actually cooks the dish for the customer—mixing ingredients, timing, plating, and delivering it on time. It’s the real‑world deployment of the recipe in a live setting.  

So, during an interview you might be asked: *“How do you handle inference?”* (the chef’s quick decision) or *“What steps are needed for production?”* (getting the dish onto the table reliably). Both involve the same underlying knowledge, but one is about making predictions and the other about putting those predictions into practice.
