Imagine you’re sending a recipe to a robot chef that only knows how to cook if the instructions are crystal‑clear and neatly separated.  
**Instruction Clarity** is like writing each step in plain, simple sentences—no vague “mix” or “add more.” The robot reads it straight away, no guessing needed.  
**Delimiters** are the commas, colons, bullet points, or even line breaks that tell the robot where one instruction ends and another begins. Think of them as traffic lights on a road: red means stop (the current step is finished), green means go (start the next step).  

In prompt engineering, you give an AI a “recipe” for answering questions. If you write it in clear sentences and use delimiters to separate prompts, conditions, and examples, the AI understands exactly what to do—just like the robot chef follows your perfectly organized recipe without missteps.
