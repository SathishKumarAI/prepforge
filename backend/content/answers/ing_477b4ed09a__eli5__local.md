Think of an **onsite interview loop** as a “restaurant tour” for a job candidate.  
When you arrive, the restaurant (the company) invites you to taste several dishes—each dish is a different interview with a team member who works on that part of the product.  

1. **First course:** A hiring manager asks about your background and why you’re interested.  
2. **Second course:** A senior engineer dives into technical questions, like coding or algorithm design.  
3. **Third course:** A data scientist reviews a real project you’ve worked on—showing how you turn raw data into insights.  
4. **Fourth course (optional):** You meet with product or design folks to see how your work would fit the bigger picture.  

After all courses, the kitchen staff (the interviewers) discuss whether you’d be a good fit and share their feedback. If everyone says “yes,” they’ll offer you the table—i.e., the job. This loop lets both sides get a full sense of each other before making a decision.
