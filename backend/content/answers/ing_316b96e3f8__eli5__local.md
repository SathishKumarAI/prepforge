Imagine you’re sending messages to your friend’s house, but instead of using a regular phone line (HTTP), you use a special, high‑speed walkie‑talker that instantly delivers what you say in the exact format they understand. That’s gRPC: a lightweight, fast communication system built on Google’s Protocol Buffers (a way to pack data neatly). In machine learning, your model runs on one computer while another program—say a web app—needs its predictions. gRPC lets those two programs talk quickly and reliably, sending small “messages” back and forth without the overhead of older methods like REST.
