Imagine a café that serves coffee. **Performance** is how fast each customer gets their drink when the shop is open—if the barista can hand out cups quickly, the wait time is short. **Scalability** is about what happens when more customers arrive: does the café keep serving them in the same quick way, or do lines grow longer?  

In system design, *performance* means low latency and high throughput for current users; it’s like a single barista working efficiently. *Scalability* means adding more baristas (servers) so that even with many customers, each still gets coffee fast—think of load balancing and horizontal scaling. A well‑designed system keeps both: it serves today’s traffic quickly and can grow without slowing down tomorrow.
