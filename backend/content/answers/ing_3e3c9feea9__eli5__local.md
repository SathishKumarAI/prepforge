Imagine a library where every book is written in a secret code that only the librarian can read. In AI, those “books” are **embedding models** – programs that turn words or images into long lists of numbers (embeddings) that capture their meaning. Open‑source ones let anyone look inside the librarian’s desk, see how the books were made, and even rewrite them.

These models fall into a simple taxonomy:

1. **Word embeddings** – like a dictionary that gives each word a number pattern.
2. **Sentence/paragraph embeddings** – like summarizing whole chapters into one number line.
3. **Image embeddings** – turning pictures into numeric fingerprints.
4. **Multimodal embeddings** – mixing text and images, as if combining a photo book with captions.
