**Cost Per Action – computer‑use agents**

Think of a robot waiter in a restaurant. The waiter’s job is to bring a dish (the *action*) to the table when you order it. You only pay for that one dish, not for the time the waiter sits idle or walks around. In digital advertising, a “computer‑use agent” (a software bot) works like that waiter: it watches a website and clicks a button, fills out a form, or completes a purchase—exactly the action advertisers want. The *Cost Per Action* (CPA) is the price you pay each time the bot successfully finishes one of those actions. It’s a way to measure how much you spend for every real conversion your site receives, just as you’d count how many meals a waiter delivers before deciding if the service costs worth it.
