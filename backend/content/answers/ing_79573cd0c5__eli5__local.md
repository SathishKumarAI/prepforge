**Prompting** is like giving the recipe’s starter: “Make me a salad with spinach, tomatoes, and feta.” The AI follows that instruction and produces something close to what you asked.

**Retrieval‑Augmented Generation (RAG)** is adding fresh ingredients on demand. The model looks up real documents, then stitches that information into its answer.

**Fine‑tuning** is like training the chef to specialize in Italian cuisine. You feed them many Italian recipes so they learn the flavor profile and can cook Italian dishes more naturally without extra instructions.
