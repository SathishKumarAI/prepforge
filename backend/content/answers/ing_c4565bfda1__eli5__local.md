**Conversational AI latency** is the pause between when you say something to a chatbot (or voice assistant) and when it replies. Think of it like ordering at a café: if the barista takes a long time to make your coffee, you feel frustrated even though the coffee itself tastes great. In the same way, a quick reply keeps a conversation natural; a lag makes it feel robotic or cut‑off.

Why it matters?  
1. **User experience** – People expect instant answers, just as they expect a phone call to ring back immediately.  
2. **Real‑time tasks** – Voice assistants that control lights or cars need near-zero delay to be useful and safe.  
3. **Business impact** – In customer support, long waits can push users away, hurting satisfaction scores.

So latency is the “wait time” between input and output; keeping it short keeps people engaged and systems reliable.
