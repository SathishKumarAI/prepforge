Think of a **priority queue** as a busy airport security line that lets passengers board the plane in the order that matters most to them.

1. **FIFO (First‑In‑First‑Out)** – like a regular line: the first person who arrives gets checked first, no one jumps ahead.
2. **LIFO (Last‑In‑First‑Out)** – imagine a stack of plates; you always take the top plate. The last person to arrive is served first.
3. **Priority Queue** – now the airport gives special treatment: people with urgent medical tickets, infants, or elite members get checked before others, regardless of arrival time.
4. **Circular Queue** – picture a revolving door that keeps moving; when it reaches the end, it starts again at the front, so no one gets stuck in the middle.

In all cases, you “enqueue” (add) people and “dequeue” (remove) them according to the chosen rule, but only the priority queue cares about each person’s importance level.
