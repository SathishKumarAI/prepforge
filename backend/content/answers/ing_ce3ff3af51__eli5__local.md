Imagine you’re reading a long novel in one sitting. Instead of trying to remember every single word, you pause at natural “story beats” – the moment a character enters a room, a new scene starts, or a key line is spoken. You jot down a quick note for each beat, capturing its main idea. That’s what semantic chunking does for AI: it slices text into meaningful chunks based on ideas or topics rather than just random word groups.

A “chunking strategy” decides where those beats fall—like choosing to break at every dialogue line, at every paragraph, or only when a new subject appears. By picking the right strategy, the AI can later ask, “What happened in chapter 2?” and pull the exact chunk that contains that story beat, just as you would recall the scene from your notes.
