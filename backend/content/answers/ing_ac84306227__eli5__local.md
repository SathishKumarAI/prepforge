A **greedy algorithm** works exactly like that: it makes the locally best choice at each step, hoping those choices will lead to an overall good solution.

In Neetcode’s “Greedy (8 Problems)” set, you’ll see this pattern in different puzzles—like picking the shortest path, scheduling the most jobs, or selecting the fewest coins.
