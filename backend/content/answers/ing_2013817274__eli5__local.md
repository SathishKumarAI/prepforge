**Lost‑in‑the‑Middle – Context Engineering**

If the hallway is too long and full of detours (a huge “context window”), the AI can get lost before it reaches you. *Context engineering* is the art of trimming that hallway: we pick only the most relevant rooms (pieces of text) and put them right in front of the AI, so it never has to wander through irrelevant corridors.
