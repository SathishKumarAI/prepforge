**Spec‑driven development for agentic AI** is like giving a robot a recipe book before it starts cooking.

1. **Recipe (specification)** – A clear list of what the AI should do, how it should behave, and which rules it must follow.  
2. **Chef (agentic AI)** – The AI learns from that recipe but can choose the exact steps to reach the goal, just like a chef decides whether to stir first or add spices later.

By writing the recipe first, developers know exactly what “success” looks like; the AI then explores different ways to finish the dish. This keeps the AI’s actions predictable while letting it act independently within the boundaries set by the spec. The result is safer, more reliable “agentic” behavior that still feels creative and flexible.
