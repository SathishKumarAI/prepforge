When an interviewer asks about an **LLM Gateway** or a **Serving Platform**, they’re usually probing how you’d let people talk to large language models (like ChatGPT) in real‑world apps.

Think of the gateway as a *smart receptionist* at a big office building. Clients (your app’s users) knock on the door, and the receptionist checks who they are, what questions they want answered, and routes them to the right room (the correct model). The receptionist also handles security—making sure only authorized visitors get in—and keeps an eye on how many people are inside at once.

The serving platform is the *office building itself*. It’s where all the rooms (models) live. The platform manages the computers that run each model, balances traffic so no single room gets overcrowded, and scales up by adding more rooms when demand rises.

So, interviewers want to see if you can design a receptionist that smartly directs traffic and an office that stays fast, secure, and expandable as usage grows.
