**Native Hybrid (Single‑System) Architecture**

Think of a smart robot that can both *think* like a brain and *act* like a muscle in one body. In this design the “brain” (the AI model, e.g., a neural network) is built into the same hardware that does the work (sensors, motors, processors). No separate server or cloud connection is needed; everything lives inside the device.

- **AI model** – the set of learned rules and patterns that decide what to do.  
- **Hardware** – the physical chips, sensors, and actuators that gather data and move things.  

Because the brain and body share a single system, the robot reacts instantly—no delay from sending data off‑site. It’s like a chef who can taste (sense) and cook (act) right in the kitchen without having to call an external assistant. This tight coupling gives fast, reliable performance but limits how much you can grow the model unless you upgrade the whole device.
