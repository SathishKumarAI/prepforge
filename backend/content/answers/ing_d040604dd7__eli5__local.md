Imagine a giant digital library that’s not organized by shelves or categories but by *tags*. Each book (file) is wrapped in a plastic envelope that carries its own label and a tiny barcode. That’s an object store: it keeps any kind of file—images, videos, logs, sensor readings—and lets you find them with a search key instead of a fixed location.

**Top 6 AI use‑cases**

1. **Training data vault** – Store millions of labeled images or text for model learning.  
2. **Raw data lake** – Keep every click, log, or sensor output before it’s cleaned.  
3. **Model artifacts archive** – Save trained models and checkpoints in one place.  
4. **Inference results cache** – Quickly retrieve past predictions to avoid re‑computing.  
5. **Backup & disaster recovery** – Protect critical AI data by replicating it across regions.  
6. **Streaming analytics buffer** – Buffer real‑time video or sensor streams until they’re processed.

Just like a library where you can pull out any book with its barcode, an object store lets AI systems grab exactly the file they need, no matter how big or varied.
