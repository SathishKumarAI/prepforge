Think of an AI system as a new student in a school that wants to know how well they can do different subjects before choosing a major. **Capability assessment** is the teacher’s test‑sheet: it asks the student (the AI) to solve a set of problems—math, language, vision, reasoning—and records their scores.

The “test‑sheet” has two parts:

1. **What the AI should be able to do** – like reading comprehension or recognizing faces.  
2. **How well it does each task** – measured by accuracy, speed, and how often it fails.

Just as a student’s report card tells parents where they need help, a capability assessment shows developers which parts of an AI are strong, which need improvement, and whether the system is ready for real‑world use. It’s a clear, objective snapshot that guides training, safety checks, and deployment decisions.
