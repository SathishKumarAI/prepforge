Imagine you’re writing a story together with friends, and everyone works on their own copy of the book.  
**Merge** is like taking all your friends’ chapters, cutting them out, and pasting them into one big book at once. The original chapter order stays the same, but you end up with an extra “chapter” that says *“Here we combined A and B.”* This keeps a clear record of who added what.

**Rebase** is like taking your friends’ chapters, copying their words, and then writing them into your own book as if you had written them from the start. The final book looks linear: “I wrote this first, then I added your parts next.” It hides the fact that you worked separately, making the story easier to read but also erasing the separate histories.

Use **merge** when you want a full audit trail; use **rebase** when you prefer a tidy, straight‑line history.
