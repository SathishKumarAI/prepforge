Imagine you’re at a big buffet that offers thousands of dishes, but only a few are relevant to your taste each time you sit down. **Just‑in‑Time (JIT) Loading** in AI is like the kitchen staff preparing and serving those dishes right when you order them instead of cooking everything ahead of time.

In an AI system, *context engineering* means shaping the little “story” that tells the model what to focus on—like giving it a recipe that lists only the ingredients you want. When the user asks a question, the system pulls in just the relevant data (the JIT loading) and feeds it into the model with that precise context.
