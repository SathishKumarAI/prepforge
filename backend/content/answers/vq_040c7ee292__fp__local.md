At its core, a book delivers *information*—a sequence of symbols that reduces uncertainty about some domain. The fundamental problem for any reader is to maximise this reduction while minimising physical effort (carrying weight). Thus, any device that can approximate this bound (e‑ink screens, e‑books) achieves the same informational value with orders of magnitude less mass.
