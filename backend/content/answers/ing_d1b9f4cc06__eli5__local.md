Think of a cloud data center as a giant apartment building.  
With **containers**, each app gets its own tiny, neatly boxed room inside a shared floor—many apps share the same walls but stay isolated by their boxes.  

**Isolates** are like giving each app its own separate floor entirely: no sharing any walls or plumbing with other floors at all. The building’s infrastructure (the elevators, heating, and electricity) still serves everyone, but each app runs on a distinct, private slice of the building that can be shut down, moved, or upgraded without touching anyone else’s space.

So, isolates give you the security and independence of separate apartments while still benefiting from the shared building services—exactly what Cloudflare offers for cloud computing without relying on container technology.
