**Conversation history management is like a kitchen’s pantry and recipe book for an AI agent.**

*Context* is the pantry: every ingredient (message) you put in now can be used immediately by the AI to cook up a reply. The more items it keeps on hand, the richer its answers become, but too many cans clutter the space.

*Memory engineering* is the recipe book: after cooking, the agent writes down only what matters—like “use 2 cups of tomatoes next time” or “avoid adding salt again.” This distilled information can be recalled later, even after the pantry is emptied.  

So, context supplies fresh data for a single conversation; memory turns that data into reusable knowledge for future chats, keeping the AI both quick and smart over time.
