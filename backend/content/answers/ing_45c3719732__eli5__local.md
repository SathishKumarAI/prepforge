Imagine you’re cooking a fancy dish for a picky guest. The recipe (the model) knows how to blend ingredients, but the taste depends on the table setting, lighting, and music—those are the *context*. Context engineering is like preparing that perfect setting: choosing the right props, adjusting the light, playing suitable background noise so the dish shines. In machine learning, context engineering means giving the AI extra clues about the situation before it makes a decision—like telling a translation model whether the text comes from a legal document or a casual chat. By shaping this backdrop, we help the model understand what “right” looks like in that specific scenario, leading to clearer, more reliable answers. It’s not changing the recipe itself; it’s making sure the environment is just right for it to work best.
