Think of an **agent** like a single, smart worker in a factory. It watches its surroundings (the “environment”), decides what to do next, and then acts—maybe picking up a part or turning on a light. Each agent is focused on one task at a time and can adapt when things change.

A **workflow** is the whole assembly line that shows how many workers, machines, and steps fit together to finish a product. It’s a plan: step 1, then step 2, maybe back‑and‑forth if something fails. The workflow tells the agents what order to work in and when to hand off items.
