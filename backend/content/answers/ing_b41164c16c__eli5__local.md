**Context & Memory Management**

Think of an AI like a very busy chef in a kitchen that never stops cooking.  
*Context* is the recipe sheet the chef keeps on the counter— it tells them what ingredients are currently being used, which dishes have been started, and what flavor profile the customer wants. If the chef looks at the wrong sheet, they’ll add salt instead of sugar or forget a step.

*Memory Management* is like the pantry and fridge: it decides how much space to keep for each ingredient, when to restock, and when to throw out spoiled items so the kitchen stays clean and ready for the next order. The AI keeps track of recent words (short‑term memory) and also stores useful patterns from past conversations (long‑term memory), deciding what to keep visible and what can be set aside.

Together, context lets the AI know *what* it’s doing right now, while memory management decides *how much history* it should remember so every new request feels natural without getting lost in clutter.
