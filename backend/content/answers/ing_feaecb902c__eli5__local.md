Imagine you’re training a dog to fetch a ball, but instead of just giving it a treat when it brings the ball home, you first show the trainer exactly how many treats the dog should get for each successful fetch. The trainer writes down that rule on a card and keeps it safe so no one can cheat by adding extra treats later.

Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) works the same way in AI. An agent learns to do tasks by receiving “rewards” for good actions, just like the dog gets treats. In RLVR, those rewards are written down on a transparent ledger that anyone can check—so no one can secretly change the reward scheme to favor the agent. This keeps learning honest and trustworthy, making sure the AI behaves as intended.
