Think of building a robot that learns from pictures like training a child to recognize shapes.  
**Machine‑Learning (ML)** is the school where the child practices by seeing many examples and getting feedback (“right” or “wrong”).  
**Deep Learning** is a special kind of ML that uses layered “neural networks” – tiny math circuits called *neurons* that pass signals forward, just like dominoes. Each layer learns a different feature: first edges, then shapes, then whole objects.  

Key ideas you’ll see on the cheat sheet:  
- **Data** (the pictures you give it).  
- **Model** (the network that guesses).  
- **Loss function** (how wrong the guess is; the teacher’s scorecard).  
- **Optimizer** (the way we adjust the model to reduce loss, like nudging a ball downhill).  
- **Epochs** (full passes through all data).  

With these terms in mind, you can read the cheat sheet as a quick recipe for turning raw images into a smart recognizer.
