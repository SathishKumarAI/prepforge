**Pitfall 16: Not Managing Time**

Imagine you’re baking a giant cake for a school fundraiser. If you don’t set timers and check on the oven, the cake might burn or stay raw—no one will taste it, and the whole event fails. In AI projects, “time” is just as critical. Developers often jump straight into coding without planning milestones, testing phases, or release dates. The result? Models that work only in theory, bugs that pile up, or deadlines missed, which can cost money, reputation, or even safety.

**Concrete fix:** Treat each model iteration like a slice of the cake: set clear checkpoints (data collection, training, validation, deployment), assign owners, and review progress regularly. By “time‑boxing” tasks, you keep the project on track and ensure the final product is ready when people need it.
