**Agent memory** is like the chef’s recipe book—everything learned from past meals (data and experience). The chef can flip back to any page when needed, but the book only stores what was written down; it doesn’t remember how the dish tasted until the chef tastes it again.  

**State** is the kitchen at a given moment: which ingredients are in the fridge, how many pans are on the stove, and whether the oven is pre‑heated. The state changes as the chef moves pots, adds spices, or turns off lights.  

When the AI (chef) decides what to do next—pick a recipe page, grab an ingredient, stir—it looks at its memory (recipe book) and the current state (kitchen snapshot). Together they guide actions that produce the final dish (output). This dynamic pairing lets the AI adapt while keeping track of past learning.
