Imagine your company’s information as a giant library.  
**Data architecture** is the blueprint that tells where each book goes, how shelves are arranged, and which librarian (or system) fetches what when you ask for it.  

In the DAMA‑DMBOK 2 framework, data architecture is the set of rules and diagrams that describe:  
- **What data exists** (the “books”) – its types, formats, and quality.  
- **Where it lives** (physical storage, cloud or on‑premise servers).  
- **How it moves** (flows between systems, pipelines, APIs).  
- **Who owns it** (responsibility for accuracy, privacy, and security).

Just as a library catalog helps you find a book quickly, a well‑designed data architecture lets people in the organization locate, trust, and use information efficiently, ensuring that every “book” is in the right place at the right time.
