Think of building an AI model like baking a cake.  
**Step 2 – “Define Evaluation Criteria”** is deciding how you’ll judge whether the cake turned out right.  
You pick simple, concrete tests: Is the texture light? Does it taste sweet enough? Did it rise evenly? These are your **evaluation criteria**—clear standards that tell you if the finished product meets your goal.

In AI, the “cake” is a trained algorithm, and the criteria become questions like:
- *Accuracy*: How often does it get the right answer?
- *Speed*: Does it respond quickly enough for users?
- *Fairness*: Is it unbiased across different groups?

You write these down as an **answer framework**—a checklist that every model must pass before you consider it ready. Just as a baker checks taste, rise, and texture, a data scientist tests accuracy, latency, and fairness to ensure the AI delivers what it promises.
