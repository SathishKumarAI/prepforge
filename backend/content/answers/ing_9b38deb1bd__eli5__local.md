**Contextual retrieval** is like looking for a forgotten recipe in your kitchen by first remembering the ingredients you already have. Instead of searching every cupboard blindly, you ask yourself, “What do I need right now?” and then only open the drawers that contain those items. In AI, the system first reads the whole document to understand its overall theme (the “ingredients”), then pulls out only the parts that answer a specific question (the “recipe”).  

**Late chunking** is the trick of waiting until after you know what you’re looking for before breaking the text into pieces. Think of it as slicing a cake only after you’ve decided which slice size you need, not chopping it up in advance. This saves time and keeps the search focused on relevant chunks, avoiding unnecessary processing.  

Together they let AI answer questions quickly and accurately by focusing on the right parts of a large text just when you need them.
