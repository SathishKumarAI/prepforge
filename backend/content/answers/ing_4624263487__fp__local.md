**Why an Enterprise MCP Knowledge Agent is Needed**

At its core an organization faces a *constraint‑optimization* problem: allocate limited resources (budget, staff, time) to achieve multiple, often conflicting, business goals (profit, compliance, innovation). Traditional planning systems treat each goal in isolation, leading to suboptimal allocations. A **Multi‑Criteria Planning (MCP) Knowledge Agent** reframes the problem as a *joint probability distribution* over all relevant variables—market signals, internal constraints, regulatory stances—so that every decision is evaluated under the same Bayesian framework.
