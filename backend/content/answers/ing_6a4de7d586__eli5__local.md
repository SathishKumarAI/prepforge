In machine learning, latency is the same idea: how long it takes for a model to look at new data (the ingredients), run its calculations (mixing and baking), and spit out a prediction (the finished pizza).  

When we talk about “latency numbers,” we’re looking at those times—often measured in milliseconds or seconds.
