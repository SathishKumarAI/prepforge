**Context is like the size of a notebook page.**  
When you ask an AI a question, it writes its answer on that page. The page can only hold so many lines—this limit is called the *context window*. Every word you give it (your prompt) and every word it produces (its reply) takes up space on that page. Once the page is full, the oldest words have to be erased or “pushed out” so new ones can fit. That’s why an AI can only remember a finite amount of conversation: its memory is literally limited by how many tokens—tiny pieces of text—it can keep at once. So context isn’t endless; it’s a hard, physical cap on what the model can see and use at any moment.
