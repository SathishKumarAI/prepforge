Imagine a bustling city where every shop (service) offers a different product—one sells coffee, another books, another tech gadgets. A *service discovery* system is like the city’s phone directory: when someone wants something, they look up which shop sells it and get the address.  

An **API Gateway** is the city’s main information desk at the subway station. Instead of asking each shop separately, you walk to the desk, tell it what you need (e.g., “I want a coffee and a book”), and the desk forwards your request to the right shops, collects their answers, and gives you everything in one package. It also handles extra chores: checking if you’re allowed to buy (authentication), translating different shop languages into one common language (protocol translation), or making sure no single shop gets overwhelmed by too many orders at once (rate‑limiting).  

So, service discovery finds the shops; the API Gateway is the friendly desk that coordinates and simplifies your shopping experience.
