Think of training an AI like teaching a child to speak a new language before they start school. **Pre‑training** is the time the child spends listening to many conversations, reading books, and watching videos in that language—without any teacher’s corrections yet. The goal is just to get comfortable with the sounds, grammar patterns, and common words.

**Training stability** is how steady that learning process stays. If the child hears a mix of clear, consistent examples (good data) and confusing or contradictory ones (noisy data), their vocabulary can become shaky—just as an AI model can start oscillating between different “opinions” about what a word means. A stable pre‑training phase gives the AI a solid foundation so later learning (fine‑tuning on specific tasks) is smoother, like a child who has mastered basic grammar before tackling advanced literature.
