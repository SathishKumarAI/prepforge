**Agentic Retrieval‑Augmented Generation (RAG)** is a way to make an AI that can *act* like a helpful assistant while also looking up fresh facts on the fly.

Think of it as a chef who has a pantry full of ingredients (the stored knowledge) and a recipe book (the language model). When you ask for a dish, the chef first checks the pantry for the right items. If something is missing or needs a special flavor, they quickly browse the recipe book to find the exact instructions. The chef then mixes everything together and serves a finished meal that’s both tasty and up‑to‑date.

In AI terms:
- **Agentic** means the system can decide what to do next (like choosing which ingredients to fetch).
- **Retrieval‑Augmented Generation** is the process of pulling relevant information from a database or internet source before generating an answer. This keeps responses accurate, current, and tailored to your question.
