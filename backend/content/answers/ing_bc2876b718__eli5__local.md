Think of a huge library that contains every book ever written, but each page is broken into tiny words called **tokens** (like “the”, “cat”, “runs”). A language‑model’s training is like a student who reads that entire library over and over, learning how the tokens usually follow one another.  

The *100 T Token Horizon* means the student looks back at about 100 trillion of those tokens while learning—a huge window into past context. That’s far more than most models can remember at once (they might only see a few thousand tokens). By training on this gigantic horizon, the model gets a broader sense of long‑term patterns—like how a story arc develops over chapters—so it can predict what comes next with far better “memory” and coherence.
