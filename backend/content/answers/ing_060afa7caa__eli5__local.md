**Step 4: Review the Output**

Think of an AI coder like a chef who follows a recipe (the code). After the kitchen’s “autocook” finishes, you taste the dish before serving it to guests.  
*Reviewing the output* means looking at the program the AI just wrote and checking that it does what you asked:  

- **Does the code run?** (Can you press “run” without crashing?)  
- **Are the results correct?** (Do the numbers or text match your expectations?)  
- **Is the style tidy?** (Are variable names clear, is indentation consistent?)  

If something tastes off—an error message, a wrong calculation, or a messy line—you tweak the recipe: adjust the prompt, add more examples, or fix the code manually. Then you let the AI cook again until the dish meets your taste buds.

*Unavoidable terms:*  
- **Prompt** – the instructions you give to the AI.  
- **Output** – the code or text the AI produces.  

Just like a chef checks before serving, reviewing the AI’s output ensures the final product is safe, accurate, and ready for use.
