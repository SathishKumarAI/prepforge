Think of an artist who can paint, write poetry, and play music all at once.  
When the artist is asked to “tell a story,” they don’t just use one medium; instead, they sketch scenes, write verses, and hum a tune that all fit together. That’s multimodal generation in AI: the system takes several kinds of input—text, images, sound—and produces several kinds of output at the same time.

**Key idea:**  
*Multimodal* means “multiple modes” (different forms of data). *Generation* is creating new content from those inputs.  

So a multimodal AI might read a photo of a beach and, while writing a description in English, also generate a short audio clip of waves. It blends visual, linguistic, and auditory signals to produce richer, more natural results—just like our artist who paints, writes, and plays simultaneously.
