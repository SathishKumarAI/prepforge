**Error analysis** is like a detective looking at why a smart assistant made a wrong answer.  
Imagine you ask a friend to sort a mixed pile of red, blue, and green marbles into three boxes. When the friend misplaces some marbles, you step back, open each box, and see which colors ended up where they shouldn’t be. You note patterns—maybe the friend keeps putting reds in the blue box when it’s cold, or confuses orange with red because they look similar.

In AI evaluation, we do the same: after a model answers many questions, we collect all the wrong ones and examine them carefully. We label each mistake (e.g., “misunderstood the question,” “missed a key fact,” “confused two terms”) to find recurring problems. This tells us where the system needs improvement—just as your friend would learn to watch out for reds in cold weather or to remember orange’s distinct shape. The goal is clear, actionable insight that turns errors into learning opportunities.
