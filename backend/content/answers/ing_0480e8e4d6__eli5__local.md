Think of building a machine‑learning system like planning a road trip.  
**Architecture patterns** are the proven routes you can take to get from point A (your data) to point B (useful predictions).  

- **Batch processing** is like taking a long, scenic drive: you collect all your stops first, then drive once, making the journey smooth and predictable.  
- **Real‑time streaming** is a fast highway run: cars (data points) keep coming in, and you must decide instantly which lane to take.  
- **Microservices** are separate carriages that each handle one task—like a GPS, a weather checker, or a fuel gauge—so if one breaks, the others keep moving.

Each pattern gives you a clear map for where data goes, how it’s processed, and who talks to whom, just as a good itinerary keeps your trip organized and on schedule.
