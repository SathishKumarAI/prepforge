Think of an AI as a librarian who can pull out the exact book you need, but only after you tell them what kind of story you’re looking for.  
When you ask a question, the AI first scans its “library” (all the text it has learned from) to find passages that match the *context* you gave—like the genre, tone, or specific facts in your prompt. Then, among those matched passages, it picks the one that best fits the exact wording of your request.  

So, contextual retrieval is like asking a librarian: “I want a mystery set in 1920s Paris with a detective who loves jazz.” The librarian first finds all mysteries from that era and setting, then chooses the one whose plot details match your clues. It’s not just looking for any mystery; it looks for the mystery that fits every piece of context you supplied.
