**Cascade speech‑to‑speech** is like each friend telling the story in their own voice first, then another friend rewrites it in a new voice for everyone to hear. One “translator” turns your words into text (speech‑to‑text), a second “writer” crafts a reply (text‑to‑text), and a third “voice actor” reads that reply aloud (text‑to‑speech).
