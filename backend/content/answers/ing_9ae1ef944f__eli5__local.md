Think of a huge library (the big AI model) that knows a lot but is heavy and slow to use.  

**Distillation** is like taking the most useful books from that library, summarizing them into a slimmer book, and giving it to a student who can read quickly while still getting the key ideas.

**Synthetic data** is when the student writes their own practice questions instead of copying real ones—filling in gaps so they learn patterns the original library didn’t cover.  

**Fine‑tuning** is the student reading those new practice questions and adjusting their notes to match a specific exam style (e.g., focusing on math).  

**Alignment** is making sure the student’s answers follow the teacher’s values—so the model behaves politely, safely, and in line with what users want.  

Together, they let a lightweight AI answer like a well‑trained expert while staying trustworthy.
