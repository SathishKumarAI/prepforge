**How Password Managers Work – Steps 3‑5**

Think of your password manager as a *digital safe* in a bank.  
Step 3: **Master key** – you choose one strong password (the master). This is the only secret you remember; it unlocks the vault.  
Step 4: **Encrypting the contents** – every site’s username and password are turned into unreadable code by a computer algorithm that uses your master key. The bank never sees the original passwords, just the encrypted data.  
Step 5: **Retrieving on demand** – when you visit a website, the manager asks for your master password once more, unlocks the vault in memory, and automatically fills in the right login fields. It’s like pulling out a specific deposit box with a key that only you have.
