Imagine you’re building a smart robot that learns from the world—just like any machine‑learning model. **“Have Backbone; Disagree and Commit”** means the team must feel free to challenge ideas, but once a decision is made they all pull together.

Think of it as a group of chefs in a kitchen. If one chef thinks a sauce needs more salt, she can loudly voice that concern—her “backbone.” The head chef listens, weighs the evidence, and may decide to keep the recipe unchanged or tweak it. After the decision, every chef, even those who disagreed, sticks to the chosen recipe, preparing dishes with full enthusiasm. In ML projects, this keeps ideas healthy while ensuring the model is trained, deployed, and maintained consistently by everyone.
