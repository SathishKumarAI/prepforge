The director must therefore *solve a constrained portfolio selection problem* at scale: identify high‑value segments, design product bundles that satisfy both profitability and regulatory constraints, and orchestrate cross‑functional teams (compliance, risk, tech) to deliver those bundles.

- **Information asymmetry** between financial institutions and corporate clients is asymmetric; the director must distill market signals into actionable insights—an application of *Bayesian inference* in a high‑stakes environment.  
- **Network effects**: large enterprises often own multiple subsidiaries, so selling a “suite” yields incremental cross‑sell opportunities, a classic case of *economies of scope*.  

A non‑obvious insight: the *sales cycle length* in finance is less a function of product complexity and more a reflection of internal decision‑making topology (e.g., board approvals).
