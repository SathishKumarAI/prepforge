When a hiring pipeline is engineered around *data*, every touchpoint must be a data source that can be modeled and optimized. The “AI‑native interview” is the last experimental valve before an offer: it turns qualitative human judgment into a learnable decision function.

**Why it works:**  
1. **Signal amplification** – A structured AI interview collects thousands of micro‑behaviors (speech rate, pause length, lexical diversity). These features are far more granular than a recruiter’s intuition and can be weighted by a trained classifier.  
2. **Bias mitigation** – By constraining the questions to a fixed set and using algorithmic scoring, we reduce *human* variability that often propagates systemic bias downstream.  
3. **Feedback loop closure** – The interview’s outcome (score) feeds back into the model used in earlier stages (resume screening, video analysis). Thus the system learns which signals actually predict job success.

Because human recruiters are prone to over‑confidence on rare, high‑variance candidates, the AI model can downweight such outliers, thereby improving overall predictive accuracy without sacrificing diversity.
