* **SDK** (Software Development Kit) is just a toolbox full of ready‑made parts so developers don’t start from scratch.  
* **Autogen** means the system can automatically generate and fine‑tune these robot chefs’ recipes, learning what works best for your questions.  
* A **Crewai** is a group of those robot chefs that talk to each other, split tasks, and combine their outputs into one polished dish (the final answer).
