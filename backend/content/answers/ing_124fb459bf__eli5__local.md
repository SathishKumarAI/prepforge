Imagine building a smart robot that learns to sort colored blocks.  
**Evaluation** is like the teacher who checks each block after the robot sorts it: “Did you pick the right color?” The robot’s performance score (accuracy, speed, fairness) tells us how well it learned and whether we should tweak its training.

**Observability** is the set of cameras and sensors that let us watch the robot in real time. We can see which parts of its brain (the algorithm) fired, track data flow, and spot hidden bugs—just like watching a video feed to catch a misstep before it repeats.

In AI frameworks, *evaluation* gives the final grades; *observability* provides live, transparent insight into how those grades were earned. Together they ensure models not only score high but also behave predictably and safely.
