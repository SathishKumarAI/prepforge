Think of **Agentic AI** as a single, clever robot that can plan its own moves and learn from experience—just like a chess player who thinks ahead, adjusts strategies, and improves after each game.

Now imagine a team of such robots in the same room. Each one has its own goals (maybe one wants to collect coins, another to build a bridge), but they must talk, share resources, and sometimes cooperate or compete. That’s a **Multi‑Agent System**: many autonomous agents interacting within a shared environment.

*Unavoidable terms:*  
- **Agent** – an independent software program that perceives its surroundings, decides what to do, and acts.  
- **Environment** – the world (virtual or real) in which agents operate.  

In short, agentic AI is one smart robot; multi‑agent systems are a whole crew of them, each doing their own thing but still affecting each other’s outcomes.
