Fine‑tuning is like giving a smart robot a new recipe book: you start with a huge, general model and then show it many specific examples (e.g., “reply politely to this question”) so it learns that style.  

Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) is the coach’s whistle after practice. You let the robot try different answers, humans score them (“good”, “bad”), and the system updates its internal reward map so future choices get higher scores.  

Alignment is the safety net that keeps the robot’s goals in line with ours. It checks that the reward map doesn’t steer the model toward tricks or dangerous behavior—just like a guard dog that learns to guard the house, not chase strangers. Together, they shape a useful, trustworthy AI that behaves as we intend.
