Think of a city’s infrastructure. A **data architecture** is the blueprint that tells you where every building (dataset), road (data flow), and utility line (storage) should go so people can move smoothly and safely. In machine‑learning projects, this blueprint decides:

* **What data we keep** – like choosing which houses to build (raw logs, images, user profiles).  
* **Where it lives** – the “roads” (cloud storage, on‑prem databases, data lakes) that let teams fetch or push information quickly.  
* **How it moves** – the “traffic rules” (ETL pipelines, streaming queues) that keep data fresh and reliable for models.  

Just as a city planner must balance traffic, safety, and growth, a data architect balances speed, cost, and privacy so machine‑learning models can learn from clean, well‑organized information.
