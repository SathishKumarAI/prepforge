**Pattern 3 – “Vision‑First (Page‑as‑Image)” for multimodal RAG**

Imagine you’re a detective looking at a crime scene photo instead of reading the written report first. The image is your *first clue*; it tells you where people are, what objects look like, and how they’re arranged. Your brain then pulls in background knowledge—facts about cars, street signs, or clothing—to fill in gaps. In multimodal RAG (Retrieval‑Augmented Generation), the system does exactly that: it treats a scanned page, a screenshot, or a diagram as an image, “reads” it with computer vision, and uses what it sees to fetch relevant text snippets from a database. These snippets are then blended back into the answer, just like you’d combine visual evidence with written testimony. The result is richer, more accurate responses that respect both the picture and the words.
