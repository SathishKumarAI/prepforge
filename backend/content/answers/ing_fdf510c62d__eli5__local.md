Imagine building a smart library assistant that can answer any question you throw at it. The **functional requirements** are the clear, concrete rules telling us what this assistant must do.

1. **Listen and Understand** – like a librarian who first reads your request carefully to know exactly what book you need.  
2. **Find the Right Information** – the system searches its vast “catalog” (the trained model) for the best answer.  
3. **Speak Clearly** – it must write or say the answer in plain, friendly language.  
4. **Keep Learning** – after each conversation, it notes what worked and what didn’t, so future answers improve.  
5. **Stay Safe** – it must never reveal private data or give harmful advice, just like a librarian following privacy rules.

These rules ensure ChatGPT behaves predictably, safely, and helpfully—just as a good library assistant would.
