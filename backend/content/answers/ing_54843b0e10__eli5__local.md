Imagine a recipe book where every ingredient is a tiny “weight” that tells the kitchen what to do. In AI, those ingredients are the words you give the model—your *prompt*.  
The **Prompt as Weight** idea says: instead of hard‑coding how the model behaves (like setting the oven temperature forever), we let the prompt itself act like a dial that adjusts the model’s “taste” for each task.  

When you tweak the prompt, it changes the internal balance of these weights just enough to make the AI answer in the style or detail you want—just as adding more salt shifts a dish’s flavor.  
Prompt optimization is simply dialing that prompt‑weight dial until the output tastes exactly right.
