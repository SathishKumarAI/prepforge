**Step 4 – “Scale the Design”**

Imagine you’re building a new playground for kids. First you sketch the layout (the “design”). Then you test it with one child to see if the swings and slides fit comfortably—that’s your prototype. **Scaling** is like expanding that playground so every child in town can play at once. You keep the same basic plan, but add more swings, longer slides, and extra safety fences. In machine‑learning systems, scaling means adding more servers or increasing storage so the model can handle many users or larger data sets without slowing down. It’s not about changing how the playground works; it’s about giving it enough space and power to serve everyone safely and efficiently.
