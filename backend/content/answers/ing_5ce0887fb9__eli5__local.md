Think of an AI “agent” like a tiny robot helper that works for you every month.  
**The money you pay is split into three parts:**

1. **Hardware “rent.”**  
   This is the cost to keep the robot’s brain (the server) running, just as you would pay rent for a house where the robot lives. It covers electricity, cooling and the physical machine.

2. **Software “subscription.”**  
   Like buying a library card that lets the robot read new books every week, this fee gives the agent access to updated models, training data, and any premium features (e.g., faster response or more memory).

3. **Maintenance “labor.”**  
   Even a good robot needs a little hand‑on care—monitoring performance, fixing bugs, and tweaking settings. This part is the human effort that keeps the agent reliable.

So each month you pay for the place it stays, the knowledge it uses, and the people who keep it running smoothly.
