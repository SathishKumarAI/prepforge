### What “Agentic” Means for AI (2026)

At its core **agenticity** is the *ability of a system to act purposefully in an environment*—to decide what to do, why it matters, and how to adjust when things change.  
In 2026 this translates into three intertwined properties:

| Property | Why It Must Hold | What It Looks Like |
|----------|------------------|--------------------|
| **Intentionality** | Without a goal, the system can’t prioritize or learn efficiently. | An AI that frames “improve user retention” as a measurable objective and breaks it into sub‑tasks. |
| **Autonomy of Decision‑Making** | Human oversight alone would bottleneck scaling; decisions must be made in real time. | A model that selects its own data augmentation strategy during training, without human prompt each epoch. |
| **Self‑Regulation & Adaptation** | Environments drift; the agent must detect and correct bias or performance decay. | Continuous monitoring of fairness metrics, with automatic policy updates when thresholds slip. |

#### Non‑obvious Insight  
Most people think *agentic* is just “self‑learning.” The deeper requirement is **explicitly modeling a utility function that can be optimized under uncertainty**. In practice this means the agent must maintain a probabilistic belief over *why* it’s making a choice (e.g., expected revenue vs. compliance risk) and trade off those utilities in real time—a blend of Bayesian decision theory and reinforcement learning.
