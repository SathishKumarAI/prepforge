Imagine building a robot helper in your kitchen.  
First, decide **what job it will do** – chopping veggies or brewing coffee? That’s the *goal*.  
Next, tell it where to find ingredients and tools; that’s its *environment*—the kitchen layout, cupboards, stove.  
Then give it a clear *plan*: “If the vegetables are too big, cut them smaller.” This is the *policy*, the set of rules it follows.  
You also need a way for it to **measure success**—does the dish taste good? That’s its *reward function*.  
Finally, decide how much it can change itself over time; if you let it learn from each batch, it will improve.  

The checklist asks: What goal? Where does it act? How does it decide next step? How do we know it succeeded? And can it adapt? Answering these keeps your kitchen‑robot (or AI agent) focused, safe, and useful.
