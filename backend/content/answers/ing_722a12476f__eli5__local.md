Think of your data as a precious recipe book.  
**Backup** is like making photocopies of that book and keeping them in a safe drawer. Whenever you lose the original—say it’s misplaced or torn—you can simply flip to the copy and continue cooking.  

**Disaster recovery** is what you do if something catastrophic happens, such as a fire destroying your kitchen entirely. You don’t just need copies; you need a whole backup plan: a spare kitchen, an alternate location, a way to bring the recipe book (or its digital version) into that new space and get cooking again.  

So, backup = “copy for everyday loss.” Disaster recovery = “full strategy to rebuild after a major event.” Both protect information, but disaster recovery is broader, planning for the worst‑case scenario.
