Think of a bakery that makes cookies.  
**Batch processing** is like the baker waiting until all dough has risen, then baking every batch at once in a big oven. The process runs, finishes, and only then does the baker check how many cookies came out, taste them, and decide if any changes are needed. It’s efficient for large orders but you can’t get results until the whole run is finished.

**Stream processing** is like having a conveyor belt that brings dough to a small oven one piece at a time. The baker tastes each cookie immediately, adjusts seasoning on the fly, and keeps the line moving without waiting for a full batch.  

So, batch = “cook everything first, then evaluate”; stream = “evaluate as you go.” Batch is great when you have lots of data that can be processed together; stream shines when you need instant feedback or real‑time updates.
