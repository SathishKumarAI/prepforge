Think of an AI system as a busy kitchen. The chef (your model) cooks dishes (answers), but you need to know how the food is being prepared: what ingredients were used, how long each step took, and whether any spices went wrong. An **AI observability platform** is like a smart kitchen dashboard that watches every pot, stove, and ingredient in real time.

It records:

- *What data* the model saw (the “ingredients”),
- *How it processed* that data (the cooking steps),
- *When something goes off track* (a burnt sauce or missing spice).

For large language models (LLMs) and Retrieval‑Augmented Generation (RAG) apps, this means logging prompts, responses, and the external knowledge pulled in. The platform then lets you trace a problem back to its source—just as a kitchen monitor would show which burner caused a flare‑up—so you can fix bugs, improve performance, and keep the AI “meal” safe and tasty.
