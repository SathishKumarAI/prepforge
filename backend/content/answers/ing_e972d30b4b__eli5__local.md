**Fine‑tuning, RLHF and Alignment – a kitchen recipe**

Imagine you have a basic “soup” (the large AI model) that can cook many dishes but needs a taste test to become delicious for your family.

* **Fine‑tuning** is like adding a handful of spices from a favorite cookbook. You give the soup a small, specific set of examples (“this recipe works well”) so it learns those flavors quickly without changing its whole cooking style.

* **RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback)** is the tasting session. People sample the soup and rate how good it tastes. The model uses these ratings as a guide to adjust its “cooking policy” – it keeps what earns high scores and discards what doesn’t, learning which ingredients produce the best outcome.

* **Alignment** is the overall goal: ensuring the soup’s taste matches your family’s preferences (ethical safety, no harmful content). It’s like setting the rule that the soup must never be too salty or contain forbidden ingredients. Together, fine‑tuning gives it a starting flavor, RLHF polishes it with human judgment, and alignment guarantees it stays on course.
