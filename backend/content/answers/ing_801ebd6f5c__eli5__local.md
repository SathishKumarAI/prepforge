A **behavioral interview** is like the kitchen’s tasting menu: instead of asking you to whip up code on the spot, they ask you about past recipes you’ve cooked. Typical questions map to kitchen scenarios:  
- “Tell me about a time you debugged a hard bug.” (How you trace the issue)  
- “Describe a project where you had to collaborate across departments.” (Teamwork)  
- “Give an example of when you learned something new quickly.” (Adaptability)

Each answer should follow the **STAR** method: *Situation*, *Task*, *Action*, *Result*. Think of it as describing the dish, the goal, your steps in the kitchen, and how diners reacted.
