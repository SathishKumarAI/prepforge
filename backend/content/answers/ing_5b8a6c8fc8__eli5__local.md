Imagine you’re a chef who wants to serve the best pizza every day.

**Continuous Integration (CI)** is like mixing all your dough, sauce, cheese and toppings in one big pot before cooking. Every time someone adds a new ingredient (code change), it’s stirred right away so everyone can taste it immediately. The pot is tested for flavor and texture; if something goes wrong, the chef fixes it before moving on.

**Continuous Delivery/Deployment (CD)** takes that mixed pizza and puts it into the oven automatically. Once the dough is ready, the oven runs on a schedule, baking each batch without anyone having to start it manually. If you choose “delivery,” the finished pizza is sent out to customers right away; if you pick “deployment,” it’s just prepared in your kitchen for later use.

So, CI keeps the recipe (code) consistent and ready by constantly blending new ingredients, while CD ensures that once blended, the pizza is baked, tested, and shipped without human intervention.
