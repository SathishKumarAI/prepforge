Think of a chatbot as a very fast‑talking waiter in a busy restaurant.  
When you ask it a question, the waiter has to listen (you speak), think about what dish to recommend (the AI processes your words), and then speak back the answer.  

**Latency** is simply how long that whole cycle takes from the moment you finish speaking until you hear the reply. If the waiter pauses too long, you feel stuck; if it’s instant, the conversation feels natural.

In technical terms, latency measures the time between receiving your input and delivering a response. Lower latency means the AI feels more human‑like, while higher latency can make interactions feel sluggish or robotic. It’s like the difference between a waiter who instantly returns with your order and one who takes minutes to bring it back.
