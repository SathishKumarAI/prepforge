Imagine you’re learning a new language by studying a whole textbook (batch) versus listening to a live podcast that streams in real time (stream).  

**Batch processing**: The model looks at a *fixed set* of data all at once, learns from it, and then moves on. It’s like reading the entire book before you speak—complete, but slower, and you can’t react until the next session.  
**Stream processing**: The model receives data piece by piece as it arrives, updates its knowledge instantly, and keeps going. Think of chatting with a native speaker who corrects you on the spot—fast, adaptive, but each correction only uses what’s in front of you.

In machine learning, batch training trains on stored datasets; stream training (online learning) continuously learns from incoming data. Batch gives deep, stable models; streaming offers real‑time adaptability.
