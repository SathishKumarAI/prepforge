**Data engineering** is like building a big library where every book (data) has to be in the right place so people can find it quickly.  
Just as libraries must follow rules about who can borrow books and how long they keep them, data engineers must obey **compliance requirements**—laws that tell them how to collect, store, protect, and share information.

*Compliance* means following legal or regulatory rules (for example, GDPR in Europe or HIPAA for health records).  
When a company handles personal or sensitive data, the engineer must:

1. **Collect only what is needed** – like asking permission before borrowing a book.
2. **Store it safely** – using encryption (locking books) so outsiders can’t read them.
3. **Track who accesses it** – keeping a log of every “borrower” for accountability.
4. **Delete or anonymize data when no longer required** – removing personal details so the library stays clean.

If these steps are skipped, the company risks fines, lawsuits, and loss of trust—just as a careless librarian can lose patrons’ confidence.
