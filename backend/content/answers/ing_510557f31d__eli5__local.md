Think of an AI project as a kitchen where you want to bake a cake that needs several steps—mixing dough, baking, frosting, and plating.  
In **multi‑agent AI**, each step is handled by its own “chef” (an agent). One chef mixes the batter, another heats the oven, a third spreads icing, and a fourth arranges the final plate. They talk to one another, share their progress, and adjust if something goes wrong.  

A **subagent** is like a sous‑chef inside a bigger chef’s station. For example, the frosting agent might have its own subagents: one that measures sugar, another that stirs, and another that checks color. Each subagent focuses on a tiny part of its parent’s job.  
Together, agents and subagents form a team that can tackle complex tasks by dividing work into clear, communicable pieces—just like a well‑coordinated kitchen crew.
