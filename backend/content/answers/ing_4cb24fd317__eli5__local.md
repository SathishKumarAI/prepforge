Think of a multimodal model as a chef who can cook a full meal from many different ingredients—spices, vegetables, meats, and sauces—all at once.  
The “ingredients” are the *modalities*: images (visuals), text (words), audio (sound), or even video. The chef’s recipe book is the model’s learned knowledge, telling it how each ingredient tastes alone and how they combine to create a dish.  

When you give the chef a picture of a sunset and a sentence “It feels calm,” the model blends the visual pattern with the emotional word meaning, producing an answer that captures both what you see and how you feel—just like a meal that satisfies your sight and taste at the same time.

**Unavoidable term:**  
*Modality* – a type of data (e.g., text or image) that the model can read.
