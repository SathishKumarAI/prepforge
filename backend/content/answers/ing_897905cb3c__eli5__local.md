Imagine you’re a chef with a huge, fancy kitchen (the big language model) that can cook almost any dish but takes forever to prepare each meal. Distillation is like hiring a sous‑chef who watches the master cook and learns how to make the same dishes much faster, using fewer ingredients and less time.

In AI, the “big kitchen” is a large language model (LLM) trained on massive data—very powerful but slow and expensive to run. The “sous‑chef” is a smaller model that learns from the big one by mimicking its answers. During training, the small model sees pairs of prompts and the big model’s responses; it adjusts itself until its replies are almost identical but with far fewer parameters.

The result: a lightweight LLM that runs quickly on ordinary hardware while still delivering high‑quality language skills—just like your sous‑chef can serve tasty food in minutes.
