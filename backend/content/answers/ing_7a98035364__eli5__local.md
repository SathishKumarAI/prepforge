Imagine a QR code as a *digital address book card* you hand out at a coffee shop.

**Static QR** is like writing the same name and phone number on every card. No matter how many customers scan it, they always get the same “hello” message—your fixed business details or a single payment link that never changes. It’s simple but can’t adapt to new orders or different amounts.

**Dynamic QR** is like having a smart card that updates its information each time you press a button. When a customer scans it, the code pulls fresh data from your server: the exact amount, order ID, and even a personalized thank‑you note. It’s flexible, secure, and can handle multiple transactions without creating new codes.

So, in payment systems, static QR is one fixed link; dynamic QR is a living link that changes with each scan.
