Think of an AI as a smart robot that can answer questions or do tasks.  
**Closed‑API** is like renting the robot from a company: you call it over the internet, pay a fee, and the company controls its code, updates, and how much data you can send. You’re safe but can’t look inside the robot’s “brain” or change its behavior.  

**Open Self‑Hosted** is like buying a blueprint and building the robot yourself in your own garage. The source code is public, so you can inspect every part, tweak it to fit your needs, and run it on your own hardware. You pay for electricity and maintenance instead of a monthly fee, but you get full control and privacy.

Both let you use AI, but one trades flexibility for convenience; the other gives freedom at the cost of more hands‑on work.
