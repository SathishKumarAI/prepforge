Imagine a busy kitchen where chefs keep adding ingredients to a pot while the soup cooks. If you only taste the soup after it’s finished, you miss how each new ingredient changes its flavor in real time. Machine learning that “processes data at rest” is like tasting that final soup—everything is already cooked and frozen together.  

Real‑time or “in motion” processing treats the soup as a live stream: every spoonful of fresh milk, a pinch of salt, or a new spice is examined immediately. The model learns on the fly, adjusting its recipe as ingredients arrive, so it can instantly suggest the next best seasoning or predict when the dish will be ready.

**Key terms:**  
- **Data at rest** – information stored and processed only after collection.  
- **Streaming data** – continuous flow of information that is analyzed as it arrives.
