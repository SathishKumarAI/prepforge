Think of an AI like a smart kitchen robot that can cook any recipe it’s given.  
**Input guardrails** are the “ingredients list” rules: they check what the user feeds into the robot—no toxic chemicals, no instructions that could make the robot do something illegal or harmful.  
**Output guardrails** are the “final dish” rules: once the robot has cooked, these filters look at the finished food to make sure it’s safe, respectful, and not misleading before it leaves the kitchen.

You need both because a good ingredient list can’t stop the robot from accidentally mixing something wrong in its cooking process, just as a perfect recipe can still produce a bad dish if the cooking steps go astray. Together they keep the whole system—from what you give to what it gives back—safe and reliable.
