Think of an AI agent as a traveler exploring a city.  
**State** is the traveler’s current situation—what street they’re on, how many coins they have, whether they’re carrying a map. It tells the agent “here I am right now.”  

**Memory** is the notebook the traveler keeps in their backpack. Every time they pass a landmark or pick up a coin, they jot it down so they can look back later and decide what to do next. Memory holds information that isn’t part of the immediate state but influences future choices.

Together, state gives the agent a snapshot of the present, while memory stores past experiences that help shape smarter actions as the journey continues.
