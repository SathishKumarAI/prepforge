Think of an integration runtime as a “delivery truck” that moves data from one warehouse to another.  
* **On‑premises truck** – runs inside your own office (like a local server). It can pick up files stored on your internal network, but it can’t drive outside your building unless you open a gate.  
* **Cloud‑based truck** – lives in the sky (a cloud service). It can pick up anything that’s already online and drop it wherever you want in the cloud, no matter where the data started.  
* **Hybrid truck** – has two engines: one for the office road and one for the highway. It can start on your local network, then hop onto the cloud to finish the journey, keeping a secure bridge between the two places.

Each type is chosen by where your data lives, how fast you need it moved, and how much control you want over the route.
