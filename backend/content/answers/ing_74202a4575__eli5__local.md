**Runway** in an AI‑company interview isn’t a runway on a plane—it’s the *time you have left before your model or product fails*.

Imagine a bakery that must finish its daily bread before midnight. Each loaf takes time to bake, and customers will leave if it’s still in the oven after midnight. The “runway” is how many hours of baking remain until the deadline. In AI terms, it’s the period between when you launch a new model or feature and when you’ll run out of data, compute power, funding, or user interest.

Interviewers ask about runway to see if you can:
1. **Plan** how long your project will last.
2. **Prioritize** tasks that keep the bakery (or AI product) alive.
3. **Spot risks** that might shorten the time before “midnight.”  

So, think of runway as a countdown clock: you need to know when it hits zero and what you can do to extend it.
