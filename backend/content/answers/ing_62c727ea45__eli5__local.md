Imagine your computer is a castle and the data it learns from is its treasure. **Defenses** are guards that stop bad guys (hackers) from sneaking in or stealing treasure.

1. **Firewalls** – Like a moat: they let only trusted travelers through, blocking unknown packets that might carry malware.  
2. **Encryption** – Think of it as a secret code written on the walls; even if someone breaks in, they can’t read the messages unless they know the key.  
3. **Authentication (passwords, biometrics)** – The castle gate only opens for people who prove their identity with a key or fingerprint.  
4. **Intrusion Detection Systems** – Sensors that chirp when an intruder tries unusual moves—like a guard spotting a thief in the night.  
5. **Patch Management** – Regularly repairing cracks so attackers can’t use them later.

Together, these layers make it hard for cyber‑criminals to access or tamper with your machine‑learning treasure.
