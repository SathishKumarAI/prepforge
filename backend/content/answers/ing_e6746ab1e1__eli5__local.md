**Time to First Byte (TTFB)** is like the pause you feel when you order a pizza online and have to wait for the first slice to arrive before you can taste anything else.

*Imagine you call a pizza place, give your address, and then sit in silence.*  
The **TTFB** measures how long it takes from that moment until the *first bite of dough* (the very first piece of data) lands on your screen.  

In website terms, TTFB is the time between sending an HTTP request and receiving the first byte of the server’s response. It tells you how quickly the server can start delivering content, which matters because if that first bite takes too long, the whole page feels sluggish. Like a pizza that arrives cold, a high TTFB means users wait longer before seeing anything useful. Keeping it low is essential for a fast‑feeling site.
