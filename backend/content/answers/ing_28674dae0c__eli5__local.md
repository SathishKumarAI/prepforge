**Hybrid Search** is like looking for a friend in a big city by using two tools at once: a map and a phone call.

*Map (vector search)* shows you where people with similar interests live—think of it as finding “near‑by” neighborhoods that match your friend’s hobbies.  
*Phone call (keyword search)* asks the exact name or address, giving precise hits.

A hybrid system first narrows the field with the map (finding likely clusters) and then checks each cluster with a phone call to confirm the exact match. This way you get both speed—because you’re only looking in relevant neighborhoods—and accuracy—because you still verify the exact details. The result is a faster, smarter search that feels almost instantaneous.
