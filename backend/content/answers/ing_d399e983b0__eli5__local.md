Think of an AI system as a garden that keeps growing better with each season.  
At the bottom, you plant seeds (data and code) but only water them when something breaks—this is **Ad Hoc**: fixes are random and hard to repeat.  
Next, you set up a simple irrigation schedule (basic monitoring), so problems trigger a single alarm; that’s **Reactive**: you notice issues after they happen.  

Now imagine installing a smart sprinkler that watches the soil moisture, predicts when plants will need water, and automatically adjusts itself—this is **Proactive**.  
Finally, you add a sensor network that learns from every watering cycle, refines its predictions, and even tells you how to prune for better yield; that’s **Predictive**.  

The “Maturity Ladder” climbs these stages. Loop Engineering means each level adds a closed‑loop: data → analysis → action → new data, so the AI garden keeps improving without human hand‑tuning every turn.
