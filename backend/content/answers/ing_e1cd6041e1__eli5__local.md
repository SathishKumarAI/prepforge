**The Retrieval Boundary is like a librarian who knows exactly which books to pull from the library’s shelf for each question you ask.**

Think of your AI as a giant, ever‑growing library (its *memory*). Every time it learns something new, that knowledge gets stored in a specific “section” of the library. When you give the AI a prompt (*context engineering*), you’re essentially telling the librarian what kind of books you want—maybe science fiction or history.

The *retrieval boundary* is the rule that decides which shelves to look at and how many pages to read before answering. It prevents the AI from scrolling through every book (which would be slow) and keeps it focused on the most relevant information. So, just as a good librarian quickly finds the right volume for your question, the retrieval boundary lets the AI fetch only what matters, making answers faster and more accurate.
