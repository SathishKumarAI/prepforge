Imagine you’re baking a cake that needs to taste the same every time and can be made quickly whenever someone orders it. In machine learning, that “cake” is a predictive model, and the kitchen where it’s baked is your cloud environment.

**Continuous delivery** means you keep updating the recipe (the code) and automatically test it so new versions reach customers without manual hand‑offs. Think of it as a robot chef that, whenever a new ingredient arrives, adds it, tastes the batter, and if it passes quality checks, puts the cake on the shelf.

**Automation pipelines** are the step‑by‑step recipe instructions: gather data, train the model, evaluate its performance, package it, and deploy it to production—all without human intervention. The cloud provides the ovens (compute resources), storage (data warehouses), and monitoring tools that keep everything running smoothly.

Together, continuous delivery and automation pipelines let machine learning models be updated, tested, and released reliably, just like a well‑run bakery that always delivers fresh cakes on demand.
