**Fine‑tuning and adaptation** are ways to make a general AI model—think of it as a blank sketch artist—draw exactly what you want.

Imagine the model is a master chef who knows how to cook every dish in the world, but you only need him to bake a perfect chocolate cake. Fine‑tuning is like giving that chef a handful of your favorite recipes and letting him practice just those cakes until his technique matches yours. Adaptation is similar, but instead of retraining from scratch, you give the chef a quick cheat sheet (a few example cakes) so he can adjust on the fly without re‑learning everything.
