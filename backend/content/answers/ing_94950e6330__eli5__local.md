Imagine an AI helper as a chef in a kitchen that must deliver the right dish every time.  
**Action Success Rate (ASR)** is the proportion of times this chef actually serves the correct dish out of all the orders it tries to fulfill.  

- **Agentic system**: any program that takes goals, chooses actions, and acts on its own—like a robot vacuum or a virtual assistant.  
- **Success**: the action produces the intended outcome (the vacuum cleans the right room; the assistant schedules the meeting correctly).  
- **Rate**: count successes ÷ total attempts, expressed as a percentage.

If the chef serves 9 correct dishes out of 10 orders, its ASR is 90 %. A higher ASR means the agent reliably turns plans into reality. This metric tells developers whether their AI behaves consistently or needs more training, just like a chef’s reputation depends on how often diners get exactly what they ordered.
