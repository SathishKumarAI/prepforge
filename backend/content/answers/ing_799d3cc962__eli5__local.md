Imagine your machine‑learning model is a high‑stakes kitchen recipe that you must serve at a fancy banquet every night.  
A **hot site** is like having an identical, fully stocked backup kitchen right next to the main one—everything is ready and running, so if the main kitchen burns down, chefs can just walk over and keep cooking with no delay.  
A **multi‑site configuration** expands this idea: instead of one backup kitchen, you have several kitchens spread across different neighborhoods. Each one keeps a copy of your recipe, ingredients, and even the same ovens (cloud servers). If one city’s power goes out or a hurricane hits, another kitchen continues serving guests.  
In cloud terms, a hot site means an instant replica of all data and services in a separate data center; multi‑site means multiple replicas spread globally, automatically routing traffic away from any failed location. This keeps your ML models available and protects against disasters.
