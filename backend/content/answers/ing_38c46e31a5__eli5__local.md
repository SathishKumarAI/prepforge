Think of training an AI as learning to play many different musical instruments at once, all in the same rehearsal room.  
**Data mixture** is that single rehearsal room where you hear piano, violin, drums, and saxophone simultaneously—each instrument represents a different data source (text, images, code, etc.). The model listens to all of them, so it learns patterns common to every “instrument” and also how each one sounds.  

**Pretraining** is the first few months of this rehearsal: you practice endlessly on the whole mix before ever performing for an audience. You’re not yet trying to write a specific song; you’re just building muscle memory—understanding rhythm, harmony, and texture across all instruments. Later, when you “fine‑tune,” you take that broad skill set and focus it on a single instrument or genre, like composing a jazz solo.  

So the Data Mixture Standard is simply training once on a big, diverse blend of data so the AI can later specialize quickly.
