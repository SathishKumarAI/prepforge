Think of a robot learning new skills as a student studying for exams.  
**LoCoMo (Local Memory Test)** is like a quick quiz that checks if the robot can remember facts it just learned in one session—does it still know the shape of a cube after ten minutes?  

**LongMemEval (Long‑Term Memory Evaluation)** is a final exam spread over months. It asks the robot to recall a story told yesterday, a math trick from last year, and a recipe it read five years ago, testing how well its memories stick when time passes.  

**BEAM (Benchmarks for Everyday AI Memory)** is the real‑world test: the robot uses all those remembered facts while navigating a busy kitchen—remembering where the pans are, what’s on sale, and the owner’s favorite song—without forgetting earlier lessons.

Together they show whether an AI can quickly retrieve new information, hold onto it over long periods, and use both in everyday tasks.
