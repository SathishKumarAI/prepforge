### Why Ergonomics Matters for Remote Work  

When you sit for hours, the body behaves like a **mechanical system**: joints are levers, muscles are actuators, and your spine is a load‑bearing beam. Poor posture shifts loads from the neutral spine into the lumbar region, causing pain and chronic injury—an optimization problem where the objective is *minimal muscle effort*. An ergonomic setup re‑balances that system, reducing torque on joints and keeping the spine near its natural curvature.
