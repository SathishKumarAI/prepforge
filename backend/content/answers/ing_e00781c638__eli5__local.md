**What is Machine Learning?**  
Think of a kitchen where you’re trying to make the perfect soup. You’ve never tasted it before, but you have a handful of ingredients and a few bowls. Instead of following a recipe (a fixed set of instructions), you let the soup “learn” itself: you add a spoonful of broth, stir, taste, then adjust by adding more herbs or salt. After many tries, the soup’s flavor stabilizes—it has discovered a good balance on its own.

That’s *machine learning*: computers are given data (the ingredients) and a goal (good soup), but no explicit recipe. They try different combinations, evaluate how close they come to the goal, and gradually improve their choices. The result is an algorithm that can predict or classify new data without being told exactly what to look for.
