The old way to share the chef’s skill was to give each apprentice a recipe book that simply copies the chef’s cooking style word‑for‑word—this is “on‑policy distillation.” It works fine when the kitchen is small, but as the menu grows huge, copying every detail becomes impossible and the apprentices can’t improvise.
