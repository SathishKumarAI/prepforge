Think of a busy kitchen where orders must keep coming in even if something goes wrong.  

**Fault tolerance** is like having extra chefs who can step in automatically when one chef drops a pan or forgets an ingredient. The kitchen keeps running smoothly because the system “tolerates” that fault and recovers without stopping.

**High availability** is more about keeping the kitchen open all the time. Even if the main stove burns out, you have a backup stove ready to fire up right away so customers never see a delay. The focus is on uptime, not necessarily fixing the original problem immediately.

So:  
- Fault tolerance = *recovering from failures while still working*.  
- High availability = *keeping the service running continuously*, often by switching to backups before any interruption is noticed.
