**Stage II: Focused Adaptive Gate Training**

Think of a busy intersection where each road has a traffic light (the *gate*). In the first stage, every light just follows a fixed schedule—no matter how many cars arrive on one street or another. Stage II is when the system learns to adjust those lights based on real‑time traffic patterns.

- **Gate**: A control that lets signals (data) pass through only if certain conditions are met.
- **Adaptive**: The gate changes its behavior in response to what it “sees” during training.
- **Training**: Repeating the process, giving the gates examples of good and bad traffic flow so they learn the best timing.

During this focused phase, we fine‑tune only the gates that matter most for a specific task—just like turning the lights on one busy street to green when cars pile up while keeping others off. The result is a network that routes information efficiently, just as an adaptive intersection keeps traffic moving smoothly.
