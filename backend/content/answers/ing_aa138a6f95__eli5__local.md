Think of building an AI like learning to bake a cake with a recipe you’re still figuring out.  
**Observation (Gather data)** – You taste many cakes and note what makes them good or bad.  
**Hypothesis (Model design)** – You guess that adding more sugar will sweeten the flavor, so you write a “recipe” (the algorithm).  
**Experiment (Training)** – You bake several cakes following that recipe, tweaking ingredients each time.  
**Analysis (Evaluation)** – You taste each cake and compare it to your goal. If it’s too dry, you adjust the batter.  
**Conclusion (Iteration)** – The new insight becomes the basis for the next recipe round.

Just as bakers refine a cake by testing and learning from results, AI developers cycle through data, models, training, evaluation, and improvement—exactly the Scientific Method in action.
