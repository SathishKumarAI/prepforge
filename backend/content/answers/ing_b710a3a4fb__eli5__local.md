Imagine a smart home robot that doesn’t wait for you to tell it what to do; it watches the house, learns your habits, and acts before you even think of it. That’s *proactive* behavior.

In an AI agent, proactiveness means taking actions based on predictions about the future, not just reacting to current inputs. The agent has a model (a little “brain”) that simulates possible outcomes, then picks moves that will bring it closer to its goal. It’s like a chess player who imagines several turns ahead and chooses the best opening move before the opponent even puts a piece on the board.
