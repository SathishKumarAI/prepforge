**Groundedness (or Faithfulness)** means that an AI’s answer actually reflects the facts or data it was given, rather than making things up.  
Think of a student who has just read a chapter and is asked to write a short essay. If the essay uses only ideas from the book—no invented details—that student is “grounded.” The AI does the same: it pulls directly from its training material or from a specific source it was told to use, and it doesn’t add extra claims that aren’t supported.

So groundedness is like keeping your writing strictly inside the boundaries of what you know. It keeps the answer honest, accurate, and trustworthy.
