**Should I use an open‑source AI model?**

Think of an open‑source model like a community recipe that anyone can copy, tweak, and share. The “recipe” (the code) is public, so you can see exactly how it works, add your own spices (custom data), or fix a mistake. If you’re learning or building something unique, this openness lets you experiment freely without paying for a closed‑door cookbook.

**When to choose it:**

- **Learning & prototyping:** You want to understand the inner workings and test ideas quickly.
- **Custom projects:** Your needs differ from off‑the‑shelf solutions; you can adapt the model.
- **Cost sensitivity:** No license fees mean lower startup costs.

**When to be cautious:**

- **Production safety:** Open models may lack rigorous testing, so extra validation is needed.
- **Compliance & data privacy:** You must ensure your use complies with laws and that no sensitive data leaks through the public codebase.

In short, if you value flexibility, transparency, and cost savings—and are ready to handle the extra responsibility—open‑source AI can be a powerful ally. If you need guaranteed support, strict compliance checks, or minimal maintenance effort, a commercial solution might suit better.
