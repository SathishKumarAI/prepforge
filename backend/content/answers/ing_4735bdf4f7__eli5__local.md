Think of an AI like a very eager librarian who only knows what’s in the book you hand them.  
If you give the librarian a short note—just “Tell me about climate change”—they’ll pull the first chapter they find and give you a quick summary.  

Now imagine instead you write a longer, more detailed note: you list the specific facts you already know (e.g., *“I read that CO₂ levels hit 420 ppm in 2023”*), ask for connections to those facts, and hint at what you want next (like “Explain how this affects polar bears”).  
The librarian now uses your notes as a roadmap. They search the library for passages that link directly to your clues, instead of blindly skimming from the top.  

That’s contextual retrieval: by feeding a richer prompt, the AI can “look up” only the parts of its training that match your context, giving sharper and more relevant answers.
