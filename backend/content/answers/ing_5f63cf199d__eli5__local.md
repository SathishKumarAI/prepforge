Hybrid search is like a two‑step treasure hunt in a library.  
First, you use **keyword search** (the “fast scanner”) to pull out every book that mentions the word *“rainbow.”* It’s quick but sometimes brings in books about rainbows on paper or rainbow‑shaped clouds that aren’t what you want.  

Next, you apply **semantic understanding** (the “smart librarian”). The librarian reads the titles and a few sentences from each candidate to see if it really talks about *rainbow physics* versus just the color.  

The final result is a list of books that both contain the keyword *and* truly match what you’re looking for. Hybrid search blends speed with deeper meaning, giving you more accurate answers than either method alone.
