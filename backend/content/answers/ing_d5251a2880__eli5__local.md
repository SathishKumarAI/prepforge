Think of a grocery store that sells the same items every day. **Anomaly detection** is like having a smart eye that watches each shelf and flags anything out of place—say a banana that looks rotten or a price tag that’s wrong. In machine learning, an algorithm learns what “normal” looks like from lots of past data (the regular shelves). Then it scans new data and points out the odd one‑offs.

- **Algorithm**: A recipe of steps the computer follows to spot those weird items.  
- **Anomaly**: Something that doesn’t fit the pattern, like a missing ingredient or an unexpected spike in sales.

Applications are everywhere: fraud detection (spotting unusual credit card use), network security (flagging strange traffic), health monitoring (detecting abnormal heart rhythms), and more. The goal is simple—find the “odd shelf” before it causes trouble.
