**Depth**  
- **AI**: goal‑oriented, encompasses reasoning, planning, perception, language understanding. Historically rooted in symbolic logic, later expanded with statistical methods.  
- **ML**: data‑driven algorithms that fit a model \(f_\theta(x)\) to minimize loss over training set \(\{(x_i,y_i)\}\). Key families: supervised (regression/classification), unsupervised (clustering, dimensionality reduction), reinforcement learning. ML is the primary engine for most modern AI products today because it scales with data and can generalize beyond handcrafted rules.  
- Example: A rule‑based spam filter vs. a deep neural network that learns to classify spam by training on millions of labeled emails.

**Edge Cases**  
- Pure logic systems (e.g., chess engines) are AI but not ML.  
- Statistical pattern matching without intent or understanding can be ML but not full AI.  
- Ensure you differentiate “AI hype” from genuine intelligence (common interview pitfall).
