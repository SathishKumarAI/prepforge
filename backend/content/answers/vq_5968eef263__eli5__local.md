Think of a **class** as a recipe for making cookies. The recipe tells you the ingredients (data) and the steps (behaviour). A **class** is just that instruction sheet – it’s not a cookie itself, but it shows how to make one.

An **object** is the actual cookie you bake using the recipe. It has a specific taste, size, and maybe a chocolate chip on top—those are its *attributes* (data). The object can also perform actions like “chew” or “share,” which come from the class’s methods.

So, the class is the blueprint that defines what a cookie looks like and how it behaves; an object is a concrete instance created from that blueprint, with its own unique state.
