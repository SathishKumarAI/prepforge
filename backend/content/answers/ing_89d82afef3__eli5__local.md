Think of a job interview like sending a postcard into a mailbox that only opens when the right key is inserted.  
Your résumé is the postcard; it must show the most eye‑catching picture (skills) and a clear note (why you’re useful). The interviewer’s question is the key: it tells you whether your postcard fits their lock.

To get feedback, ask politely after you’ve received a decision or at the end of an interview.
