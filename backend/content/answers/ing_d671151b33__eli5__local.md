Imagine you’re hosting a big party in your house (the cloud). Everyone brings their own snack boxes (applications) that need to be set up on the kitchen counter. If every guest has to unpack and arrange their food in the same space without any organization tools, a few things go wrong:  

* **Messy setup** – One person’s snacks can spill over into another’s area, causing confusion or even damage.  
* **Slow service** – The host (the cloud provider) must keep track of each box and clean up after every guest, which takes time and effort.  
* **Hard to move** – If the party moves to a new house, you’d have to re‑unpack everything from scratch because there’s no standardized way to pack the boxes.

They keep each guest’s food separate, let the host serve them quickly, and make it easy to relocate the party without tearing apart every box again. Without containers, cloud resources become tangled, slower, and less portable.
