**Deploying a big language model is like putting a very smart but temperamental chef into a busy kitchen.**

The “chef” (the LLM) knows how to cook many dishes (answer questions), but it needs a steady supply of fresh ingredients and a clear recipe book. In real life, the kitchen has limited space, noisy traffic, and sometimes missing spices—just as an LLM must run on powerful GPUs, handle lots of user requests at once, and stay within memory limits.  

Other challenges are:  
- **Speed vs. accuracy** – like a chef who can cook fast but might miss subtle flavors; the model must balance quick replies with thoughtful answers.  
- **Safety filters** – similar to a kitchen rule that stops the chef from using harmful ingredients; the LLM needs safeguards against generating dangerous or biased content.  
- **Updating recipes** – the chef’s cookbook changes over time, so the model must be retrained or fine‑tuned without losing its core skills.
