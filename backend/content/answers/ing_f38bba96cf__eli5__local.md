Over many tastings, it starts noticing patterns—maybe sweet ones are lighter and rounder. Eventually it can guess correctly without your help.

In machine learning this process is similar: a computer receives lots of labeled data (the apples + their labels) and uses simple rules to find hidden patterns. Once trained, it can predict the label for new, unseen examples. Think of training as teaching the robot by example; inference is the robot using what it learned to make decisions on its own.
