**Back‑of‑the‑envelope analysis** is like doing a quick sketch before you build a house.  
Imagine you’re planning a new building: first you estimate how many bricks and how much concrete you’ll need, just to see if the budget can cover it. In machine learning, that same quick check tells you whether a model will fit on your computer or finish in a reasonable time.

*Key terms:*  
- **Model size** – number of parameters (the “bricks”).  
- **Compute cost** – how many operations are needed to train (the “concrete”).  
- **Memory footprint** – the RAM required while training (how big the building site must be).  

If your rough calculation shows you’ll need more memory than you have, you can switch to a simpler model or use a larger machine before you waste hours of coding. This simple, intuitive check saves time and keeps projects on track.
