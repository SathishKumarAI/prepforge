Imagine a smart vending machine that never keeps the same price for its drinks.  
When it sees lots of people buying cola in the afternoon, it nudges the price up a little; when the fridge is empty and sales are slow, it lowers the price to attract customers. That’s what a **dynamic pricing engine** does—its job is to set prices on the fly based on real‑time data.

*Dynamic pricing*: adjusting a product’s price continuously instead of keeping one fixed price.  
*Engine*: the software that collects data (sales volume, time of day, competitor prices), runs quick calculations, and outputs a new price for each item.

The engine uses **machine learning** to learn patterns: it watches how customers respond to different price points, builds a model that predicts “how many will buy if I set this price,” and then picks the price that maximizes profit or volume. In short, it’s a vending‑machine brain that learns from every purchase and always prices itself for success.
