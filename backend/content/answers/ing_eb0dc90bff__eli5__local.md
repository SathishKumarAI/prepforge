Imagine you’re hosting a huge birthday party where guests keep arriving all the time. If everyone had to sit in one long line at a single table, it would take forever for each person to get a slice of cake. Instead, you set up several small tables around the room and let people choose any open spot. The cake gets shared evenly, no one waits too long, and the party runs smoothly.

It’s a technique that distributes incoming work (like website requests or data tasks) across many servers instead of sending everything to just one.
