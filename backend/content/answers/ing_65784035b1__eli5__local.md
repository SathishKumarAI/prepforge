Imagine you’re at a busy pizza place where the kitchen can’t make every order right away. Instead of each customer waiting in line for their pizza, the cashier puts the order on a “delayed queue” – a list that keeps track of what to do and when. The kitchen staff pulls orders from this list only when they have enough time or ingredients ready, just like a message queue that holds data until a worker can process it.

**Delayed/Scheduled Queue:** A storage spot where messages (tasks) sit for a set period before being sent to the next step.  
**Message Queue:** A waiting line of data packets that servers pick up one by one when they’re ready.
