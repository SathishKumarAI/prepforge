Imagine you’re a detective on a big case (the AI) that needs clues from a huge library of books (data).  
**Context retrieval** is like the detective asking a librarian, “Show me all the pages about this crime.” The librarian pulls out only the relevant chapters so the detective doesn’t waste time sifting through every book.  

**Agentic search** is when the detective decides what questions to ask next on their own—“What else might explain this clue?” They choose new angles and look in different sections, guided by the clues already found.  

Together, *effective context engineering* means giving the AI a smart way to fetch useful snippets (context retrieval) and then letting it decide its own next move (agentic search). The result is an agent that’s fast, focused, and self‑directed—just like a detective who knows where to look and how to keep chasing leads.
