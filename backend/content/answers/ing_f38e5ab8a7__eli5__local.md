Imagine you’ve learned to read in English, but now you want to read Spanish books. You keep practicing with Spanish texts so your brain gets used to the new sounds and vocabulary. In AI, *continued pretraining* works the same way: a big model first learns general language patterns from lots of data (like learning English). Then, we give it extra training on text from a specific field—say medical journals or legal documents—so it “adapts” to that domain’s jargon and style. Think of it as polishing a tool for a particular job: the model stays strong overall but becomes especially good at handling the nuances of its new specialty.
