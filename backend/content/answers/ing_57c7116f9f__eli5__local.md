**Cost analysis** is like figuring out the total expenses of running that building—maintenance, electricity, internet, and staff—and then dividing those costs among all 500 families. Because every family shares the same facilities, a single set of costs (the “shared infrastructure”) applies to all. You add up everything the landlord spends, then spread it evenly or proportionally (perhaps by how much space each family uses).
