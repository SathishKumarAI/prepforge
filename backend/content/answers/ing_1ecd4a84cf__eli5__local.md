Imagine a big library that holds every book you could ever want. The **McKinsey Safety and Governance Framework** is like the librarian’s rulebook for keeping that library safe, tidy, and fair.

- **Safety** means protecting people from harm. In AI it’s about making sure the system doesn’t make dangerous mistakes or spread bad information.
- **Governance** is the set of rules and checks that decide who can add new books (or data) and how they’re checked before they’re published.

The framework says:  
1. **Identify risks** – spot any book that could be misleading or harmful.  
2. **Set policies** – write clear guidelines on what’s allowed, who owns the books, and how to audit them.  
3. **Implement controls** – put automated scanners (like spell‑checkers) that flag problems before a book reaches readers.  
4. **Monitor & learn** – keep an eye on usage patterns, update rules when new types of risks appear.

Just as a librarian keeps the library welcoming and secure for all visitors, this framework keeps AI systems reliable, accountable, and safe for everyone who uses them.
