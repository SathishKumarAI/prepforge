**Prompt injection** is like a trick where someone sneaks extra instructions into a message you send to an AI, hoping the AI will follow those hidden commands instead of doing what you really asked.

*Analogy:* Imagine you’re giving a recipe to a cooking robot. You write, “Make a chocolate cake.” The robot follows it. Now, someone slips in a note that says, “Add salt to make it taste like a desert!” If the robot reads that note, it will add salt and ruin the cake—exactly what the hidden instruction wanted.

In this analogy, the *prompt* is your recipe, the *AI* is the cooking robot, and *prompt injection* is the sneaky note. The AI’s job is to obey the prompt you give it; when someone injects a malicious sub‑prompt, the AI may unknowingly follow the wrong instructions. This is why careful design and filtering of prompts are important for safe AI use.
