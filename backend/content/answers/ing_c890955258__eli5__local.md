1. **Ask a question (prompt)** – The teacher gives the robot a new puzzle.
2. **Robot answers** – It tries to solve it using its knowledge.
3. **Teacher checks** – We compare the answer with the correct solution.
4. **Give feedback** – Tell the robot what was right or wrong.
5. **Adjust learning** – The robot updates its internal rules (weights) so next time it will do better.
