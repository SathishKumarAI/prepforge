Imagine building a library that anyone can use to write any kind of book, not just one genre. A **foundation model** is like that library—it’s trained on a huge mix of data so it can help create many kinds of content.

There are two main flavors:

1. **Multimodal models** – they read and understand several senses at once, such as text *and* images or video. Think of them as librarians who can read both the written word and the pictures in a comic book, then answer questions about either.

2. **Monomodal models** – they specialize in one sense, usually text. They’re like expert writers who know every style, from poetry to technical manuals, because they’ve read an enormous amount of that single type of material.

Both kinds share the same core “brain” but are tuned differently, just as a librarian might be better at books than photographs.
