Imagine you’re baking a cake for a special guest who only wants the ingredients to stay in their country.  
**Sovereign AI** is like that cake: it’s built and run inside one nation’s borders so that the data never leaves, keeping privacy and local laws intact.  
**Data residency** is the rule that says all the flour, sugar, eggs (the raw data) must be stored in that same country.  

Now think of different cakes as **model taxonomy**—a way to organize AI models by what they do: a “baking” model (image recognition), a “frosting” model (text generation), etc. Just as you choose the right cake for the occasion, developers pick the right model type that fits their task while respecting sovereignty and residency rules. This keeps data safe, compliant, and the AI working exactly where it’s supposed to.
