Imagine you’re a chef who has just invented a new sauce. You don’t just taste it once; you put it on different dishes—pizza, soup, salad—to see how well it works in each setting. In AI, *application‑specific evaluations* are the same idea: we test a model not only with generic questions but with real tasks that matter for a particular job (e.g., diagnosing medical images, translating legal contracts, or recommending music).
