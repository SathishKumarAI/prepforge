Think of a marathon where only the finishers are shown on the leaderboard. If you look at that list and say, “All runners who trained hard must be great athletes,” you’re ignoring everyone who started but didn’t finish—maybe because they got injured or lost motivation. That’s **survivorship bias**: focusing only on those who survive a process while forgetting those who didn’t.

In AI, we often celebrate models that pass every benchmark and ignore the countless experiments that failed. We then think “AI is perfect” when in reality many trials didn’t work out. Remember to look at both the winners and the losers—only then do you get the full picture of what’s possible and what still needs improvement.
