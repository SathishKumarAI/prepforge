   • **Competitive moat**: Firms that embed AI early secure features no one else offers; the cost of waiting grows with each iteration of GPT‑4 and beyond. This creates *network externalities*: the more talent a firm has, the faster it can iterate, making the demand curve steep.

2. **Supply side – human capital constraints**
