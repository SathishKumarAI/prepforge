Think of a robot as a student who wants to understand the whole classroom.  
**Multimodal** means it can read the blackboard (text), listen to the teacher’s voice (audio), and see students’ gestures (vision).  

A **World Model** is like the student’s mental notebook: it stores what has happened, predicts tomorrow’s lesson, and lets the robot “rehearse” different actions before trying them.  

**VLAs** (Vision‑Language Agents) are the student who can ask questions about a picture—“What color is the ball?”—and get answers that connect words to images. They blend seeing and talking so the robot knows what objects mean in context.  

**Omni** takes this further, letting the same brain handle all senses at once—speech, sight, touch—so it can answer a phone call while watching a video, just as a human does effortlessly.
