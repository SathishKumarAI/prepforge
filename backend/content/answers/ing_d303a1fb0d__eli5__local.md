Think of an AI as a chef who has a huge pantry (all the data it’s seen) and a recipe book (the math that turns input into output).  
**Context engineering** is like giving the chef a clear, tasty menu before cooking: you pick exactly which ingredients (words or phrases) to put in the pot so the dish comes out just right.  
When we talk about **reasoning effort**, imagine the chef deciding how many steps of prep to do—mixing, simmering, tasting—before serving. A little reasoning is quick chopping; deep reasoning is slow braising that lets flavors mingle.  

OpenAI’s “o3” framework says: give the chef a focused menu (context) and let it decide the right amount of cooking time (reasoning effort). The result? Food that tastes good because the AI used just enough steps to understand your request, without over‑thinking or under‑delivering.
