Imagine a chef who already knows how to cook many dishes—this is your pretrained AI model. Fine‑tuning is like teaching that chef one new recipe, but you don’t want to forget everything else they know. First, you give the chef a small cookbook of the new dish (a set of new data). Then, while the chef cooks, you gently nudge them: if they add too much salt, you correct it; if they miss an ingredient, you point it out. Over time, the chef learns to make that special dish exactly right, but still keeps all their other cooking skills intact. In practice, the AI’s internal “weights” are updated only a little with the new data, so it adapts without losing its original knowledge. That’s fine‑tuning in plain terms.
