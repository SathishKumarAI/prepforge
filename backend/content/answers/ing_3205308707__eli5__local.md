**Prompt engineering is like giving a recipe to a chef who only knows how to cook, not how to read recipes.**  
You tell the “chef” (the AI) what dish you want: “Make me a short story about a brave cat.” The more precise your instructions—like the ingredients and cooking steps—the better the final dish will taste.  

- **Prompt** = the instruction or question you give.  
- **AI model** = the chef that follows the prompt to produce text.  
- **Fine‑tuning** = training the chef with many recipes so it gets faster at specific dishes.

Just as a clear recipe prevents burnt food, a well‑crafted prompt guides the AI to generate useful, accurate answers instead of random guesses. Think of each prompt as a bridge: the clearer the bridge’s design, the smoother the journey from your question to the answer.
