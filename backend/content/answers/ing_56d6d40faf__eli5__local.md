Imagine your computer’s logs are like a long diary that you keep writing in every day. If you never stop adding pages, the diary will grow until it can’t fit on your shelf or even into your desk drawer. Log rotation is the tidy way of keeping that diary manageable.

**What log rotation does:**  
1. **Creates a new page (file)** when the current one gets full or reaches a set age.  
2. **Renames the old page** with a number or date so you still have a record of what happened before.  
3. **Keeps only a limited number of pages** – older ones may be deleted or compressed to save space.

So, just like you’d fold away an old diary and start a fresh one while keeping the previous volumes in order, log rotation lets your system keep logs useful without filling up all its storage.
