Imagine the EU AI Act as a big, well‑guarded library where every book (data) must have a clear owner and a purpose for being read.  
Under **Data Processing Legal Basis**, companies that build or use AI must prove two things before pulling any data into their system:  

1. **Consent** – Like asking a reader if it’s okay to copy a page, the user must explicitly agree to how their data will be used.  
2. **Legitimate Interest** – The company can claim the data is needed for its AI project only if it shows that the benefit (e.g., better safety or efficiency) outweighs any privacy risk and no alternative exists.  

If neither condition holds, the data cannot be processed.  
Enterprises must keep a clear record of which basis they used, how they verified it, and what safeguards (encryption, anonymisation) protect the data. This ensures every AI model is built on a solid, legally sound foundation, just as every library book has a proper checkout label.
