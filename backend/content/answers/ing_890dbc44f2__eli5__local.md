Guardrails engineering is like putting safety rails on the road and a watchdog in the car’s brain.  

**Safety** means designing rules so the robot never hurts people—like setting a hard limit on how loud it can speak or ensuring it doesn’t make decisions that could endanger lives.

**Responsible AI** is the moral compass: we program the robot to respect privacy, avoid bias, and explain why it made a choice.
