Think of your brain as a library that can both *look up* exact books and *mix* ideas from different shelves to answer a question.  
**Hybrid search** is like having two librarians: one (the *retrieval* part) quickly pulls the most relevant books based on keywords, while another (the *fusion* or *RAG* part – Retrieval‑Augmented Generation) reads those books and writes a fresh, personalized summary.  

So when you ask “What causes rain?” the retrieval librarian fetches meteorology texts; the fusion librarian blends that information with your own past questions and writes an answer that feels both authoritative and tailored to you.  
In short: hybrid search = “find the right sources”; fusion (RAG) = “combine them into a new, helpful response.”
