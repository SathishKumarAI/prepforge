Imagine you’re ordering a pizza. With **REST** you call the “pizza shop” once and ask for a whole menu: every topping, size, crust option, even recipes you might not need. The shop sends back a big box full of everything, and if you only wanted a thin‑crust pepperoni slice, you still get the entire menu.

With **GraphQL**, think of a smart waiter who can hear exactly what you want. You say, “Just give me the name, price, and calories of a thin‑crust pepperoni pizza.” The waiter fetches only those pieces from the kitchen and hands them to you—no extra ingredients or data.  

**GraphQL** is a query language for APIs that lets clients specify *exactly* which fields they need; the server returns just that shape, nothing more, nothing less. It’s like a personal assistant that tailors every answer to your request. This contrasts with REST, where each endpoint delivers a fixed set of data regardless of what you actually want.
