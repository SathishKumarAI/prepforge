Imagine you’re baking a cake for a big event, but the recipe is still new.  
**Evals** are like taste‑tests: after each bake, you sample the cake, score it on sweetness, texture, and appearance, then tweak the batter or oven time. In AI, evals are systematic tests that measure a model’s performance on diverse tasks—much like a chef’s tasting panel.

**Observability** is the kitchen’s monitoring system. Sensors track temperature, humidity, ingredient flow, and even the smell of the dough as it rises. When something goes wrong—a too‑hot oven or a missing spice—you see the data instantly and can fix it before the cake burns. In AI systems, observability means logging inputs, outputs, latency, and resource use so engineers spot problems early.

Together, evals give you quality feedback and observability gives you real‑time insight—just as a great chef needs both taste tests and kitchen sensors to perfect every batch.
