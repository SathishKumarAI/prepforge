Think of building a robot that can talk to people.  
**Guardrails** are like safety fences: they make sure the robot never says something harmful or illegal. They set hard limits—if the robot tries to use profanity, it stops immediately. Examples: *no hate‑speech score*, *no sexual content threshold*.  

**Quality metrics** are like performance gauges: they measure how well the robot is doing its job, not whether it’s staying safe. They judge usefulness and politeness—examples include *accuracy of answers*, *clarity rating*, or *user satisfaction score*.  

So guardrails protect against bad behavior; quality metrics check that the robot still works smoothly and help us improve it over time.
