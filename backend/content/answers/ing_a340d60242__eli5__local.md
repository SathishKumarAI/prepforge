Imagine you’re ordering coffee at a café that doesn’t have a phone line.  
With **polling**, you keep checking the counter every minute to see if your cup is ready. You’ll know only when you look, and you waste time walking back and forth even if nothing has changed.

A **webhook** is like the barista tapping your shoulder as soon as the coffee arrives—no waiting or extra trips. The café sends a message (the webhook) to your phone instantly whenever something happens.

So, polling = “I ask repeatedly”; webhook = “you’re notified automatically.” Polling can be simple but slow and resource‑heavy; webhooks are faster and lighter because they push updates only when needed.
