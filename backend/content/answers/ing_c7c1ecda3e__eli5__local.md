A **data abstraction layer** is like a kitchen station that takes all those varied containers, cleans them up, and puts the ingredients into uniform bowls labeled “Protein,” “Vegetable,” “Spice.” Your recipes (the machine‑learning models) can then pick what they need from these bowls without worrying about how the food arrived.
