Think of Apache Kafka as a city’s main post office that everyone trusts to deliver packages quickly and reliably.  
*What it does*: Kafka stores streams of messages (like letters) in “topics” and lets thousands of applications read or write them at the same time, without losing any.  
*Why people love it*:
