Imagine you’re a detective learning to read clues in two ways.  
**Regression** is like predicting how tall a plant will grow based on the amount of sunlight it gets. The “clue” (sunlight) points to a single number (height). You learn to draw a line that best fits all past plants, then use it to guess new ones.  

**Classification** is more like sorting mail into two bins—“spam” or “not spam.” Here the clues (email content, sender address, etc.) lead you to pick one of two categories. You train your detective by showing many examples and learning which clues push the decision toward each bin.
