Think of an AI system as a kitchen robot that can cook many dishes (tasks).  
**Evaluations** are like taste‑tests: after the robot makes a dish, we score it on flavor, texture, presentation—these scores tell us how well the robot is doing and help us tweak its recipe.  
**Observability** is the set of cameras, microphones, and temperature sensors that watch the robot while it cooks. They record every step—how long it chops, how much heat it uses, whether a pan over‑heats—so we can see why a dish failed or succeeded.

Together, evaluations give us the final verdict on performance, while observability gives us the detailed logs and metrics needed to fix problems quickly. In short, evaluations tell “Did it work?” and observability tells “How did it work?”.
