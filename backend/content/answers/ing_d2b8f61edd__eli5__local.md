**Machine learning is like teaching a child to recognize pictures.**  
Imagine you have a box full of photos—some of cats, some of dogs. You show the child many examples and say “This is a cat.” After seeing enough, the child learns patterns: whiskers, pointy ears, etc., that usually mean *cat*. When a new photo appears, the child can guess whether it’s a cat or not.

In **Python**, you do the same with code.  
1. **Collect data** – gather many labeled pictures (cats = 0, dogs = 1).  
2. **Choose a model** – pick a simple “rule‑maker” (like a decision tree) that will learn from the examples.  
3. **Train it** – let the model look at all the photos and adjust its internal rules so it gets more correct guesses.  
4. **Validate** – test on fresh pictures to see if it still works.  
5. **Deploy** – once satisfied, use it to label new images automatically.
