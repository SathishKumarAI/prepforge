**Why “forward‑deployed” engineers are the new tech currency**

In a world where data is abundant but *actionable insight* is scarce, the bottleneck is not algorithms— it’s people who can *deploy* those models into production and iterate on them in real time. A forward‑deployed engineer (FDE) bridges that gap: they own end‑to‑end pipelines, from feature extraction to monitoring, and they are comfortable with both ML theory and ops tooling.

**Why the salary premium?**

1. **Opportunity cost** – Companies can’t afford a 10‑year learning curve; they need immediate ROI. The FDE’s ability to reduce model drift costs $X per month translates directly into profit.
2. **Risk mitigation** – Deploying ML models without rigorous observability is a systemic risk (e.g., bias amplification). Firms pay for engineers who embed bias audits, version control, and rollback mechanisms from day one.
3. **Network effects** – An FDE builds reusable “model‑as‑service” components that other teams can consume, multiplying the engineer’s impact across products.

**Interview blueprint**

1. **Systems design (20%)** – Build a latency‑bounded recommendation engine that auto‑scales with traffic spikes.
