Imagine a kitchen where you want to cook a big meal. **Tools** are the knives, pots, and measuring cups—each does one simple job (cut, boil, measure) but you can mix them together however you like. An AI agent that uses tools is like a chef who follows a recipe: it tells the language model when to “use the knife” or “stir the pot.”  

**Subagents**, on the other hand, are mini‑chefs that already know how to cook a specific dish (e.g., a sous‑chef for soup). They can be asked to “make soup” and will handle all the steps themselves. When an AI agent calls a subagent, it delegates the whole task instead of juggling individual tools.  

Using many small tools keeps things simple and flexible; using a few powerful subagents saves time but can hide complexity. The trick is to choose the right mix so you don’t build a kitchen that’s either too cluttered or too opaque.
