Imagine a kitchen that keeps buying ingredients without checking the pantry or watching the stove’s fuel use. In AI projects, **cost anti‑patterns** are those habits that let expenses pile up silently—like ordering huge GPU clusters for every experiment, or paying for more storage than needed, then never cleaning it up.

**FinOps** (financial operations) is like a smart budget planner who checks the pantry daily, tags items with “use before,” and only opens the stove when there’s a recipe. It teaches teams to monitor cloud bills in real time, right‑size resources, and set alerts so the kitchen never burns money on unused capacity.

**Token economics** works the same way but for data tokens: each token represents a unit of compute or storage. If you treat tokens like loose change—spending them haphazardly—you’ll waste your budget. A good token economy assigns clear value to each token, tracks consumption, and rewards efficient use, ensuring every AI “meal” is made with the right amount of ingredients.
