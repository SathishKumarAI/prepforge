Imagine your AI is a new recipe you’re baking in the kitchen. **Evaluation** is like tasting it at different stages to make sure it turns out just right.

1. **Unit (Ingredient) Tests** – Check each ingredient separately, making sure the sugar isn’t too sweet or the flour isn’t clumpy. In AI, this means verifying individual components (data, models, code) work correctly on their own.

2. **Integration (Mixing) Tests** – Stir the batter and taste a small bite to see if flavors blend without surprises. For AI, you test how data flows into the model and how the model’s output feeds back into the system.

3. **Performance (Cooking Time) Tests** – Measure how long the dish takes to cook and whether it stays warm. In AI, this is about speed, memory usage, and scalability—how fast the model responds under load.

4. **User‑Experience (Taste Test) Evaluations** – Invite friends to eat and ask for honest feedback. For an AI product, you gather real users’ reactions: accuracy, fairness, usefulness, and trustworthiness.

5. **Robustness (Stability) Tests** – Drop the dish on a table to see if it falls apart. In AI, you challenge the model with noisy or unexpected inputs to ensure it still behaves safely.

By running all these “taste tests,” you can confidently serve an AI product that’s not just functional but delightful and reliable.
