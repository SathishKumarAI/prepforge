Imagine building a house.  
**Raw SDK** is like buying all the raw lumber, nails, and paint – you have everything but must decide how to put it together yourself.  
A **thin layer** is a simple set of instructions that tells you where each piece goes (e.g., “hang these panels on the left wall”), saving you a little time but still leaving most of the work to you.  
A **framework** is the full blueprint and scaffolding: it already has rooms, doors, plumbing plans, and even safety checks built in so you can focus on decorating instead of figuring out every detail.

When new frameworks appear (like a newer house‑design), developers face “churn”: they must learn a new set of rules and may need to rewrite parts of their code. Choosing between raw SDK, thin layer, or full framework is like deciding how much of the construction you want to control versus rely on pre‑built plans. The right choice balances speed, flexibility, and future maintenance.
