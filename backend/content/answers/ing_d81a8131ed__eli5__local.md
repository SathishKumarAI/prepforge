Imagine a big, versatile recipe book (the **base‑model**) that contains the basic flavors for many dishes. A company (a **tenant**) wants its own special pizza, so it tweaks the recipe by adding extra toppings and seasonings—this is **fine‑tuning**.  

The **model lifecycle** is like the journey of a dish from draft to dinner:  
1. **Build** – the base recipe is chosen.  
2. **Train** – the tenant adds its own ingredients (data) and cooks it.  
3. **Deploy** – the finished pizza is served to customers.  
4. **Monitor** – taste tests are taken; if flavors shift, a new batch may be made.  

When the original recipe book gets updated—new spices or cooking techniques—the platform can **refresh the base‑model** so every tenant’s next batch uses the latest, tastier foundation without starting from scratch. This keeps all fine‑tuned models fresh and relevant while letting each tenant keep its unique flavor.
