**Context Engineering in Machine Learning**

Imagine you’re teaching a child to recognize animals, but the classroom has different lighting and backgrounds each day. *Context engineering* is like giving the child a “context guide” that tells them what’s relevant—“the light is dim today, so focus on shape rather than color.” In ML, context is any extra information (time of day, location, user history) that helps the model decide which features matter most. Engineers build this guide by selecting, cleaning, and formatting those clues before feeding them into the algorithm, just as a teacher might highlight the important parts of a picture. The result: the model can adjust its predictions on the fly, just like the child adapts to new lighting conditions.
