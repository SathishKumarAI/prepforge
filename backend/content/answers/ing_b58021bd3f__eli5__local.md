Imagine you’re building a house and need a blueprint first. In AI, the **initial architecture** is that blueprint—it’s the overall layout of how all the parts (data, models, algorithms) fit together before you start construction. Think of it like a kitchen design: you decide where the fridge, stove, sink, and cabinets go, but you don’t yet pick paint colors or appliances.

Once the skeleton is set, you choose **answer frameworks**—the tools that help the AI answer questions. A framework is like a recipe book for your kitchen: it tells you which ingredients (training data) to use, how to mix them (model training), and what final dish (prediction) to serve.
