**Step 2: Label the Ground‑Truth Data**

Think of training an AI like teaching a child to recognize fruit.  
First you gather many pictures (the raw data). The next step is to *label* each picture with the correct answer—“apple,” “banana,” or “orange.” These labels are the **ground truth**: the reliable answers that humans know are right.

In AI evaluation, we do exactly this for every example in our test set. We ask expert annotators (or use verified sources) to write down what each input truly means. The model’s job is then to guess those same labels. If it matches the ground truth often enough, we say it “understands” that task.

So, labeling is simply giving each piece of data a trustworthy tag that the AI can later try to predict—just like marking the correct fruit in a photo.
