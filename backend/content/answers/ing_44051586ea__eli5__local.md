**Regression in machine learning**

Imagine you’re a gardener trying to predict how tall a tomato plant will grow based on the amount of sunlight it gets each day. You measure many plants: some get lots of sun and become very tall, others get little sun and stay short. Regression is like drawing a line that best fits all those measurements, so when you later give the line a new “sunlight” value, it can tell you the expected height.

In machine learning, *regression* is this kind of “prediction line.” The model learns from past data (inputs → outputs) and then estimates a numeric output for new inputs. It’s one way to turn patterns in data into useful forecasts—just as our gardener uses sunlight to guess plant height.
