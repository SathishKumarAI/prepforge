Imagine you’re learning how to cook a new dish. A **use case** is like the recipe itself—step‑by‑step instructions that show exactly what ingredients (data) are needed, what tools (algorithms) to use, and how to combine them so the meal turns out right.  

A **case study**, on the other hand, is a real kitchen story: a chef tried that recipe in a busy restaurant, ran into hiccups (like slow servers or noisy data), tweaked the method, and finally served a crowd of satisfied diners. The lesson comes from watching what worked, what failed, and why.

In AI, use cases tell you *what* problem an algorithm can solve; case studies reveal *how* it behaves in the messy world—helping us learn best practices, spot pitfalls, and build more reliable systems.
