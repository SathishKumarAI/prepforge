Imagine you’re deciding which recipe will make your guests happiest. You bake two batches of cookies—one with extra chocolate chips, one without—and give each group a taste test. If the first batch earns higher smiles, you’ve proven that more chips actually improves the cookie’s appeal; if not, you’ll stick to the simpler version.

In AI projects, an **A/B test** is like that taste‑test but for algorithms or features. Use it when you want to see whether a new model or tweak really changes user experience, performance, or revenue. Put one group on the old system (Control) and another on the new one (Variant), then measure outcomes—click‑through rates, error counts, or sales lift. If the Variant shows clear improvement over time, adopt it; if not, keep the Control.
