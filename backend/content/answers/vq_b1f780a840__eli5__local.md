Imagine you’re a detective looking for clues in a crowded room. Every time you point out something that might be a clue, you hope it’s actually useful—no false alarms. **Precision** is the detective’s “accuracy rate”: the proportion of clues you marked as important that truly mattered.

In AI terms, precision measures how often the system’s positive predictions are correct. If the model says 10 items are spam and 8 really are spam, its precision is 80 %. It doesn’t care about missed spam (that’s recall), only about avoiding false positives. Think of it as the “trustworthiness” of a yes‑answer: when the system says “yes,” how often can we trust that answer?
