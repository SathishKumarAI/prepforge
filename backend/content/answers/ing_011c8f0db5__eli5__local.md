**Strategy 3: Table‑Aware Chunking – Multimodal Retrieval‑Augmented Generation (RAG)**  

Think of a big library that has books and also a spreadsheet of recipes. When you ask the librarian for “best chocolate cake,” she doesn’t just read every book; she first looks at the recipe sheet, picks the most relevant rows (the “chunks”), and then pulls out the exact lines from both the book and the sheet to answer.  

*Table‑aware chunking* is that step of zooming into the spreadsheet’s specific cells instead of treating it as one big block.  
*Multimodal* means she can use text, images, or numbers—anything in the library.  
*Retrieval‑augmented generation (RAG)* is how the librarian writes her answer: she first fetches the right chunks and then stitches them together into a new, helpful response.
