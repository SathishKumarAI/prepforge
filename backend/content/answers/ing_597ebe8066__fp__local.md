### Deeper principle

Salary is essentially a *market‑derived utility function*: it balances the **expected marginal benefit** (reduced time to insight, higher revenue) against the **cost of scarcity** (training time, competition). The AI stack’s modularity means each skill can be mixed and matched; thus salaries reflect not only individual expertise but also the *combinatorial synergy* that a talent brings.
