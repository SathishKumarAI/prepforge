Think of Elasticsearch as a giant library that can read any book you throw at it and then answer questions about the content instantly.  
**Versatile data** means you can put almost anything into this library—text messages, logs from servers, images’ tags, or even GPS coordinates—and it will store them in a way that’s easy to search.
