These events culminate in endothelial dysfunction—tight‑junction leakage, pro‑coagulant surface expression, and microvascular inflammation—which contributes to COVID‑19 morbidity.

| Feature | Virus infection | Vaccine |

Vaccination lacks that context; therefore, it preserves vascular integrity while still presenting the immunogenic epitopes necessary for neutralizing antibody production.
