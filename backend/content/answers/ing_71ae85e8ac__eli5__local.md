When we talk about **prompt optimization with DSPE (Dynamic Structured Prompt Engineering)**, think of the chef writing a recipe. The prompt is the recipe’s instructions. DSPE helps the chef rewrite those instructions step by step: first it asks “What flavor do you want?” (objective), then “Which ingredients will give that flavor?” (constraints), and finally “How to combine them for best taste?” (execution).
