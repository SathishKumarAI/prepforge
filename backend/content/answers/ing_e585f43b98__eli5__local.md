An **attention mechanism** is like a smart DJ who picks only the most relevant conversations for you: it listens to all voices but highlights the ones that matter, letting your brain focus on key words instead of every noise.

In standard attention, the DJ checks every pair of people (every word with every other word) to decide what matters—slow and heavy when the crowd is huge. **Efficient attention variants** are clever shortcuts:
