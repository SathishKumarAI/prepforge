In an interview loop, we let the student (the AI) give a response, then a human reviewer plays the role of the interviewer: they read the answer, ask follow‑up questions or point out mistakes, and provide corrections. The student uses that feedback to adjust its next attempt.

- **Human reviewer** – a person who checks accuracy and style.  
- **Feedback** – notes on what was right, wrong, or unclear.
