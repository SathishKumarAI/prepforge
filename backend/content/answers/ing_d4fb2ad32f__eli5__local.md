Imagine you’re baking a cake: each ingredient (data) and step (analysis or model) is written down in a notebook so anyone can follow it exactly. DSPy records every “step” – loading data, cleaning it, training a machine‑learning model, evaluating results – and links them together into a single, reproducible script.
