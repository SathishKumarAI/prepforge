Think of a transformer like a bustling newsroom where every journalist (a word in a sentence) can instantly read the headlines from every other journalist, no matter how far apart they are. In a traditional “recurrent” model, journalists would sit in a line and hand the story to the next one; this slows down the flow and makes it hard for early reporters to influence later ones. The transformer gives each reporter a direct phone line to all others, so they can weigh every headline at once. It uses *attention* (the “phone call”) to decide which other headlines matter most, and *self‑attention* lets them do this in parallel, not step by step. This architecture allows the system to understand context quickly and accurately, making it a powerful tool for language tasks.
