Imagine your kitchen is a **data architecture**—the way all the drawers, cabinets, appliances, and counters are laid out so you can cook any meal quickly and cleanly.  
A data architecture is the blueprint that shows where raw ingredients (raw data) come in, how they’re stored (databases, files), how they’re transformed into useful recipes (data processing pipelines), and where the finished dish appears on the table for customers (analytics dashboards or applications).  

Just as a chef needs an organized kitchen to avoid stepping on hot pans, software teams need a clear data architecture so that engineers can add new “recipes” without breaking existing ones.  
Key terms: **Data pipeline**—the sequence of steps that moves and reshapes data; **data lake**—a big pantry where all raw ingredients are kept before being sorted. This structure lets you serve fresh insights on demand, just like a well‑organized kitchen serves delicious meals at any time.
