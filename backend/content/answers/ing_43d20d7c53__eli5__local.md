Imagine you’re at a big library that’s actually a giant art gallery. Every book (or piece of text) is turned into a small, colored marble. The color and size of each marble represent its meaning—similar books get marbles that sit close together, while very different books are far apart. That “placement” space is the **vector space**.

The process of deciding how to paint each marble comes from **training data**: millions of real texts that the AI has read. The AI looks at patterns in those texts and learns rules (like a sculptor learning how to shape clay). When it finishes, every new piece of text can be turned into its own marble and placed automatically next to related marbles.

So, training data are the raw books that teach the AI; embeddings are the colored marbles that capture meaning; vector spaces are the gallery floor where those marbles sit close or far based on similarity.
