A recommendation system is like a friendly librarian who knows every book in the library and can suggest titles you’ll love. When you check out “The Hobbit,” the librarian looks at all other books you’ve borrowed, sees patterns (you also liked “Lord of the Rings” and “Harry Potter”), and then points to new ones that fit those tastes. In machine learning, we give the system a big list of items (movies, songs, products) and records of what people have chosen or rated. The algorithm learns the hidden “style fingerprints” of each item and of each user, then predicts which unseen items you’ll enjoy next. Think of it as matching your personal taste profile with new titles before you even think to ask.
