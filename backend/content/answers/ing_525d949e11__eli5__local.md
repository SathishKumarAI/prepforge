Imagine you’re a detective who can read a huge library of books and then predict what sentence comes next before you finish reading. That’s the core idea behind **perplexity** in AI: it measures how surprised a language model is by the text it sees. Lower perplexity means the model is “confident” – it thinks the next word is almost certain, just like an experienced detective who knows the plot.

1. **Collect data** – gather millions of sentences.  
2. **Train a model** – let it learn patterns by predicting words.  
3. **Measure perplexity** – check how well it predicts unseen text; the lower, the better.
