Think of a neural network like a big, friendly kitchen where recipes are learned by tasting and adjusting ingredients.

**Neural network = Kitchen**  
Each **neuron (ingredient station)** takes in some “flavors” (numbers), mixes them with a tiny bit of seasoning called *weights*, adds a pinch of *bias* (extra flavor you can tweak), then uses a simple rule (*activation function*) to decide whether to pass the dish on or not.  

**Learning = Tasting and adjusting**  
You give the kitchen a sample dish (input) and tell it how good the final result should be (desired output). The kitchen tastes its own work, compares it with what you wanted, and then quietly nudges each station’s seasoning so next time it’ll be closer to the perfect flavor.  

Just like a chef improving recipes over many tastings, a neural network slowly refines all its stations until it can produce the right answer every time.
