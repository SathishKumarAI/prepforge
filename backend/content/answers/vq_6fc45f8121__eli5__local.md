Think of an office building where each room holds a different kind of valuable information—finance, HR, or engineering.  
**Role‑Based Access Control (RBAC)** is like the security system that gives employees keys only to the rooms they need for their job.  

- **Roles** are job titles such as “Accountant,” “HR Manager,” or “Data Engineer.”  
- **Permissions** are the rights to open specific doors, e.g., “view payroll data” or “edit database schemas.”  
When you’re assigned a role, the system automatically bundles all its permissions and hands you the corresponding keys.  

So if you’re a Data Engineer, you get the key to the analytics room but not the HR vault. If you later change jobs, your keys shift with your new role—no manual re‑granting needed. This keeps data safe while letting people do their work efficiently.
