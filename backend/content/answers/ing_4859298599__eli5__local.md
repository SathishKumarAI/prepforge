Imagine a busy kitchen where chefs (your machine‑learning models) need ingredients (data). In **advanced queuing**, the kitchen uses a *publish‑subscribe* system: every time fresh produce arrives, the “produce station” posts an announcement (“new carrots available”) to a bulletin board. Any chef who needs carrots puts a *subscription* on that topic and receives a copy of the news whenever it’s posted.

In this setup:

- **Publisher** = data source (e.g., sensor or database) that *publishes* new records.
- **Subscriber** = ML model or service that *subscribes* to relevant topics (e.g., “temperature updates”).
- **Queue** = the list of pending messages for each subscriber, ensuring no data is lost even if a model is busy.

Thus, models get only the information they care about and can process it at their own pace. This decouples data production from consumption, just like chefs getting ingredients on demand without waiting in line.
