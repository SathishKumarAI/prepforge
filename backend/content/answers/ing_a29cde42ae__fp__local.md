### Why “Together‑AI” interview questions exist

In the real world, an AI system rarely lives in isolation: it sits inside a product, talks to other services, and must obey business rules. The *fundamental problem* is **integrating intelligence with existing infrastructure while preserving safety, fairness, and interpretability**.  
To guarantee that integration works, interviewers ask candidates to reason about:

1. **Data flow & latency** – How does a model’s inference time affect the user‑experience pipeline?  
2. **Versioning & rollback** – What happens if a new model introduces bias or performance drift?  
3. **Explainability across teams** – Can a non‑technical stakeholder understand why the AI behaves a certain way?  

These questions are rooted in **optimization under constraints**: you must maximize predictive accuracy while minimizing cost, risk, and regulatory violation. They also tap into **information theory**—how much uncertainty is reduced by the model versus how much noise it introduces.

#### Non‑obvious insight

Most people focus on *model accuracy*, but a perfect algorithm is useless if it cannot be *reconciled* with business logic. A candidate who can articulate **cross‑domain governance** (e.g., mapping model outputs to legal compliance checks) demonstrates mastery of the *holistic AI system* rather than isolated ML tricks.
