**Why it’s great for machine learning**

* **Fast, real‑time flow** – Cars travel at high speed, so models can get fresh data instantly.  
* **Durable storage** – All cars are kept on the highway’s side lanes; even if a lane closes, you still have a copy to replay later.  
* **Scalable lanes** – Add more lanes (brokers) or widen them without stopping traffic—your pipeline grows with your data.  
* **Built‑in load balancing** – Traffic is split automatically across many “parking spots” (partitions), so no single spot gets stuck.  
* **Easy integration** – Drivers from any car brand (Python, Java, Spark) can hop onto the highway; Kafka speaks a common language called *producer/consumer*.
