Imagine building a city of toy blocks that represent AI models. **Scale** is like adding more blocks—each new block makes the whole structure taller and stronger, letting it handle bigger tasks (like answering many questions at once).  
**Fragmentation** happens when different builders start making their own tiny towers with slightly different rules. The towers don’t fit together perfectly, so you can’t easily move a block from one tower to another; they’re separate.  
**Dependency chains** are the strings that tie blocks together. If one block depends on another for support, a problem in the first block pulls down everything linked to it. In AI, a small bug or data issue in an early model can ripple through all later models that rely on its output.
