Imagine learning to play a musical instrument. At first you can copy sheet music—your fingers move exactly where the notes say they should. Grokking is like finally feeling the song in your bones: you don’t just repeat; you sense the rhythm, anticipate the next phrase, and improvise naturally. In AI training, we start with data (the “sheet music”) that teaches a model patterns. Grokking happens when those patterns become internalized so deeply that the model can apply them to new situations without explicit guidance—like improvising on a familiar chord progression. While every training step builds toward this deep understanding, grokking isn’t a separate algorithm; it’s the moment the model’s knowledge shifts from surface copying to genuine insight, making its predictions more flexible and robust.
