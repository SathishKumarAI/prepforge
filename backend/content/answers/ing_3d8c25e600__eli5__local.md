Think of your mobile app as a recipe you’ve cooked in the kitchen (the code) and now want to share it with friends (users).  
**Release** is like setting up a small pop‑up shop: you put the finished dish on a table, label it (“Version 1.0”), make sure the ingredients (dependencies) are listed, and let people taste it.

Steps in plain terms:

1. **Build** – Compile your code into a single file that runs on phones (an *APK* for Android or an *IPA* for iOS).  
2. **Test** – Let a few friends try it; fix any bugs you spot.  
3. **Publish** – Upload the built file to the Google Play Store or Apple App Store, filling in a short description and screenshots.  
4. **Monitor** – After launch, watch download numbers and crash reports, just like checking how many people liked your dish.

*Version* is simply a label that tells users which iteration of the app they’re running. *Dependencies* are extra libraries you borrowed to make features work. Once the store approves it, everyone can install the app from their phone’s “app shop.”
