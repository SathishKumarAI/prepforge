### Why a *Better Summary* Matters

When hiring, recruiters face an information‑capping problem: they receive thousands of resumes but only minutes to decide who merits a call. The résumé’s first paragraph is the **signal** that must survive this bottleneck. If we treat each résumé as a noisy channel, the summary is the encoder that compresses *relevant* skill‑experience pairs into a concise vector.

A well‑crafted summary solves three optimization subproblems:

1. **Relevance filtering** – select only those competencies that map onto the job’s objective function (e.g., “deploy ML models at scale”).  
2. **Differentiation** – encode a unique *utility* vector so the candidate’s signal stands out from competitors with identical skill lists.  
3. **Readability compression** – balance information density against cognitive load, ensuring the receiver can extract meaning in < 5 seconds.
