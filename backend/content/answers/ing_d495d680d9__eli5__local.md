Think of an AI as a very curious traveler who keeps a photo‑journal.  
**Episodic memory** is that journal—each page records a *specific event* the traveler has just experienced: where it went, what it saw, how it felt, and what it did next. In AI terms, an **agent** is the traveling program; its **state** is the current snapshot of everything it knows (location, sensor readings, internal variables). When the agent encounters something new, it writes a fresh entry that ties the event to that state. Later, when faced with a similar situation, the agent can flip back through those pages, recalling exactly how it handled comparable events before. This “past‑experience” memory lets the AI learn from real instances rather than just from abstract rules.
