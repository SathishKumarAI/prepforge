Imagine you’re baking a cake in a kitchen that’s locked down for safety—no open flames, no messy chemicals, and every step is monitored. That’s what an **E2B Sandbox** does for AI: it gives the model a safe, isolated “kitchen” where it can run code, access data, or use APIs without touching your personal computer or exposing sensitive files.

If you let the AI execute locally on your own machine, it could accidentally overwrite important documents, download malware, or leak private information. In the sandbox, all actions happen inside a controlled container that is automatically wiped after each task, so nothing spills out and your system stays protected.

Think of the sandbox as a secure, disposable lab bench: you can experiment freely, but once you’re done, everything is cleaned up and your own environment remains untouched.
