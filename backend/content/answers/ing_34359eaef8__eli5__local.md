Think of an **AI observability platform** like a smart home security system, but for the “brains” inside computers—large language models (LLMs), apps that use them, and autonomous AI agents.

*Observability* means watching what’s happening inside so you can spot problems early. Just as a security camera records video, an observability platform logs every request, response, error, and decision made by the AI. It stitches these logs into a clear story: “At 3 pm, the model gave answer X; it used dataset Y; latency was 120 ms.”  

The platform also watches *performance* (speed), *health* (is it crashing?), and *behavior* (are outputs safe or biased?). If something looks off—like a sudden drop in accuracy—it alerts you before users notice.  

In short, an AI observability platform is the dashboard that keeps your AI systems running smoothly, just as a home security system keeps your house safe.
