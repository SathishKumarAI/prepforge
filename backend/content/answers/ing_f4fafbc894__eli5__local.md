Think of Langfuse like a **smart dashboard for a factory that makes language models**.  
When you run your own “factory” (your private LLM), the dashboard shows every part: how fast each machine (prompt) runs, whether any parts overheat (errors), and what products (responses) came out.  

With *self‑host Langfuse* you install this dashboard on your own servers instead of sending data to a third party. That means:

- **Full control** – you decide where the data lives, keeping sensitive conversations private.  
- **Real‑time monitoring** – instantly see latency, success rates, and resource usage.  
- **Audit trails** – logs every prompt‑response pair so you can review or debug later.  
- **Open source flexibility** – tweak the code, add custom metrics, or integrate with your own tools.

So Langfuse is the observability cockpit that keeps your LLM’s inner workings clear, safe, and adjustable right at home.
