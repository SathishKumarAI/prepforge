Think of a machine learning *agent* as a little robot explorer in a big maze.  
It has **three parts**:  

1. **Perception (the eyes)** – it reads the current state of the maze (e.g., what walls are visible).  
2. **Policy (the brain)** – it decides which direction to move next based on its “experience” so far.  
3. **Action (the legs)** – it moves forward, turns left or right, and receives new sensory input.

Just like a real robot learns from trial‑and‑error: every time it chooses an action, it gets a reward signal (a bump of “good” or “bad”). The agent updates its policy to favor actions that earned more rewards.  

In short, an *agent* is the entity that observes, decides, and acts in an environment, learning over time to achieve a goal.
