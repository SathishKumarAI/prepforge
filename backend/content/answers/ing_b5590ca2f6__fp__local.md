**Why an LLM Engineer is a “Data‑to‑Decision” Specialist**

Modern AI systems are built on *parameterized probability models* that learn patterns from data. In 2026 the core problem is turning raw, heterogeneous information into a *compact, interpretable representation* that can be queried, fine‑tuned, and deployed at scale. An LLM engineer sits at the intersection of **optimization**, **information theory**, and **system design**: they craft the loss landscape (training), compress it for inference (quantization, pruning), and embed it in production pipelines (serving, monitoring).
