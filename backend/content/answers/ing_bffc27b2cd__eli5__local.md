Imagine a self‑driving car that can’t decide when to pull over for a broken brake light. A **Human‑in‑the‑Loop (HITL) feedback loop** is like having a safety driver in the passenger seat who watches the road and, if something looks off, gently nudges the car or pulls it over. In AI, the “car” is an automated system that learns from data; the “safety driver” is a human operator who reviews model predictions, spots errors, and feeds corrections back into training.

An **AI Site Reliability Engineer (SRE)** is the person who builds and maintains those safety drivers and the surrounding infrastructure. They set up monitoring dashboards, alert rules, and automated retraining pipelines so that when a model misbehaves, the human can quickly intervene, record the fix, and the system learns from it—keeping the AI reliable, safe, and continuously improving.
