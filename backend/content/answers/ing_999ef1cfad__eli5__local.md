**Short‑Term Context Management** is like keeping a notebook on your desk while you work on a project.  
When you start writing a paragraph, you jot down the main idea so that you can refer back to it later. The notebook holds only what matters right now—your current topic, the key points you’ve already mentioned, and any quick notes about where you’re headed next. It doesn’t keep every single word you ever wrote; instead, it stores a concise “snapshot” of the conversation so far.

In an AI chat, this snapshot is called the *short‑term context*. The system remembers just enough information from the last few turns—what was asked, what answers were given, and any important details—to give relevant, coherent replies. It discards older parts that are no longer needed, freeing memory for new inputs. Think of it as a tidy desk: you keep the most useful notes at hand, while the rest goes into an archive.
