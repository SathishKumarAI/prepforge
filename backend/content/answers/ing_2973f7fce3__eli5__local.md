Imagine a big library that never closes because it has two identical copies of every book, each on its own shelf. If one shelf breaks, the other still holds all the books and visitors can keep reading without interruption. That’s “high availability” for AI systems: they’re built with duplicate hardware, backup power, and software that automatically switches to a spare when something fails.

**Where to learn more**

- **Books:** *Designing Data‑Intensive Applications* (Martin Kleppmann) – explains fault tolerance in detail.  
- **Online courses:** Coursera’s “Architecting Highly Available Systems” (Google Cloud).  
- **Websites:** AWS, Azure, and GCP documentation on “Availability Zones” and “Auto Scaling.”
