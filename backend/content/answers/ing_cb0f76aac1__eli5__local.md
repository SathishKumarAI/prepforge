**Managed Platform** is the restaurant: you bring your ingredients, pick a menu item (a pre‑built model), and the kitchen handles everything—preparing, cooking, plating, and cleaning up. You get great results fast but can’t see or tweak the stove’s heat settings.  
**Self‑hosting** is like buying a pot, an oven, and all the spices to cook at home. You control every knob: you can change the temperature, add extra herbs, or swap out ingredients as you learn. The trade‑off? You must set up the kitchen (install software, manage servers), keep it running, and fix any glitches yourself.
