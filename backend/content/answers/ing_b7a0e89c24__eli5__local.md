Think of an agentic AI as a chef who runs a busy kitchen.  
**Memory maintenance (context)** is like the chef’s *current recipe list*—the dishes they’re cooking right now, the ingredients on hand, and the orders that just came in. It changes every few minutes, so the chef can adapt instantly to new requests.

**Memory engineering**, by contrast, is the chef’s *long‑term cookbook*. It contains all the recipes the chef has learned over years—how to bake a cake, how to make stock, seasoning blends—and it never disappears when a new order arrives. The chef consults this cookbook only when they need a specific technique or ingredient that isn’t part of the immediate kitchen setup.

In an AI system, context is the short‑term data fed into each decision, while memory engineering builds and preserves the deeper knowledge base that guides decisions over time.
