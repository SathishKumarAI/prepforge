Think of a computer system as a busy kitchen.  
**Failover** is like having a backup stove ready to light up automatically if the main stove goes out. The backup runs quietly until it’s needed, then takes over cooking without anyone noticing.  

**Failback** happens when the main stove is fixed and you want to switch back. You turn off the backup, re‑light the main stove, and resume normal service—like returning to the original setup after a repair.

- **Failover:** automatic transfer of workload to a standby system when the primary fails.  
- **Failback:** moving work back to the primary once it’s healthy again.  

This ensures food (data or services) keeps cooking smoothly even if one burner hiccups.
