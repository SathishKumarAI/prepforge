**Inference fundamentals** are what happen after an AI model has learned from data. Think of it like a chef who’s practiced recipes (training) and now must cook a dish for guests (inference). The chef already knows the ingredients and steps, but when a new order comes in, they quickly pick out the right recipe, measure the amounts, and put everything together to serve the final meal.

In AI terms:

- **Model** = the chef’s cookbook.  
- **Weights/parameters** = the exact measurements of each ingredient.  
- **Input data** (e.g., an image) = what the chef sees on the counter.  
- **Inference** = the act of following the recipe to produce a result—like classifying that image or translating text.

The key point: inference is fast, deterministic, and uses only the stored knowledge; no new learning happens during this stage.
