You look at three simple cues: the color of the top, how firm it feels when you press it, and whether the smell is sweet and even. In AI “RAG” (Retrieval‑Augmented Generation) evaluation, we use similar quick checks—called **Key Production Signals**—to decide if the model’s answer is good.

2. **Coherence** – Just as a firm cake stays together when sliced, coherence ensures sentences flow logically and don’t jump or contradict themselves.  
3. **Accuracy** – Think of the sweet scent; accuracy verifies that the facts are correct and not invented.
