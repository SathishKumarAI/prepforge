**Why the “Cursor” interview matters**  
At its core, a hiring manager wants *trust* that you can build reliable, maintainable code under pressure. That trust is built by observing three things: (1) your *problem‑solving intuition*, (2) how you *communicate* your reasoning, and (3) the *quality* of your implementation. The Cursor interview forces all three to surface.

### 2. Question Types  
1. **Algorithmic puzzles** – O(n log n) sorting, DP, graph traversal.  
   *Why?* They expose your ability to reduce a problem to known patterns and optimize.  
2. **Coding‑style problems** – Implement an API or small library.  
   *Why?* Demonstrates clean code, edge‑case handling, and test writing.  
3. **Design scenarios** – “Build a URL shortener” or “Cache layer for high‑traffic service.”  
   *Why?* Reveals how you balance scalability, consistency, and cost.

Mastering this storytelling turns even a mediocre algorithm into a standout performance.
