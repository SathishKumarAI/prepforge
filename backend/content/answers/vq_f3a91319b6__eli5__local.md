Think of a library where every book is stored on shelves that are labeled by subject, author, or year.  
A **SELECT** statement is the librarian’s request: “Show me all the books that match these criteria.”  

In a database, data lives in tables—like rows on shelves. A SELECT asks for specific columns (the information you want) and can filter rows with conditions (only 2023 releases, only books by J.K. Rowling). It can also sort or combine tables, but at its core it’s just *pulling out* the requested pieces of data from the stored “books.”
