Imagine a chef preparing a meal. First the chef *plans*—choosing ingredients, deciding on flavors, and sketching out steps. Then the chef *executes*—actually chopping, sautéing, plating. In AI, a **Plan‑and‑Execute** design pattern works the same way: one part of the system builds a plan (a sequence of actions or decisions), while another part carries it out.

- **Planner**: looks at the goal and current situation, then lays out steps to reach that goal.  
- **Executor**: follows those steps, adjusting as things change on the fly.

This split lets each part stay simple: the planner doesn’t have to know how to stir a pot; the executor doesn’t need to decide what dessert to make. Together they produce flexible, reliable AI behavior—just like a well‑coordinated kitchen.
