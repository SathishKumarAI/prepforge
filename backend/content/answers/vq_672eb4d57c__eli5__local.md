**Key advantages**

2. **Functional features** – Higher‑order functions, pattern matching, and immutability help avoid bugs that come from shared state.  
3. **Concise syntax** – A single line can replace many lines of boilerplate, speeding up development.  
4. **Strong static typing** – The compiler catches errors early, giving you confidence as your data pipelines grow.
