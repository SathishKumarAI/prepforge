### How to prepare

1. **Master fundamentals** – arrays, strings, trees, graphs, DP, hashing.

* Forgetting to ask clarifying questions—shows engagement.

2. **Outline your solution** (pseudocode + complexity).  
3. **Write clean, commented code**; test edge cases mentally or on paper.  
4. **Explain your thought process aloud**—Microsoft values transparent reasoning.
