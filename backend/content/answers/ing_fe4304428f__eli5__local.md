Imagine a librarian who wants to answer your question, but instead of looking in the whole library she only checks a few shelves she thinks are relevant. If those shelves don’t contain the right book, her answer will be weak—even if she’s good at finding books. That gap between what she can retrieve and what you actually need is the **Retrieval Quality Gap**.

In AI, *retrieval* means pulling useful documents or data from a huge knowledge base. *Quality* refers to how well those items match your query. The gap happens when the system pulls items that are only loosely related or misses the best ones, so the final answer feels fuzzy or incomplete. Closing this gap—by better searching and smarter filtering—is key to making AI assistants truly helpful.
