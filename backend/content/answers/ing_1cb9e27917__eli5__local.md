**Chunking strategies in AI are like packing a suitcase for a trip.**  
When you travel, you first decide what items belong together: shirts go with socks, toiletries with toothbrushes, and a jacket with a scarf. You group these “chunks” so that the suitcase stays organized and you can find anything quickly.  

In machine learning, a model receives a long string of data—text, numbers, or images—and splits it into meaningful groups (tokens). It processes each chunk separately, then stitches the results back together. This reduces complexity, speeds up computation, and helps the system remember context over longer distances.  

*Unavoidable term:* **Token** – a basic unit of input (a word, sub‑word piece, or image patch) that the model works with.  
By chunking data into tokens, AI “packs” information efficiently, just as you pack your suitcase for a smooth journey.
