**Zero‑shot “Chain of Thought” (CoT)**  
Imagine you’re at a kitchen table with a recipe book that never mentions the dish you want to make—say, a new dessert. You look at the ingredients and decide step by step how to combine them: first mix the sugar, then fold in the eggs, then bake for 20 minutes. That mental walk‑through is *zero‑shot CoT*: the model hasn’t seen that exact problem before (zero‑shot) but it “thinks” out loud—generating a short chain of reasoning steps—before giving an answer.

**Why “Let’s think step by step” blew up**  
When researchers asked language models to say *“Let’s think step by step”* and then write the reasoning, the models produced surprisingly correct answers. It’s like asking a student to explain how they solved a math problem; writing out each move forces the model to follow logical rules instead of guessing. The phrase became famous because it unlocked a simple prompt that made large AI systems much more reliable on tricky questions without extra training.
